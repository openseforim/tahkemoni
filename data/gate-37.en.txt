Thus says Heman the Ezrahite: I journeyed from Tyre
as far as Hazor,
with a company
of men of arm and might.
And while we were going on the path in the wilderness, in the Arabah, we found a broad meadow [Isa 30:23],
and a valley pleasant and beloved.
Its plants were clothed in settings of gold [Ps 45:14],
and on it its blossoms were like sparks of flame.
Its winds blew
from its four quarters,
its dews dripped,
and its shades were thickly tangled.
Its plants went up to the heart of heaven [Deut 4:11],
and the ground was a land of brooks of water [Deut 8:7].
And when we came upon it,
we rejoiced with great joy,
and in it we rested.
We chose it as a lair for the beasts,
and a rest for souls.
We sat there to find repose
from our great weariness,
and a deep sleep fell upon us [Gen 15:12],
and we were caught in the cords of slumber.
While we were still asleep, we heard a voice of trembling [Jer 30:5],
and fear and trembling came upon us [Job 4:14].
When we rose up, we looked, and behold, a man of the company
was crying out with a great and bitter cry [Gen 27:34].
He would split the flinty rock with his cries,
and shatter hearts with his roaring.
And when we asked him,
and searched out his secret,
he told us that a snake had bitten him,
one that no charm can master [Jer 8:17].
We found no counsel for him
as the soul desired,
for we were in a parched land [Ps 63:2],
no place of healing or bandage,
and no cure for a fresh wound [Isa 1:6].
And while we were grieving at his grief,
and weeping for his pain,
a man stood over him,
whose tongue spat like an adder [Prov 23:32].
He said to us: "Why do you look at one another [Gen 42:1],
and see a terror and are afraid [Job 6:21]?
If you give me my wage, I will heal his sickness,
and the wound of his blow [Isa 30:26],
and you will see his healing eye to eye [Isa 52:8];
and before he sets out from here he will return to his former state ⚠1."
We said to him: "If you make good your words,
and pay your vows [Ps 50:14],
we will make your wage great,
and then your light will break forth like the dawn [Isa 58:8]."
He hurried and made a circle of stones on the ground,
and set small sticks upon them,
and covered the sticks with earth, and made it into a kind of cave,
and made the shape of an opening in the cave.
He stood like a watchman at his post,
and began to burn incense,
and he spoke up and said:

"Hear, O mountains, my case [Mic 6:2],
and give ear to my speech,
and incline your ears as I preach,
to the words of my mouth [Ps 78:1].
I adjure the wilderness and the plain,
the sea and the dry land,
the pits and the valleys,
the drought-lands [Hos 13:5] and the parched places,
the dens of lions [Song 4:8],
and the ravenous beasts [Isa 35:9]:
by my drawn sword,
and by my bared arm [Isa 52:10],
by the hovering storm,
and by the burning fire,
by the radiance that rushes on,
and by the flood that sweeps along,
I have decreed upon you all to rouse the snakes from their hiding places,
and drive the adders from their holes,
and bring out the scorpions and the asps from their haunts,
and every one of them that is a winged flier,
that makes the pillars of heaven tremble [Job 26:11]:
let them all come
from the ends of the world,
and gather on the wings of the winds
from the four quarters.
They shall have no escape and no refuge,
no stronghold and no city of refuge [Num 35:11],
and let not one of them get away,
until they all come into the double loop of my bridle [Job 41:5], and are caught in my cords,
and in the bonds of my yoke.
Make yourselves ready and draw near;
take heart and gather [Isa 46:8] [Zeph 2:1];
run about and burst forth,
and gather from every side.
Let every adder come out of its chamber,
and every snake from its hole,
and every viper from the cleft of its rock,
and every scorpion from its wall,
and every fiery serpent from its den,
and every asp from its lair,
by the strength of the might of Him who exalts it and makes it fierce ⚠2,
and by the decree of Him who draws it out and puts it away,
and by the rebuke of Him who rouses it and drives it from its chamber.
Hurry and stream here,
and do not delay;
and seek my face,
draw near,
and do not tarry;
gather yourselves,
and do not be slow;
make haste,
and do not linger,
before I send against you my evil judgments [Ezek 14:21],
and pangs of plagues every moment ⚠3,
and a band of angels of evil [Ps 78:49],
and a bereaving blow,
and devouring fire [Deut 4:24],
by the strength of Him whose is the dominion,
and the might and the power."
And he took up his parable and said:

I adjure you, all who crawl in the dust [Deut 32:24], / who dwell in parched lands and drought-lands,
and you winged ones too, and every fiery serpent / that dwells in wildernesses and in wastes,
by God and His glory and the strength of His hand, / and by the clouds of the winds and the thick clouds:
come at my command, and for my wish / be ready and standing,
before I send against your host the seraphs / of wrath, hewn of fire and flames [Ps 29:7].

He was silent for about an hour, sitting on the ground,

and he listened, diligently, with much heed [Isa 21:7].
And behold, a voice of uproar from the city [Isa 66:6] ⚠4,
that wakes the sleepers,
and sets hearts aflame,
and crumbles rocks,
and stirs the dead.
Dust went up to the heart of heaven [Deut 4:11],
the sun went down at noon [Amos 8:9],
and the eyes grew dark.
And when we lifted up our eyes,
we looked,
and behold, from every side, living creatures full of eyes [Ezek 1:18],
and fiery serpents with wings;
some of them standing without legs,
and some with horns on their heads.
And after them snakes,
and adders strange and hard;
some of them standing without legs, like planks,
and some with two heads.
They made the earth quake with their running,
and the world tremble with their gathering,
and all of them were coming toward us.
They set their faces toward us [Hab 1:9],
and shot at us with the arrows of their eyes,
and they had nearly swallowed us alive [Ps 124:3].
When we saw this great sight, we made haste to flee,
but we had no strength left [Dan 10:8].
The man laughed at us,
and said to us: "Let each of you sit still where he is ⚠5,
and let no man go out of his place [Exod 16:29].
Be strong, do not be afraid;
stand still and see [Exod 14:13]
the wonders of the Creator, which He taught His servants,
and revealed to them His secrets [Amos 3:7]."
He rose quickly,
and opened the mouth of the cave,
and in went
all the snakes.
He shut the mouth of the cave on them; after that he spoke in a whisper,
and adjured every snake.
Then he opened the mouth of the cave, and they all came out one by one,
and went off together,
and none was left in the cave but one.
And whenever it wished to flee,
it had no strength [Dan 11:6].
At times it would gather its strength and think to escape,
and it went out, to and fro [Gen 8:7],
but it could not flee or turn aside,
as if it were bound in fetters of iron [Ps 149:8].
And when we saw it holding fast to its place,
we knew that it was the one that did the harm ⚠6.
The man called and said: "Bring the sick man to me,
and you will see a wondrous deed."
They brought the sick man to him, and he turned the place of the wound toward the mouth of the cave,
and cried out at the snake with a voice of rebuke.
And the snake was cunning [Gen 3:1], and understood his rebuke:
a rebuke goes deeper into one who understands [Prov 17:10].
It put its head out of the hole,
and it was black as coal.
It set its mouth on the place of the bite,
and sucked out all the poison and all the venom poured out [Ezek 20:33] ⚠7,
and at once healing came up for the sick man [Jer 8:22],
and the snake fell dead in the battle line ⚠8.

Said the teller: When we saw the awesome things he had done and his wondrous deeds,
we said: "Blessed is He who has given of His wisdom to those who fear Him [b. Berakhot 58a]."
And each man gave him of his wealth,
as much as his right hand could reach.
After that we asked him about his dwelling places,
and his generations by his families [Num 1:20],
and he answered and said:

When it is time for speech, I am a master of words, / and when battle draws near, I lay warriors low.
I am Ḥever, a companion to companions, / and for snakes I am a charmer of charms [Ps 58:6] ⚠9.

Said the teller: When I heard his song,
I knew him,
and made known to all the company his rank,
and the honour of the glory of his greatness.
We rejoiced all the way in his company.
After that we parted, and each man went his own path,
and he too set his face toward his own land.
