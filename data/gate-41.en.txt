Thus says Heman the Ezrahite: I joined company with merchant men,
who passed from town to town,
carrying all kinds of wares:
spices, embroidery and precious stones.
And while we were going on the road to one of the towns
that lie among the villages,
I saw people gathering,
and looking out from every hill.
My desire drew me
to leave my companions,
and run with them
to the place where their crowds were collecting,
until we came into a square,
and there was a great crowd.
And behold, an old man was waging a quarrel
with a bold and hard woman,
whose clothing was the clothing of wickedness,
and whose face was like brass.

The man said to her: "Praise to God, who has made us great among those who fear Him,
and made us rulers over His creatures,
and set us first among those He called,
and brought forth from us the stock of His prophets.
He gave men an advantage over women,
because they are sanctified by His holiness.
He withheld His commandments from the women,
and did not bind them to Torah and commandment,
because they are nakedness;
and He commanded them to keep hidden in their houses,
for the shame of their faces.
This is to make known that the world and all that is in it was created for the honor of man,
and is called by his name.
The woman too, had it not been for the man, would not have been created,
nor called by a name [Gen 2:23]."

The woman said: "Ah, sinful nation [Isa 1:4],
babbling like the thrusts of a sword [Prov 12:18],
and leaning after falsehood!
In a refuge of vanity you have hidden,
and in a fortress of lies you have walled yourself in [Isa 28:15],
and you have not spoken what is right.
You ought to know that, were it not for women,
men would not have been created.
For we are the root and you are the branch; and how can branches grow without roots?
But you are brazen of brow and hard [Ezek 3:7],
and you do not know the truth,
and what is right is hidden from you.
If you judged rightly,
you would take care for the high rank of women,
and you would serve them.
And if you have an advantage over us, we too have an advantage over you,
for your need of us is like our need of you."

The man said: "Put your hand to your mouth [Prov 30:32], you despised and loathsome one,
trampled like dung on the dunghill,
loaded with every disgrace,
and full of scorn and derision!
Would you liken yourself to males,
and not know your own blemish?
For woman was created only to serve man like a handmaid,
and to lick his dust [Isa 49:23] like a maggot and a worm [Job 25:6].
And she was cut from his ribs
only so that you may know that she is bone of his bones [Gen 2:23],
a limb of what came forth from him ⚠1,
and a maid among his maidservants."

The woman said: "Are you not ashamed, you fool and brute [Ps 49:11],
that you open your mouth wide against me [Job 16:10],
when all of you is full of filth,
the spring of excrement,
and the root of uncleanness?
With your words you have shown your crop and its filth ⚠2,
and laid bare the evil of your ways,
and brought your filth out of your mouth.
Now turn back and cover your excrement [Deut 23:14],
and hide your slander.
Do you not know that if you are honored, all your honor is through us,
and we are your rest as long as you live?
But you, in the hour of your need of us,
bow down to the soles of our feet;
and when you get what you want, you grow proud over us,
and want to bring us low.
Such is the way of worthless men: they delight in the goodness of God [Neh 9:25] and do not thank Him for it,
nor pay it any heed.
In times of ease they grow proud against God and say, 'Who will humble us?'
and in their time of trouble they say, 'Arise and save us' [Jer 2:27]."

The man said: "If woman is the foundation of man,
she is a foundation that shames and stinks [Prov 13:5].
So too the lofty pearls come forth
from the depths of the abyss,
and from the deeps of the seas.
So too the earth is the foundation of all creatures,
and by its name the sons of man are called;
from it the prophets came forth,
and from its dust the righteous and the God-fearing were created.
Yet it is not right to say, because dust is their foundation,
that dust is better than man."

The woman said: "Do you not know that through us your joy is made full,
and without us your sighing grows great?
How then do you lift yourselves over us,
when need bows you down to our feet?
And if you behave toward us with pride,
desire will bow you down to us."

The man said: "The desire of a man for a woman is an evil desire,
and the need of an hour,
like the craving of a sick man for meat,
by which his sickness grows and does not lessen.
The sick man knows that by eating it
his illness will grow,
and his harm will double;
and yet he cannot keep away from it,
for desire draws him to it.
So too woman is a cause of harm to man,
and like an adder she stings [Prov 23:32] ⚠3;
and yet he cannot keep away from her,
for desire draws him to her,
though he knows her for what she is.
For when a man takes a wife,
he has fallen into an evil and hard net [Eccl 9:12];
for on her account he brings his soul into troubles,
and has his fill of bitterness,
to clothe her and give her drink;
and he may not lessen her food, her clothing and her conjugal rights [Exod 21:10].
Who made us need this bitter trouble,
and this terrible hardship?
Is not the grave better for a man
than this grief and breaking?
It is better to dwell in a desert land [Prov 21:19]
than with a contentious woman in a shared house [Prov 21:9] ⚠4."

The woman said: "May God, terrible in His deeds [Ps 66:5], cut off
the tongue that speaks great things [Ps 12:4],
and the lip that contrives wicked deeds!
For your tongue is longer than the sea [Job 11:9],
set in order like a sword,
to make falsehood prevail over faith,
and to raise the hand of wrong over what is right.
And if the man prides himself on the vision of prophecy,
and speaks great things because God spoke with him,
has He not spoken with us also [Num 12:2]?
From among us came many prophetesses,
who stood in visions like the women who served with mirrors [Exod 38:8] ⚠5,
and the spirit of prophecy came mightily upon them,
and they were called to the Most High [Hos 11:7]."

The man said: "The prophecy of women
comes from the many sins of men.
For when men are masters of piety and holiness,
the spirit of prophecy does not come upon a woman.
Only when their wickedness grows great
is their longing put in the hand of a woman ⚠6,
as Deborah prophesied
when Israel was worshipping idols,
and as Huldah prophesied
when the sins of Israel had grown beyond measure.
And a woman, even if she set her dwelling in the skies,
and the lights of heaven bowed down before her,
a man would buy her for a maidservant,
and lie with her and humble her [Gen 34:2];
and the woman and her children shall be her master's [Exod 21:4]."
And he took up his parable and said:

Blessed is He who allotted honor to man, / and set glory on man above woman,
and made him head and lord over her, / while she is bound to him as a slave and subdued ⚠7.
We in truth are the holy seed of the living God, / who took His Law for an inheritance [Deut 33:4].
The prophets too were created from us, / who turn the sea into dry land [Ps 66:6].
For there is nothing upright in women, except / that they are to man nakedness and shame.

The teller said: And when they had finished their enmity
and their quarrel,
I set my heart upon the man,
and turned to him with the eye of my mind;
and I saw, and behold, it was Ḥever the Kenite, the father of great schemes,
and the chief of cunning and villainy.
I knew that all his words were craft,
and all his deeds deceit.
But I was in haste to go,
and my soul was drawn to my companions;
so I did not make myself known to him, and left him,
and about the woman I did not ask him.
