Thus says Heman the Ezrahite: the sons of my thoughts carried me,
and the musings of my heart,
to journey from the home where I dwelt
to the land of beauty [Dan 11:16].
And I, on the mountains of Bether [Song 2:17], was lighter than a gazelle [2 Sam 2:18],
pursuing every lovely turn of eloquence,
and panting to hear every parable and riddle.
I journeyed to Bethlehem of Judah [Ruth 1:1].
And while I was in one of its towns,
at one of its gates,
cherishing its dust [Ps 102:15],
and the pasture of its fawns,
I saw men gathered together,
coming and running from every corner.
And when I asked them the reason for their gathering,
and the cause of their running,
they told me that a wise man had passed by them,
and camped within their borders;
in the eloquence of song there is none who can summon him [Jer 49:19],
and in contests of wisdom none who can stand against him.
I said in my heart: Let me run among them,
and hear what is in the mouth of the wise man.
And when I came near the camp
where he was encamped,
I saw among them an old man,
setting in order the choicest eloquence [Eccl 12:9].
And he was saying: "Hear my words, you wise men,
and you who know, give ear to me [Job 34:2].
For in my tongue is the fountain of wisdom and of eloquence,
and in my thoughts the treasury of inlaid epistles,
and of forceful compositions [Job 6:25].
Were the lightnings to run in my courses,
they would stray on my paths,
and would not reach the dust of my chariots."
The chief of the guests said to him: "You have bared a lofty arm [Isa 52:10],
and drawn a sword of pride.
We have heard your speech, but we have not tested you;
when your word comes true, we will honor you [Judg 13:17]."
The old man said: "Here I stand before you,
and I stand between the Lord and you [Deut 5:5].
Choose for yourselves this day [Josh 24:15] the deepest eloquence,
and the strongest poems,
and test me as you wish,
and search as you please;
for all my words are in righteousness [Prov 8:8],
and you will find no briar among my lilies."
They said to him: "If your words are true [Gen 42:19],
and your sayings just,
make us a poem built of three languages,
a deep work, that every man of understanding will recognize."
And he took up his parable and said [Num 23:7]:

The word of God is faithful, ⚠2 / and obedience to the Merciful, / and beautiful are His works.
I offer Him my song first, / and the praise of the Creator, / at the head of every word.
His name is exalted for ever; / He is the Lord, the Most High, / and the Master of the worlds.
By the light of His righteousness the brightness shines, / like a light that gleams / in the middle of the nights.
Over every secret He looks down, / with an essence not revealed / to any of those who stand.
His mercies are mighty; / His rivers give drink / in a place where there is no water.
In the world He has set dominion; / He holds all the regions, / and His height reaches the heavens.
By Him every living thing lives, / and the balance of the world / hangs in His right hand.
Lord of all the living, / His is the kingship that lasts, / and kings perish.
His wonders are precious; / and though time grows long, / He changes the times [Dan 2:21].
He makes the world quake, / and He brings the miracle / at the end of the times.
His salvation will be revealed: / He will raise the lowly, / and bring down the high.
The voice of the shofar will sound / for those who sleep in the waste ⚠3, / and the dead will live.
His secret He will show: / He will give life to His dead, / and redeem the living.
His house He will build, / and raise its dwelling / upon the top of the mountains [Isa 2:2].
And there we will set out a song / to the Most High, in thanks, / to Him who is fearful in praises [Exod 15:11].
My song shall be sung of Him, / and my praises are spread abroad / in every tongue.
The envious will moan at them; / its arrow has gone out from them / to the heart of the wise ⚠4.
Over the foes they have hissed, / and the East sends it as a gift ⚠5 / to the land of Spain.
In words they have dripped honey, / like musk in its fragrance, / and its memory reaches the heavens.
It is a precious poem; / it has become a glory / at the head of all nations.
The edge of its sword is cast / upon the heads of the heroes, / and of all the mighty.
By it every heart is watered, / and its memory remains / to the end of days.

He said to them: "Here is a precious poem, bound with a cord of three tongues, that shall not be loosed [Eccl 12:6],
and the threefold cord is not quickly broken [Eccl 4:12].
For fear of it the hearers trembled, and were nipped to the quick ⚠6,
and under three things the earth quakes [Prov 30:21].
I built it in the tongue of Aram, of Eber, and of Arabia,
and there is none who comes near it,
in the East or in the West."
All the people said: "You have done well, you have done great things [Joel 2:21],
you have been wise and shown insight,
and made the wise foolish [Isa 44:25].
We testify that in the craft of poems there is none to compare with you [Ps 40:6],
in the sweetness of your words,
and in the eloquence of your parables.
And if you can show your strength in a work set apart ⚠7,
you will be head over all the learned of Sepharad.
Now make us an epistle,
that gathers every good turn of eloquence,
desirable to all who seek it [Ps 111:2],
and let there be a resh in every word of it,
and let it be in praise of one of the nobles,
and of the delightful princes."
He said: "Incline your ear to my words,
and give ear to me."

And he answered and said: Cry aloud with the throat [Isa 58:1] ⚠8,
speak sayings of joy,
rouse your thoughts,
and widen your throat;
make straight the steps of your sayings,
race the chariots of your songs through the broad street of speech,
and let the showers of your words drip down [Deut 32:2],
to water with your rains
the land of the plain [Deut 3:10],
and to lay out a resting place of splendor;
to make blossom the topmost boughs [Isa 17:6] of pure sayings [Ps 12:7],
and to make fruitful the branches of glorious songs;
to set in order lamps,
shining like the lights of heaven [Gen 1:14],
to give light to blind eyes,
shut up in blindness [Gen 19:11];
and to make shine the heights of splendid glory,
with the light of the lords of dominion,
and the glory of the heads of the companies:
the majestic cherub,
the glorious lord,
the splendor of rule and of uprightness,
the beauty of discipline and of worth,
a shoot of the lofty princes,
of the seed of the nobles [Dan 1:3].
His sayings are sapphires,
in glorious books,
and his poems are beryls in rows set together [Exod 28:20].
His rebuke tears lions,
his word burns up the pyres,
his might sweeps away the rivers [Judg 5:21],
his thoughts are burnished swords, drawn out,
and the gallopings of his steeds [Judg 5:22] run like lightnings.
He walls up the breach [Isa 58:12],
and heals the decreed ruin ⚠9,
and removes the destroyer [Jer 46:20];
to heal what was torn down,
and to fasten the clasp [Exod 26:11],
and to make their light shine like the light of the sun [Job 9:7];
to make scattered flocks lie down in the choicest pasture,
as a shepherd pastures his flock [Isa 40:11].
He heals the broken,
and seeks the scattered [Ezek 34:16],
and looses the bound,
and lifts up the heads of Jeshurun,
who wake the dawn [Ps 57:9] to sing his glory.
His memory is perfumed with myrrh [Song 3:6],
and for his sake glory sings a song, a psalm.
His favor rouses the shades,
as his wrath slays the giants ⚠10.
His wealth he scatters to those who seek him [Ps 112:9],
and his goods he strews to the poor [Dan 11:24],
and he helps the prisoners of the foe.
He inherited uprightness from his parents,
and stored up worth from his youth,
and heaped up wealth for those who seek and court him.
Happy the pure parents, who taught him their way,
and roused him to run on the level path [Ps 27:11]!
Nobles saw him and called him happy [Song 6:9],
princes and rulers, and honored him.
He runs like lightning in their paths,
and has flowered like the cypress at the springs of their road.
May his Maker fashion him,
and his Creator make him mighty and glorious,
and to seek His will
may He rouse him and help him,
and from His wrath guard him and keep him,
and lift up the crown on his head for generation after generation,
and make his lamp shine [Ps 18:29],
and make his light dawn.
And he said: "Here is your desire, which you asked for,
and I shall yet make poems on the matter you sought."
And he took up his parable and said:

Lord of the generation, glory of rule and of discipline, / created and formed of precious seed,
fruit of the goodly tree [Lev 23:40], a light majestic and glorious, / he has kept and guarded the ways of his Maker.
His rule is bound up with the fear of God, / and he runs after his Creator and presses on.
Glory has bound a crown on his head, / and he has prevailed over the rulers of his generation and held them back.
May they exalt the Rock: his Maker formed him, / in his distress, as a fortress from the terror of the foe ⚠11.

And when he had completed his epistle,
and finished the wonders of his thought,
and rested from all his work [Gen 2:2],
they said to him: "You have dismayed every mind,
and made every tongue cleave to its palate [Lam 4:4].
But make us an epistle desirable to all who seek,
and from it drive out the resh utterly.
By this you will reveal the excellence of your deeds,
and let your work appear to your servants [Ps 90:16]."

And he answered and said: Our noble one ⚠12,
the pride of our ornaments,
and the headband [Isa 3:18] of our jewels,
brightness to our eyes,
and radiance to the pupils of our eyes,
the star of our twilight [Job 3:9],
and the splendor of our eyelids;
who fills our right hand with our wealth,
and our palm with our silver.
His fame is pleasant to all ears,
and sweet to all lips,
and his form is a delight to the eyes [Gen 3:6].
His name is the desire of hearts,
his face a sun without clouds [2 Sam 23:4],
his hands a rain of bounty [Ps 68:10],
and his virtues are entwined with the virtues of the fathers.
God sent him as a savior to His people,
and anointed him prince over His nation,
to establish and to provide a rising for the fallen,
and strength to him who has no might [Isa 40:29],
and rest to those weary of strength [Job 3:17],
and a resting place to the wanderers;
to give the thirsty to drink in the parched land
streams of honey and butter [Job 20:17],
and to strengthen the hands of the stumbling,
and by his bounties to make servants into rulers.
His kindness upholds the lowly and the feeble [Deut 25:18],
his righteousness strengthens the falling and the stumbling,
and his hands give what the petitioners desire.
Singular in his multitude,
and a wonder among the men of his time,
a ruler in his congregation,
a prince and commander over his community [Isa 55:4].
Perfect in righteousness,
repairing every breach [2 Kgs 12:6],
a lily among thorns [Song 2:2].
He learned the path of his fathers,
and he delighted to sit in the shade of understanding [Song 2:3],
in order to attain honor,
and to inherit greatness and majesty.
How good he is, and how fair [Zech 9:17]!
How pleasant his love and his beauty,
how delightful the pride of his ornament,
how upright his deeds,
how pleasant his portions [Ps 16:6],
how goodly the tents of his virtues [Num 24:5],
how lovely his dwellings [Ps 84:2]!
Like streams they stretch out [Num 24:6];
in their midst the weary are at ease,
and wanderers take shelter,
and the thirsty drink the waters of salvation [Isa 12:3].
The dead heard his fame and lived,
and the living, but for his kindness, would perish and be as if they had not been [Obad 1:16].
The tongue of the days tells his praises,
and the mouth of time pours forth the goodness of his deeds.
Grace is turned toward him,
and righteousness has pitched its tents in his heart,
and lifts its banners in his name.
May God set him as a banner of the peoples [Isa 11:10],
and make him strong above his adversaries,
and lead him beside still waters [Ps 23:2],
and lift him up and carry him [Isa 63:9].
And he took up his parable and said:

Beloved of God, perfect in lovely virtues, / and his heart is a fountain of the testimonies.
Prince of God's people, and he is singular in the world, / and so his praises too are singular.
He has built towers of kindness and righteousness, / and set his fathers' virtues as his foundations.
And since his deeds are lovely, / his name is known among God's people as a man greatly beloved [Dan 10:11].

The teller said: When the people heard the wonders of his thought,
and the marvels of his musing,
and the purity of his tongue,
they bore him on palms and on shoulders,
and opened their hands to him,
until they loaded him with the abundance of their bounty,
and he grew weary of thanking them for their goodness.
And when he journeyed from them,
he called down peace upon them,
and answered and said to them:

Truly I am Ḥever, a composer ⚠13 of song, for whose sake / the souls of the envious grow weary.
I rest quiet and at ease in my house, but my eloquence / travels as far as the east and the sea.
I have made my words like burnished arrows in this age, / and they rend the enclosure of my enemies' heart [Hos 13:8].
They go without feet as far as the east and the sea; / even the everlasting mountains [Gen 49:26] they split with might.
I join together the tongue of Eber, of Kedar and of Aram, / for all three of them bow to me in truth.
I kindle their fire up to the skies, and day / and night it rises on high and does not sink.
From the radiance of my light the blind see, and the lips / of the mute pour forth the goodness of my speech.
The souls of the men of song moan in envy / against me every moment, and find no rest.
They run after me to reach the height / of my poems, and they toil, and they do not touch it.
