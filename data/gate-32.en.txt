Thus says Heman the Ezrahite: In the days of my youth I was
in a gathering of sages
who sent men of intellect back abashed,
and left masters of the tongue struck dumb.
And while we were speaking words of eloquence,
and forceful riddles,
and weaving from poems robes of state ⚠1,
set in the gold of beauty [Ps 45:14],
there stood over us a man of forceful tongue,
and through his mouth the vision of poetry broke forth [1 Sam 3:1].
And he was boasting
that with his poetry he could explain everything sealed,
and light up everything dark;
that with his thought he could tear the sea of poetry into pieces [1 Kgs 11:30],
and with the rock of his tongue shatter rocks [Jer 23:29].
And one of the choicest young men drew near to him,
skilled in the craft of poetry,
and all his words were filled with pearls and set on sapphires [Isa 54:11].
And he said to him: "If you are right in your words,
and found just in your sayings [Ps 51:6],
come now, let us both run in the lists of poetry,
and divide two rhymed verses between us.
I will build one line and lay the foundation of the building ⚠2,
and you shall make a second line with a figure that completes the matter.
And if you fulfil this condition,
you shall find what you ask and see what you desire."
The old man said: "Good is the thing that you have spoken [Deut 1:14],
and here I am, ready for you in all that you have decreed."
And the young man came forward and spoke of the pen, and said:

"How lovely is the scribe's pen, pale green and yellow,
pleasant and beloved!
It storms against the mighty [Isa 3:5],
and loves to draw up a battle of words."
The old man said:

"It is like a golden scepter [Esth 4:11],
and its lines like a pile of flame [Isa 30:33];
in them are grace and love,
and wrath and pride."
And the young man sang and said:

And a scribe's pen runs over a scroll, / and embroiders on the dawn a row of darkness ⚠3.

The old man said:

Like a snake that runs in the dust, / and leaves behind it a beaten road.

And the young man spoke of the letter,
and said: "How lovely is a letter
that gathers delight,
as if perfumed with all the powders of the merchant [Song 3:6]!
It is a chain upon the settings of beauty."
The old man said:

"Like a comely maiden, swarthy [Song 1:5–6],
open and piercing,
mute yet speaking,
without a tongue she reveals and tells secrets,
and proclaims the praise of the Lord [Isa 60:6]."
And the young man sang and said:

And a letter filled with understanding, / on the bosom of faith she is nursed ⚠4.

The old man answered and said:

Like a maiden whose cheek is white, / and her hair over her light like a cloud.

And the young man spoke of the sword and said: "See a drawn sword,
cut from refined silver,
and wound in the turban of beauty,
now hidden and now bared,
as if snatched from the lightnings,
or plucked from the lights of heaven."
The old man said:

"It is like a rose, red and lovely,
whose fruit and branch are in the blood of the slain;
without a wing it flies,
to fight battles of brandishing [Isa 30:32]."
And the young man sang:

See a sword drawn in a warrior's hand: / even to the eye of the blind it flashes in the dark.

The old man answered and said:

I thought it, in the midst of the battle line, / a tongue of lightning running to and fro over the sky [Nah 2:5].

And the young man spoke of the coat of mail and said:

"See a coat of mail whose thoughts are deep [Ps 92:6] ⚠5,
and its wisdom broad,
and its rings narrow;
and whoever puts on its festal robes
laughs at battle and its tumults [Job 39:22],
and at death and its hosts."
The old man said:

"It is like a strong tower [Ps 61:4] whose doors are shut,
open are the engravings of its frames ⚠6;
its rings like the body of camels ⚠7,
and its nails like the eye of flies."
And the young man sang:

See a coat of mail, mighty in its designs: / by the hand of wisdom its frames were made.

The old man answered and said:

Its rings are like the body of camels, / and its nails the eyes of locusts.

And the young man spoke of the horse and said: "How lovely is a horse that runs and gallops [Judg 5:22]!
At its light the one who walks in darkness [Isa 9:1] grows radiant ⚠8.
It ravishes the heart of every swift and hasty man,
and swoops like an eagle [Deut 28:49] from mountain to mountain."
The old man said:

"It gleams like lightning,
and runs like the wind, and hastens;
it scatters splendor and radiance to every side,
like the very heavens for clearness [Exod 24:10]."
And the young man sang and said:

And a horse runs as the lightning runs, and all but / from the heat of its heart it melts like wax [Ps 22:15].

The old man answered and said:

On a mountaintop it is like a gazelle, but in the desert / like an eagle, and in the river like a fish.

And the young man spoke of the torch and said: "How lovely is a burning torch,
that empties out arrows from its sparks [Ps 35:3],
and hurls spears!"
The old man said:

"A virgin of love, full of desire,
thin and pale green,
stricken with fever [Deut 28:22],
weeping and laughing,
and the hands of the flame hasten to pour tears on her cheek;
they bring them to her and she pours [2 Kgs 4:5]."
And the young man sang and said:

Look at a wax torch burning, / that shares out its brightness to the eyes of friends.

The old man answered and said:

It is like a branch of Ophir gold, on which, / above its top, an emerald has sprouted.

And the young man spoke of the bag of myrrh and said: "How lovely is the bag of myrrh [Song 1:13] in its ways,
made to hide the compound of its oils,
and to keep secret the scent of its delights;
yet its good scent lays bare its hidden things [Obad 1:6]!"
The old man said:

"It is like a lover in his fancies,
who hides his desire in his thoughts,
and his secret is revealed by the look of his eyes,
and the likeness of his face."
And the young man sang and said:

A bag made to hide flowing myrrh [Exod 30:23], but / the sweet scent of its oils reveals it.

The old man answered and said:

Like a talebearer whose tongue they cut out, / and he does not stop, but winks with his eyes [Prov 6:13].

And the young man spoke of the wine and said: "See the son of the choice vine [Isa 5:2],
who whistles for joys ⚠9,
and gnashes his teeth at sighs [Lam 2:16];
his sweetness is the life of souls,
and joy is laid upon his shoulder [Isa 9:5].
He shoots at grief at a venture [1 Kgs 22:34],
and brings out against it the weapons of his wrath [Jer 50:25]."
The old man answered and said:

"He is like the sun in its heat,
and like the moon in its fullness,
and like honey from the comb in his taste,
and like myrrh in his fragrance."
And the young man sang and said:

The hands of the cupbearer pour out red wine; / they revive the heart of man when they shed blood.

The old man answered and said:

Its taste is sweet as the spittle of a gazelle's mouth, / and its look like her reddened cheek.

And the young man spoke of the hawk and said: "See a hawk that flashes,
that runs to and fro like the wind;
its wings are checkered with scarlet,
and its pinions like rows of gold set in order [Ps 68:14],
ready to overtake the lightnings."
The old man said:

"As though it looks out of two pearls,
and bites with two thorns,
and seizes with two sharpened arrows ⚠10,
and flies on two clouds."
And the young man sang and said:

And a hawk spreads its wings toward the heights of heaven, / and brings the eagles down from the skies.

The old man answered and said:

Its two eyes are hewn from pearls, / and its wings he made of lightnings.

And the young man spoke of the wolf and said:

"A wolf that tears [Gen 49:27],
cruel and stiff-necked,
does not stop and does not let up from doing evil."
The old man said:

"He is like a burning fire,
and like a sweeping torrent,
in summer and in winter;
he does not lie down until he eats the prey [Num 23:24]."
And the young man sang and said:

A wolf tears whatever he finds and snatches, / and his eyes are like lightnings when he runs to and fro.

The old man answered and said:

I thought him a thief who comes by night, / with a torch flashing between his two eyes.

And the young man spoke of the lightning and said:

"And the light of lightning, pale green like fine gold [Ps 68:14],
burnished and polished,
holding the edges of the sky,
drawing near at one time and far at another."
The old man said:

"Like a row of carnelian set in turquoise [Exod 28:17–18],
erased before it is written;
as though upon the face of the sky
a Cushite raged and laughed ⚠11."
And the young man sang and said:

And the lightning plays upon the clouds / like a warrior who runs and does not weary or tire [Isa 40:31].

The old man answered and said:

Like a night watchman who has dozed off, and opens / his eye a little, and it closes in a moment.

And the young man spoke of the waters of the streams and said:

"See a stream paved like rows of onyx,
or like the edge of a bared sword [Ps 89:44];
its source floats over the face of the earth,
and its taste is honey of the comb [Ps 19:11]."
The old man said:

"It is like the downpours of dripping rain [Deut 32:2],
or showers of falling dew,
or chains of refined silver,
or the flash of a drawn sword,
or a lover's pent-up weeping,
that goes on and does not end ⚠12."
And the young man sang and said:

And a stream whose waters go softly [Isa 8:6], / as though its currents were chains of silver.

The old man answered and said:

And the hand of the wind, like the hand of a craftsman and artist [Song 7:2], / joins rings of mail in its waters.

And the young man spoke of the pomegranates and said:

"See pomegranates,
pure and ruddy,
nursed in the bosom of grace [Lam 4:5],
and hidden in treasuries of delight."
The old man said:

"They are like chests
full of dainties,
in which are pearls
laid out for eating,
and standing ready."
And the young man sang and said:

And pomegranates, their cheeks like maidens', / and they are hidden from the light of a man's eye.

The old man answered and said:

When you open them, you would think them / a chest of gold, and it full of pearls.

And the young man spoke of the nuts and said:

"How sweet are the nuts,
stored in the treasuries of grace!
They are the longing of those who wait,
and the joy of those who weep."

The old man said: "They are hard and soft,
pleasant and pure,
like beaten slaves [Exod 5:16],
and crushed by every hand,
and they give the cheek to those who strike [Isa 50:6]."
And the young man sang and said:

And how pleasant is a nut: its inside is the fruit of grace, / but it holds back the fruit until they break it.

The old man answered and said:

As though it were a fool who refused to take understanding / and instruction, until they chastised him with blows.

And the young man spoke of the lyre and said:

"See a lyre that is mute,
yet pours forth words of delight [Ps 19:3];
deaf, and its voice is the voice of thunder.
In its mouth is good sense [Ps 119:66],
and its voice is sweet to those who hear it."
The old man said:

"Its form is like a thigh pressed close against a foot ⚠13.
It awakens good sense,
and sinks wrath,
and strikes the griefs
like a hammer and one who strikes the anvil [Isa 41:7]."
And the young man sang and said:

See a lyre singing in a maiden's lap, / and the heart is drawn by the glory of its voice and its beauty.

The old man answered and said:

Like a suckling who weeps in his mother's lap, / while she sings and laughs at his weeping.

And the young man spoke of the wheel turned by the water and said:

"How lovely is the wheel!
It is a treasure of every delight,
turning like a bowl,
and drawing from within the spring ⚠14."
The old man said:

"She is like a loving doe [Prov 5:19], wasted thin,
sick with the pangs of desire;
she cries aloud like a sick woman,
and goes down and comes up,
and draws tears from the spring of love."
And the young man sang and said:

She carries water, and in it she is carried; / pure, yet she immerses in the waters of rivers.

The old man answered and said:

At times she sings like the singing of harps, / and at times she roars like the roaring of young lions.

And the young man spoke of the citron and said:

"See a citron, pleasant and lovely,
in which is delight for every eye,
and its beauty cannot be measured."
The old man said:

"As though it had learned the ways of lovers,
and clung to the sons of grace;
its beauty has not left it nor been destroyed,
but has stayed in its look [Lev 13:5]."
And the young man sang and said:

The fruit of the goodly tree [Lev 23:40], fair to look on and splendid: / has not God hung on it a necklace of grace?

The old man answered and said:

Pale green without sickness, like the look / of a lover's face when he meets his beloved.

And the young man spoke of the lamp and said:

"How lovely is a burning light,
that hotly pursues the troop of darkness,
and kindles its arrows to destroy [Ps 7:14] ⚠15,
and shares out light to all around it!"
The old man said:

"In it is delight and desire for those who see it,
and its sparks are like weapons;
as though it were robbing the stars,
or kissing the cheek of the moon."
And the young man sang and said:

Look at the lamp shining in the darkness, / and revealing what the gloom had laid up in store [Deut 32:34].

The old man answered and said:

When its light rises on the lampstand, / it is like a tongue of gold that has melted.

And the young man spoke of the light of the sun and said: "How lovely is the sun, whose dwellings are on the height of the sky,
and its paths on the earth,
to light up the earth's dark places,
and to strip from it its garments of gloom!"
The old man said:

"She is like a lady who musters her strength [Eccl 10:10],
and when she takes her veil from upon her,
she gladdens all who long for her,
and all who mourned for her rejoice with her for joy [Isa 66:10]."
And the young man sang and said:

See the sun, that spreads its wings / over the earth to light up its twilights.

The old man answered and said:

Like a fresh tree that has grown in the sky, / and its branches have reached down to the earth.

And the young man spoke of the moon
that shines on the face of the waters,
and said: "See the moon, who has her station in the skies,
and rules over the earth,
and goes like a ship on the face of the waters."
The old man said:

"She is like a tongue of molten gold,
or the flame of the turning sword [Gen 3:24];
you would think her standing still, and she is going,
drawn by cords of love [Hos 11:4]."
And the young man sang and said:

See the moon upon the waters of the streams, / like a piece of fine gold set on beryl ⚠16.

The old man answered and said:

As though the moon had laid bare upon / the face of the waters the likeness of a drawn sword.

Said the teller: And when the two of them had finished the flourishes of their poems,
and the sweetness of their answers,
the young man said to the old man: "I swear by the Living One who teaches man knowledge [Ps 94:10],
and whose soul knows every secret,
that I have not seen your equal [Ps 40:6],
and there is none who reaches the dust of your feet,
or the flourishes of your parables."
The old man said:

"Nor have I seen a native vine-shoot as lovely as you ⚠17.
Wisdom is your choice fruit,
and understanding your fruit;
your eyes are your bow and your quiver [Gen 27:3],
and hearts are your captives;
and the fair turns of speech, as though you stole them from the glory of your beauty,
and the lovely parables, as though you robbed them from the radiance of your cheek."
And the young man and his companions set aside for him, each man, of his free gifts [Num 11:25],
until he was weary of carrying his many gifts.
After that the young man said to him: "I adjure you by God, who apportions honor and delight,
and has given you intellect without measure,
and made your mouth like a sharp sword [Isa 49:2],
to make known to me your name,
for your speech has astounded me."
And he answered and said:

I am a wonder in the eyes of the understanding, / and a wreath of grace [Prov 1:9] for the heads of the testimonies ⚠18.
Did they not call my name Ḥever, so that / I might join together ⚠19 all the scattered treasures?

And when I heard his speech,
I knew him by his name,
and smelled the scent of his spices ⚠20,
and fell on his neck,
and kissed his moustache,
and stayed some days with him,
to take away my heart's grief and its anger,
until the turns of Time parted us,
and his wandering left in my heart a faithful envoy [Prov 25:13] ⚠21,
and he also left his memory in my mouth like the jar of manna [Exod 16:33].
