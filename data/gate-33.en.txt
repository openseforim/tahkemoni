Thus says Heman the Ezrahite: I inclined my heart [Ps 119:112],
while I was still fresh in my greenness [Job 8:12],
and my help was in me [Job 6:13],
to hear the riddles of the wise,
and to gather from their mouths things fit for heaven ⚠1,
and to compound from the powder of their intellect a perfume of spices,
to spend my days in good and my years in pleasures [Job 36:11].
One day, when I was in Mount Seir,
walking about the city,
I saw a company of poets,
filling up pearls of speech,
and pouring rivers of song from their tongues.
I drew near to them,
to drink my fill of the rivers of their waters,
and to gaze on their delights.
One of them said to his companions: "Is there one among you who can compose a linked poem ⚠2,
in which every heart will find delight,
and whose beauty cannot be measured?"
Some of them asked him about the linked poem.
He said to them: "Know that the linked poem
is one in which the end of every two lines is a single word, and the word does not change,
but the meaning is changed."
When they heard his words,
and understood the plan of his speech,
each of them ran in the array of his eloquence,
and composed what God had put in his heart.
There was an old man there, listening and keeping silent,
who did not move from his place [Exod 33:11],
like the flint rock [Deut 8:15].
When he saw that they had toiled,
and not reached it,
and did not know the craft,
he said to them: "Know that you have ridden a hard road,
drawn from a deep pit,
and hewn from a strong mountain.
If you wish to hear the linked poem,
and the delightful speech,
incline your ears to me,
and listen to my words."

He answered and said: "Praise to God, whose praise is mighty,
and the earth is full of His praise [Hab 3:3] ⚠3.
Light and glory are His crowns and His diadem,
and those who thirst for His goodness drink their fill at the spring of His well ⚠4.
He gives as the healing of every sinner his repentance,
that his spirit may go up on high, and his return be to the height [1 Sam 7:17] ⚠5,
to hide from the anger of the LORD and His wrath,
and to wash his steps in His cream [Job 29:6] ⚠6.
He makes parting from the impulse a man's good ⚠7,
and the company of the upright the fruit of his faith;
to draw from the spring of the intellect and its channel,
and to find in it balm for the heart and its healing [Jer 30:13] ⚠8;
to raise his soul from the dust of contempt and its dunghill [1 Sam 2:8],
and to send into the kidneys of desire the sons of his quiver [Lam 3:13] ⚠9;
to be his God's portion and His treasure,
and to make His law the choice of his desires and his treasure;
in the wars of troubles let His righteousness be his shield [Ps 91:4],
to hide in His shelter from its heat and its cold [Prov 25:13] ⚠10;
to run after his God and not cease from his running,
to put an end to the oppression of the impulse and its crushing [Jer 22:17] ⚠11;
to gather from the fruit of knowledge its understanding and its choicest fruit [Gen 43:11],
and to set out over it his song and his music ⚠12;
to go up out of the roaring of sin and its estate ⚠13;
to make it his business, while his only one is still with him ⚠14,
to return to God his only one [Ps 22:21];
to fence the vineyard of uprightness and not remove its hedge [Isa 5:5],
that in the garden of God every precious stone be his covering [Ezek 28:13] ⚠15;
to subdue his desire under the yoke of the intellect,
and to make the abode of peace his border and his boundary [Num 34:7] ⚠16;
to remove the blemish of wickedness and its corruption [Lev 22:25],
and to make his anointing with the oil of a good name [Eccl 7:1] ⚠17;
with the hands of thought to clear the stones from his highway [Isa 62:10],
and to make innocence a support for his house and its terraces [2 Chr 9:11] ⚠18.
Then he will see the splendor of his Maker eye to eye [Num 14:14],
and flourish like a fruitful bough by a spring [Gen 49:22],
when he searches whence he came and from where,
and recognizes that his end is nothing.
He will know that in his world he is a guest and a wayfarer [2 Sam 12:4],
and no flow from the honey of its pleasures [1 Sam 14:26] will entice him ⚠19.
With the arrows of his intellect he will strike the impulse and shoot it down,
and in the good way he will cry to God, and He will teach him [1 Sam 12:23] ⚠20.
And then his God will make His light shine,
and spread over him the cloud of His lightning [Job 37:11] ⚠21.
The angel of His presence [Isa 63:9] will be his messenger and his envoy,
to bind up his wound and his pain ⚠22.
And if he abstains from desire, he will rejoice at the end of the days of his Naziriteship [Num 6:13],
and upon him his crown will flourish [Ps 132:18] ⚠23."

Said the teller: When he had finished his inlaid letter [Song 3:10],
and his refined word [Ps 18:31],
and the drawn sword of his eloquence,
and his praise, wound in a turban of glory,
all the people wondered at the force of his words and his meanings,
and at the sweetness of his thoughts.
After that he said to them: "I will tell you more: lovely poems,
and linked sayings,
with linked words at their beginnings and their ends ⚠24,
full of wisdom and riddles."
And he took up his parable and said:

The letter Alef

Faith is the ornament of nobles and princes; / she is a queen, and every king is her prisoner.
My soul was nursed in her bosom, / and so I will not remove her from my heart ⚠25.

The letter Bet

The Tester of hearts [Prov 17:3] searches the secret of your heart; / so hold fast always to uprightness, my son.
In Him you will find grace and good when Time / changes your joy and gladness into calamity and wailing ⚠26.

The letter Gimel

Gilead has no balm [Jer 8:22] for your pangs, / until you drive out the army of your sin.
Then you will find everlasting joy, when / the sea of Time casts up mire and mud [Isa 57:20] ⚠27.

The letter Dalet

A worthless generation seeks pleasure; so seek / God, and turn away from the mockeries of your generation.
Those who seek Him with all their heart found His salvation when / the hand of Time was bending a bow against them ⚠28.

The letter He

Save your only one [Ps 22:21] by your righteousness; only / against your impulse unsheathe the edge of your sword [Ps 89:44].
The shadow passes in a moment, and so those who pursue / the good of an evil Time pursue the empty [Prov 12:11] ⚠29.

The letter Vav

The years pass like a shadow, and the soul / of man flies away like a sparrow and a stork.
And the soul strips off scarlet and wraps itself in sackcloth / when the day of judgment comes, the sinful soul and the pious ⚠30.

The letter Zayin

Sow truth, son of man, sowing, / and you will reap sheaves upon your arm.
This is evil: the one who sows sin / will reap calamity after the sowing ⚠31.

The letter Ḥet

Encamp, my soul, in a precious house; and happy is / the man whose soul has righteousness as ornament and as jewel [Prov 25:12].
Grace will be its outcome, and to God / it will play a new song [Ps 33:3] on its flute ⚠32.

The letter Tet

You who sink in the sea of wickedness, know that / happy is the man who has dipped himself in the river of truth.
He is good with his God, and so with every / man; therefore he will lack no good ⚠33.

The letter Yod

The words of the wise are loathsome to the fool; / he loves only the world and seeks every precious thing.
His days melt away like water [Ps 58:8], and he sinks down / to Sheol, and not to his upper chambers in a lofty abode of honor ⚠34.

The letter Kaf

Sparks [Job 41:11] of the burning of Tophet are prepared for you; so / awake, and long for the service of your God.
For no uncle or brother will help you at the time / of your death, and the beginning of your world will turn into its end ⚠35.

The letter Lamed

You who press your heart after desire, / go after your intellect, and learn its ways.
It has an arrow with which you will pierce the heart of the enemies, / and when you are naked it will be a garment for you [2 Sam 20:8] ⚠36.

The letter Mem

You who rebel against God's word and are proud, / you will dwell in graves, you who sit on rich carpets [Judg 5:10].
Aim your hearts at God; / perhaps you will go out clean from judgment ⚠37.

The letter Nun

We look to You, our God, for Yours / is the greatness [1 Chr 29:11] poured out over the children of men.
We overlay our words with false gold, / and a veil is spread over every heart [Isa 25:7] ⚠38.

The letter Samekh

The garden bed of desire is hedged with lilies [Song 7:3]; / keep far, and do not enter into the council of its people [Gen 49:6].
Their soul has turned far back, full of sin; / yet its secret is not hidden from God ⚠39.

The letter Ayin

Naked, without righteousness, will you not be ashamed before / God? Take the garment of innocence, and adorn yourself with it [Job 40:10].
You will be wise if you weaken the force of your impulse; / so roar over it, and pass over it like a lion [Job 28:8] ⚠40.

The letter Pe

The heart of the wise man has cleared his path / from sin, and throws off the yoke of Time [Gen 27:40].
God will be to him a cornerstone and a tower / against the force of Time, which tears apart the bones of men ⚠41.

The letter Tsadi

The snares of my sin have hunted my steps [Lam 4:18]; / they have flowed over me like the waters of a torrent [Lam 3:54].
They have laid waste the pastures of my joy, until my enemies / looked on the day of my calamity, and gazed ⚠42.

The letter Qof

A war of days without end; and the pangs / of hardships hovered over it like slingstones.
Draw near, my heart, to the service of God, and do not turn aside / to a spacious house [Jer 22:14] carved with open flowers [1 Kgs 6:29] ⚠43.

The letter Resh

Heads were all brought low to the dust, / after they had ruled over Time.
The poor and the lowly were overturned in the end, / for bitter to the palate is the honey they scraped out [Judg 14:9] ⚠44.

The letter Shin

My heart has weighed the service of God, / for I find good and rest there.
The gate of desire's inner shrine I will shut, / until it is laid waste and becomes a desolation ⚠45.

The letter Tav

The world: its sorrows are without measure, / as its distresses are without end [Ps 119:96].
Leave perversion, son of man, and return to God, / and set out praise to Him, and do not cease ⚠46.

Said the teller: When he had finished his sayings,
and the eloquence of his words,
all who sat there marveled at his good taste,
and the sweetness of his speech.
They asked him his name,
and he answered and said:

I am Ḥever, and within my mouth are hidden all / the scattered ones of song and the outcasts of eloquence [Isa 11:12] ⚠47.
And if wisdoms are scattered to every wind, / are they not gathered by the breath of my mouth? ⚠48
