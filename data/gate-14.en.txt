Thus says Heman the Ezrahite: I crossed the sea to the land of Gaza,
after toil hard and fierce ⚠1.
And when I came to its pastures,
and walked about in its paths,
I saw, one day, a palace
full of a throng,
as full as a pomegranate.
And in the midst of the throng was an old man,
who set fine sayings in order with his mouth,
and gathered the scattered pieces of understanding as one gathers eggs from a nest [Isa 10:14].
And before him were students,
learning the kinds of wisdom.
And I heard one of them say to him: "If it seems good to our master, let him compose for us, from the pearls of his thoughts
and the crystal of his meditations,
a prayer or a supplication,
to be for us a shield and a buckler [Ps 91:4],
to break through the gates of His dwelling,
and to reach the Throne of the Divine Presence."
He answered and said to him: "Incline your ear and hear [Prov 22:17] your request which you have asked [1 Sam 1:17],
and your prayer which you have sought."
And he waited no more than a moment, as long as it took to wake the sleepers of his thoughts,
and he spoke to the rock of his tongue, and it gave forth its water [Num 20:8].

He answered and said to them: "Hearken to me, you who pursue righteousness [Isa 51:1]: here is a prayer for you,
the glory of praise
and the crown of greatness.
If a man should pray with it with the full intent of his mind,
the Creator would hear his prayer.
And this is its beginning: I beseech You, Master of the world,
Creator of all creatures,
Dwelling of the souls,
and Foundation of the worlds.
I have come to bow down before You, for You are the Lord who is One alone,
One, and not like any one;
the true Good,
the eternal Delight,
the inner Light,
the radiance seen by the eye of thought,
the supreme Glory;
Foundation of every foundation,
and Secret of every secret,
and Root of every root,
and Cause of every cause,
and Fountain of what is right,
and Source of faith,
and Foundation of what is true.
He moves the creatures from Himself to Himself,
and carries all that exists from His domain to the domain of its Owner ⚠2.
He is near, even as He is far;
He is far, even as He is near.
He exists of Himself,
and there is no other with Him.
He is wise, and not by an added wisdom;
mighty, and not by a might given Him;
living, and not by a life acquired from another.
He guides to the path of righteousness,
and leads in the way of uprightness.
He is hidden from the eyes of the supernal souls,
and found by everyone who is contrite and lowly of spirit [Isa 57:15].
He does good, and His goodness is a free gift,
not a debt.
He shows kindness, and His kindness is not under compulsion, but in love.
He is mighty, and in the greatness of His might
He forgives when His wrath rises.
He is generous, and from the abundance of His generosity
He gives freely to sinners in His pardon.
He creates, and was not created;
He brings into being, and was not brought into being;
He rules, and none rules over Him;
He grasps, and is not grasped;
He acts without a partner;
He decrees without a helper;
He reigns without a counselor;
He judges, and He Himself is the one to whom the debt is owed.

To You I call, O supreme Light,
exalted in hidden chambers,
toward whom is the intent of all who intend,
the yearning of every soul and the longing of every thought.
I ask of You Your help and Your nearness, by the life of the secret in which You are exalted,
and stand where there is no place,
and are far and are near,
and created all from nothing.
I make supplication to You by the glory of Your eternal Godhead,
and by Your everlasting existence,
and Your inner greatness,
the eternal majesty ⚠3,
and by Your enduring might,
and by Your hidden Oneness:
to set me on high and deliver me from the pit of lust,
and bring me out of the waves of the sea of perversity,
and hearten me with a supernal spirit, pure and clear,
and strengthen me with upright counsel,
and gird me with the pure and good intellect, without blemish,
and sanctify me with lucid knowledge,
that is emanated from Your spirit,
and make me strong with a soul that knows the worth of truth, that I may be the fruit of my creation,
and my forming be not in vain,
and that I may not leave the world as I came [Eccl 5:14],
naked of the garments of uprightness,
and bare of the robes of worthiness.

I beseech You, O God, who created me from nothing and formed me from naught.
You are the Lord, You alone [Neh 9:6], who made His kindness mighty over me before I was,
who made His goodness great over me before I lived.
From ancient times,
and years without number,
He brought me up in His thought before I was found,
and visited me with His care when I was not yet created,
and remembered me for good before I was called.
And He decreed, He who is in the heavens of might,
to create me upon the earth.
He commanded the spheres, and they revolved,
and spoke to the forces of formation, and they wrought,
and called to the heavens above and to the earth below [Ps 50:4], and from the two of them joined a supernal soul
with a lowly body.
And before He created me, He bestowed His generous spirit [Ps 51:14]
and His good power
upon my forefathers of old,
and that good came down to my father and my mother.
He commanded the naught, and it was split open,
and the nothing, and it was spread out;
and from its midst He brought me out,
and from the heart of the naught He brought me into being.
He bestowed on me His spirit and His breath,
and lavished on me His goodness.
In my mother's womb He prepared me,
and to the light of the world He brought me out,
and breathed into me the breath of life,
and gave me to drink from the channels of the breasts, fat things full of marrow [Isa 25:6].
He gave me favor in the eyes of my parents, so that they bore every harm and did me good,
and carried every pain and provided for me.
And when I grew, He sustained me with dainties and raised me,
and led me beside still waters [Ps 23:2],
and taught me the way of good,
and made me understand His Torah,
and showed me, in the eye of my heart, the light of the world to come.
How many times I went on backsliding in the way of my heart [Isa 57:17], and He did not repay me;
He was long-suffering with my sin, and chastened me with chastisements of love [b. Berakhot 5a].
How many times I forgot Him, and He did not forget me;
I did not remember Him, and He remembered me;
I forsook Him, and He did not forsake me.
His kindnesses and the goodness of His deeds clung to me,
as though they were a debt upon Him.
In how many troubles He tried me,
and from all of them redeemed me.
And what can a despised slave like me,
a despised broken vessel [Jer 22:28], base and lowly as I am,
do to repay, with his poor praises, even one of these many good things?

May favor and mercy come before You, O Dwelling of favor
and Seat of truth,
to make my end better than my beginning.
Do not let Your hand drop from Your servant [Josh 10:6];
do not tear down the good that You have built;
do not uproot the kindness that You have planted.
As You have begun, so continue Your kindnesses with me,
until I set right what is crooked [Eccl 1:15] and make straight what is spoiled,
and purge the dross of my heart,
and cleanse the impurity of my only one ⚠4,
until I return it to You clean, as You placed it in me.
Help me to free my soul from Your reckoning,
and to save my only one from Your punishment,
and to rescue my spirit from the pain of Your judgment.
For I know that You can do all things [Job 42:2]: to teach sinners [Ps 25:8],
to purify the unclean in Your righteousness,
to bring back the transgressors to You,
and to teach Your ways to those who stray [Ps 51:15].

I beseech You, my God, how many kindnesses You have done with Your servant [Gen 32:11]!
For without compulsion and without necessity You created me,
and gave life to my body,
and hewed my frame,
and made my soul grow,
and set my bones in order,
and stretched out my heart like a tent,
and in its midst built Your tent and dwelt.
You saw my unformed substance [Ps 139:16],
and cleared my way,
and before I called You, You answered [Isa 65:24].
If I stood a thousand thousand years bowing down,
giving thanks, praising and extolling,
I could not repay the good that You have done me;
how much less, when I have forsaken You and provoked You,
and sold myself to do evil [1 Kgs 21:20], and did not fear You,
and made my face bold and was not ashamed,
and walked in the stubbornness of my heart [Deut 29:18] and did not repent.
And I knew and believed
that were You not merciful and gracious, long-suffering [Exod 34:6] toward the wicked,
I would deserve that judgments be done on me for my many sins
and my few good deeds.
But You wish to show how Your works surpass the works of flesh and blood.
For when a servant sins before the king,
the king repays him according to his wickedness and his rebellion,
and withholds from him his kindness.
But You are not so in Your works;
You do good to the wicked and deal kindly with the guilty,
so that no plea may be left to them,
and they may have no excuse in their end.
For You know
that if You are long-suffering with the sinner all the days of his life,
he will not escape Your hand at his death;
but when his soul goes out,
his return is to Your hand,
and then You can take Your vengeance for the evil of his deeds.

May it be Your will, O Foundation of will and Root of the true good,
that the intent of all my deeds be toward You,
and all my work be for Your name,
and all my desire for Your glory,
and all my thoughts for You,
and all my meditations for Your secret,
and all my concerns in You [Ps 87:7],
and all my longing toward You,
and all my trust upon You,
and all my greatness to humble myself before You,
and all my pleasure to afflict myself in Your service.
Do not set my portion with those who pursue the lusts of this world and its good things,
and who take delight in its pleasures,
and who reckon Your service a yoke of iron on their neck [Deut 28:48], and Your Torah words of mockery.

I beseech You, O Fountain of life [Ps 36:10] and Spring of will,
may it be Your will to save me from their evil faith
and their foolish and base intent.
Incline my heart to the fear of You,
and bring into my thought dread of Your day of judgment,
and that I remember at all times Your punishment,
and that the dread of You be before my eyes as frontlets [Deut 6:8] all the days that I live.
And hear my prayer when I cry out to You.
Blessed are You, O Lord, who hears prayer.
May the words of my mouth and the meditation of my heart be acceptable before You, O Lord, my Rock and my Redeemer [Ps 19:15].

And he said to them: "And here is another prayer, fair and lovely [Jer 46:20];
it has no second in the world,
and for it silence is praise [Ps 65:2],
for the strength of its matter
and the force of its building."
He answered and said: "My God, I am ashamed and confounded [Ezra 9:6],
and when I remember how I have sinned,
I lay my hand on my mouth [Prov 30:32];
and for the sheaves of sins that I have bound,
I have set my face to the ground and am dumb ⚠5.
My thoughts cover their lip [Mic 3:7],
for the Inclination has swallowed them up,
and refined them in the furnace of grief,
and did not burn them.
It tore the robe of my splendor,
and crouched over me like a lion,
gulping the blood of the slain,
and kicking in the net of pleasures.
And through my insolence the chain of my strength was poured down into the valley [Mic 1:6],
and my glory was girded on the edge of its sword [Ps 89:44].
Its sparks [Job 5:7] pursue me every moment,
and subdue me without a hand [Dan 8:25].
For the many terrors and alarms of Time, its sweet things have turned bitter for me,
and its songs like its leopards ⚠6.
My heart was enticed by its pride and its haughtiness,
and did not understand that what a day brings forth [Prov 27:1] can turn its height into its pride ⚠7.
And if Time does not set its heart upon him,
his pride will leave him in a moment.
Therefore his flame burns with a burning [Isa 10:16];
he makes the world heave with his sighs
and the bitterness of his complaints,
and sinks the everlasting hills in the depths of his rivers
and the waves of his deeps,
until he cleaves channels in the rocks [Job 28:10] in the days of drought ⚠8,
and brings back the thick clouds flowing, that were held back.
He bound up the rivers from weeping [Job 28:11],
and in his bosom, for his guilt, he scorched my inside.
And within him the beast of his sighing encamped,
and the hand of its bow
was hard upon him,
and its quiver rattled against him [Job 39:23].
Wake and rouse yourselves, you who have deeply revolted [Isa 31:6],
who drink the juice of lust to drunkenness,
struck down by the wine of pleasures [Isa 28:1],
who have turned back and gone astray from the straight path,
stricken and grieving with the lust of the world,
moaning like doves [Ezek 7:16],
and oppressing their own souls ⚠9.
You have emboldened a troop of strife,
and made your faces hard as flint,
and from the fence of the garden you have turned aside.
You have set, in the evil of your lust,
your bounds in the pastures of kindness ⚠10,
and refused that kindness be your desire.
Awake, O moaning soul,
loose yourself from the bonds of your neck, O captive [Isa 52:2],
bound in the pit of lust,
exiled and wandering [Isa 49:21],
struck down by the choice vines [Isa 16:8], a degenerate vine.
Murmur and complain,
and lament over your sin,
and make your tears like the sea, and your eyes a fleet,
and over your end, mourn ⚠11.
My sons, understand and be wise,
O people who presume to climb the mountain of pride [Num 14:44];
for today they are exalted,
tomorrow they are bowed down to Sheol and taken away.
And those who slumber in their lifetime
will rise in their death to stand in judgment.
Against them, in the furnace of death, he runs with the thick bosses of his shields [Job 15:26],
and their strength he crushes
with a sharp threshing sledge [Isa 41:15],
with a destruction decreed [Isa 10:22],
and gold heaped upon gold will not avail ⚠12.
Woe is me [Mic 7:1], for my iniquity has put on its armor again and again
over the pastures of my joy, and they lie waste,
over my rejoicings, and they have fled ⚠13;
and it has cast its cords upon me,
and has taught the children of my grief to walk [Hos 11:3], and rules over them.
Lord, what is man, that You take knowledge of him [Ps 144:3],
when death shepherds him [Ps 49:15],
and its rod breaks him at every time [Ps 2:9],
and his tender soul is grieving,
and his anointed breath is turned back,
and his remnants are weary and slack,
and his troops weak and bowed down,
and his lights do not shine,
and his wounds do not heal [Hos 5:13],
and the children of a day wail over his calamity and his ruin?

May favor and mercy come before You, O Dwelling of favor,
inner and outer,
to gird me and to help me,
to hearten me and to uphold me,
with a spirit pure, clear and bright,
quick in Your work [Prov 22:29],
and shining in the light of Your truth,
that will soar to the heights of Your skies,
and not rest
until it subdues the evil inclination.
And perhaps, though the troops of lusts pursue it
to strike it down,
it will tear apart the chains of distress,
and still the waves of its tears,
and blot out the head of its griefs,
and leap like a lion [Deut 33:22] from the mountains of prey [Ps 76:5].
But what can be done, when my inclination has brought me to silence [Jer 8:14],
and laid me desolate until it consumed me?
It has set me as its target [Job 7:20],
and with its sickness my heart is stricken and wearied.
I went in company [Job 34:8] with the yoke of every delight,
and befriended [Prov 22:24] the men of wickedness, their wings clipped,
and forgot Him by whose spirit the heavens are made fair [Job 26:13].
And lust became a midwife to the children of my iniquity, until it was turned into a Puah and a Shiphrah [Exod 1:15] ⚠14.
The burning of my sighs shatters me with its rod,
and struck the sea of my eyes with its staff,
until the parched flint dissolved into the streams of my cloud,
that was not held back; and in the gardens of my grief were entangled
its blossoms and its buds,
and in my heart rolled upward its column of smoke [Isa 9:17].
And in the flames of the scorching of seething sighs,
and the blaze of burning griefs,
my eye shut the doors of its heights,
and the fountains of its deeps were stopped [Gen 8:2],
and it blew with its harsh wind [Isa 27:8],
and turned the sea into dry land [Ps 66:6].

I beseech You, O Light of the world, supreme Light,
enlighten the eyes of my intellect
out of the darkness of my folly.
Remove my secret sins from before Your eyes [Ps 90:8],
take away the curtain of sin that separates between me and You [Isa 59:2],
and scatter the cloud of Your wrath
that hides Your face from me.

I beseech You, my God:
before You measured in the hollow of Your hand [Isa 40:12]
the measures of below and above,
You brought down and brought up
healing and cure ⚠15
for my turning back from above;
and You sent a remedy
to heal the wound
that was not bound up nor pressed [Isa 1:6],
to a heart turned away backward [Isa 1:4].
Therefore in You I have trusted,
and spread out my hands to You [Ps 88:10],
to heal the ruin,
and to fasten the clasp,
and to light my darkness like the light of the sun.
Alas for the days of my prime [Job 29:4],
that turned their back,
and after they loathed me and reviled me,
wearied of me and taunted me,
and passed like a shadow,
and their locks passed away from me ⚠16.
The troops of my iniquities parched me in their fire and roasted me,
and after my briers [Ezek 2:6] they heaped me up and trampled me ⚠17,
and in their wiles they beguiled me [Num 25:18],
and from birth and from the womb [Hos 9:11] they bereaved me,
and from knowledge made me a fool,
and with their high hand [Exod 14:8] they shattered me and crushed me,
and in the battle line of grief they made me run,
and from the roaring pit [Ps 40:3] they did not bring me up in haste [Gen 41:14],
and from within and without they cut me off,
and with swords they cut me off and cut me down,
until they broke me and subdued me.
And the perverse Inclination,
that longs for the vanities of the world,
and in the dwelling of folly makes its dwelling, circling and craving:
its flatteries seduced me, and its portions led me astray,
its perfumes delighted me,
and its scourings struck me ⚠18;
the waves of its straits stormed,
and its pillars quaked against me,
and covered me, and my arrows swerved from its channels ⚠19.
Like a lion it roared against me,
and shook off the mighty ones of my joy,
and its troops loathe the strongholds of my prayer,
and rouse the sleepers of emptiness ⚠20.
Time stripped from me the mantle of its splendor,
and the hand of its stroke, in a moment, cast its weight upon me ⚠21.
Its distresses encompassed me [Ps 18:5],
and swept me away like a torrent [Judg 5:21],
and devoured me and consumed me [Jer 10:25],
and from the hand of my grief did not redeem me,
and in darkness defiled me,
until my shadows prevailed over me;
and it cast upon it, and the work of my mind was despised, and its weary ones fainted, and its yoke was upon it; and Time turned my ease into ruin ⚠22,
and kindled a torch in my innermost parts.
Its terrors have beguiled me,
and with no good they have forgotten me ⚠23.
Therefore awake, O moaning soul, tossed like a ship,
mourning in the depths of the sea ⚠24.
How long will the vanities of the world entice you?
Let them not entice you and destroy you,
until they shatter you,
and for a long time
make you prey for their teeth [Ps 124:6], bound and brought to your knees,
struck down, set before a steadfast inclination [Isa 26:3],
and in the pit of silence silenced and cut off.
Therefore, before your seducer who leads you astray,
sprinkle your lifeblood [Isa 63:3]
over the bitter things,
with a voice of bitter weeping [Jer 31:14].
Turn aside from the Inclination [Prov 4:15],
and turn away from its ways,
and strip off the garment of wickedness,
and sweep out upon the city of faith.
Perhaps you will make level its rough places [Isa 40:4],
and heal its fragments,
and rebuild its ruins,
and forsake the peopled dwellings of lust,
and restore the ruined castles of righteousness ⚠25.
Do not trust in the world, which bereaves its peoples [Ezek 36:13],
like a falling booth [Amos 9:11],
and like a withering terebinth [Isa 1:30].
Kindle your spark,
and open your fetters ⚠26
that press hard upon you,
until they set you up as an empty vessel [Jer 51:34].
And return to the secret of the foundation,
on which your building will stand;
for His name is a source,
and His will is a source,
and His forgiveness is a source.
And may God teach you, until you are worthy to see His face,
and find favor in His eyes.
And so may it be His will. Amen."

so that I might come to know his secrets.
And when he heard my words,
he said to me:

I am Ḥever, composer of praises ⚠27, / and in my heart are highways to every wisdom.
For fools I compose jests, / and for the pious I set out the words of prayers.
