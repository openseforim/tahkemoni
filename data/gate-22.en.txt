Thus says Heman the Ezrahite: I was in the days of my prime [Job 29:4],
when the stars of my twilight [Job 3:9] were shining,
and the sphere turned at my command.
I took my delight [Prov 7:18] in the gardens,
and grazed among the lilies [Song 2:16],
by the places where water is drawn from the streams [Judg 5:11],
and under the thickets of the shade.
I would breathe in breezes,
compounded with the oil of myrrh,
in which there was rest for the soul,
and joys for the heart.
And at the end of some days,
I went out to roam in the desert and the steppe,
and I walked about at large [Ps 119:45].
And I saw at the gate of the city a man standing,
anxious and sad.
And when I set my eyes upon him,
I found him to be Ḥever the Kenite.
My grief over his sorrow grew great,
and I hurried to him to ask him
about the thing that had happened to him.
He said to me: "Yesterday I was with some companions,
of the children of the Hebrews [Exod 2:6].
While we were still in the city gate, we saw a great crowd gathered,
coming and running from every side,
pressing and jostling one against another [Isa 3:5].
I and my companions hurried to them,

and we asked one of them
to tell us where they were set on going [Hab 1:9].
He said to us that at the city gate there was a sage, one of the sages of the Arabs,
who gazes at the stars;
he reveals what is to come before it comes to be,
and tells the secrets before they have settled ⚠1.
My companions said to me: 'Come, let us hear his prophecies,
and we shall see what will become of his dreams [Gen 37:20].'
And when we came to the crowd, we saw an old man well on in years [Gen 24:1],
and from the splendor of wisdom there was awe upon him [Job 20:25].
In his hands was an instrument of bronze,
and on it a work of network [Exod 27:4] ⚠2.
And he was measuring the sun in its spheres,
and its course in its circuits,
and the breadth of its regions,
and the slant of its shadows.
And he was saying to all who stood around him: 'Ask me about the things to come [Isa 45:11],
and question me about the future.
Do not test me in the secrets of the earth;
only in the secrets of heaven put me to the proof.
For I have not spoken in secret, and I have not said, Seek me in vain [Isa 45:19].
I will tell you the ordinances of heaven [Jer 33:25],
and the secret of the two great lights [Gen 1:16].
And for the five wandering stars
there is an array in my heart.
I know the secrets of their signs,
and their courts and their castles [Gen 25:16],
and their going down and their going up ⚠3,
twelve princes according to their nations [Gen 25:16] ⚠4.
If they have paths in the sky,
in my heart they have their courses;
and if their circuits are on high,
in my thoughts are their spheres.
And there is not hidden from me Saturn and its evils,
and Jupiter and its blessings,
and Mars and its wars,
and the sun and its dominions,
and Venus and its delights,
and Mercury and its wisdoms,
and the moon and its births ⚠5,
and the secret of the sphere and its degrees,
and the ascendant and its signs ⚠6,
and its chords and its pegs ⚠7,
upon its four feet [Exod 25:12].
And the sign of the Ram ⚠8
hangs on the ropes of my mind;
and the horns of the Bull
I bind without cords [Ps 118:27].
And I have left the Twins
orphans in their mother's lifetime.
And the height of the Crab
is small beside my rank.
And the sign of the Lion
at the dread of me is sick and faint [Dan 8:27].
And with me the Virgin
is taken as a wife in the chamber of my heart ⚠9.
And I count the Scales
as the small dust of the scales [Isa 40:15].
And the Scorpion is bitten
by the scorpion of my tongue.
And he who handles the bow [Amos 2:15]
is afraid to come near me [Exod 34:30].
And I have torn the sign of the Goat
as one tears a kid [Judg 14:6] ⚠10.
And beside the waters of my mind,
the waters of the Bucket
are counted as a drop from a bucket [Isa 40:15].
And the sign of the Fishes
is caught in my net like fish [Eccl 9:12].
I know what is to come from them,
and I have searched out their hidden things,

and brought their judgments out to the light.
I judge by them in righteousness,
and through them I repair every breach [2 Kgs 12:6].
I make men hear awesome things,
and words like the words of prophecies.
And I tell every man what will befall him,
from the day he came to be
until the day of his death,
whether he will prosper or prevail,
and whether he will die at sea or in the desert.
I make known to him his fortune and his success,
his going down and his going up,
and the full account of his greatness [Esth 10:2],
and all the acts of his power and his might [Esth 10:2].
The number of his years is cut off [Job 21:21] in my heart,
and on my tongue it is determined [Job 14:5].
I know when he will grow rich or poor,
and be brought low or grow great.'
And he took up his parable and said:

I am a tongue for the events of the times; / I reveal to man the hidden secrets.
I tell him what will befall him before / it comes to be, and what was before.
Whether he will prosper, whether grow poor or great, / whether he will go backward or forward.
Whether he will grow rich or be dispossessed, and when, / and whether he will be granted daughters or sons.
Whether he will live, and whether his days will be long, / or whether he will die short of days and years.
Whether he will wander far from the city where he dwells, / or heap up wealth and riches like the sand.
I make known to him what he has hidden in his heart; / and who can understand the hidden thoughts?
I tell him the future of his fortunes, / by signs, and seasons, and days, and years [Gen 1:14].
Go now, inquire on my behalf and search: / is there a man like me in all the times?

The teller said: When the people heard the wonders of his sayings,
and the power of his words,
they bowed down before him,
and were terrified before him.
And they came, each man to ask about his secrets,
and to see what was to come for him.
This one asked about his grief,
and that one inquired about the events of his time,
and this one searched about his son's fortune,
and that one inquired about his work and his deeds,
and this one told the plague of his heart [1 Kgs 8:38] and his pain.
And he gave each man an answer to his question,
and they gave him each according to his blessing [Gen 49:28].
And I, by my bad luck,
and the grief that fell to my lot,
said to my companions: 'How can we test him,
and tell his lies from his truth,
and try his sense against his folly,
and separate his gold from his dross [Isa 1:25]?'
They said to me: 'Come, let us all join in one question, and go to him together,

with our hearts as one heart,
and let us ask about the matter of the redemption,
and when salvation will come to the children of the exile,
and whether there is a rising again from this fall.'
I said: 'The thing is good.'
And when we stood before him we said to him: 'Tell us our question,
and you will find the reward of our kindness.'
He said: 'Let each man of you put his hand on the sand ⚠11,
for by it the holy is known from the profane [Lev 10:10] ⚠12.'
We did as he commanded.
And he cut lines in the sand, and made marks [1 Sam 21:14].
And when he had cast his points,
and his lines,
and made his figures,
and counted his counts,
and hung the instrument of bronze on his fingers,
and measured the sun in its circuits,
and set the ascendant on its four pegs,
and cut every star, each by its own standard, with its signs [Num 2:2],
the stars of heaven ⚠13 all but bowed down to him,
and sat with him on the ground [Job 2:13].
He was astonished for a while [Dan 4:16], and he was thinking to himself.
After that he lifted up his head,
and he was amazed at us,
and looked at us in anger,
and said to us: 'I swear by the Maker of the rising light,
and the sun and the moon,
and every rising sign, and Him who turns the globe of the spheres,
that you are not Ishmaelites,
nor uncircumcised ⚠14,
but from a despised and lowly people:
perhaps you are Israelites.'
We said: 'You have spoken rightly [Exod 10:29].'
He said: 'I have lifted up my hand to God Most High [Gen 14:22],
who searches every thought, who separates the holy from the profane, and teaches his servants the secrets of the stars and the sand,
that you have asked about a great secret, and asked a hard thing [2 Kgs 2:10],
and gone deep down to Sheol [Isa 7:11].

You have asked about a tower that fell, whether it will be built again for a showpiece [Song 4:4]; and about a scattered sheep [Jer 50:17], whether it will escape from the teeth of lions ⚠15,
while it walks among the beasts.
You have asked about a sinking ship, whether it will come up from the lowest pit [Lam 3:55],
and about a new world, whether it can come to be.
You have asked about the ingathering of the exiles,
and about the ruin of kingdoms,
and about bringing the dead to life.
As the LORD lives, you are worthy of death [1 Sam 26:16],
for you have asked about the ruin of the world,
and searched into the harm of the kings,
and laid a hand on the kingdom [Esth 2:21].'

The teller said: And all the people gathered against us, multitudes, multitudes [Joel 4:14].
And all the congregation said to stone them with stones [Num 14:10].
After that they agreed to lead us to the judge, and they took us,
and the people dragged us
on our faces
and on our eyes,
with the blows on our backs,
and the spitting in our faces.
And we reached the judge only with our lives between our teeth ⚠16.
Now the judge was one of the pious of the nations of the world,
and no secret was hidden from him.
They said to him: 'Here are sons of perverse rebellion [1 Sam 20:30];
they have laid a hand on the kingdom.'
The judge took us and brought us to a hidden place, and asked us
the matter of these events,
and we told him according to these words [Gen 43:7].
He said to us: 'Peace be with you, do not fear [Gen 43:23],
for you will come to no harm.'
And he called one of his servants and said: 'Put these in the prison house [1 Kgs 22:27].'
We stayed there all night,
until the whole assembly had broken up.
And when the dawn came up [Gen 19:15],
he ordered us to be let go, by the mercy of our Creator,
and he sent a messenger and brought us out [Num 20:16].
And now I have escaped from their tumult,
and from the jaws [Ps 58:7] of their crowds.
Blessed be the LORD, who has not given us as prey to their teeth [Ps 124:6]."
