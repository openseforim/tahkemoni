Thus says Heman the Ezrahite: When my glory was shaken off ⚠1,
I longed to see the land of Shinar [Gen 11:2].
I set my course to go there,
and panted for the scent of its tents;
and some days later, as I was walking in one of its cities,
I lifted up my eyes,
and looked, and behold, Ḥever the Kenite.
My heart was glad when I saw him,
and I hurried to meet him.
I said to him: "Who brought you here [Judg 18:3]?
May the eternal God bless you."
He said: "The serpent beguiled me [Gen 3:13],
and bore me on eagles' wings [Exod 19:4]:
that is Time, who makes men tremble,
and parts brothers from each other."
I said to him: "What have you here [Isa 22:16],
and where are you going?"
He said: "I was in the land of Sefarad,
and every day I heard of the wonders of Ezra, the holy one of the Lord.
These things parted my thoughts,
and drove the sleep from my eyes.
God stirred up my spirit [Ezra 1:5] to ride on ships,
and to tread the dry places,
the mountains of leopards and the dens of lions [Song 4:8],
and to leave the home of my birth, forsaken and abandoned [Isa 27:10],
until I could test the truth from the lie.
And it came to pass at that time that Judah went down from his brothers [Gen 38:1] ⚠2,
and set his paths in the heart of the sea,
and crossed the wilderness, the desert plain,
by Him who makes a way in the sea and a path in the mighty waters [Isa 43:16].
And when I had escaped like a gazelle [Prov 6:5],
and reached the land of beauty [Dan 11:16],
the reports knocked at the doors of my heart [Judg 19:22],
and the children of my schemes struggled together in the womb of my thoughts [Gen 25:22].
For I heard many saying,
and telling wonders;
all the tongues agreed together,
and met with one mouth [1 Kgs 22:13]:
the word was heard that between the land of the Chaldeans and Shushan the capital [Esth 1:2]
there is a place called the (river) Samarra ⚠3,
and in the holy tongue it is called Ahava [Ezra 8:15].
About three parasangs ⚠4 from there was at first the grave of our lord Ezra, peace be upon him, from the beginning of the building of the Second Temple (may it be built in our days),
until the end of one thousand three hundred years of our exile.
And when the days grew long for him there [Gen 26:8],
his place was ruined and left desolate.
That land became a land of drought and desolation [Isa 24:12],
and ostriches and kites dwelt in it [Isa 34:13].
It lay there like a pearl hidden in the earth,
and stored away in dark places.
We heard from many people that a hundred and sixty years ago,
it was told in a dream to a herdsman of cattle [Gen 46:32],
that there the angel of the Lord encamps [Ps 34:8].
The dream came to him once and twice [Neh 13:20],
and they gave him a sign, for he was blind in one of his eyes: his eyes were opened.
And because the dream came to him again and again,
the herdsman gathered all the men of his place,
and told them his dream.
He said: "Hear now, all my multitude,
what was told me in my vision:
that this is the grave of a prophet of the Lord.
And if you do not believe my words,
see now how my eyes have brightened [1 Sam 14:29]."
And when he had warned them time after time,
the people believed, and they dug and delved in that place, and found an iron coffin, closed, and on it a sealed inscription,
sealed with the ring of holy ones [Esth 8:8].
Their sages said: "This is a writing in the holy tongue;
none can read it but a man of the holy people."
They brought a Jew to read its writing,
and he found written in it
that this is the grave of Ezra the priest,
and on it his name was engraved, and the names of his fathers up to Aaron the priest.
The fear of the Lord fell on the people [1 Sam 11:7], and they took him and brought him by way of his border
to the place that he had prepared for him ⚠5.
And from then until now, on some nights there rises over his grave
a light that splits the darkness.
By it the peoples believe that the glory of the Lord has shone upon him [Isa 60:1],
and all the nations stream to him [Isa 2:2].
Around him are graves
of seven upright righteous men.
On some nights there rise over them sparkling lights from the terrible heavens,
and their tongue walks through the earth [Ps 73:9].
The narrator said: When I heard these sayings,
I thought them lies,
and did not believe the words,
until I came and my eyes saw it [1 Kgs 10:7];
then my thoughts believed,
and I knew that the Lord is in our midst [Exod 17:7].
Before that I used to say in my heart: How has there remained to the Jews in their exile any name or remnant [Isa 14:22]?
For since the day Ariel [Isa 29:1] was destroyed,
glory has departed from Israel [1 Sam 4:21].
So I said: If there is any root and truth to this thing,
I will go and see it before I die [Gen 45:28].
I girded up my loins [1 Kgs 18:46]
to go to the place that the Lord had chosen [Deut 12:5].
I went from land to land,
and the troop broke me with breach upon breach [Job 16:14],
until I came to the station of Ezra, the holy one of the Lord,
and bowed down on his grave before the Lord.
On one of the nights I went out with a great company to see his light,
and to gaze on the radiance of his glory.
When we saw it with our eyes I did not believe, for I said: Perhaps the dwellers in the villages,
and the gentiles around the towns,
want to mock us and deceive,
and have kindled high bonfires before our eyes.
But I gave my mind to it, and saw that the light of fire is red,
while the light of the Glory is clear as the sun [Song 6:10].
Men also testified to me that they went by night, a great crowd,
to the ancient grave,
and stood there a long hour,
until they saw a light in the darkness;
for it rises from there,
and panting it shines there [Eccl 1:5].
Some of the lawless and the heretics,
who do not believe in God,
say that in that land all its dwellers broke ⚠6 the covenant,
and its streams are turned into pitch and its dust into brimstone [Isa 34:9],
and when the brimstone burns,
a flame like a scarlet tongue rises from it.
But this is an appalling and horrible thing [Jer 5:30]:
none will believe it but a wicked man, a sinner, a breaker of the covenant.
For the flame of brimstone, as it rises, cannot hide its gloom,
because the smoke of blackness is around its light;
it has no gleaming light,
no pure and clear brightness.
But this light is pure and clear,
and shines in the darkness;
it dances right and left,
rises and falls.
The flame of brimstone rises heavily and low,
but the light of the Glory rises swiftly and lightly.
And I saw another sign, true and awesome,
that no creature can belie:
I saw the light rising before our eyes
run from the west to the east across the face of the sky,
up to the place of the grave of Ezra, peace be upon him;
and this is a sure sign, clear as the shining stars,
that it is no work of men but the work of God.
And when I had searched and delved,
and sought out with my heart [Eccl 2:3],
and touched the truth,
then I believed.
I blessed the Lord, who has not left us without a redeemer [Ruth 4:14],
and has left a remnant to His people Israel [Gen 45:7].
And I took up my parable and said:

The Shekhinah has returned to Jerusalem; / then the gates of heaven opened to me [Gen 28:17].
Or has the camp of God met me [Gen 32:2–3]? Or is / this the house of God [Gen 28:17], or a dwelling of priests?
Or is this the light of Ezra our lord, / by whose light the blind have had their eyes opened [Isa 35:5]?
For the nations knew, and believed it: has / the glory of God come to dwell among the sons of Ephraim?
A brightness that lights my darkness, yet / it makes the face of the sun ashamed [Isa 24:23] sevenfold [Isa 30:26].
A brightness that spread its wings over the face / of the earth, with rays to the height from its hand [Hab 3:4].
A brightness: the sun has put on no ornament / until it borrowed from it a covering of the eyes [Gen 20:16] ⚠7.
Red, then green, then yellow: in / these lights mixed kinds are sown [Deut 22:9].
The rain of my tears fell around his grave, until / the rivers of water had nearly swallowed it up.

And this song, hewn from the quarry of delight,
I made at the grave of Ezekiel our lord, the prophet, peace be upon him.

Grave of the prophet of truth, happy is he and happy are you; / peace be within your walls [Ps 122:7], and peace upon your works.
You are like the body; only Ezekiel is your soul; / or you are in heaven, and the son of Buzi [Ezek 1:3] is your light.
By day the sons of men seek you out, and by night / the angels of God knock at your gates.
For the Shekhinah has dwelt with you, until she has set / her threshold by your threshold, and her splendor like your splendor.
Your grave is a bundle of myrrh [Song 1:13]; and if men hide your memory, / your flowing myrrh [Exod 30:23] is known by its scent from within your bundles.
I say to whoever treads your dust and your soil: / Put off your shoes [Exod 3:5], and kiss the path of your steps.
Kings take pride when they come down / from their chariots to lick your dust [Isa 49:23].
Does not the leopard lie down with the kid [Isa 11:6] around you, so that / your young lions have muzzled their mouths from tearing prey?
Nations who denied God and did not know / the Creator bear witness and acknowledge your worth.
Priest and prophet: is there a rank like your rank? / You are like a king, and the fear of God is your crown.
The heart in which the Shekhinah is / is holy and blessed in the mouth of the assembly of your chosen ones ⚠8.
Source of the word of God, fountain of testimony, / spring of prophecy: blessed be your fountain [Prov 5:18].
A breastplate of faith is on the tablet of your heart, and in it / are the secrets of God, in your Thummim and your Urim [Deut 33:8].
The radiance of holiness on your forehead is like the frontlet [Exod 28:36], and there / the words of God are set in your rows.
At all times the tongue of prophecy talks with you when you / awake [Prov 6:22], in visions too, and if you sleep it wakes you.
Happy the soil that bore you in life, and happy / the house of your lodging, when you rest in your grave.
Happy the pious woman who bore you, and the nurses who nursed / you in their bosom, and your parents, your pure ones.
Your soul is by the throne of Yah [Exod 17:16] ⚠9, and the radiance of His majesty is around your grave: / it dwells in His sanctuary, and He dwells in yours.
For the angels of your Maker joined you, as / if you were their brother, and they are all your companions.
If your precious deeds and your great righteousness were written down, / the heavens would be too short to be a part of your books.
Holy is your silent dust, and holy your place, and all / around you is holiness, and no evil sojourns with you [Ps 5:5].
Were it not for God's right hand, which widens the place of your rest, / the lands would be too narrow to contain your splendors.
Death rules over all the sons of men, and their / bodies it wears out, but it has not ruled over your body.
Since the day your soul went up, your vigor has not fled [Deut 34:7] ⚠10; / mountains wear out, but your limbs do not wear out.
In visions the angel of God appeared to you in the / chariot, and living creatures flew at your sides [Ezek 1:5].
The likeness of their faces: a man's face, and a lion on the right, [also] / an ox on the left, and behind, your eagles [Ezek 1:10] ⚠11.
In visions of your God you came to a valley that / was full of bones [Ezek 37:1], and they were the stock of your men.
God commanded you to give them life by the breath of the word / of your mouth, and they lived when your words went out.
And it was a sign and a token [Ezek 24:24] that God will / give life to the shades [Isa 26:19] when you rise from your dust.
He will yet renew the heavens, and the sleepers [Dan 12:2] will / come up from Sheol on the day you come up from your grave.
From the end of Sefarad, the land of the north, and the far corners / of the west, the blind will walk by your light.
If souls long for the home of their loved ones, / my soul longs, it faints [Ps 84:3], for the home where you dwell.
O that I might dwell all the days of my life in your house! Happy / the man whom you bring near, to dwell in your courts [Ps 65:5].
A servant of yours came from Sefarad to bow down; / he turns around you as a door turns on its hinges [Prov 26:14].
If your love is shared among the sons of your people, / give me a double portion, like your firstborn son [Deut 21:17].
The cords of my love for you draw me [Hos 11:4], / and my loves bind me among your prisoners.
The fiery flashes of love [Song 8:6] burned my heart for you / like a strand of tow [Judg 16:9], yet your bonds did not melt [Judg 15:14].
The flood of my tears is red as blood, and in the river Chebar [Ezek 1:1] / your streams would have reddened, were it not for your holiness.
I have no gift to bring you, but only to sweeten / my tongue with the honey [Prov 16:24] of your name and your memory.
Let your servant stand by me on the day he stands before you: / the greatness of your merit, your righteousness, and your uprightness.
And may he be an advocate for him before God, the Rock your Maker; / perhaps He will heal the wound of the heart along with your healed ones.
From the ends of the lands he will cry aloud and call to you: / "Grave of the prophet of truth, happy is he and happy are you!"
