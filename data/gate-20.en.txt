Thus says Heman the Ezrahite: The delights of youth and childhood drew me on,
and upon me, from the radiance of youth, were the crown and the testimony [2 Kgs 11:12],
to ride in ships,
and to cross rivers and dry lands,
in the dens of lions [Song 4:8],
and among ravening beasts [Isa 35:9].
I harnessed the mules of parting ⚠1,
and flew on the wings of the stork [Zech 5:9],
and took longing as my provision for the road,
and set out from Bethlehem in Judah [Ruth 1:1],
while the bough of childhood was still in bloom,
and the mandrakes of youth gave forth fragrance [Song 7:14],
and the light of boyhood was shining,
and the sun of grey age had not yet covered the radiance of the moon.
And while I was walking in the cool of the day [Gen 3:8] over the face of the field,
I lifted up my eyes and saw [Gen 18:2] on the face of the wilderness seven maidens,
like seven lights.
The earth shone [Ezek 43:2] from where they stood ⚠2,
and a hidden light dwelt upon them.
When they saw my form,
they came to meet me with a greeting.
They were covered in their veils,
and wrapped in the mantles of their head-wraps;
but the radiance of their beauty sent lightnings to my eyes from their faces,
and shot firebrands [Prov 26:18] into my heart from their eyes.
All of them were of pleasing stature,
terrible as an army with banners [Song 6:4],
and their breasts stood like walls and lofty heights [Song 8:10];
they stood like cedars,

hewn from the quarry of beauty,
and cut to shape from the trees of Eden.
Each one was perfumed [Song 3:6] with cane and cassia [Ezek 27:19],
clothed in pleasant pictures [Isa 2:16],
tripping along [Isa 3:16] in the field,
as though beauty were a circle and she the point within it,
with a cheek white as hail ⚠3;
grace and beauty were gathered in her and became one bundle.
And one among them,
from her shoulders and upward, was taller than they [1 Sam 9:2],
with eyes
like doves by the streams of water [Song 5:12],
and eyelids
that would bewitch the sorcerers of Egypt [Exod 7:11],
and breasts
that tear hearts in two,
and loins
for desire of which every heart melts and all hands grow slack [Ezek 21:12],
and legs
like two pillars of marble [Song 5:15],
and hands
as though shaped from the hoarfrost of heaven [Job 38:29],
and cheeks
that would turn evenings into noon,
and teeth
like two rows of crystal,
shut in by the lips
as with bar and doors [Job 38:10];
and from that crystal drip drops of water,
like honey from the comb upon the palate ⚠4,
and whoever draws from them hungers not for bread nor thirsts for water [Amos 8:11].
She said to me: "Peace be upon you, young lion of lions,
leaping forth from the mountains of sound wisdom!"
I answered and said to her: "May hosts of peace attend you,
and gather to you.
But I adjure you, daughters of Jerusalem [Song 2:7],
who walk with outstretched necks and ogling eyes [Isa 3:16]:
by the grace of the lips,
red with the blood of my inward parts,
and by the eyes painted with the kohl of my heart's darkness,
and by the eyelids that shoot at my ribs and do not miss [Judg 20:16],
and by the cheeks whose glowing coals kindle a fire within me,
and by the teeth whose rows were frozen from the crystal of my tears,
and by the fingers cut from the tree of life,
and by the palate from which living water flows,
and by the radiance of the face that turned the dawn to black ⚠5,
and by its light turned back the shadow on the steps [Isa 38:8] – give back the stolen heart that you have robbed [Lev 5:23],
and my spirit, which you have ruined,
and let me know where you come from,
and whither your faces are set [Gen 16:8]."
And I took up my parable and said:

O doe, who drew my heart like a thread / and burned it in the coals of her love as on the burning day ⚠6,
have pity on a lover whose soul she has sold, / and whose eye she has put in the prison of love.
Have mercy on him, and turn to him with grace, / lest they say of him: "A woman slew him [Judg 9:54]."

One of them answered, and the moist crystals scattered from between her teeth,
and lightnings ran to and fro [Nah 2:5] between the pupils of her eyes,
and Grace cried before her, "Bow the knee [Gen 41:43]!"
But the veil was between her eyes,
and she was hidden from the eyes of all living [Job 28:21], for she had covered her face [Gen 38:15].
She said to me: "Far be it, far be it from us to do you harm, for we have not touched you [Gen 26:29],
nor wearied you;
but you came up against the face of the fierce battle [2 Sam 11:15], and so we struck you.
And you, son of man, what business have you to search into the secrets of great ladies,
and to knock on the barred doors of desire?
How did you come into the palace of kings and not fear,
and draw near to the inner court without being called [Esth 4:11]?
How were you not afraid lest the arrows of the eyes strike you,
and the swords of the eyelids,
and cut your liver in two,
and steal your heart without hands,
and your soul fly away without wings?
Why did you not dread lest the eyes, those fiery serpents, bite you [Num 21:6],
and the cheeks burn you with coals of flame?
How were you not dismayed at the brightness of the dawns and the darkness of the twilights,
nor trembled at the pomegranates of the boughs ⚠7
that are under the veils,
and at the night of the tresses [Judg 16:13] that is under the head-wraps?"
I answered and said: "Gently with me [2 Sam 18:5], gazelle,
who tears like a lioness,
who draws a treacherous bow [Hos 7:16],
who kills without lying in wait [Exod 21:13]!
For since the day I was born
I have shone in the heavens of honour,
and flowered on the rivers of ease.
From my youth the nobles of the people have borne me on their palms [Ps 91:12]
and on their shoulders [Isa 49:22],
and the lords have dandled me on their knees [Isa 66:12].
For my fathers were the nobles of the land, men of honour,
and my forebears stout of heart and mighty men;
the lion's heart would melt before them [2 Sam 17:10],
and Time was too dismayed to stand before them.
And between my ribs a precious, faithful soul
lodges in the shade of humility [Ps 91:1],
and dwells with the stars on high,
and became a nurse to them [Ruth 4:16] ⚠8.
It was hewn from the quarry of glory,
and set upon the throne of honour.
How many lionesses I have hunted,
and hosts I have captured
in the net of my love,
and afflicted their hearts by my parting,
and kindled within them the fire of longing for me!
And since I came to be, I have searched and dug
into the hidden secret,
and my heart still rules with God, and is faithful with the holy ones [Hos 12:1].
It befits me to search out every secret and to ask,
for there is none but me to redeem [Ruth 4:4] ⚠9."

She answered and said to me: "How pleasant are your sayings,
and how sweet your words!
Grace is poured on your lips [Ps 45:3],
and your love to us is wonderful [2 Sam 1:26].
Therefore it is right for us to grant your request,
and to do what you ask [Esth 5:8].
Know for certain [Gen 15:13] that this delicate lady
is the glory of every province,
in brightness of form,
with a cheek like the moon,
and hair like a cloud,
and there is no end to her perfection [Nah 2:10].
She is the only one among daughters,
and the glory among the delicate,
and a princess among the provinces [Lam 1:1].
This very day she put on the garments of her finery,
and tied on the hair-nets [Isa 3:18] of her jewels,
and came out to show the peoples and the princes her beauty [Esth 1:11].
And she too flows with milk and honey, and this is her fruit [Num 13:27]."
And she took up her parable and said:

A gazelle of grace, white as the moon ⚠10, / and her hair a cloud over the light of her cheek.
Maidens took only a little of grace, / but all beauty fell to her as her portion.
As though the lights were jealous of her beauty, / and drew a whetted sword to make war:
the stars on high like the blades of spears, / and the moon like buckler and shield [Ps 91:4].
If a man held captive by grey age should look at her, / he would turn back from the time of old age to delight [Gen 18:12].
Her lip is nectar, her cheek manna, and her temple / like the dew of lights [Isa 26:19], and her face like a garden.
Beauty was a wanderer and an exile, / until it came upon its dwelling in her and encamped.
At her light the blind shine bright at midnight, / and a song of joy is heard from the tongue of the mute [Isa 35:6].
And if a wise-hearted man should see her beauty, / it would leave him without sense or understanding.
In her all majesty and all glory are joined, / and all beauty, and there is no end to her perfection [Nah 2:10].
How can you seek an equal to her beauty, / when it is not found in any nation or province?
And if you say, "Have you seen the like?", / know that you have seen no form at all [Deut 4:15].

After that she said to me: "You too, tell us your affairs,
and from what place your dwelling is."
I said: "I am the man [Lam 3:1],
of the choicest of the children of Eber [Gen 10:21].
I have roamed on every side,
and flown from the sea to the sunrise without a pinion [Isa 40:31].
From Spain I parted,
and to the land of Greece I fluttered [Hos 11:11];
I broke through breakers,
and crossed over crossings.
I came from No-amon [Nah 3:8] ⚠11
to Mount Hermon,
and I wandered ⚠12 from Zoan
to the land of Canaan,
and from Noph
to the one beautiful in height [Ps 48:3],
and from Egypt
to Jerusalem,
and from Tyre
to Hazor,
and from Dibon
to Heshbon,
and from Arnon
to Lebanon,
and from Dan
to Dedan,
and from Ramathaim
to the land of Merathaim [Jer 50:21],
and from Keilah
to Havilah,
and from Ashur
to Geshur,
and from Arbel
to Babylon.
And in all that I have beheld,
I have not seen the like of this sight.
And now, if I have found grace in your eyes [Gen 18:3],
take off the veil that is on your face,
and bring back my spirit,
and renew my strength [Isa 40:31],
and by this request I shall go out of my prison free [Exod 21:2].
Is it not a little one? And my soul shall live [Gen 19:20]."
When she heard my utterance she laughed at my words, and took hold of me, and said: "Behold, I have hearkened to your voice,
and have done what you asked."
And she removed the veil from her eyes,
and uncovered her face;
and I looked, and behold, a man, and his beard was long,
as though a serpent were coiled there.
And he brought out from under his garment
his sword, drawn in his hand [Num 22:23].
I saw, and fell on my face [Ezek 1:28],
and my thoughts were lost.
And when he saw that my strength had left me [Judg 16:17],
and my spirit had all but gone out,
he began to laugh,
and his voice was heard afar off;
and the maidens' mouths were filled with laughter [Ps 126:2],
and they fell on their faces.
He said to me: "Open your eyes and look at me;
perhaps you will know me."
I gazed at him, and behold, it was Ḥever the Kenite,
the father of great wiles,
and the head of blasphemies and villainies.
I said to him: "The Lord trouble you, for you have troubled me [Josh 7:25],
and you have brought me very low [Judg 11:35],
and brought me to death."
He said to me: "Peace, peace to you while you live;
be strong, and stand in your place [Dan 10:11]."
And my spirit returned to me [Judg 15:19] when I heard his sayings,
and I took strength from his words.
And I said to him: "What do you mean by all this company which I met [Gen 33:8],
for desire of whom I was punished,
and in the net of whose love I was snared?"
He said: "These are the hosts of my love,
in whom is my delight,
and for whom is my longing.
I was walking with them,
to gather the pearls of their sayings,
and to delight in the sweetness of their words.
And when we saw you afar off, I told them
that you are the choicest of my friends and companions,
the treasure of my beloved ones and my acquaintances;
and they entreated me and coaxed me,
and adjured me by the life of your love
to make sport of you and deceive you,
and to test your strength and try you."
Then he hastened to wish me peace,
and went from me;
and the maidens vanished from my eyes,
and the spirit of the Lord carried them away [2 Kgs 2:16].
And I was left alone, astonished in my anguish
at the thing that had befallen me.
And I turned back to my place, my heart grieved at his doings,
cursing Ḥever the Kenite and his longings.
