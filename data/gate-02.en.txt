Thus says Heman the Ezrahite: I have surely heard [Jer 31:18]
that there was a wise preacher in the isles of the sea,
who could still the waves of eloquence and their breakers ⚠1.

And with his words he took from hearts their hardness,
and from souls their chill,
until their rebellion was gathered in [Num 12:15] ⚠2.
With his rebukes he crumbled the rocks of the hearts and made the eyes shed tears:
behold, he struck the rock and the waters gushed out [Ps 78:20].
And when I heard the noise of the people as they shouted [Exod 32:17],
telling one another of the preacher's insight and his knowledge,
I longed to subdue by his rebukes my harlot inclination,
which turns to its pleasures,
and encamps at the Graves of Lust [Num 11:34],
so that I might break its pride and bring it under,
perhaps I shall be able to smite it and drive it out [Num 22:6].
So I arose and journeyed from my land,
and left the pasture where I lay,
and rode upon a ship.
And when I reached the districts of the preacher and his pasture lands,
the place where he was encamped,
I came into the house of prayer
with the congregation,
and set out to God my praise.
And I looked, and behold, the preacher
from the place of his habitation gazed out [Ps 33:14];
he shouted, yes, he roared [Isa 42:13],
and with his words he rinsed away the blood of the sinners [Isa 4:4].
And he answered and said:

"To you, O men, I call [Prov 8:4]:
remember the awesome God,
and awake, you who are asleep to knowledge,
and put away your wine [1 Sam 1:14], you who are struck down by error,
who sell the life of the world to come for the life of an hour.
Know that this is not the place of rest.
Therefore make ready for the day of the journey,
and turn, go down from the hill, and arise, go out to the valley [Ezek 3:22],
the place where your fathers dwell,
and the land of your sojournings.
Do you know
that you are strangers and sojourners [Lev 25:23]
in a land that eats up its inhabitants [Num 13:32],
and is the enemy of those who love her?
Her taste is bitter,
and in her hand is a cup of foaming wine [Ps 75:9];
for fear of it all flesh bristles [Ps 119:120],
and every heart is in turmoil [Lam 1:20].
They called her Tevel, the world,
and she is as her name: tevel, perversion [Lev 18:23] ⚠3.
Her beginning is mourning,
and her end is pangs and pain [Isa 13:8].
She beguiles you with her ornaments,
and dotes on you with her beauty.
She is like a bride who adorns herself with her jewels [Isa 61:10]:
ruin is her coronets,
and treachery her robes,
and stumbling blocks her skirts,
and the twistings of misfortune her cords,
and the chambers of Sheol her palaces [Prov 7:27],
and the blood of the innocent the blood of her maidenhood.
Every day she sends away her husbands,
and kills every man who comes in to her.
Her dawns are darkness,
and her lights are snuffed out,
and her wise men are perplexed [Exod 14:3],
and her kings brought low,
and her merry ones weep,
and her gardens are tangled thorns [Nah 1:10],
and her wines are mixed with wormwood.
She humbles her kings and destroys her princes,
a swift young camel running back and forth on her ways [Jer 2:23].
Her good is uprooted,
and her sickness stirred up [Ps 39:3],
and her hope is a lie,
and her cup shatters,
and her shame remains from generation to generation.
And now, you who trust in despised wealth,
let not this little honey be sweet to you,
for beneath it is the venom of asps [Deut 32:33],
and sicknesses evil and lasting [Deut 28:59].
Do you not know that the end of riches
is dismay,
and the outcome of money
is ruin; and the goal of silver
is wrath,
and the root of gold
is flame,
and the likeness of wealth
is an eagle ⚠4?
If today you seize it with your hands,
tomorrow it will make itself wings [Prov 23:5].
Therefore do not think of this world as your dwelling house, in which you may rejoice;
it is rather an inn, where the guests have gathered:
the morning was light, and the men were sent away [Gen 44:3].
And now, my sons, save yourselves, and do not be slack;
keep apart, and take warning,
and purify yourselves,
and draw near ⚠5,
and sanctify yourselves [Josh 3:5], and kindle within you
flames,
and draw from the rocks of your ribs
tears [Jer 6:7],
and stir up against your hearts
battles.
Make your intellect your prince ⚠6,
and put away your folly,
and change your inclination,
and exalt your uprightness,
and call to mind your transgression,
and acknowledge your wickedness.
And bring to your hearts the bitter and dreadful day,
a day of anguish as of a woman bearing her first child [Jer 4:31],
a day when the books are opened [Dan 7:10],

and the secrets laid bare;
a day when sins will be bars upon the neck,
and death will stand in a narrow place where there is no way to turn [Num 22:26];
a day when a man falls sick with the sickness of which he will die [2 Kgs 13:14];
a day when he burns in the coals of his own spark;
a day when his soul loathes the food it craved [Job 33:20],
and he is sick of every lovely delight;
a day when he chokes on his spittle,
and his throat is stopped from eating the bread of his portion [Prov 30:8],
and every food and dainty
he refuses to swallow;
and he receives his due;
the day he is buried,
and all his work,
that he gathered and heaped up,
will find him in a desert land [Deut 32:10].

Awake, you drunkards [Joel 1:5],
and wail, you mighty men!
What have you to do with love of the cursed world?
And why do you suck the breasts of a barren woman?
You hold on to her, and she flees from you;
you draw near to her, and she drives you away.
Do you not know that on chaos are her foundations and her counsels,
and her ravages and her calamities came to meet you,
and her torches and her ruin have burned your heart,
and her seasons and her loves have lied to you,
and you have sucked wormwood from the milk of her breasts?
Betrayal is her garments ⚠7,
and rebellion her mantles,
she whose heart is snares and nets, and her hands are fetters [Eccl 7:26].
And if you seek to know your foundation,
from which you were taken [Gen 3:19]:
my brothers, where are you from [Gen 29:4]?
Were you not formed from chaos,
and born from nothing?
And when you beget children and children's children, and have grown old [Deut 4:25],
death will eat what you have begotten,
and your own carcasses [Num 14:32].
Therefore rouse yourselves, and awake, and entreat; perhaps you will be heard,
and on the day of the LORD's fierce anger you will be hidden [Zeph 2:3];
and from the ancients who passed away before you, take warning.
For go now and stand by the graves of the dead, and remove the dust from their eyes [m. Sotah 5:2],
and look closely at them:
perhaps you can tell apart the king
and the wretch,
and the highest
and the needy,
and the steward
and the pauper,
and the nobles
and the slaves.
And see what death has left of their pampered bodies
and their fat corpses.
Their beauty is lost,
and the blemish remains;
the flesh wastes away,
and the worm does not leave.
The limbs dry up,
the tender and the moist;
the sinews are torn,
and the veins are cut;
the heart is pierced through to its hollow,
and his spinal cord is cut.
And the limbs are filled
with dust,
and the bowels
with clods,
and the ribs
with worms,
and the bones
with lumps of earth and maggots [Job 7:5].
And nothing is left of the guarded forms
and the figures reared in luxury ⚠8
but signs
engraved upon the bone.
The eyes of those who see them stream with tears,
and they pain and grieve the heart of those who hear of them.
Therefore, men of heart, turn back and turn others back [Ezek 14:6],
and amend your ways [Jer 7:3],
and contend with your inclination,
and offer your fat on the fire of your heart as a sacrifice.
And lift up your eyes
to God in heaven [Lam 3:41],
in integrity of heart and innocence of hands [Gen 20:5],
and do not come near him with a double heart.
And do not be of those who come before him with bowed head
and stricken body,
while in their heart is a mixed multitude gathered [Num 11:4],
that grows and never ends.
Beauty is on their faces,
and blemish under their clothes;
the garments are pure,
and the deeds are black;
their turbans are white and fair,
and their actions are filthy;
honey is in their mouth,
and wormwood in their hearts [Prov 5:3–4].
They wrap themselves in fringes
like seraphim and holy creatures,
and they tear at people
like wolves and wild beasts
and lions' whelps.
They are pious in their speech,
and their ambush is within them [Jer 9:7];
their heart is divided [Hos 10:2],
and the look of their faces testifies against them [Isa 3:9].
On their tongue is prayer,
and in their heart is folly ⚠9;
in their mouth are supplications,
and in their heart are harlotries.
As the LORD lives, unless every man refines his heart
from the dross of his thought,
and from the hypocrisy within him,
he shall not come to see the face of the Ark of the LORD,
and he shall not approach the sanctuary, for he has a blemish [Lev 21:23].
And God has decreed in his word
that no hypocrite shall come before him [Job 13:16].

Woe to you who dwell in the houses of the ancients!
Make room for those who come last,
as the first ones made room for you.
And arise, go out of your houses,
for others are coming to dwell in your place (and you will journey from your place)
to the station of judgment and covenant,
and you will be hidden at the brook Cherith [1 Kgs 17:3] ⚠10,
and the breath of the LORD in it like a stream of brimstone [Isa 30:33].
And if today you are in the river of lust with no ford,
tomorrow turn and journey into the wilderness [Deut 1:40].
You deaf, hear awesome things,
and you blind, look and see [Isa 42:18].
How often Time called to you, and you did not hear,
and drew his sword, and you did not fear;
and you turned aside after the world,
and plucked the fruit of her gardens [Song 5:1],
and did not see your end.
You have done evil in what you have done [Gen 44:5].
On the spring of pleasures you blossomed,
and by the rivers of ease you sprouted;
and now, on whom do you trust [Isa 36:5],
that you rejoice?
What is this lying joy,
and the cup of forgetting that makes drunk?
Hurry now, and save your souls
from the snare of your traps,
before you go out of the dwellings
in which you are encamped,
as the first ones went out,
who built the buildings,
and acquired the possessions,
and stored up the treasures,
and went out from all their wealth empty-handed,
and carried nothing but their shroud and their sackcloth;
and clods and maggots lay in their bosom,
and the crawlers of the dust [Deut 32:24] joined them and ate their portion,
and the worms, they take their share.
And when they go, they shall not go empty [Exod 3:21].
Where are those who wore robes of state [Isa 3:22]
and woven gold?
Where are those wrapped in fine garments
and linen wraps [Isa 3:23]?
Where are those who wore the cloaks
and tied the cords?
Where are those who bore the bracelets,
and the necklaces,
and the anklets,
and the headbands [Isa 3:18],
and the shoes,
and the veils [Isa 3:19],
and the turbans,
and the mantles [Isa 3:22]?
Did they not, on the day of their death, leave the robes of state
stripped off,
and the turbans
soiled,
and the woven gold
rolled in the mire,
and the necklaces
plundered,
and the neck chains
torn off,
and the anklets
trampled,
and the shoes
loathed?
And the world gave them graves in exchange for her inner rooms,
and for her chambers a land without order [Job 10:22],
and for palaces dark places [Isa 59:10],
and for her resting places her pits,
and for her upper rooms her depths.
And they went out from the castles
to the pits,
and from the shade of the walls to the beams ⚠11,
and from the chambers of fine images [Isa 2:16]
to lands of desolation,
and they fell from the skies
to the depths,
and went down from the heights
to the abysses.
And they prepared for them in the wildernesses
houses of graves, and afterward the people journeyed from Hazeroth [Num 12:16] ⚠12.
The mighty who dwelt in chambers did not escape death,
nor the Avvim who dwelt in villages [Deut 2:23];
they only went out from their court to the court of death,
and from the shade of their chamber to the shadow of death,
and they all lay down in the dust [Job 21:26],
and slept an everlasting sleep [Jer 51:39].
Alas for that day, which has no morrow after it,
and for that night, which has no dawn!
How great is its downfall,
and where is its escape?
And how mighty is its distress,
and what will come to its help?
Therefore, men of heart, let not the wealth of the world that you heap up beguile you,
and prepare provision before the hands of Time bury you,
and sustain your hearts; after that you may pass on [Gen 18:5].
May God open for us and for you the locked doors of repentance,
and grant us and you the choicest of requests."
And he took up his parable and said [Num 23:7]:

Wake, you whose hearts sleep: how long will you sleep? / Arise, journey, for you dwell in the house of others.
Wake! You have long enough, sons of man, circled the mount [Deut 2:3] / of lust; and to the wilderness tomorrow turn.
Woe, wanderers without rest! Hurry, / and for your souls build houses in the house appointed [Job 30:23].
And know that tomorrow, for every deed, / you will be punished in judgment and give account [m. Avot 3:1].
How often you stopped your ears to those who called, / until Death called, and you answered.
If you wash the heart and grow very pure, / you will see the face of God and encamp before him.

The teller said: When I heard these right rebukes
and forceful phrases [1 Kgs 2:8],
my heart was humbled for its guilt,
and I purified my thoughts
from the blood of my iniquity,
and washed away the blood of backsliding
in the waters of repentance.
And when the people had scattered,
and gone on their journeys,
I longed to know the preacher by his form,
as I knew him by his wisdom.
I drew near to him and set my eyes upon him [Gen 44:21],
and behold, it was our friend Ḥever the Kenite.
And my heart rejoiced in his company ⚠13,
and I stayed with him a few days, to pluck of the fruit of his eloquence [Song 5:1],
and to gather of the pearls of his wisdom.
After that I bade him farewell,
and journeyed to my place.
