Thus says Heman the Ezrahite: I journeyed from Pathros
to the land of Caphtor ⚠1,
at the time when the voice of the turtledove is heard [Song 2:12].
And while I was going on the way,
strong in pain and weak of knee,
the heat of the day [Gen 18:1] was
dreadful and terrible [Hab 1:7];
it burned bodies with its fire,
and tore lions with its might,
and drove serpents and beasts out of their dens,
until my spirit grew short.
I got down from my chariot
to seek out a place to sit,
and to rest my beast.
And I saw, under the shade of the plants,
among the reeds [Hos 13:15] ⚠2,
a man caught in the cords of his sleep,
and bound with the ropes of his slumber.
When I drew near to him and stood over him, he awoke, and he started from his slumber,
and fled from before me,
and turned aside from me.
I said to him: "Peace be with you, do not fear [Judg 6:23].
You have found a brother for adversity [Prov 17:17],
and met a man of company ⚠3.
Now tell me, what have you here [Isa 22:16],
and where are you going?
And pray, what is the city of your birth,
and what is your work [Jonah 1:8]?"
He said: "My parents are a faithful seed,
of the children of the Kenite [Judg 1:16],
and my dwelling is the oak in Zaanannim [Judg 4:11]."
When I smelled the myrtles of his sayings,
and tasted the honeycomb of his words,
I knew him,
and I knew that he was our friend Ḥever the Kenite, whom I had sought.
So I sat with him,
to glean in the streams of his speech ⚠4.
I said to him: "Tell me of the great things [2 Kgs 8:4] that your eyes have seen,
and your ears have heard."
He said: "You have spoken well [Exod 10:29],
and I am ready for all that you have asked.
We have heard that in days of old, from ancient times,
the delicate cities all boasted,
those from Egypt even to Elam,
and they broke off their yoke [Gen 27:40].
Each city praised herself for her beauty and her splendor,
and each city was jealous of her sister.
No-amon ⚠5 answered,
at the head of the throng,
and said: 'Kingship befits me [Jer 10:7],
for an everlasting covenant is kept for me, and ordered [2 Sam 23:5].
God called my name No-amon,
for I am to Him as a daughter [Esth 2:7], and I was beside Him as a nursling [Prov 8:30] ⚠6.
On my neck is the necklace of the sea, which gives me strength and might,
and clothes my neck with thunder [Job 39:19].
My beauty is like fine gold that does not grow dim [Lam 4:1],
and my ways are ways of pleasantness [Prov 3:17].
At the crossroads I stand [Prov 8:2],
and at the entrances of the sea I dwell [Ezek 27:3].
Who is she among the cities who could touch my hems,
and reach to me?
Over her head my banners would be raised,
and the multitude of my waves would cover her.'
And she took up her parable and said:

Were every city to wrap herself in a robe of radiance, / and the host of heaven be as bells for her,
or set her throne on the circle of the sky, / or build a palace above the heavenly dwelling,
my throne is above their heads; and though they grow proud, / they all give their hand [1 Chr 29:24] to No-amon.

Then Egypt ⚠7 answered and said: 'I am a delight to the eyes [Gen 3:6],
comely as Jerusalem [Song 6:4].
I am a bustling city [Isa 22:2],
and for me silence is praise [Ps 65:2];
mine are strength and sound wisdom [Job 12:16].
All my men are nobles of the land,
and my merchants the honored of the earth [Isa 23:8],
and to me the nations shall come from the ends of the earth [Jer 16:19].
Around me is the Nile,
the resplendent,
by whose wonders light rose in the darkness [Ps 112:4],
whose source is from the Garden of Eden,
and whose course runs to Egypt.
In it awesome deeds were seen,
which were to God for a witness and a sign [Isa 19:20].'
And she took up her parable and said:

Were the lands to go up to heaven, / I would count them as a place for the sole of the foot to tread [Deut 2:5];
or should the rank of their glory rise to the heavenly dwelling, / the rank of my glory has risen twofold.
For the cities are as handmaids, or as / daughters, and their mother is counted Egypt.

Then Jerusalem answered and said: 'When I was in the days of my prime [Job 29:4],
the Shekhinah dwelt at my threshold,
the angels on high desired my land,
and I was the camp of God [Gen 32:3],
and the shining stars were jealous of me.
For the glory of God was in my temple,
and holiness in my tent,
and eternal light over my banner,
and piety in my nobles,
and prophecy in my elders and my infants.
And now, through the sin of the men of my council [Job 19:19],
my glory has gone into exile [1 Sam 4:21],
and my foundation has been laid bare [Ps 137:7].
And the place where the Shekhinah dwelt,
a cloud settles upon it [Job 3:5].
And in the place of holiness and prophecy,
the rebel is defiled [Zeph 3:1] ⚠8,
and the spirit of impurity [Zech 13:2] is there.
And in the abode of the halls
are mute idols [Hab 2:18],
and the forms of images.
Yet for all this I have rank and pre-eminence in the eyes of the peoples,
and my name is over all the lands for glory and remembrance.'
And she took up her parable and said:

Let every man's tongue cleave to his jaws [Ps 22:16] ⚠9 / in the abode of the Lord, the city of Jerusalem.
She is the camp of God, and the Rock opened her gates / facing the gates of heaven.
For she is the light of the world for every eye; she is / the life of hearts, the desire of the eyes.

Then Acre answered and said: 'I am the headstone [Zech 4:7],
and my land is a holy land,
for I am the rose of Sharon [Song 2:1],
and around me are Carmel and Lebanon,
Tabor and Hermon [Ps 89:13].'
And she took up her parable and said:

I am Acre, I am the splendor of every city, / perfect in grace, and there is no end to the store [Nah 2:10].
Carmel is around me, with Lebanon, / and there the glory [Isa 40:5] of Him who dwells on high was revealed.
And my land from the days of old is holy, / and I am a neighbor to the Shekhinah ⚠10.

Then Damascus answered and said: 'Kingship befits me,
and my rank was set up from the beginnings of the earth [Prov 8:23].
My name has gone out in all the world [Ezek 16:14],
until Shinar and Elam were forgotten beside me,
for the pleasantness of my treasures,
and the number of my garden beds,
and the breadth of my highways,
and the splendor of my streets.
The sun is a turban at my head,
and the stars kiss my feet.
All my chambers are upper rooms and windows,
and all my roads springs [Josh 15:19] and places of drawing water [Judg 5:11].
Within me every delight is pleasant and fair,
and around me are Amana and Pharpar [2 Kgs 5:12] ⚠11.
All my hills and mountains are gardens and orchards,
as the waters cover the sea [Isa 11:9],
and from them the mountains drip sweet wine [Joel 4:18].'
And she took up her parable and said:

Kingship befits me, because in me / the Glorious One set the throne of His praise.
Behold, all the glory of the world and all its splendor, / its greatness is brought low before my rank.
For my Creator made His glory dwell in / Hadrach, and Damascus is His resting place [Zech 9:1] ⚠12.

Then Zobah ⚠13 answered and said: 'I am a lamp set in order ⚠14,
and a royal city.
My rank has endured from generation to generation,
and leaned on the pillar of kingship.
My land is white as the moon ⚠15,
and in all kinds of dainties fat and fresh [Ps 92:15].
I am perfect in beauty [Ezek 27:3], without a blemish,
whether you go down to the valleys or walk on a bare height [Num 23:3].
In me, of the men of wisdom, are nobles and lofty ones,
and they are exceeding wise [Prov 30:24].
Of them are physicians who heal ⚠16,
and of them those who make the simple wise [Ps 19:8],
and of them scribes who set out arrays of fine gold,
and wield the scribe's rod [Judg 5:14].'
And she took up her parable and said:

When the lands of the age are named together, / behold, they give their hand to Aram-zobah.
My throne is at the head of them all, and before my height / they sank low, and even lay down in sorrow [Isa 50:11].
And when all the sheaves were bound, / my sheaf arose and stood upright [Gen 37:7] ⚠17.

Then Ashur ⚠18 answered and said: 'Happy am I, for the daughters call me happy [Gen 30:13],
and the delicate ones praise me,
and the cities are jealous of me.
I am Ashur, made happy in my rank,
and as my name, so is my praise [Ps 48:11].
Every praiser falls short of setting out my greatness,
for I gather in all precious things,
and contain all the honored qualities.'
And she took up her parable and said:

Above every city God has honored me, / and set for me a precious throne, and lifted me up [Isa 63:9].
All who see me in darkness behold light, / and I sweeten a man's mouth when he remembers me.
I am Ashur, made happy among the daughters, / and the ear heard and called me happy [Job 29:11].

Then Babylon answered and said: 'I am the delicate one [Isa 47:8],
the glory of every land.
Before my splendor and my greatness,
behold, the nations are as a drop from a bucket [Isa 40:15].
And I am a sister to the holy city;
for since the day the holy people went into exile
from the holy land,
I have gathered them to me,
and in my shadow I have hidden them,
and from then until now I have not cast them away,
nor abhorred them [Lev 26:44].
From me went forth the Geonim of wisdom ⚠19,
and the sages of judgment [Isa 28:7].'
And she took up her parable and said:

I am the crown of the age, a delicate land, / and my soil is the glory over every country.
Are not all the lands like stones / in a sling [Prov 26:8], and I a pearl?
The sages of God dwelt in me, / and the Shekhinah dwelt within their hearts."

Said the teller: When he had finished his parables,
and the sweetness of his words,
I said to him: "How precious to me [Ps 139:17] are your sayings,
and how sweet your riddles,
and how pleasant the parables of your testimonies!
May God reward your deeds,
and spread the tabernacle of His peace over you."
And he asked after my welfare,
and went away from me.
