And I made one on the great Nasi, our master Sheshet, who was the head of the generous of the world, and gathered in himself all the treasured virtues:

If you ask about the master Sheshet: / he is left in our time as the head of the corner [Ps 118:22].
He has reached old age, and his generosity / has not worn out, but has gained new youth [Gen 18:12].

And I made one on the matter of friends, the pleasant and the beloved [2 Sam 1:23]:

The breadth of the lands is narrow, and cannot / hold two enemies;
while a place of a span is very / wide for a thousand friends.

And I made one on the congregation of Lunel,
may God keep it:

Shall a lip of excellence [Prov 17:7] attain the praise of a people / whose mind breathes the fear of God [Isa 11:3]?
He who sends a word of wisdom to them / sets up the moon against the light of their sun,
and sends drops of dew to the Sea of Reeds, / and brings a gift of dates to Jericho ⚠48.

And when I was in Lunel, Rabbi Jonathan ha-Kohen, of blessed memory, asked me to translate for him the Order of Seeds
of Rabbi Moses, the mightiest of the shepherds [1 Sam 21:8] ⚠49;
and I translated it, and sent it to him with these poems:

May God command peace / to the company of the blameless,
the wise, the pleasant, / the understanding, the known [Deut 1:13],
who make darkness as light [Ps 139:12] / as they sit in the Torah;
and it is like the lampstand, / and they are its cups [Exod 25:31].
I come before the exalted ones / and the shining lights,
and the priests of God, / with an offering of Seeds ⚠50.

And I wrote to Rabbi Joseph, the source of sound wisdom,
son of the Egyptian prince Rabbi Obadiah, the memory of the righteous for a blessing:

O prince, wisdom and understanding trace their descent to you, / and within your heart they were found on the day they were sought.
On the day God created the treasures of time, / and they roamed to find in whose shadow to take refuge,
God said: "Hurry and go to / Joseph; what he says to you, do [Gen 41:55]."

And on the matter of love I made these poems
in the days of youth:

The gazelle of grace, whose cheeks / are overlaid with sapphires [Song 5:14],
and whose eyes draw against me / the flash of a flying sword;
his lips are honey, his face a garden, / his teeth pearls, and his mouth honeycomb.
In the garden of his cheeks is my eyes' joy, / when they watch and gaze;
but my soul in the fire of his love / is scorched and burned.
My two eyes are in the Garden of Eden, / but my heart is in Topheth.

And I made one in the city of Calneh on a man whose name was Blessings,
and baseness was with him [1 Sam 25:25]:

A fool, jealous for his father's baseness, / until in baseness they were alike, as the son, so the father.
He is called Blessings, but Curses is his name, / as they call the blind man "full of light" ⚠51.

And I made one in Adina on a man of the nobles
who used to practise wanton deeds:

In Adina there are men like beasts, / who are lain with by the wanton and base.
And like them, those who lie with them are beasts; / and the vessels are like the feet of the owners ⚠52.

And I wrote to a man among the nobles and grandees of the generation,
because the steps of his letters were late in coming to me [Judg 5:28]:

Bring greetings to David ⚠53, who is left-handed and dumb, / low of spirit, though his pride is lifted over me:
left-handed in writing a letter to his friend, / and dumb, for the speech of his lips is withheld from him.

And I made one for a man who married a holy woman,
and kept company with every harlot ⚠54:

He lusts after daughters of strangers, and forsakes the wife / of his bosom. Come near and hear his sins!
In place of holy daughters he takes strange women in exchange, / for he counts the rejected ones as the fixed ⚠55.

And I made one on the matter of the tears of the eyes,
which are driven out like the River of Egypt [Amos 8:8]:

Evil Time, the eyes of your grief have darkened, / and your hand is lifted up, so that they do not see [Isa 26:11].
My thoughts, how long shall griefs / take refuge under your wings at all times?
You kill my joy in the daughter of my eye / by my much weeping; but the daughter you keep alive [Exod 1:22] ⚠56.

And I sent from Damascus to Ḥamath, to the physician Rabbi Moses bar Zedakah:

Master of the generation, fence your breach and your wall, / and return to tend the rest of your flock and your herd.
Do not forget the friends who hope for you, / and wait for the word of your mouth and your vow.
For you are an anointed cherub [Ezek 28:14]; therefore / return to your city, and set your splendour in your chamber.

And I made one on the soul,
and on the wise spirit:

Encamped and shut in the pit of the body, / from the day she encamped she was stripped of her ornaments [Exod 33:6].
High, yet joined to a worm ⚠57: / who can set her blemish beside her great beauty?
On the day she strips off the body, then she puts on / her ornaments, and puts off the garment of her captivity [Deut 21:13].
She shines like the sun; she has gone up from the house / of prison, the dwelling of darkness and gloom.
She praises her Maker in the sky; for so / let every soul praise the Lord [Ps 150:6].

And I made one on the length of the exile,
and the many years of poverty and lowliness:

Mighty One, reveal the strength of Your hand / today; how can You hide Yourself?
The one crushed in the pit of exile dreams of Your salvation, / like a slave dreaming of redemption.
He asks about the end, and none answers; / he is like a blind man asking a dumb one.
This is his thousandth year that he hopes that perhaps / You will visit the iniquity of his foes, which is not yet complete [Gen 15:16].
Lord, return, and restore the altar hearth [Ezek 43:15] to Israel, / and let Jacob come whole [Gen 33:18].

And I made one on the matter of visiting a house of prayer,
to set out praise to God:

The court of Your holy house I have risen to visit, / to behold Your pleasantness evening and morning [Ps 27:4].
See my pain, and look at the hidden places of my heart, / You who see the hidden things of every heart, and search them.
My heart hopes, and has set all its hope on You there, / and knows in truth that You will not lie.
Scatter my enemies like a menstruous thing [Isa 30:22], and the eyes / of those who envy me, that they may not see my good, put out [Num 16:14].
Set Your right hand on the vine You planted [Ps 80:16], / and do not be the one who uproots it and tears it out.

And I made one on men who make their tongue smooth,
and their throat is an open grave [Ps 5:10]:

There are men sweet of speech and lip, / and with their tongue and their speech they entice me.
They show me a face of favour and love, / but I know that their ambush is in their heart.
And how could they hide the evil of their heart, / when the look of their faces testifies against them [Isa 3:9]?

And I made one on the matter of parting,
in which my foot is caught:

How long shall I be a wanderer and a fugitive like Cain [Gen 4:12], / struck down by grief, and not by strong drink and wine?
I hope for salvation and do not see it; but / distresses I see eye to eye.
Like a blind man in the dark places of prison, / at midnight I hope for light, and there is none [Job 3:9].

And I made one on proud men,
who are worse than all creatures:

May God cut off the proud, / despised, yet proud up to the stars of the heavenly dwelling.
They wear clothes that are new, but / their deeds are worn out and patched [Josh 9:5].
They seem to those who see them like a building / that is handsome, while its foundations are crushed.
They are wicked; and far be it that like them should be / the men of Sodom, wicked and sinners [Gen 13:13].

And I made one on men generous with their mouths
and shutting their hands:

Alas, my eye has seen men who are like / Nabal the Carmelite, and like the brother of Cain ⚠58.
Their name is princes, but in their giving they are / clouds, and there is neither wind nor rain [Prov 25:14].

And I made one on a man whom I thought faithful,
and who turned into an enemy when I tested him:

Your friendship I thought like manna / hidden and laid up in a precious jar [Exod 16:33].
I tested it, and the test was like the sun / that came out and grew hot upon it, and it melted [Exod 16:21].

And I made one on a man who bought many books,
and he was of the brutish fools:

See a simpleton grown old in his folly, / while his bad deeds are new every morning [Lam 3:23].
He buys many books of testimony, / and with them he is like a donkey carrying books.

And I made one for a man whom I praised in my verse,
and who recognized the eloquence of my words:

O prince, put on the fine robes of my eloquence, / since the balm of my envoys binds up every pang ⚠59.
He who scoops honey from my words will know it. Truly, / he has scooped honey from the body of the lion [Judg 14:9].

And I made one in Damascus on Rabbi Zedakah the judge,
for a cause and a matter:

A man planted in his vineyard a fruit tree of the garden; / he tended it day and night, and watered it.
And when it began to give its fruit in its season [Ps 1:3], / and to every palate its taste was sweet,
he uprooted it, and his labour was spent for nothing; / and such is the deed of Rabbi Zedakah.
Yesterday he built a shrine of discipline, and today / he has torn it down, and it has become a deep pit [Prov 23:27].
I hope for justice, and behold, bloodshed and despair, / and for righteousness, and behold, a cry [Isa 5:7] ⚠60.
He did not know that the Rock has set within my mouth / a sword polished against all my enemies.
When I draw it, the giants / I bring down in wrath to the leech ⚠61.
I keep silent; but when my verse speaks, / it shakes Time with its strong hand.
Who has come near to war with me and escaped, / and who has put forth his hand against my splendour and been clean [1 Sam 26:9]?

And I made one in Egypt for the judge Rabbi Menaḥem, of blessed memory, on the matter of a man in whom I had trusted,
and I stumbled through his speech:

In my distress I called upon an idol, / and trusted in a despised image.
He was silver dross [Prov 26:23], and I refined / him, so that perhaps I might see his secret;
and I cast him into the fire, / and there came out this calf [Exod 32:24].

And on the matter of love I made one,
and built a house of love:

The gazelle of grace has wounded me with his beauty, / and kindled in me the burning of his love and its pain.
With the measure he measured I repaid him [m. Sotah 1:7], / and fed him the fruit of his sin and his guilt:
when I showed his eyes a mirror of cast metal, / I turned the burning of my love back into his own heart.

And I made one in Ashur on a wicked, domineering woman [Ezek 16:30],
who blazed like fire,
and who had a wanton son like his mother
in his anger and his wrath:

A wife of folly: may the Rock hide her in the pit; / may He quickly put her into Sheol and break her.
She is like Athaliah, only her son is like Ahaz- / iah [2 Kgs 8:26]. May her Maker not help her, but profane her.

And I made one on a bird caught
in a hunter's snare:

See a bird that has gone up into a snare [Amos 3:5], / and its wing is of no use to it in its distress.
I reckoned it like a son of man in the world, / whose honour is of no use to him at his end.

And I made one on a polished sword [Ezek 21:14]:

A sword in the hand of men of war flies, / and like a tongue of fire it goes among them.
My friends asked me: "What is it like?" / And I said: "It is like the tongue of the wise."

And I made one to teach men discipline,
and to behave with piety towards all flesh:

You must honour your rank, / for your rank will not honour you.
As you do, so you will find; / ask your father, and he will tell you [Deut 32:7].
If you do good, it will exalt you; / or if you do evil, it will destroy you.

On a base man who shuts his hands
against gifts and kindnesses:

If a base man heaps up wealth, / he will quickly leave it to his enemies.
He swallowed wealth and vomits it up; / God will drive it out of his belly [Job 20:15] ⚠62.

And I made one on a man haughty of heart,
going on backsliding [Isa 57:17]:

When you see a man in whom there is nothing upright, / only his soul is precious through wealth,
pay no heed to his pride, / for his rank is the raising of the cud [Lev 11:3] ⚠63.

And I made one on a man who was always spitting in the synagogue:

It is fitting that over all who come to the sanctuary / the waters of purification should be sprinkled,
because of base men who despise it, / for they did not withhold spittle from before it [Job 30:10].

And these I made for a man who made silver engravings and a crown
for a Torah scroll:

Apples of gold [Prov 25:11] are the likeness of the / rings on the poles of the ark.
From the sanctuary their sound is heard [Exod 28:35], / like the bells on the robe of Aaron.
Such is the gift of the prince Joseph; / every man of understanding will sing of it.
Let all who come confess his kindness to God, / a memorial for the children of Israel [Exod 30:16].

And I made one for taking pleasure in gardens,
and drinking deep of the daughter of the vines:

My friend, drink with me in the shade of a garden; / I will set myrtle before you, and a rose.
I will rejoice in the daughter of the vineyard, whose joy is / new, though she is worn and old.
Behold, a day in God's eye is like a thousand / years [Ps 90:4], and the year like a season.
Would that I might be in the cup / until in God's eye it is a year!

And again I made one on the matter of longing,
and the beloved company:

A doe brought me to the house of love, / pure and perfect like Abigail.
And when her veil was removed, she revealed a form / that put to shame with its beauty the daughter of Abihail [Esth 2:15].
Her light shone in the night, and all trembled, / and the mountains skipped like rams [Ps 114:4].
I said: "Alas!" Her hidden charms were revealed, / and she bared her arm like a woman of valour.
Then she turned over me a cloud of black / hair, and the day turned to night.

On the Book of Knowledge ⚠64:

A book like a tree planted by the waters of knowledge and understanding, / for in it every seeker finds his desire.
The books of our Geonim, though they are very many, / are its branches, and it is the root.

And I made one on a man in Jerusalem about whom evil reports went out:

Is a man appointed on Mount Zion, unclean, as head / of the holy, one who has a plague spreading in the skin?
He opens himself wide to every passer-by; therefore / all who know him call his name Rosh ha-Peor [Num 23:28] ⚠65.

And I made one on a man who left his wife
for his maidservant,
and she bore him a daughter who looked like him;
and his name was Jacob:

The ways of a flattering, worthless man / are gathered here: hear, sons of Jacob [Gen 49:2].
He lusted after his maidservant in place of his wife, / and turned the level ground into crooked [Isa 40:4];
until she bore him a daughter who was like / him in a brazen forehead and a crooked heart [Jer 17:9].
His evil name has sworn never to forsake / her; and he had no delight in the daughter of Jacob [Gen 34:19] ⚠66.

And these I made for a foolish man,
who could hold two thousand baths of folly [1 Kgs 7:26]:

My friend, I have put the vowel points in my verse for you, / since your mind is feeble and faint,
and I have cleared its paths for you, / so that perhaps your heart may master it.
For I remembered God's command: before / the blind you shall not put a stumbling block [Lev 19:14].
