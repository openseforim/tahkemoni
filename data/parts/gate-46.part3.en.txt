And the rest of the congregation of Damascus are pious and worthy;
only there are, among the pearls,
stones,
and among the lilies,
nettles,
and among the righteous,
men who do harm,
and among the holy, the defiled ⚠1,
and within the fine flour,
refuse,
and among the blossoms,
thorns.
For there are among them cruel and hard men,
who bite like serpents,
poor in deeds,
wearing lovely garments,
naked of honorable qualities,
full of pride,
and empty of humility.
And of the wicked who are in it,
and the evil in its midst,
is Barukh ⚠2 the physician, the villain, the accursed,
from Mount Ebal [Deut 27:13],
gatherer of every rebellion and trespass,
of the family of the sons of Belial,
who have denied God above [Job 31:28],
and of the knees that have bowed to Baal [1 Kgs 19:18].
He is a physician of no value [Job 13:4],
a fool, son of a knave,
who boasts of his cures up to the heart of heaven [Deut 4:11];
and he is the pit, only there is no water in it [Gen 37:24].
For in medicine he does not know his right hand from his left [Jonah 4:11],
but God made his luck prosper.
He feeds a man who is sick and wasting away
the flesh of a dog or a donkey, and by it he is healed.
And when he comes to the sick, he stands before them like an ox and a donkey,
and "the donkey spoke with them, saying" [Gen 34:8] ⚠3. His fathers were the fathers of impurity,
men of the arm, men of wickedness and sin,
all of them full of transgressions,
empty of upright works,
wearing lovely garments,
naked of honorable qualities,
hard as iron,
laden with iniquities like the goat for Azazel [Lev 16:22],
swift as gazelles to uncover nakedness,
lazy to do a commandment.
In them is every loathsome soul,
and every beast that parts the hoof [Lev 11:3],
laden with transgressions,
and lying under its burden [Exod 23:5];
and in them the abomination and the swarming thing
that swarms upon the earth [Lev 11:41].
These are the deeds of his fathers,
in whose measure he goes down.
And his son Isaac provoked the Creator with his abominations,
and with his villainies made men forget
the villainies of his fathers.
For he is haughty of eyes [Ps 101:5],
short of temper [Prov 14:17],
unclean of lips [Isa 6:5],
shut of hands,
crooked of ways [Prov 28:6],
and hasty with his feet [Prov 19:2].
He learned the ways of his cursed fathers,
and added to them a thousand times as many as they [Deut 1:11].
For you find in him the cunning of foxes,
and the thieving of cats,
the insolence of a dog, and the craft of a bear lying in wait [Lam 3:10],
the uncleanness of the raven,
the filth of the abomination,
the scum of the swarming thing,
the stubbornness of the donkey,
the folly of the ox,
the beard of the he-goat,
and the cruelty of the lion.
He has gathered every evil quality,
and every base deed.
If they dipped him in the sea,
and washed him of his uncleanness,
the waters of the sea would be made unclean before they made him clean;
and if they passed him through fire,
every fire would fail and go out before they refined him.
His father called his name Crown of the Lights ⚠4,
and erred, for his name is nothing but Rest of the Curses.
May his name be blotted out for generations,
and may he be borne to the graves [Job 21:32].
And the son, when he stands before the wise, there is no fool like him;
and when he stands before his father, there is none so understanding and wise as he [Gen 41:39].
And because the father is as stupid as an ox and a donkey,
therefore I called the name of his son Shechem son of Hamor [Gen 34:2] ⚠5.
And from the abundance of folly in him,
his heart lifted him up [Exod 35:21]
to compose wanton and empty poems,
for the emptiers have emptied them [Nah 2:3]:
loathsome poems and snares,
unclean as a filthy rag [Isa 64:5],
as though he had stolen them from his father's lineage,
or hewn them out of his parents' qualities;
poems that lay bare to all who see them
the nakedness of those who made them,
making their masters despised in their eyes [Esth 1:17],
full of vomit and filth [Isa 28:8],
and of the unclean spirit [Zech 13:2];
poems
like the crackling of thorns [Eccl 7:6],
and verses
like the howling of dogs.
And he barks in his poetry among the sons of his people,
and called it Nobah, after his own name [Num 32:42] ⚠6.
His poetry is a harlot,
not holy,
and profaned,
not praised ⚠7.
She sits at the entrance to Enaim [Gen 38:14],
and gives her adulterers food, clothing and conjugal rights [Exod 21:10].
And Judah ⚠8 saw her and took her for a harlot [Gen 38:15],
and cried out against her before every reviler and blasphemer:
"Bring her out, and let her be burned" [Gen 38:24].
I sent him a poem before which the lights of heaven blushed,
and Shechem son of Hamor saw her, and lay with her, and humbled her [Gen 34:2].
And she was defiled under him, and I visited her iniquity upon her [Lev 18:25],
and I put her in the prison house, wearing the garment of poverty,
until the day of her death, in living widowhood [2 Sam 20:3].
And if the son is a fountain of filth and excrement,
are not his fathers the fathers of impurity?
For his father, by his abominations, has raised his horns up to the heavens,
and he is the ram which you saw, having the horns [Dan 8:20].
There is no villain like him,
and none who does villainy as he does.
For I have felt through all my stuff [Gen 31:37],
and none like him has come into my hand or reached me.
He climbed on high steps,
and was chosen among the angels of God,
and married into the family of Zadok, head of the academy Ge'on Ya'akov;
and the bad was joined to the good,
and an angel was coupled with a harmful Satan,
and a wicked man married into a righteous one,
and gold was bound to iron:
one lot for the Lord and one lot for Azazel [Lev 16:8].
He is base in his purse,
loathsome in his cup,
despised in his lineage,
cruel in his anger [b. Eruvin 65b],
and faithless to his Maker.
And the wealth that he has heaped up in his house
is riches kept by their owner to his hurt [Eccl 5:12].
And the gold and the silver in his hand are oppressed and afflicted [Isa 53:7],
crushed and tormented.
And when it comes into his hand, he locks it up like a slave in the pit,
and it never sees freedom.
And every day his wealth cries out in the ears of every living thing:
"My master has given me into hands from which I cannot rise [Lam 1:14].
Have pity on me, have pity on me, you my friends, for villains rule over me [Job 19:21],
and unclean men abuse me.
For from the day I fell into the hand of the accursed, I went down to the chambers of Sheol,
and in dark places I dwell [Lam 3:6];
he has walled me in so that I cannot go out, he has made my chain heavy [Lam 3:7]."
And if he knew that the lice on his flesh would profit someone,
all the people of east and west would gather to bring out the lice, and they could not [Exod 8:14].
And if he makes a feast,
it is a witness to his villainy.
For instead of rams of the breed of Bashan [Deut 32:14],
you find stinking and stale meat,
and smoked food,
and its smoke goes up like the smoke of a furnace [Gen 19:28].
And instead of hens and fattened fowl [1 Kgs 5:3],
he brings the flesh of oxen and donkeys,
as though his pots were a pit,
and an ox or a donkey fell into it [Exod 21:33].
And his bread is unleavened bread, without leaven,
and the wine is vinegar ⚠9.
His lambs are lean and poor,
and his oxen stunted, not long of limb [Lev 22:23].
And his pots are full of grass and greens,
that steal the meat and leave the broth.
And the bowls are full of greens,
and empty of meat;
and there is nothing in the pot but water and herbs,
and all flesh is grass [Isa 40:6].
And if you ask about the meat, they tell you: "There is none,"
for it is meat hidden from the eye.
And his fruits are stale and bad,
full of worms [Exod 16:20],
and all the choice fruits
thin and blasted by the east wind [Gen 41:6].
And the radishes are withered and lean,
and the cucumbers, like the lineage of his fathers, bitter and hard.
And the pears are sewn on threads,
and the sesame seeds counted out by number.
And the quinces
are a disgrace,
and the grapes
rotten,
and the figs
neither fat nor rich,
and the pomegranates
white within and red without,
and the apples
puffed up with wind,
and the dates,
like his evil name, bitter,
and the raisins
shriveled like his mother's breasts [Hos 9:14].
And the almonds are shaped like almonds on the branch of contempt [Exod 25:33] ⚠10,
blasted by the east wind like the ways of his fathers [Gen 41:23],
and the nuts
hard and stubborn like his two hands.
And instead of myrtles and spices he sets out
the leeks and the onions and the garlic [Num 11:5] ⚠11.
And of everything there is a lack at his feast;
only of water and herbs there is excess,
because they are the food of his fathers and his own food,
and he lows over them like an ox over its fodder [Job 6:5].
And when a beggar comes to the gate of his house,
he says to him: "There is no bread and no water with us [Num 21:5];
only herbs we have in plenty,
and both straw and fodder enough with us [Gen 24:25]."
And at the time of his meal,
he does not feed his house dog from his bread.
And if the dog could speak, it would say to him: "Do you think to betray your companion?
Your friend and your father's friend do not forsake [Prov 27:10]."
Transgressions have no abode but his heart,
and villainy no camp but in him,
and insolence no place but in his face,
and impurity no foundation but his fathers and his sons.
And his custom on festivals and Sabbaths,
and on the days of miracles and signs,
is to flee from the house of prayer to his garden,
and to separate himself from the congregation,
to fulfill in himself what is said: "And the Lord shall separate him for evil out of all the tribes of Israel" [Deut 29:20].
Like a barren tree, he has neither taste nor smell,
for he flees from before the Lord [Jonah 1:3].
For he is afraid lest they take up a charity collection or a pledge,
and it be to him a stumbling block and an offense [1 Sam 25:31].
Therefore he despises the house of prayer,
as his fathers despised praise.
For so his wicked father and his grandfather commanded him,
that he should not come to the house of his Maker and Owner,
nor give the Creator a share in his wealth,
to fulfill what is said: "The money of guilt offerings and the money of sin offerings was not brought into the house of the Lord" [2 Kgs 12:17].
And of his wealth it is said: "You shall not bring the hire of a harlot or the price of a dog into the house of the Lord your God" [Deut 23:19].
Not even once a year does he come,
and like a leper he does not draw near to the sanctuary, for he has a blemish [Lev 21:23];
and he shall dwell alone all the days the plague is in him,
his dwelling outside the camp [Lev 13:46].
And so he despises prayer for these reasons,
and for other reasons too:
for he is busy with disgrace ⚠12,
and with disgraceful doings,
to fulfill what is said: "Those who sanctify and purify themselves to go into the gardens" [Isa 66:17].
And they gave me clear testimony about his father,
that he burns incense to idols,
and goes out in the middle of the nights
to set out prayers to the stars,
and to offer up burnt offerings to them,
and to make cakes for the queen of heaven [Jer 7:18].
And for this it is right to excommunicate him in all the cities,
and it is permitted to kill him on the Day of Atonement [b. Pesahim 49b].
May God destroy them and make them perish, and not be willing to forgive them [Deut 29:19],
and may the two of them burn together, with none to quench them [Isa 1:31].
