Thus says Heman the Ezrahite: Ḥever the Kenite told me plainly and said: I was in the land of Thebez [Judg 9:50],
among the dwellers of Jabez [1 Chr 2:55],
weaving the fine robes of eloquence in checker-work,
and gathering the scattered insights.
One day, as I was at the gate of the city, I saw under a leafy terebinth [Ezek 6:13] a great crowd gathered,
a mixed multitude [Num 11:4].
When I drew near with a wondering heart
to see the sight,
I found a company of men of understanding,
sons of governors and of nobles,
speaking in words of eloquence,
and composing pleasant songs.
From the quarry of their hearts they hewed out pearls,
and from the spring of their minds they drew living water.
Each wished to test the other,
so that they might learn to practise the craft of verse.
When I saw that they had come to the craft of poetry,
and had mustered for its battle line,
I knew that I was their prince,
the one who sits on the throne of their glory,
and that to me they would give their hand.
I spoke up and said: "Men of heart, hear me [Job 34:10],
and listen to the eloquence of my mind.
I am the man
who with his speech shakes the everlasting mountains [Hab 3:6],
and with his verse puts the pearls to shame,
and with his mind dries up the mighty streams [Ps 74:15] ⚠1,
and brings water out of the flinty rock [Deut 8:15].
Whoever wishes to test his poems, let him bring them near to him;
and he who believes shall not make haste [Isa 28:16]."

The heads of the guests said to him: "Wherein is this strength of yours,
and by what shall we know your mind and see it?
Is it in poems,
or in letters,
or in lovely rhymed lines,
or in separate sayings?"
I said: "Know that my heart slings true at every mark,
and my mind breaks out in every kind of wisdom [Prov 18:1],
and in whatever you test me,
you will find me."
They said to me: "If so, give us to drink from the spring,
and let us hear the poems you have composed on every subject."
I said to them: "I will do as you say,
and my secret I will not hide from you.
First I will recite to you poems of repentance,
which I made to ask forgiveness for backsliding."
And I took up my parable:

My God, let my memory rise before You for good, / and be not far, for trouble is near [Ps 22:12].
Look, I pray, at the greatness of the hardship, / and do not look at the greatness of the backsliding.
With the waters of my tears wipe out my sin, and let not, I pray, / the sin of Judah be written any more [Jer 17:1] ⚠2.

And I wrote these poems on the hall of the synagogue,
shining in their beauty:

Shaddai, turn to the voice of those who cry to You, / who bow down before Your footstool.
You are exalted and mighty, and hidden where there is no / place, and revealed in Your works.
Therefore Your servants' hands were too short / to build You a dwelling to match Your greatness;
but by their strength they set up a little sanctuary [Ezek 11:16], / to turn their gaze toward Your temple.
Let Your heart and Your eyes be there [1 Kgs 9:3], for / their heart and their eyes are lifted up to You.

And these I made on the city of Jerusalem,
when I came to it from Egypt:

Peace to the city of Salem, whose beauty / is hidden from those who see it, so that they say: Where is it?
Every day I cherish her dust [Ps 102:15], for / she is the sickness of my soul, and she is its balm.
My eyes, at the time they saw her light, / could not hold back the clouds of weeping in them.
The God who desired her for His dwelling [Ps 132:13] / will see her great affliction, and bring back her captivity.
She weeps for her beloved's wandering; when He returns to her, / He will wipe the tears from her cheeks.

And again I spoke,
and on this subject I composed:

For Zion my soul and my spirit long, / to make in her my rest all the days of my life.
In love of her, hardships are sweet to me, / and for her I give my head and my cheek to the smiter [Lam 3:30].
I will not consent to be without her all my days, / until I die, and my strength and my vigour have fled [Deut 34:7] ⚠3.
I cherish her dust at all times, / until her dust leaves a mark on my forehead.
How should I long for the best of perfumes and spices, / when she is my spice, my best perfume and my fragrance?
Her dust I make a healing for my wounds; / I spread it on the boil of my heart, and it lives [Isa 38:21].

And these I made in my sickness,
when God granted me my balm:

Let Your anger not burn, my God, / against a poor man who takes refuge in Your shadow.
Take me not up in the midst of my days [Ps 102:25]; / will my death profit You, or be of use to You?
I do not ask for wealth: give me the / soul, and the goods take for Yourself [Gen 14:21].

And I made one on a man among the pious of His people,
who had departed to his eternal home [Eccl 12:5]:

For fear of Him you afflicted your soul with fasting [Ps 35:13], / until you were like a hair's thread.
The sons of the base toiled, and you toiled for good; / for they went down, and you went up.
Happy is your only one [Ps 22:21], which dwells by the throne / of the Most High; and it you ransomed from Sheol ⚠4.

And these I made at sea,
when I was saved from the roar of its waves and breakers:

With what shall I come before God [Mic 6:6], and who am I, / that His grace has brought me thus far [2 Sam 7:18]?
From the end of the earth and its corners [Isa 41:9] / He called me to come to His holy mountain.
When I crossed the sea, He gave me good tidings: / "When you pass through the waters, I am with you [Isa 43:2]."

And these I sent to the tomb of our lord Ezekiel,
to lighten the weight of my sins:

To visit your tomb my eyes are pools, / and my tears are rolled in blood [Isa 9:4].
Prophet of God, Ezekiel, honoured / in the mouth of the hosts of the heights and of the footstool ⚠5.
My innards long to lick your dust [Isa 49:23], / and to gather from the dust of your tomb for spices ⚠6,
and to breathe in perfumes of spice from your spirit, / burned as incense with all the chief spices [Song 4:14].
And then I will sing to the God of my life [Ps 42:9] and say: / "The lines have fallen to me in pleasant places [Ps 16:6]."

And these I made for the tomb of our lord Ezra the Scribe,
to find atonement:

To the everlasting light, which put the lights to shame, / while around it they stand like lampstands.
To it come those who hear its fame, / and lift up their souls to it as gifts.
How can they deny its wonders, / when those who pass by it see mighty deeds?
The glory of the Most High sparkles around his tomb, / and spreads light to closed eyes:
the glory of Ezra, the honoured holy one of God, / who is to all that lives a help in troubles [Ps 46:2] ⚠7.

And I made one on a fierce and stubborn man,
who wrote answers against the work of our master Moses ⚠8:

Whoever answers against the words of Moses / has broken the yoke and snapped the bonds [Jer 5:5].
Let the lying lips be dumb, / which speak arrogantly against the righteous [Ps 31:19].

And I made more on this matter, against the one who boasted over him:

The weakling, when he is in his house, / shows off his might to his wife;
but when he must come near to the battle and approach, / then his disgrace and his shame are laid bare.

And these I made,
and sent them from Spain to our master Moses:

From you, O prince, comes our praise, / and in your exalting is all our greatness.
Your ways are high and deep, and the excellence / of your work is above our works.
You are an angel of God, and you were created in the image / of God, though you are of our form.
Because of you God said: "Let us make / man in our image, after our likeness [Gen 1:26]."

And I made one on another matter, its opposite,
on men who had corrupted their way [Gen 6:12]:

Woe for men who were not created to help [Isa 30:5]; / therefore at the mention of them our faces fall.
They are like oxen without a heart; and how / should they know a word of wisdom, or understand?
Not for them did God say: "Let us make / man in our image, after our likeness [Gen 1:26]."

And these I made for Rabbi Menaḥem bar Isaac, the judge of Egypt,
who has no equal, open of hand and clean of palms [Ps 24:4],
may God who dwells in heaven bless him:

Your right hand is wider than the river Pishon [Gen 2:11], / and for you God created generosity.
Yours is a soul that pours out rivers, / though it is hewn from flames of fire [Ps 29:7].
If the right hand of all who shut their hand / and of every miser touched your generous hand,
then the men of your generation would turn generous, / and not one miser would be left in Zobah ⚠9.

And these I made on Rabbi Abraham Maimon, of blessed memory.
I made them in Jerusalem,
and sent them to Egypt:

If the precious one is lord over every lord, / he is in truth a servant to Abraham.
Since the day his glory was hidden from me, / I go roaming for his sake, bewildered.
My thoughts long for him as I cross the land / of Nod [Gen 4:16], and with me there passes Chimham [2 Sam 19:39] ⚠10.

And I made one for a man who had a precious virgin daughter,
and she was found with child:

A graceful dove nesting in a father's bosom, / who filled her with prey day by day and provided for her.
They thought her a he without a dot; but when / they examined her, they found her with a mappiq ⚠11.

And I made one for a man of the base,
the chief of the cheats:

See a miser whose hand is a virgin / to every asker, and to miserliness a wedded wife.
In him is a wonder and a sign: for he is one [Job 23:13], / yet he is reckoned as a Great Sanhedrin ⚠12.

And I made one on the head, Rabbi Samuel bar Nissim, and sent it to him:

O prince who has a camp among the host on high, / my heart at parting from you is crushed and afflicted.
You are the light of the world, yet you dwell in darkness; / so the Presence encamps in thick darkness [1 Kgs 8:12].
I call, and my joy does not answer, without you; / but weeping answers before I call it [Isa 65:24].
The fire of the bush was seen in you, O prince; for / you are like the angel of God in the bush [Exod 3:2].

And I made one for a man whose hand was shut,
and the cloud of his bounty held back:

Your hand to the asker is shut, / a virgin, whom no man has known [Gen 24:16].
Had your mother been so, / there would be no blemish in her seed.

And I made more on him,
and sent them to him:

See a miser whose hand is like a rock, / and his heart makes it strong to hold it back.
Men point his hand with a pataḥ, / but he changes the pataḥ into a qamats ⚠13.

And I made one for a young man who married an old woman,
who had wealth, and no end to her store [Nah 2:10]:

You are a wonder, lady of grace, in your affairs: / for your great honour, your sons take you to wife [Isa 62:5].
If you saw the form of your likeness in a mirror / of cast metal [Job 37:18], your face would fall for fear of yourself.
Your beauty is as few as the days of your husband's years, and your / wealth is great without end, like your years.

And I made one for a base man who shut his hand against me,
and had learned miserliness from his father:

If a son of the base shut his / hand against me, he kept his father's command.
God has sworn that the kindness of the empty / shall never rest on the honoured.
For the rod of the / wicked shall not rest upon the lot of the righteous [Ps 125:3].

And I made one for a man generous with his tongue,
and a miser with his wealth:

If men of office are like an eye, / you are truly to them like its pupil.
And when they run on the path of honour, / you alone pass over first.
And what they fill by the work of their hands, / you fill by the speech of the tongue.

And I made one for a man who sows strife [Prov 6:19],
whom I saw praying in humility and supplication:

See an old man going up to lay down supplications, / with a heart that gathers iniquity [Ps 41:7] and full of harlotry.
Wrapped in white like a dovecote, / whitened outside, filthy inside.

And I made one for a man who had a name for generosity,
while his hand was dry of the dew of giving:

I wonder at the clouds of your goodness, how they were shut in, / my friend, and the streams of your hand, how they froze.
You are like a drum ⚠14 whose voice is very / loud; and those who split it open find emptiness and wind.

And I made one for a man of the people of Calneh ⚠15,
whom I had praised, and he fled from me to Ḥaran and hid from my eyes:

Born of vileness, brother of baseness and miserliness, / in whom all base deeds are gathered:
I praised his name in two tongues, / and stumbled in the speech of my mouth and lips.
But when I sought him, I did not find him [Song 3:1], / and they said that his path was to Ḥaran.
And if he has hidden and concealed himself from me, / he has left his evil name in the mouth of my pen,
like a mouse that has gone into a hole / and left its tail outside in the mouth of the cat.

And I made one in Calneh on one of the base men who make verse,
a maker of poems,
who hews broken cisterns [Jer 2:13],
and their waters are bitter;
and his name is Blessings ⚠16:

Curses is he. He composes verse like excrement, / and the excrement in his mouth he counts as honeycomb.
His musing lays bare his disgrace, / and his nakedness is uncovered in his verse.
His father was enslaved to baseness, / and his mother was given over to reproaches.
Both of them have been gathered to their eternity, / but their reproach is not gathered in; it stays as a sign.
And the son still keeps to his fathers' uncleanness, / like a foul smell over a privy.

I asked a book of him;
all his parables are parables of ashes [Job 13:12].
And right after he had sent it,
he came to me and took it back.
And I made on him:

Curses came and lent me his book / one day, and on the second he snatched it away.
I reckoned him, in his giving and his taking, / like a dog that returns to its vomit and eats it [Prov 26:11].

And I made one for a miser who promised me his bounty and vowed vows,
and it brought forth buds, and put out blossoms, and bore ripe lies [Num 17:23] ⚠17.

When a miser vows a gift with his mouth, / he counts what he uttered rashly [Lev 5:4] as a sin.
Let him shut both his hands, / and what he sinned will be forgiven him.

And again I made one on him,
and this is what I saw:

Miser, will you hide your blemish? / Behold, your witness is in your hand.
Miserliness clings to you as an ornament; / it has become a sign upon your hand [Exod 13:9] ⚠18.

And I made one on wine,
pleasant to the sight of the eye:

Everything new is very sweet and pleasant, / and when it grows old, souls loathe it;
but wine, the more it ages and grows old, / the more it cheers God and men [Judg 9:13].

More on wine:

The son of the choice vine I thought faithful to me, / but he stings like a serpent [Prov 23:32] and beguiles.
I loved him, and he betrayed me with cunning, / and drew my heart and my soul after him.
I set him to be kept within me, / and he rose up to be the keeper of my head [1 Sam 28:2].

And I made one on love, in the days of youth
and the delight of young men:

A gazelle of grace, in his beauty the likeness of Joseph, / and on his head a crown of splendour, like a ḥolam.
And my soul is at the sole of his foot like a ḥiriq, / and he is proud and high above me like a ḥolam ⚠19.
I stole into the garden of his face like a thief, / to gather the choice fruits of his mouth and eat them.
If the sun of his cheeks has risen upon me, / there is blood guilt on me, and I will pay with the wealth of my spirit [Exod 22:2] ⚠20.

And on this subject
I built him a building:

The eyes of a gazelle have bent a bow at me; / therefore my thoughts are afraid to come near.
My soul was caught like a bird in her net; / but her love was the net.
Her eye is like a bow; but she spread / a cloud, and on it the bow was seen [Gen 9:14] ⚠21.

More on love:

A lover went about by the bank of the Nile, / and the pain of his love was the wounds of a weapon.
He saw a maiden washing / her beloved's garment at the spring.
And she kissed it, not for its own honour, / but for the honour of the dark eyes [Gen 49:12].
So there is a drunkard who drinks / the vinegar for the honour of the wine.

And on the matter of the tears
that come down from the fire of the ribs,
I said:

I hide the hidden love in my heart, but / my tears are like an ensign, revealed at the head of the standard.
My heart dwells by the river Amanah, / but my tears are at En-rogel ⚠22.

And I spoke on this matter,
making the streams of verse flow from the spring:

Alas, the secret of my love is revealed, after / it was pressed down in the prison of my heart.
My tear went as a spy to my eyes, and / told his two brothers outside [Gen 9:22] ⚠23.

And I made one on a Greek woman who knew how to read the Torah,
and who had known every man by lying with a male [Num 31:17]:

They asked me about a wise woman; / I answered them: she learned wisdom to her shame.
Her scheming for her lewdness is a proof, / and a faithful sign is her knowledge for her knowing ⚠24.

And I made more on her doings,
to lay bare her harlotries;
and her name was Pure ⚠25:

A woman wise to do evil [Jer 4:22], cunning: / she clears the stones from the path of sin, and makes the straight crooked.
Her parents called her name Pure, / and so she is: pure indeed, with the purity of Ezekiel ⚠26.

And I composed a book of lots
for a man among the notables of the communities,
and wrote these poems:

Prince of honour, you are our chief, / and a prince of God are you among us [Gen 23:6].
Here for you is a book whose words / say: "Come, let us bow down to our king."
They call to you: "Rise, rule over us [Judg 8:22], / for your mind will light up our darkness."
Words of truth you will find in our mouth, on the day / you cast your lot among us [Prov 1:14].

And I made one for a miserly Nasi whom I saw worrying over the withheld cloud
in the year of drought [Jer 17:8]:

Do not tremble for the clouds, prince of honour, / if they do not pour streams upon you;
for they learned from you to shut the hand, since their name / is your name, and their deeds are your deeds ⚠27.

And I made one on the sage Rabbi Joseph the Maghrebi,
when I traveled to Zobah from the land of the gazelle [Dan 11:16] ⚠28:

Through you God dried up a sea of tears; / through you He said to their deep: "Be dry [Isa 44:27]."
In your heart He built a lofty house for His dwelling [1 Kgs 8:13]; / the holy tongue, and an Arab pitched his tent there [Isa 13:20] ⚠29.
You were a master and a great man in the West, / and in the East God anointed you as a prophet.
And truly Aram Zobah is like a sanctuary, / and you in it are like the western lamp ⚠30.

And I made one on the congregation
that despised praise:

Shaddai, send wanderers [Jer 48:12] to strip all the / goodness of the people of Calneh, and make an end of her.
Give her wealth and all her substance to strangers, / and lay bare the beauty of her hidden parts, and uncover her.
If she is like a wide ship, cut her / short, and finish her at a cubit [Gen 6:16] ⚠31.

And I made one on the sons of Adina ⚠32,
who in days of old were the glory of every province,
and now are left as a byword and a taunt [Deut 28:37]:

The men of Adina are despised and low; / the choicest of their crowd is dung and refuse.
I will not keep silent until I set their heart / as a quiver for the arrows of my verse.
Even if yesterday they were nobles, / today their honour is lost and swept away.
Today in the pit, and yesterday in the skies; / today on the dunghill, and yesterday under the canopy ⚠33.
They are not for help nor for profit, / but for shame and also for reproach [Isa 30:5].

And I made one for a man more miserly than all who were before him,
who counted himself generous in his own eyes:

I saw a miserly man, and among / the generous he is, in his own eyes, the head of them all.
But he will not go up the steps of generosity / until a donkey can go up a ladder.

And I made one on men whom I had praised in poems,
and they did not understand their way,
nor know their worth:

A gift of verse I sent to men / lacking understanding and sense, and integrity;
and they took my praise for disgrace, / and count the evil upright and perfect.
All of them rejoice in baseness, / and indeed they despise pleasant words.
Their likeness is the dung-worm, / which in myrrh is lost and withers away:
put it in excrement and it lives; / anoint it with choice oil and it dies.

And I made one for a generous man who sent me a gift,
and on the second day doubled my portion:

Generous of heart, who has made the sons of men rich, / so that in his days no poor or needy man is found:
his two hands are two rival wives, jealous of each other; / this one does good, and the other weighs out gifts.
When his right hand bestows its bounty, / the other is jealous to do the same.

And I made one for a man among the generous of Spain, like whom in generosity none has been,
whose name was Rabbi Ḥiyya:

Bring greetings to the master. He joined honour with integrity, / and his fame proclaims: the generous one of the generation, Ḥiyya.
He thirsts for the waters of wisdom, and longs to do good; / he has raised the heaps of honour from the dust of lowliness [Neh 3:34] ⚠34.
If a man should want his very breath from his bounty, / he would give life to the other, and not keep his own soul alive [Ps 22:30].

And I made one on love,
on a fawn whose eyes are weapons:

The gazelle of grace who dwells in my heart, / the burning of my ribs and innards has burned him.
And were it not that he has slain me with his eyes, / and multiplied my wounds without cause [Job 9:17],
I would have set him as the apple of my eye, and planted him / like a tree planted by the streams of my tears [Ps 1:3].
But for his wickedness I set him in my heart, / until he dwells in the parched places [Jer 17:6] between my ribs.

And I made one on a man who had a maidservant,
and he and his father abused her,
this one going and that one coming:

Be appalled [Jer 2:12], men of justice and those who sit in her gate, / for lewdness has grown strong and opened wide her mouth [Isa 5:14].
How do the foundations of heaven [2 Sam 22:8] not tremble at this, / that a man and his father go to the same girl [Amos 2:7]?

And I made one on a man on whose head I saw a huge turban,
about two hundred cubits:

A turban bound on a fool's head / I reckoned a vessel full of disgrace.
It has grown very great, and it stands over emptiness, / like the world hung upon nothing [Job 26:7].

And I made one on a man crushed of testicle [Lev 21:20],
who married a beautiful woman whose radiance would light up the dark:

Embrace, my friend, the breasts of a pleasant bride, / and no good delight will be lacking to you.
Go up with her on the bed of love, / until you are like a watered garden [Isa 58:11], and moist.
See: your egg is dragged along with her, / and it is an egg laid on a festival day [m. Beitzah 1:1] ⚠35.

And I made one on the grey hair
that was sprinkled on me in the days of youth [Hos 7:9],
and broke out in my heart like a plague:

I approve the custom of the men of old, / who wear white on the day of their mourning.
So my hairs have put on white / for the passing of the days of youth, and the departing of their shade [Num 14:9].

And I made more on this matter,
and tried to go in its way:

In a moment the dark ravens flew away, which / of old kept watch at the gate of my hair.
They came and nested within the walls of my heart / from the day they fled from my hair ⚠36.

And I made one in the days of youth
on the matter of the abundance of hair:

Men quarrel with me over my abundant hair; / truly their folly is a boil breaking out [Exod 9:9].
For if Elisha had had as much then, / they would not have called after him: "Go up, baldhead [2 Kgs 2:23]!"

And in Spain I translated into the holy tongue the book Guide of the Perplexed
for one of the princes,
who longed to see it,
and whose name was Joseph;
and I sent it to him with these poems:

Peace to you! He sets in order the battle of wisdom, / for in it his thoughts contend day and night,
until those who go out to war were jealous of him, / and the archers hated him [Gen 49:23] ⚠37.
If you love to rejoice in the garden of wisdom, / here is a bed full of blossoms.
Or if you would bring your heart near to God as an offering, / here for you are the fire and the wood [Gen 22:7].

And I made one in the city of Calneh for a man who had taken scorn as his portion,
whose name was Hezekiah:

They said to the head of the people, Hezekiah: / "The One who is great in deeds [Jer 32:19] created you unique.
Base deeds had almost perished, but / your Maker sent you to them to preserve life [Gen 45:5]."
You are not Hezekiah. Far from it! / You are Manasseh son of Hezekiah [2 Kgs 21:1] ⚠38.

And I made one in the lands of the West for an official,
to teach him the way of discipline:

You who seek to acquire discipline, take every day a mirror / of cast metal, to look at the sight of your image.
For if it is handsome, take care not / to diminish your integrity by a lack in your deeds;
or if there is a blemish in it, take care not / to add the blemish of your sin to your blemishes.
If you incline to my words, you will overpower your enemies [Exod 17:13], / and in the greatness of your majesty overthrow those who rise against you [Exod 15:7].

And I made one in France for a man more of a flatterer than all his people,
whose name was Isaac ha-Levi:

Faith: there is none but her; / in the height of the sky is her dwelling.
Return, my soul, with her, / and join yourself to her ⚠39.
Honour and splendour are in her, / and the sign of her goodness
is that no share in her / has Isaac ha-Levi.

And I made one in Toledo on the Nasi Rabbi Joseph ben Shoshan, when he departed to his eternal home:

The year in which the master Jehoseph / was gathered in, so that the lost sheep [Jer 50:6] lament after him,
was a year of calamity, a year of wrath, a time / of grief; and God called its name the year of Lament ⚠40.

And a month or two before his death
we heard that there was a breaking in Egypt [Gen 42:1],
and that Moses had gone up to heaven ⚠41.
And I spoke up and said:

Breach upon breach is proclaimed [Jer 4:20]; / the hearts of those who hear it are torn as by a lion.
For after the death of Moses there died / the prince Joseph, and was gathered in;
to dwell in his partition he came, and / Moses took the bones of Joseph [Exod 13:19].

And I made one on the affairs of the world,
which fade like a fading flower:

He who sees in his heart / evil Time and its path
will go into the cleft of the rock [Exod 33:22], / and hide in a cave.
The world is like a market, / and every man in it competes.
And what in it should I choose, / when it is a sore evil [Eccl 5:12]?
The booth of its goodness / I declare unfit,
because its sun / is more than its shade [m. Sukkah 1:1].

And I made one on one of the base,
proud of his wealth up to the stars of Orion ⚠42:

When you see a fool who has / gone up and set his shrine in the heights,
suddenly he will be broken and bowed down; / for after the rise, the fall ⚠43.

And I made a poem of discipline and sound counsel
on the matter of earning a living:

Do not be lazy in seeking your / living, and make your nest a lodging place for wayfarers [Jer 9:1].
For you will not find rest in / ease, nor in spacious upper rooms [Jer 22:14].
Look at the holy tongue and see: / two resting letters do not come together in it ⚠44.

Once on a Sabbath night I woke because of fleas,
biting like serpents,
and I spoke up and said:

My heart is set against the flea that profaned my bed [Gen 49:4], / to drink its fill of my blood with all its delight.

Mighty One, reveal the strength of Your hand / today; how can You hide Yourself?

The court of Your holy house I have risen to visit, / to behold Your pleasantness evening and morning [Ps 27:4].

How long shall I be a wanderer and a fugitive like Cain [Gen 4:12], / struck down by grief, and not by strong drink and wine?

May God cut off the proud, / despised, yet proud up to the stars of the heavenly dwelling.

Alas, my eye has seen men who are like / Nabal the Carmelite, and like the brother of Cain ⚠58.

Your friendship I thought like manna / hidden and laid up in a precious jar [Exod 16:33].

And I made one on a man who bought many books,

See a simpleton grown old in his folly, / while his bad deeds are new every morning [Lam 3:23].
He buys many books of testimony, / and with them he is like a donkey carrying books.

And I made one for a man whom I praised in my verse,
and who recognized the eloquence of my words:

O prince, put on the fine robes of my eloquence, / since the balm of my envoys binds up every pang ⚠59.
He who scoops honey from my words will know it. Truly, / he has scooped honey from the body of the lion [Judg 14:9].

And I made one in Damascus on Rabbi Zedakah the judge,
for a cause and a matter:

A man planted in his vineyard a fruit tree of the garden; / he tended it day and night, and watered it.
And when it began to give its fruit in its season [Ps 1:3], / and to every palate its taste was sweet,
he uprooted it, and his labour was spent for nothing; / and such is the deed of Rabbi Zedakah.
Yesterday he built a shrine of discipline, and today / he has torn it down, and it has become a deep pit [Prov 23:27].
I hope for justice, and behold, bloodshed and despair, / and for righteousness, and behold, a cry [Isa 5:7] ⚠60.
He did not know that the Rock has set within my mouth / a sword polished against all my enemies.
When I draw it, the giants / I bring down in wrath to the leech ⚠61.

Who has come near to war with me and escaped, / and who has put forth his hand against my splendour and been clean [1 Sam 26:9]?

My friend, I have put the vowel points in my verse for you, / since your mind is feeble and faint,

Lacking sense, and light of spirit in his mind, / yet he is heavy like Tabor among the mountains [Jer 46:18].

A man of the sons of Adina made a poem full of lewdness and uncleanness:

Had the son of Amram seen the face of my beloved / flushed red at the time of drinking strong drink,
and the beauty of his locks and the splendour of his beauty, / he would not have set in his Torah the law "and with a male [Lev 18:22]" ⚠67.

When I heard his poem, I gathered ten men,
men of sense and understanding,
of uprightness and faithfulness,
and we put him under the ban in poems like his poems,
on the line of his rhymes and his words.
I said first:

Shaddai, draw the sword of vengeance against / the unclean one who has troubled Your Torah.
The mouth that opened to revile God, / let it quickly be stopped by swords.
He who sells God's holiness for uncleanness, / let him quickly be sold into the hand of a killer.

And the second answered:

Pray, Lord of the world, pour out Your wrath / upon a man drunk on the wine of lewdness and the strong drink of sin.
Rain down on him a rain of brimstone and fire [Ps 11:6], / until he is troubled by the hand of death.
Send his spirit as an offering to the fire of Topheth, / and his body as a gift [Ps 72:10] to the mouth of Sheol.

And the third answered:

Shaddai, sell quickly into the hand of death / the man who sold Your law in sin.
Your Torah cries out in his mouth always: / "Rescue me from the hand of strangers [Ps 144:7]."
On the day the remembrance of the sons of the world rises up / for good, let his name be remembered for evil.

And the fourth answered:

Woe for men who forsake the Torah of / God, and serve a strange god.
For all their great ones defile themselves: / in place of the female they lie with the male.
As they get drunk on the wine of sin, let the sword / of enemies be drunk with their blood day and night.

And the fifth answered:

Awake, son of Amram, see your law, / for the drinkers of strong drink have profaned it.
They practise wanton deeds always, / and give a harlot's fee to every male [Ezek 16:33].
May the Rock uproot their root; and with the tidings / of their death, let a lamb be sent to every land [Isa 16:1] ⚠68.

And the sixth answered:

The man who profaned the Torah of the son of Amram / in the baseness of his mouth, when he made mention of the male,
who opens himself wide to every passer-by, / and lies down on all fours for every male:
may a killer split his flesh, as the / ground is split by the plowman's plow.

And the seventh answered:

The man who profaned the law of his Maker, / and forgot the day of judgment, and did not remember,
and spoke insolently before his Rock, / and opened his mouth wide, and did not shut it:
may God make his face blacker than soot, / until those who see him do not know him [Lam 4:8].

And the eighth answered:

The man who hardened his face against God, / so far that he made mention of the son of Amram for evil,
thought he was troubling the Torah of our God, / in vain; but in truth he troubled his own soul.
May God uproot all the sons of his house, / until He destroys male together with female.

And the ninth answered:

Look, son of Amram, at him who profaned / your law, a father of uncleanness ⚠69 and a son of a stranger.
He lays himself bare in his hiding places, but within / the congregation he is hidden and disguises himself.
May God quickly destroy him, and may the / fierce anger of God be the wage of his deed.

And the tenth answered:

Woe for a congregation whose congregation's prince / has acted presumptuously to revile God, and sold himself [1 Kgs 21:20].
He went astray in the wine of lewdness, and not wine; / he is drunk on the drinking of sin, and not on strong drink [Isa 29:9].

If you spend your days in the service of God, / you will be a glory on the head of the sons of your people.
Make your portion a crust with a vessel of water, and / your Maker will bless your bread and your water [Exod 23:25].
Set up your altar in your heart; but your fat / offer up, and pour out your blood as a libation in weeping.

If you do so, you will overpower your enemy [Exod 17:13], / and in the greatness of your majesty overthrow those who rise against you [Exod 15:7].

And I made a thanksgiving for the kindnesses of the Creator;
and who is a teacher like Him [Job 36:22]?

Lord of the world, You have dealt me kindnesses, / and for Your kindness I have repaid You rebellions.
My thoughts are trained at all times in Your mercies, / but in Your law they are not learned.
Your kindnesses were not far from me while I / stayed at home, and when I wandered far [Ps 55:8].

And for all this my thoughts kneel to You, / and they bow down before You and give thanks.
Remember Your righteousness, Lord of lords [Deut 10:17], / and do not remember the iniquity of a servant of servants [Gen 9:25].

The prince who encamps among the stars on high, / and answers the one who asks him before he calls [Isa 65:24];
humble of heart, even when joined with a people who lord it over others; / generous, even when he encamps among those who shut their hand.
I wonder at his dwelling among them; yet I do not wonder, for / so the Presence dwelt in the bush [Deut 33:16].

And I made more for him,
in praise of his rank and greatness:

My heart I sold to Isaac, the chosen of God, / for the silver of friendship and the price of love.
He is the inner shrine of honour, and his heart is the ark of the law, / and his hands, when they give, are the two cherubim.
The tablets of his heart are like the tablets of the covenant, / but they are written with the finger of God [Exod 31:18].
He is high in the eyes of all, and low in his own eyes; / he is one, yet many, and the law follows the many [b. Berakhot 9a] ⚠80.

And I made one on the matter of contentment:

Is it not enough for me, my portion from my Time? / And shall I exchange my pride for disgrace [Hos 4:7]?
So that the sons of men will not say of me: / "See the kindness of So-and-so to So-and-so."
When I am not in need of his wealth, / I reckon him as he reckons me.
I love him if he seeks my love, / and break the covenant if he deceives me.

And I made one on the matter of companionship:

The days of companionship are like honeycomb, sweet to my palate, / and the nights of wandering are evil and bitter.
Joys have fled from me, and are like / souls that refused to dwell in corpses.
My griefs in the night of parting are long, / and my joys in the night of companionship are short.

More on this matter:

A fawn in whose mouth are two rows of a necklace of jacinth [Exod 28:19], / who laughs at my weeping like the bow on the day of rain [Ezek 1:28].
His body is like snow, and he has a substance of frost; / with it he kills me, and does not know, though he is guilty ⚠81.

More on this matter:

Until my death you afflict me, gazelle of grace, / and my soul is sold to you like a slave woman [Exod 21:7].
Would that I might find an end to your anger, / and the plague of your wandering were stayed [Num 17:13].
The fire of your love, beloved, you send into me, / and kindle my heart within a pyre.
It is right for you to make good my heart, / and such is the law for the one who kindles a fire [Exod 22:5].

And these I made for one of the noble princes,
who was a chief of the base:

I praised a base man in Ashur / in a lying poem that had nothing right in it.
And he vowed me vows that were like the poems: / full of vanity, with no faithfulness in them.
And never has my eye seen a lying tongue [Prov 6:17] / like his, from Spain to Adina.
When his friends asked him and said: / "Do you shut your hand, which is trained in giving?"
he answered them: "I am short of hand today, / and my soul is tried by want of everything [Deut 28:48].
I have need of expenses every day, / which are laid upon me by bond and debt.
And I have a lad who serves me as I please, / and our couch is always green [Song 1:16];
and I owe it to him to fill his needs, / and to give him his food, his clothing and his conjugal rights [Exod 21:10]."

After these things we went out of Egypt,
to see the good of Jerusalem [Ps 128:5],
and we passed by way of the wilderness,
a land of drought and distress,
and in the parching of summer [Ps 32:4] my bones burned [Job 30:30].
And I called the name of that place Taberah [Num 11:3],
for there the sons of days ⚠82 offer strange fire [Lev 10:1],
and their souls are burned in their flames,
and the jackals and the ostriches weep for the burning [Lev 10:6].
And we went through the great and terrible wilderness [Deut 1:19],
a place of pining of soul and failing of eyes [Deut 28:65],
of serpent and fiery serpent and scorpion, and thirst where there is no water [Deut 8:15];
but we made it, with the sweat of our brows
and the tears of our eyelids,
a place of broad rivers and streams [Isa 33:21].

And when we came to Gaza,
after hard and fierce toil,
we set out praise to God with timbrel and dance [Ps 150:4],
because we had made the crossing of the sand,
and gone out from the holy to the profane ⚠83.
And we did not rest from running like a rushing torrent
or snatching lightning,
and the palm of darkness hid us,
and showed us the paths in the black of night [Prov 7:9],
until the gates of heaven were opened to us [Gen 28:17]:
that is, the city of the Lord, Jerusalem.
And we bowed and prostrated ourselves to God Most High, who had visited us with a visitation of mercy,
and we encamped there a month of days.
And in all those days
the lines fell for me in pleasant places [Ps 16:6]:
days whose faces seemed hewn from pearls,
and cut from trees of life,
and stolen from the stars of the sky.
Every day we would walk over her graves and her landmarks,
and weep over Zion, who mourns for her children,
until the streams of tears dug pits in her face;
and lament over the ruins of her palaces,
and what remains of her buildings;
and go round the graves of the righteous that are about her,
and mourn over the mother and over her daughters.
And at the end of the days the hand of longing drew us with cords [Hos 11:4]
to go up the Mount of Olives,
and set out prayers
to Him who does wonders,
and bow down to the King, the Lord of hosts [Zech 14:16].
And we were with weeping eye
and humbled soul,
looking down on the place of the Temple court,
which has been turned over to strange worship.
And we hid our faces from looking at the abode of the great glory, where her tent was at first,
where of old the Presence dwelt,
and now a cloud dwells over it.
Nothing was left of the buildings but signs that pain the hearts of those who remember them,
and make the eyes of those who see them pine with weeping.
And when these days were fulfilled, we set our faces
to the wilderness of Damascus [1 Kgs 19:15].
And we called the name of that time Esek,
because they contended with him [Gen 26:20] ⚠84.
And when we reached it,
we saw a city fair and beautiful,
built for an armoury [Song 4:4];
her ways are ways of pleasantness [Prov 3:17],
and she is all beautiful, and without discretion [Prov 11:22] ⚠85;
fruitful as a fruitful vine,
with the lips of her lilies
and the eyes of her springs,
laughing and weeping.
All her pastures are beds of myrtles,
and all her woods are gardens and orchards,
as the waters cover the sea [Isa 11:9].
All her ways are streams stretched out [Num 24:6],
and on the cheeks of her mountains fire and snow are at ease together,
and from the honeycomb of her lips the thirsty drink their fill.
And on every highway
there are channels,
and along the roads
there are courses for the water,
and on the paths
the sight of the women who draw water.
Her scent is like the scent of Lebanon [Song 4:11],
and round about her are Senir and Hermon [Song 4:8],
and in summer it snows on her as on Zalmon [Ps 68:15].
And within the city, of the sons of the exile,
there is a great congregation,
and among them the great physician Rabbi Moses bar Zedakah, may his splendour be exalted.
He alone
among the people of his city honoured me,
and with the words of his mouth encouraged me,
and endowed me with a good dowry [Gen 30:20].
There is no man among them who wraps himself in the cloak of the praised as in his own garment;
they were afraid to come near to generosity, and Moses alone came near [Exod 24:2] ⚠86.
And my soul almost perished in its affliction,
and I found no balm for its sickness,
had I not found there a man of the sons of Adina: he is Ezra, who went up from Babylon [Ezra 7:6].
I found him in the sea of wandering, a great captain [Jonah 1:6],
and he made me forget, in the sweetness of his precious company,
the wormwood of parting.
And when our soul was weary of life [Gen 27:46], we sighed,
and parted from the prince, and left his house ⚠87;
and we are journeying to the abode of treasures,
and the place of honoured virtues,
that is, Aram Zobah, where the wheel of greatness turns,
and the centre of the circle.
There is the great prince, the wonder of the ages,
and the proof for the later ones
over the earlier ones,
the leader of the flock,
and the head of the order:
Joseph, he is the ruler over the land,
he is the one who sold grain to all the people of the land [Gen 42:6].
And I took up my parable:

Bring greetings to the master who gathers the scattered of understanding; / his praise is more precious than fine gold and silver.
Time longs to tell some of his treasures, / and has sought a spokesman to lay bare his secret.

And he sent Judah before him to Joseph [Gen 46:28] ⚠88.
He is the sun of praises that rose in the East from the West,
and there he became a great nation, mighty and numerous [Deut 26:5].
He built storehouses of treasures,
and filled them with the grain of testimonies,
and Joseph heaped up grain [Gen 41:49],
and came to set out a table in the wilderness [Ps 78:19],
to know how to sustain the weary with a word [Isa 50:4].
He gave drink to the thirsty,
and healed the downcast,
and turned the heart of the fathers to the sons [Mal 3:24],
and the Lord wiped away tears from all faces [Isa 25:8].
He renewed the youth of Time after it had worn out,
and it washed in pure water,
and took off its garments and put on other garments [Lev 6:4],
and on the day of its cleansing took two turtledoves [Lev 14:22]:
the turtledove of the Torah
and the turtledove of authority,
by which men are chosen;
and with both of them its cheeks are comely with circlets [Song 1:10],
its neck with strings of verse ⚠89.
And I took up my parable:

The prince: in his mouth is a tongue of gold, and the / mantle of his praise is very glorious.
He has plundered the treasures of understanding and stolen the heart of men / of sense, yet he was not found breaking in [Exod 22:1].
And when the judges searched within his tent, / they found the tongue of gold and the mantle [Josh 7:21] ⚠90.

Happy is the man who begot him,
and the bundle of myrrh [Song 1:13] that hid him.
He prevailed over the men of his generation;
as the son, so the father,
and as the root, so its topmost bough.
There is none to whom I could liken one of them, or compare him;
for of the virtues of the men of this generation, Joseph is not, and Simeon is not [Gen 42:36].
Have you seen him whom the Lord has chosen [1 Sam 10:24]?
This is Sinai before the Lord [Judg 5:5].
A precious stone whose worth no man knows [Job 28:13];
God understands its way [Job 28:23].
And I took up my parable:

There was in the land of the West a man in the midst / of a gathering, and none revealed the secret of his worth,
until the hands of honour hurried to seek him out, / and they drew and lifted up Joseph [Gen 37:28] ⚠91.

He comprises the treasured virtues;
and in the secret of the testimonies
he has ten parts [2 Sam 19:44];
and in the secret of medicines
he reveals wonders;
and in the science of measures
he has built himself a house of measures [Jer 22:14] ⚠92;
and in the questions of number
he answers to the point;
and in the paths of the sphere
he levels every course;
and all the mysteries of the Torah
he brings out to light [Job 28:11];
and the secret of grammar
is engraved on his heart;
and in the Gemara he opens
every closed door;
and in the secret of the Mishnah,
from his mouth come knowledge and understanding [Prov 2:6].
His wisdom is a king's daughter, all glorious [Ps 45:14];
and when she comes before the king,
there are shouts of "Grace, grace" to her [Zech 4:7].
And he hastened to give her her ointments and her portions [Esth 2:9],
and his allowance was a continual allowance given her [2 Kgs 25:30].
And the lampstand of his mind he set up in the tent of meeting,
to light a lamp continually [Exod 27:20].
