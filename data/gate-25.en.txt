Thus says Heman the Ezrahite: I was journeying from Ashkelon
to the valley of Aijalon [Josh 10:12].
And when we came to the lodging place,
we sat down under the oak.
Each man of the company began to tell what his eyes had seen
and his ears had heard.
And there by chance was an old man of understanding,
whose looks went about as a talebearer [Prov 11:13], telling the wisdom of his thoughts ⚠1.
He said to us: "Let me tell you what my eyes saw in this wilderness.
For many years ago I passed by this place,
and I lifted up my eyes and looked, and behold, a mighty man,
breaking the pride of the mighty [Lev 26:19];
at his rebuke the mountains melted,
at his roar the heart of young lions was dismayed.
Under him was a galloping horse,
swift as lightning,
and shining as the sun.
At the sound of his neighing
and the stamping of his hooves [Jer 47:3],
hearts trembled
and thoughts were confounded.
And when his bonds were loosed [Judg 15:14],
and he set his steps to run,
he would cleave the sea and its breakers,
and beat flat the plain and its wildernesses,
and leave behind him all that was before him.
He made of the winds his feet,
of the lightnings his wings,
and of the stars his eyes;
and the heat of his heart all but melted his body, and it became as water [Josh 7:5].
The mighty man rode on him, strong and of good courage [Josh 1:6],
and ran to and fro like a flash of lightning [Ezek 1:14].
On his hand was a hawk, glittering,
darting like the wind,
and going forth in its ranks [Prov 30:27] ⚠2.
Its pinions were embroidered with settings of gold [Ps 45:14],
and its soul was hewn from flaming pyres.
Every prey it saw
it would take with its eyes [Job 40:24].
And in its flight after the fawns
and the beasts of the forests,
when the bird of prey came down upon the carcasses [Gen 15:11],
it bound them with the cords of its terror.

And they all bowed down at the thunder of its might [Job 26:14];
the eagles recounted its strength, how it prevailed,
and that which has wings told the matter [Eccl 10:20].
And about the mighty man were hounds,
whose faces were faces of flame [Isa 13:8],
long and not broad,
thin and not thick,
as though hewn from a quarry of fire.
They overtook every fugitive, though he came into the clouds,
and brought him down, though he climbed to the top of the stars.
Not one that flees from them escaped [Amos 9:1],
until they made him their tributary
and a thing for their feet to trample;
and the heart of the lion melted before them [2 Sam 17:10].
And I lifted up my eyes and saw the mighty man on his horse, making it race in the heat of his strength,
with his hawk in his hand
and his arrow in his quiver.
With his horse's hooves he crumbled the hard rock,
and wore away the flint and ground it until it was fine as dust [Deut 9:21].
He roared with a voice of thunder and shouting,
leaping upon the mountains, skipping upon the hills [Song 2:8],
pursuing and calling out,
as one hunts the partridge [1 Sam 26:20] ⚠3,
to rouse the gazelles
from their hiding places,
to drive the beasts out of their holes,
and the conies from the clefts of their rocks [Isa 2:21],
and to bring the wild goats down from their mountains.
Now in the rock of the mountain was a great cave,
and a hollow, and within it a pathway,
a road cast up.
And inside it many kinds of gazelles were hiding,
and a herd of fawns concealed,
and a multitude of young harts, small with great [Ps 115:13],
and mothers with their little ones,
lying in their mothers' bosom,
sucking the breasts with their tongues.
And the mother crouched over them,
and took her delight with them.
Now they slept in her bosom,
and grew fat in good pasture [Ezek 34:14];
now they danced before her in their joy,
as their soul was poured out into their mothers' bosom [Lam 2:12].
And she danced with them,
going up and coming down,
and did not know that the day of her calamity was near [Deut 32:35].

And her sun had turned toward evening.
And while she was still with them, frisking ⚠4 and nestling,
hugging and kissing her young,
behold, the mighty man hastened on his mount,
and came in by the entrance ⚠5,
and ran swiftly
as a storm wind
to the mouth of the cave.
He urged on his horse and made it race,
and set his arrow to his bow [Lam 2:4],
and made the cave the aim of his desire.
He made ready his weapons to fight,
and called the hounds to him.
And the hawk was roused against them in its fury,
and in the greatness of its desire it all but
cut its cords toward the gazelles
and broke its fetters to run at them.
And when the mighty man stood at the mouth of the cave,
and roared with the voice of rebuke,
and set the land burning like a pyre,
the fawns fled from their dens,
and the young goats from their mothers' bosom,
and the young of the gazelles hid
at his roar, and it was not known where they were [Nah 3:17];
for he roars, and the children come trembling from the west [Hos 11:10] ⚠6.

And the mighty man came upon them,
and sent the hawk into the sky to bring death down upon them.
He passed before them,
and the hounds surrounded them on every side,
while he flashed to and fro with fire of steel and the glitter of the spear [Nah 2:4] before their eyes.
And the fawns were terrified and shuddered,
and quaked and writhed.
And remembering the tenderness of those who bore them,
they thought to return to them,
and found the foe that oppressed them [Num 10:9]
parting them from one another.
The mothers too:
terrors fell upon them;
some of them hid in the bowels of the earth,
and some climbed up to high hills.
And when they remembered their young,
their soul fainted within them [Ps 107:5].
And how my compassion was stirred [Gen 43:30] for the fawns
scattered upon the mountain tops!
And the mighty man did not cease leaping over the bare heights,
and pursuing after the gazelles,
and roaring over them like lions ⚠7.
And whenever they wished to flee from their pursuers,
the hawk would come from every side before their eyes,
until it turned them back.
And when their soul was weary of life [Job 10:1],
and their sun had set [Jer 15:9],
their heart melted at the voice of the roarers,
and their soul fainted before the slayers [Jer 4:31].
And the hounds closed in around them on every side:
this one seized their tail,
and that one raided at their heel [Gen 49:19],
this one bit,
and that one tore,
and that one snatched,
until death overtook some of them,
and they were caught in their pits [Lam 4:20].
And the hounds in their cruelty
tore all their flesh with their fangs,
and drew out their entrails,
and licked up their blood with their tongues [1 Kgs 21:19],
though there was no violence in their hands [Job 16:17] and no wrong in their palms.
Blessed be God, Lord of the world,
who guides all His creatures with justice,
and hides from the sons of men His secrets
and the causes of His works;
who gives some of His creatures as prey to others,
and makes the death of these a cause for the good of those and for their living;
who created the great to harm the small,
and sent the strong to afflict the poor and the troubled,
and made this one the food of that one;
and at their end, as this one dies, so dies that one [Eccl 3:19].
And all of them are made equal in Sheol and rot,
and together they lie down in the dust [Job 21:26]."
And he took up his parable:

The children of the world: their life is in their death, / and they are food, some of them for others.
This one preys on that one, mighty and weak; / and so the Rock created them from their beginning.
The weak is hunted and harmed, / and the thoughts of the strong are bent on doing evil.
The strong does harm, and in the end / is harmed himself, and his power comes to an end, until it is gone.
The eater and the eaten both perish, / and are made equal upon the dust in their death.
Blessed be God, whose wonders are exalted, / and none understands the hidden things of His works.

Said the teller: When I heard the elegance of his words
and the strength of his parables,
my heart and my thoughts were amazed,
and I said: "This is none other than Ḥever the Kenite."
And he thought what I thought,
and knew that I had known him,
and ran to meet me,
and was first to greet me.
We took our delight in each other all the days that we were joined,
until we were parted from the bosom of the company,
and wandered off, each to his own side.
