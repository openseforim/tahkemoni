שַׁעַר תְּשִׁיעִי

בִּשְׁלֹשִׁים מְשׁוֹרְרִים מִתְהַלְּלִים, מִי יַעֲשֶׂה שִׁירִים יוֹתֵר מְעֻלִּים

The Ninth Gate

Thirty poets set a disguised old man thirty biblical verses as poem endings; he caps each one, and he is Ḥever the Kenite

Gate 9 on Project Ben-Yehuda ↗

About this text

201 lines. One row is one rhyme unit of the rhymed prose, or one line of verse (its two halves set apart). References in [gray brackets] are the translator's identifications of biblical phrases. ⚠ marks an uncertain rendering. Each line number is a link to that line. Hebrew text: Project Ben-Yehuda.

1

Thus says Heman the Ezrahite: I turned from all my business to sit with the men of understanding in their houses of study,

נְאֻם הֵימָן הָאֶזְרָחִי: פָּנִיתִי מִכָּל מַעֲשַׂי לָשֶׁבֶת עִם הַמְּבִינִים בְּבָתֵּי יְשִׁיבָתָם.

2

and to hear the words of the wise and their riddles [Prov 1:6].

וְלִשְׁמֹע דִּבְרֵי חֲכָמִים וְחִידוֹתָם.

3

And it came to pass one day, when I was with companions,

וַיְהִי הַיּוֹם וַאֲנִי עִם חֲבֵרִים .

4

nobles and lords,

אֲצִילִים וּגְבִירִים.

5

drawing near to the battle-line of poetry,

לְמַעֲרֶכֶת הַשִּׁיר נִגָּשִׁים.

6

and their number was thirty.

וְהָיָה מִסְפָּרָם שְׁלֹשִׁים.

7

One of them answered and said: "Whoever goes up the ascents of the lofty songs⚠1,

וַיַּעַן אִישֹ מֵהֶם וַיֹּאמַר: מִי הָעוֹלֶה בְּמַעֲלוֹת הַשִּׁירִים הַגְּבוֹהִים.

8

and breaks out in brightness from his dawn⚠2,

וּבוֹקֵעַ מִשְּׁחָרָיו נְגוֹהִים.

9

let him reveal today before our eyes his rank,

יְגַלֶּה הַיּוֹם לְעֵינֵינוּ מַעֲלָתוֹ.

10

and bare the arm of his eloquence [Isa 52:10],

וְיֶחֱשׂף אֶת זְרוֹעַ מְלִיצָתוֹ.

11

and the precious splendour of his greatness [Esth 1:4]."

וְאֶת יְקָר תִּפְאֶרֶת גְדֻלָּתוֹ.

12

The people said to him: "By what shall the poet and his strength be tested,

אָמְרוּ לוֹ הָעָם: בַּמֶּה יִבָּחֵן הַמְשׁוֹרֵר וְכֹחוֹ.

13

and how can we know the man and his talk [2 Kgs 9:11]?"

וְאֵיך נוּכַל לָדַעַת אֶת הָאִישׁ וְאֶת שִׂיחוֹ.

14

He said to them: "Know that poems have ways,

אָמַר לָהֶם: דְּעוּ כִּי יֵשׁ לַשִּׁירִים דְּרָכִים.

15

and rhymes have paths and courses,

וְלַחֲרוּזִים נְתִיבוֹת וּמַהְלְכִים.

16

and they have crafts that are fixed⚠3,

וְלָהֶם מְלָאכוֹת נְסוּכוֹת.

17

ordered and kept [2 Sam 23:5].

שְׁמוּרוֹת וַעֲרוּכוֹת.

18

And the choicest of the crafts

וּמִבְחַר הַמְּלָאכוֹת.

19

is to take a verse from the books of the prophets,

לָקַחַת מִסִּפְרֵי הַנְּבִיאִים פָּסוּק .

20

and to build on it a theme lovely and desired."

וְלִבְנוֹת עָלָיו עִנְיָן נֶחְמָד וְחָשׁוּק.

21

Now among the guests there was an old man, terrible of appearance [Judg 13:6],

וְהָיָה בְתֹוךְ הַקְּרוּאִים אִישׁ זָקֵן מַרְאֵהוּ נוֹרָא.

22

and he sat alone, far from the company.

וְהוּא יוֹשֵׁב לְבַדּוֹ רָחוֹק מִן הַחֲבוּרָה.

23

But he split hearts with the hammer of his words [Jer 23:29],

אֲבָל הָיָה מְבַקֵּע הַלְּבָבוֹת בְּפַטִּישׁ מִלָּיו .

24

and put pearls to shame with the pearls of his parables.

וּמַחְפִּיר הַפְּנִינִים בִּפְנִינֵי מְשָׁלָיו .

25

His face was covered,

וְהָיוּ מְכֻסִּים פָּנָיו.

26

and I could see only his eyes.

וְלֹא יָכֹלְתִּי לִרְאוֹת כִּי אִם עֵינָיו.

27

He answered and said: "Hear me, men of heart [Job 34:10],

וַיַּעַן וַיּאמַר: שִׁמְעוּ אֵלַי אַנְשֵׁי לבָב.

28

and let not your heart go astray [Isa 57:17].

וְאַל יֵלֵךְ לְבַבְכֶם שׁוֹבָב.

29

If it is this way that you seek,

כִּי אִם הַדֶּרֶךְ הַזֶּה תְבַקֵּשׁוּ.

30

and to come into this gate that you ask,

וְלָבוֹא בַּשַּׁעַר הַזֶּה תִּדְרֹשׁוּ.

31

take from among you the repairer of the breach [2 Kgs 12:6],

קְחוּ מאִתְּכֶם מְחַזֵּק הַבֶּדֶק.

32

and the teacher of righteousness.

וּמוֹרֶה הַצֶּדֶק .

33

And now choose a man for yourselves, and let him come down to me [1 Sam 17:8],

וְעַתָּה בַּחֲרוּ לָכֶם אִישׁ וְיֵרֵד אֵלַי.

34

until you know my parables,

עַד תַּכִּירוּ מְשָׁלַי .

35

and test my words."

וְתִבְחֲנוּ מִלָּי.

36

A man of the company came near to him and said to him: "If you wish to kindle the fire of the intellect,

וַיִּגַּשׁ אֵלָיו אִישׁ מִן הַחֲבוּרָה וַיּאמֶר לוֹ: אִם רְצוֹנְךָ אֵשׁ הַשֵּׂכֶל לִקְדֹּחַ.

37

make a poem whose end shall be: 'But those who hope in the Lord shall renew their strength' [Isa 40:31]."

עֲשֵׂה שִׁיר יְהִי סוֹפוֹ: וְקֹוֵי ה' יַחֲלִיפוּ כֹחַ.

38

And he took up his parable and said:

וַיִּשָּׂא מְשָׁלוֹ וַיֹּאמַר:

39

Son of man, trust in your Rock on the day of your pain, for in Him the weary find rest.

בֶּן אִישׁ בְּטַח עַל צוּרְךָ יוֹם צִירְךָ כִּי בוֹ יְגֵעִים יִמְצְאוּ מָנוֹחַ

40

Those who trust in emptiness labour in vain [Isa 65:23], but those who hope in the Lord shall renew their strength [Isa 40:31].

הַבּוטְחִים עַל רִיק לְרִיק יָגְעוּ, אֲבָל קֹוֵי אֲדנָי יַחֲלִיפוּ כֹחַ.

41

The second of the company answered and said to him: "If you wish to wake the sleepers of your thoughts,

וַיַּעַן הַשֵּׁנִי מִן הַחֲבוּרָה וַיּאמֶר לוֹ: אִם רְצוֹנְךָ לְהָעִיר יְשֵׁנֵי זְמָמֶיךָ.

42

make a poem whose end shall be: 'And in the greatness of Your majesty You overthrow those who rise against You' [Exod 15:7]."

עֲשֵׂה שִׁיר יְהִי סוֹפוֹ: וּבְרֹב גְּאוֹנְךָ תַהֲרֹס קָמֶיךָ.

43

And he took up his parable and said:

וַיִּשָּׂא מְשָׁלוֹ וַיֹּאמַר:

44

If you spend your days in the service of God, you shall be a glory on the head of the sons of your people.

אִם בַּעֲבוֹדַת אֵל תְּכַל יָמֶיךָ תִּהְיֶה פְאֵר עַל רֹאשׁ בְּנֵי עַמֶּךָ.

45

For then your haters will cringe before you [Deut 33:29], and in the greatness of your majesty you will overthrow those who rise against you [Exod 15:7].

כִּי אָז מְשַׂנְאֶיךָ לְךָ יִכָּחְשׁוּ וּבְרֹב גְּאוֹנְךָ תַּהֲרֹס קָמֶיךָ.

46

The third answered and said to him: "If you desire to cleanse your poem of every taint and flaw,

וַיַּעַן הַשְּׁלִישִׁי וַיּאמֶר לוֹ: אִם תַּחְפֹּץ לְנַקּוֹת שִׁירְךָ מִכָּל שֶׁמֶץ וָחֶרֶץ.

47

make a poem whose end shall be: 'And not for faithfulness have they grown strong in the land' [Jer 9:2]."

עֲשֵׂה שִׁיר יְהִי סוֹפוֹ: וְלֹא לָאֱמוּנָה גָבְרוּ בָאָרֶץ.

48

And he took up his parable and said:

וַיִּשָּׂא מְשָׁלוֹ וַיֹּאמַר:

49

I look to the right hand and turn to the left [Ps 142:5]; I see none upright, none to fence up the breach [Ezek 22:30],

אַבִּיט לְצַד יָמִין וְאֶפְנֶה עַל שְׂמֹאל לֹא אֶחֱזֶה יָשָׁר וְגוֹדֵר פָּרֶץ.

50

but only men of wickedness and men of falsehood, who not for faithfulness have grown strong in the land [Jer 9:2].

כִּי אִם מְתֵי רֶשַׁע וְאַנְשֵׁי שָׁוְא אֲשֶׁר לֹא לָאֱמוּנָה גָבְרוּ בָאָרֶץ.

51

The fourth answered and said to him: "If you would purify your poem of sin and iniquity,

וַיַּעַן הָרְבִיעִי וַיּאמֶר לוֹ: אִם תִּרְצֶה לְבָרֵר שִׁירְךָ מחֵטְא וְאָוֶן.

52

make a poem whose end shall be: 'the putting forth of the finger, and speaking wickedness' [Isa 58:9]."

עֲשֵׂה שִׁיר יְהִי סוֹפוֹ: שְׁלַח אֶצְבַּע וְדַבֵּר אָוֶן.

53

And he took up his parable and said:

וַיִּשָּׂא מְשָׁלוֹ וַיֹּאמַר:

54

Return, O man, to God before your death, for your foot has sunk in the mire of the deep [Ps 69:3].

שׁוּבָה אֱנוֹשׂ אֶל אֵל בְּטֶרֶם מוֹתְךָ כִּי טָבְעָה רַגְלָךְ בְּטִיט הַיָּוֶן.

55

You will prosper when you take away from your midst the yoke, the putting forth of the finger, and speaking wickedness [Isa 58:9].

תִּצְלַח בְעֵת תָּסִיר לְךָ מִתּוֹכְךָ מוֹטָה שְׂלַח אֶצְבַּע וְדַבֵּר אָוֶן.

56

The fifth answered and said to him: "If you long to show us the wisdom you have gained,

וַיַּעַן הַחֲמִישִׁי וַיּאמֶר לוֹ: אִם תְּאַוֶּה לְהַרְאוֹת לָנוּ חָכְמָתְךָ אֲשֶׁר חָכָמְתָּ .

57

make a poem whose end shall be: 'What is this dream that you have dreamed?' [Gen 37:10]."

עֲשֵׂה שִׁיר יְהִי סוֹפוֹ: מַה הַחֲלוֹם הַזֶּה אֲשֶׂר חָלָמְתָּ.

58

And he took up his parable and said:

וַיִּשָּׂא מְשָׁלוֹ וַיֹּאמַר:

59

Son of man, how long will you shut your eye? Alas, you have fallen asleep in the bosom of lust.

בֶּן אִישׁ לְמָתַי עֵינְךָ עָצַמְתָּ אוֹי כִּי בְּחֵק הַתַּאֲוָה נִרְדָּמְתָּ.

60

The vanities of your dreams have ravished your heart [Song 4:9]; what is this dream that you have dreamed [Gen 37:10]?

הַבְלֵי חֲלוֹמֶיךָ לְבָבָךְ לִבְּבוּ מַה הַחֲלוֹם הַזֶּה אֲשֶׁר חָלָמְתָּ.

61

The sixth answered and said: "If you wish to lay the bases of your intellect⚠4,

וַיַּעַן הַשִּׁשִׁי וַיּאמַר: אִם תִּרְצֶה אַדְנֵי שִׂכְלְךָ לְיַסֵּד.

62

make a poem whose end shall be: 'but to do justice and to love mercy' [Mic 6:8]."

עֲשֵׂה שִׁיר יְהִי סוֹפוֹ: כִּי אִם עֲשׂוֹת מִשְׁפָּט וְאַהֲבַת חֶסֶד.

63

And he took up his parable and said:

וַיִּשָּׂא מְשָׁלוֹ וַיֹּאמַר:

64

Prepare, O man, to meet your God [Amos 4:12], who stretched out the sky like a curtain [Isa 40:22] and founded the earth.

הִכּוֹן אֱנוֹשׁ לִקְרַאת אֱלֹהֶיךָ אֲשֶׁר נָטָה כְדֹק שַׁחַק וְאֶרֶץ יִסֵּד.

65

For what does your God seek of you but to do justice and to love mercy [Mic 6:8]?

כִּי מָה אֱלֹהֶיךָ מְבַקֵּשׁ מִמְּךָ כִּי אִם עֲשׂוֹת מִשְׁפָּט וְאַהְבַת חֶסֶד.

66

The seventh answered and said to him: "If your wisdom is like a bride fair of cheek,

וַיַּעַן הַשְּׁבִיעִי וַיּאמֶר לוֹ: אִם חָכְמָתְךָ כְּכַלָּה יְפַת לֶחִי.

67

make a poem whose end shall be: 'And they laid it on the boil, and he recovered' [Isa 38:21]."

עֲשֵׂה שִׁיר יְהִי סוֹפוֹ: וַיִּמְרְחוּ עַל הַשְּׁחִין וַיֶּחִי.

68

And he took up his parable and said:

וַיִּשָּׂא מְשָׁלוֹ וַיֹּאמַר:

69

Shaddai, the boil of my sins has made my heart sick, and Your name is my healing and the life of my spirit [Isa 38:16].

שַׁדַּי, שְׁחִין חֶטְאִי לְבָבִי הֶחֱלָה וּשְׁמָךְ רְפוּאָתִי וְחַיֵּי רוּחִי

70

My thoughts⚠5 took the salves of Your good remembrance, and laid them on the boil, and it recovered [Isa 38:21].

לָקְחוּ סְעִפַּי מֶרְקְחוֹת טוּב זִכְרְךָ וַיִּמְרְחוּ עַל הַשְּׁחִין וַיֶּחִי.

71

The eighth answered and said to him: "If you choose to reveal your intellect,

וַיַּעַן הָשְּׁמִינִי וַיּאמֶר לוֹ: אִם לְגַלּוֹת שִׂכְלְךָ אַתָּה בוֹחֵר.

72

make a poem whose end shall be: 'Your fullness and your outflow you shall not hold back' [Exod 22:28]⚠6."

עֲשֵׂה שִׁיר יְהִי סוֹפוֹ: מְלֵאָתְךָ וְדִמְעֲךָ לֹא תְאַחֵר.

73

And he took up his parable and said:

וַיִּשָּׂא מְשָׁלוֹ וַיֹּאמַר:

74

Man, your days have passed in vanity; how long will you not seek the face of God?

אֱנוֹשׁ חָלְפוּ יְמוֹתֶיךָ בְּהֶבֶל וְעַד מָתַי פְּנֵי אֵל לֹא תְשַׁחֵר.

75

Your eye is full of tears, and therefore do not hold back your fullness and your tears [Exod 22:28].

וְעֵינָךְ מָלְאָה דֶמַע וְעַל כֵּן מְלֵאָתָךְ וְדִמְעָךְ לֹא תְאַחֵר.

76

The ninth answered and said to him: "If you can grasp the secret that I have set in order in my heart,

וַיַּעַן הַתְּשִׁיעִי וַיּאמֶר לוֹ: אִם תַּשְׂכִּיל הַסּוֹד אֲשֶׁר בְּלִבִּי עָרַכְתִּי.

77

make a poem whose end shall be: 'If you will go with me, I will go' [Judg 4:8]."

עֲשֵׂה שִׁיר יְהִי סוֹפוֹ: אִם תֵּלְכִי עִמִּי וְהָלַכְתִּי.

78

And he took up his parable and said:

וַיִּשָּׂא מְשָׁלוֹ וַיֹּאמַר:

79

Awake, right hand of God [Isa 51:9], for my heart is melted like water within me, and I am poured out [Ps 22:15].

עוּרִי יְמִין הָאֵל הֲכִי לִבִּי נָמֵס כְּמַיִם בִּי וְנִשְׁפַּכְתִּי.

80

If you help me, I shall be helped; or if you hold up my hand, I shall be held up.

אִם תַּעְזְרִי אוֹתִי אֱהִי נֶעְזָר אוֹ תִּסְמְכִי יָדִי וְנִסְמָכְתִּי.

make a poem whose end shall be: 'His eye was not dim, nor his vigour fled' [Deut 34:7]."

וַיִּשָּׂא מְשָׁלוֹ וַיֹּאמַר:

וַיַּעַן הַשְּׂלֹשָׂה עָשָׂר וַיּאמֶר לוֹ: אִם תִּרְצֶה לְהַעֲצִיב כָּל מְקַנְאֶיךָ וּלְהַחֲלִי.

98

make a poem whose end shall be: 'And the Lord will take away from you all sickness' [Deut 7:15]."

עֲשֵׂה שִׁיר יְהִי סוֹפוֹ: וְהֵסִיר ה' מִמְּךָ כָּל חֹלִי.

99

And he took up his parable and said:

וַיִּשָּׂא מְשָׁלוֹ וַיֹּאמַר:

100

My heart, return to God when you are in distress, for in Him is the balm for my pangs and the healing of my anguish.

לִבִּי לְאֵל שׁוּבָה בְּעֵת יֵצַר לְךָ כִּי בוֹ צֳרִי צִירִי וּמַרְפֵּא חִילִי.

Had you trusted in your Rock in the time of your pangs, the Lord would have taken away from you all sickness [Deut 7:15].

102

103

make a poem whose end shall be: 'My sheaf arose and also stood upright' [Gen 37:7]."

עֲשֵׂה שִׁיר יְהִי סוֹפוֹ: קָמָה אֲלֻמָּתִי וְגַם נִצָּבָה.

104

And he took up his parable and said:

וַיִּשָּׂא מְשָׁלוֹ וַיֹּאמַר:

105

Indeed my tongue, on the day of battles, is counted a sword, and it has never turned back.

אָמְנָם לְשׂוֹנִי יוֹם קְרָבוֹת נֶחְשְׁבָה חֶרֶב וְאָחוֹר לֹא לְעוֹלָם שָׁבָה.

106

And when the sheaves of the masters of song fall mute⚠8, my sheaf arose and also stood upright [Gen 37:7].

וּבְעֵת אֲלֻמּוֹת בַּעֲלֵי שִׁיר אִלְּמוּ קָמָה אֲלֻמָּתִי וְגַם נִצָּבָה.

107

וַיַּעַן הַחֲמִשָּׁה עָשָׂר וַיּאמֶר לוֹ: אִם תִּרְצֶה לְהַכְנִיעַ כָּל עוֹזְרֵי רַהַב.

make a poem whose end shall be: 'And they made themselves gods of gold' [Exod 32:31]."

עֲשֵׂה שִׁיר יְהִי סוֹפוֹ: וַיַּעֲשׂוּ לָהֶם אֱלֹהֵי זָהָב.

וַיִּשָּׂא מְשָׁלוֹ וַיֹּאמַר:

They forsook their God and served their silver, and made themselves gods of gold [Exod 32:31].

עָזְבוּ אֱלֹהֵיהֶם וְכַסְפָּם עָבְדוּ וַיַּעֲשׂוּ לָהֶם אֱלֹהֵי זָהָב.

make a poem whose end shall be: 'The lines have fallen to me in pleasant places' [Ps 16:6]."

עֲשֵׂה שִׁיר יְהִי סוֹפוֹ: חֲבָלִים נָפְלוּ לִי בַנְּעִימִים.

וַיִּשָּׂא מְשָׁלוֹ וַיֹּאמַר:

וְאֹמַר עֵת יְהִי חֶלְקִי בְדָתוֹ חֲבָלִים נָפְלוּ לִי בַנְּעִימִים.

וַיַּעַן הַשִּׁבְעָה עָשָׂר וַיּאמֶר לוֹ: אִם תְּבַקֵשׁ לְחַבֵּר נִיב כְּנַפְשִׂי וּלְבָבִי.

עֲשֵׂה שִׁיר יְהִי סוֹפוֹ: וְאֶקְרַא סְגוֹר לִבָּם וְאֹכְלֵם שָׁם כְּלָבִיא.

119

And he took up his parable and said:

וַיִּשָּׂא מְשָׁלוֹ וַיֹּאמַר:

120

123

וַיַּעַן הַשְּׁמֹנָה עָשָׂר וַיּאמֶר לוֹ: אִם תִּרְצֶה לְנַקּוֹת שִׁירְךָ מִכָּל מוּם וְכַחַשׁ.

138

Uncertain renderings

  • ⚠1 בְּמַעֲלוֹת הַשִּׁירִים הַגְּבוֹהִים — מַעֲלוֹת is "steps, ascents" and also "rank, excellence" (מַעֲלָתוֹ, "his rank", two lines on). The phrase plays on שִׁיר הַמַּעֲלוֹת, the "Song of Ascents" of the Psalms.
  • ⚠2 וּבוֹקֵעַ מִשְּׁחָרָיו נְגוֹהִים — Literally "and splits brightnesses from his dawns": the poet breaks out like the light of dawn. The image is uncertain.
  • ⚠3 מְלָאכוֹת נְסוּכוֹת — נְסוּכוֹת is "poured, cast" (as metal) or "set up, installed" (Prov 8:23 נִסַּכְתִּי). Read as "fixed".
  • ⚠4 אַדְנֵי שִׂכְלְךָ לְיַסֵּד — The edition points אַדְנֵי, "the bases (sockets) of" (Job 38:6), not אֲדֹנִי, "my lord", which the unpointed letters also allow.
  • ⚠5 לָקְחוּ סְעִפַּי — סְעִפַּי read as "my thoughts" (Job 4:13 סְעִפִּים; Ps 94:19 שַׂרְעַפַּי). Literally the word is "my branches".
  • ⚠6 מְלֵאָתְךָ וְדִמְעֲךָ לֹא תְאַחֵר — In Exod 22:28 these are offerings: the first of the full harvest and of the juice of the press. The poem reads מְלֵאָה as the eye "full" of tears and דֶּמַע as "tears" (דִּמְעָה).
  • ⚠7 נָחַל אֲדֹנָי אֶת יְהוּדָה חֶלְקוֹ — The author's own name is Judah (al-Ḥarizi). The verse may also be heard as "the Lord is the portion that Judah inherits".
  • ⚠8 וּבְעֵת אֲלֻמּוֹת בַּעֲלֵי שִׁיר אִלְּמוּ — A sound play: אֲלֻמּוֹת "sheaves", אִלְּמוּ "fell dumb", and אֲלֻמָּתִי "my sheaf" of Joseph's dream (Gen 37:7).
  • ⚠9 בְמַטָּיו רֹאשׁ פְּרָזָיו — The meaning of פְּרָזָיו in Hab 3:14 is uncertain (warriors, leaders, or villagers). The envier is struck with his own weapons. The poem also plays on מַטֶּה (rod, here the pen) and מַטָּיו (his shafts).
  • ⚠10 וְעַל כֵּן פָּצְעוּ עֵינֵי לְחָיָיו — Measure for measure: the beloved's eyes wounded the lover's heart, so the lover's eyes wound the beloved's cheeks, perhaps by making them blush. The sense is uncertain.
  • ⚠11 הוֹרָךְ וּמוֹרָךְ — A rhyming pair: הוֹרֶה "parent" (the one who conceived you) and מוֹרֶה "teacher"; מוֹרָא is also "fear".
  • ⚠12 אֹתוֹ וְאֶתְהֶן — In Lev 20:14 "them" (feminine) are the women; here they are his lusts (תַּאֲוֹתָיו, feminine).
  • ⚠13 וְאֵידִי יְכַסֵּם — The edition points אֵידִי, "my calamity". A play on אֵד, "mist" (Gen 2:6), fits the image of tears that freeze. The sense is uncertain.
  • ⚠14 אֵשׁ לְבָבִי — In Ps 147:18 God sends His word and melts the ice. Here the subject may be the fire of the heart, as translated, or God: "but He sends His word to the fire of my heart".
  • ⚠15 עַמָּךְ דְּלֵה מִיָּם עֳנִי נִגְרָשׁ — Literally "Your people, draw up from the sea, affliction, tossed". Read with Isa 57:20 כַּיָּם נִגְרָשׁ, "like the tossing sea". The syntax is uncertain.
  • ⚠16 וְעַל יֶחְרָשׁ — The poem has וְעַל, the challenge line and Ps 50:3 have וְאַל, "and not". Translated as וְאַל.
  • ⚠17 וּמַפְלִיא פְלִילִיּוֹת — A sound play (מַפְלִיא / פְלִילִיּוֹת). פְּלִילִיָּה is "judgment" (Isa 28:7). The sense, "who does wonders in judging", is uncertain.
  • ⚠18 נִגְלוּ פָּנָי — Literally "my face was revealed": the gloom left it. It answers the old man's covered face ("His face was covered") and leads into the heavens that are rolled up (נָגֹלּוּ) in the next line.
