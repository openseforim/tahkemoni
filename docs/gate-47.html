שַׁעַר אַרְבָּעִים וְשִׁבְעָה

בְּשֶׁבַח כָּל מְדִינָה. זֹאת אוֹמֶרֶת אֲנִי אֶעֱבֹר רִאשׁוֹנָה

The Forty-Seventh Gate

Eight cities, from Alexandria to Babylon, each boast in turn that the first place is theirs

Gate 47 on Project Ben-Yehuda ↗

About this text

199 lines. One row is one rhyme unit of the rhymed prose, or one line of verse (its two halves set apart). References in [gray brackets] are the translator's identifications of biblical phrases. ⚠ marks an uncertain rendering. Each line number is a link to that line. Hebrew text: Project Ben-Yehuda.

1

Thus says Heman the Ezrahite: I journeyed from Pathros

נְאֻם הֵימָן הָאֶזְרָחִי: נָסַעְתִּי מִפְּתוֹר.

2

to the land of Caphtor⚠1,

לְאֶרֶץ כַּפְתּוֹר.

3

at the time when the voice of the turtledove is heard [Song 2:12].

בְּעֵת נִשְׁמָע קוֹל הַתּוֹר.

4

And while I was going on the way,

וּבְעוֹדִי הוֹלֵךְ בַּדֶּרֶךְ.

5

strong in pain and weak of knee,

וַאֲנִי חֲזַק מַכְאוֹב רְפֵה בֶרֶךְ.

6

the heat of the day [Gen 18:1] was

וְחֹם הַיּוֹם.

7

dreadful and terrible [Hab 1:7];

נוֹרָא וְאָיוֹם.

8

it burned bodies with its fire,

יִשְׂרֹף בְּאִשּׁוֹ הַגְּוִיּוֹת.

9

and tore lions with its might,

וְיִטְרֹף בְּכֹחוֹ הָאֲרָיוֹת.

10

and drove serpents and beasts out of their dens,

וְיוֹצִיא מִמְּעוֹנֵיהֶם הַנְּחָשִׁים וְהַחַיּוֹת.

11

until my spirit grew short.

עָדַ קָצְרָה רוּחִי.

12

I got down from my chariot

וְיָרַדְתִּי מֵעַל מֶרְכַּבְתִּי.

13

to seek out a place to sit,

לָתוּר מָקוֹם לְשִׁבְתִּי.

14

and to rest my beast.

וּלְהָנִיחַ אֶת בְּהֶמְתִּי.

15

And I saw, under the shade of the plants,

וָאֶרְאֶה תַּחַת צִלְלֵי הַצְּמָחִים.

16

among the reeds [Hos 13:15]⚠2,

בֵּין אַחִים.

17

a man caught in the cords of his sleep,

אִישׁ נִלְכַּד בְּחַבְלֵי שְׁנָתוֹ.

18

and bound with the ropes of his slumber.

וְנִקְשַׁר בַּעֲבוֹת תְּנוּמָתוֹ.

19

When I drew near to him and stood over him, he awoke, and he started from his slumber,

וְכַאֲשֶׁר קָרַבְתִּי אֵלָיו וְנִצַּבְתִּי עָלָיו הֵקִיץ וְהוּא חָרֵד מִתְּנוּמָתוֹ.

20

and fled from before me,

וַיִּבְרַח מִלְּפָנַי.

21

and turned aside from me.

וְנָטָה מִפָּנַי.

22

I said to him: "Peace be with you, do not fear [Judg 6:23].

וָאֹמַר לוֹ: שָׁלוֹם לְךָ אַל תִירָא.

23

You have found a brother for adversity [Prov 17:17],

מָצָאתָ אָח לְצָרָה.

24

and met a man of company⚠3.

וּפָגַשְׁתָּ אִישׁ חֶבְרָה.

25

Now tell me, what have you here [Isa 22:16],

וְעַתָּה הַגִּידָה לִי מַה לְךָ פֹה.

26

and where are you going?

וְלֶכְתְּךָ אֵיפֹה.

27

And pray, what is the city of your birth,

וְאָנָא עִיר מוֹלַדְתֶּךָ.

28

and what is your work [Jonah 1:8]?"

וּמַה מְּלַאכְתֶּךָ.

29

He said: "My parents are a faithful seed,

אָמַר: הוֹרַי זֶרַע אֱמוּנִים.

30

of the children of the Kenite [Judg 1:16],

מִבְּנֵי הַקֵּינִים.

31

and my dwelling is the oak in Zaanannim [Judg 4:11]."

וּמְעוֹנִי אֵלוֹן בְּצַעֲנַנִּים.

32

When I smelled the myrtles of his sayings,

וְכַאֲשֶׁר הֲרִיחוֹתִי הֲדַסֵּי אֲמָרָיו.

33

and tasted the honeycomb of his words,

וְטָעַמְתִּי צוּף דְּבָרָיו.

34

I knew him,

הִכַּרְתִּיהוּ.

35

and I knew that he was our friend Ḥever the Kenite, whom I had sought.

וְיָדַעְתִּי כִּי הוּא חֲבֵרֵנוּ חֶבֶר הַקֵּינִי אֲשֶׁר דְּרַשְתִּיהוּ.

36

So I sat with him,

וָאֵשֵׁב עִמּוֹ.

37

to glean in the streams of his speech⚠4.

לִלְקֹט בְּדָלְחֵי נָאֳמוֹ.

38

I said to him: "Tell me of the great things [2 Kgs 8:4] that your eyes have seen,

וָאֹמַר לוֹ: סַפֵּר לִי מִן הַגְּדוֹלוֹת אֲשֶׁר רָאוּ עֵינֶיךָ.

39

and your ears have heard."

וְשָׁמְעוּ אָזְנֶיךָ.

40

He said: "You have spoken well [Exod 10:29],

וַיֹּאמַר: כֵּן דִּבַּרְתָּ.

41

and I am ready for all that you have asked.

וַאֲנִי מְזֻמָּן לְכָל אֲשֶׁר שָׁאָלְתָּ.

42

We have heard that in days of old, from ancient times,

שָׁמַעְנוּ כִּי מִימֵי קֶדֶם אֲשֶׁר מֵעוֹלָם.

43

the delicate cities all boasted,

הִתְפָּאֲרוּ הַמְּדִינוֹת הָעֲדִינוֹת כֻּלָּם.

44

those from Egypt even to Elam,

אֲשֶׁר מִמִּצְרַיִם וְעַד עֵילָם.

45

and they broke off their yoke [Gen 27:40].

וּפָרְקוּ עֻלָּם.

46

Each city praised herself for her beauty and her splendor,

וְהִתְהַלְּלָה כָּל מְדִינָה בְיָפְיָהּ וְהַדְרָתָהּ.

47

and each city was jealous of her sister.

וַתְּקַנֵּא כָּל עִיר בַּאֲחוֹתָהּ.

48

No-amon⚠5 answered,

וַתַּעַן נֹא אָמוֹן.

49

at the head of the throng,

בְּרֹאשׁ הֶהָמוֹן.

50

and said: 'Kingship befits me [Jer 10:7],

וַתֹּאמַר: לִי יָאֲתָה מְלוּכָה.

51

for an everlasting covenant is kept for me, and ordered [2 Sam 23:5].

כִּי בְּרִית עוֹלָם שְׁמוּרָה לִי וַעֲרוּכָה.

52

God called my name No-amon,

וְהָאֵל קָרָא שְׁמִי נֹא אָמוֹן.

53

for I am to Him as a daughter [Esth 2:7], and I was beside Him as a nursling [Prov 8:30]⚠6.

כִּי אֲנִי לוֹ לְבַת וָאֶהְיֶה אֶצְלוֹ אָמוֹן.

54

On my neck is the necklace of the sea, which gives me strength and might,

וּבְצַוָּארִי עֲנַק הַיָּם הַנּוֹתֵן לִי עֹז וְעָצְמָה.

55

and clothes my neck with thunder [Job 39:19].

וּמַלְבִּישׁ צַוָּארִי רַעְמָה.

56

My beauty is like fine gold that does not grow dim [Lam 4:1],

וְיָפְיִי כַּזָּהָב הַטּוֹב לֹא יוּעַם.

57

and my ways are ways of pleasantness [Prov 3:17].

וּדְרָכַי דַּרְכֵי נֹעַם.

58

At the crossroads I stand [Prov 8:2],

וַאֲנִי בֵּית נְתִיבוֹת נִצָּבֶת.

59

and at the entrances of the sea I dwell [Ezek 27:3].

וְעַל מְבוֹאוֹת יָם יוֹשָׁבֶת.

60

Who is she among the cities who could touch my hems,

וּמִי זֹאת מִן הַמְּדִינוֹת אֲשֶׁר תִּגַּע בְּשׁוּלַי.

61

and reach to me?

וְתַגִּיעַ אֵלַי.

62

Over her head my banners would be raised,

וְעַל רֹאשָׁהּ יְרוּמוּן דְּגָלַי.

63

and the multitude of my waves would cover her.'

וִיכַסּוּהָ הֲמוֹן גַּלָּי.

64

And she took up her parable and said:

וַתִּשָּׂא מְשָׁלָהּ וַתּאֹמַר:

65

Were every city to wrap herself in a robe of radiance, and the host of heaven be as bells for her,

לוּ תַעֲטֶה כָל-עִיר מְעִיל זֹהַר וּצְבָא שְׁחָקִים לָהּ כְּמוֹ פַעְמוֹן.

66

or set her throne on the circle of the sky, or build a palace above the heavenly dwelling,

אוֹ נָתְנָה כִסְאָהּ בְּחוּג שַׁחַק אוֹ בָנְתָה מֵעַל זְבוּל אַרְמוֹן.

67

my throne is above their heads; and though they grow proud, they all give their hand [1 Chr 29:24] to No-amon.

כִּסְאִי עֲלֵי רֹאשָׁם וְאִם יִגְאוּ יָד יִתְּנוּ כֻלָּם לְנֹא אָמוֹן.

68

Then Egypt⚠7 answered and said: 'I am a delight to the eyes [Gen 3:6],

וַתַּעַן מִצְרַיִם וַתֹּאמַר: אֲנִי תַּאֲוָה לָעֵינַיִם.

69

comely as Jerusalem [Song 6:4].

נָאוָה כִּירוּשָׁלַיִם.

70

I am a bustling city [Isa 22:2],

אֲנִי עִיר הוֹמִיָּה.

71

and for me silence is praise [Ps 65:2];

וְלִי תְהִלָּה דוּמִיָּה.

72

mine are strength and sound wisdom [Job 12:16].

וְלִי עֹז וְתוּשִׁיָּה.

73

All my men are nobles of the land,

וְכָל אֲנָשַׁי אֲצִילֵי אָרֶץ.

74

and my merchants the honored of the earth [Isa 23:8],

וְסוֹחֲרֵי נִכְבַּדֵּי אָרֶץ.

75

and to me the nations shall come from the ends of the earth [Jer 16:19].

וְאֵלֵי גּוֹיִם יָבוֹאוּ מֵאַפְסֵי אָרֶץ.

76

Around me is the Nile,

וּסְבִיבוֹתַי הַיְאוֹר.

77

the resplendent,

הַנָּאוֹר.

78

by whose wonders light rose in the darkness [Ps 112:4],

אֲשֶׁר מִנִּפְלְאוֹתָיו זָרַח בַּחשֶׁךְ אוֹר.

79

whose source is from the Garden of Eden,

אֲשֶׁר מִגַּן עֵדֶן מוֹצָאוֹ.

80

and whose course runs to Egypt.

וְעַד מִצְרַיִם מוֹבָאוֹ.

81

In it awesome deeds were seen,

וּבֹו נִרְאוּ נוֹרָאוֹת.

82

which were to God for a witness and a sign [Isa 19:20].'

אֲשֶׁר הָיוּ לָאֵל לְעֵד וּלְאוֹת.

83

And she took up her parable and said:

וַתִּשָּׂא מְשָׁלָהּ וַתֹּאמַר:

84

Were the lands to go up to heaven, I would count them as a place for the sole of the foot to tread [Deut 2:5];

לוּ הָאֲרָצוֹת יַעֲלוּ שָׁמַיִם לִי נֶחְשְׁבוּ מִדְרָךְ לְכַף רַגְלָיִם.

85

or should the rank of their glory rise to the heavenly dwelling, the rank of my glory has risen twofold.

אוֹ עָלְתָה מַעְלַת יְקָרָם עַד זְבוּל מַעְלַת יְקָרִי עָלְתָה כִפְלָיִם.

86

For the cities are as handmaids, or as daughters, and their mother is counted Egypt.

כִּי הַמְּדִינוֹת כַּשְּׁפָחוֹת אוֹ כְמוֹ בָּנוֹת וְאִמָּם נֶחְשְׁבָה מִצְרָיִם.

87

Then Jerusalem answered and said: 'When I was in the days of my prime [Job 29:4],

וַתַּעַן יְרוּשָׁלַיִם וַתֹּאמַר: בִּהְיוֹתִי בִּימֵי חָרְפִּי.

88

the Shekhinah dwelt at my threshold,

הַשְּכִינָה שָׁכְנָה בְסִפִּי.

89

the angels on high desired my land,

וְחָמְדוּ אַרְצִי מַלְאֲכֵי גְבוֹהִים.

90

and I was the camp of God [Gen 32:3],

וְהָיִיתִי מַחֲנֵה אֱלֹהִים.

91

and the shining stars were jealous of me.

וַיְקַנְאוּ בִי כּוֹכְבֵי נְגוֹהִים.

92

For the glory of God was in my temple,

כִּי כְבוֹד אֵל בְּהֵיכָלַי.

93

and holiness in my tent,

וְהַקְּדֻשָּׁה בְּאָהֳלִי.

94

and eternal light over my banner,

וְאוֹר עוֹלָם עַל דִּגְלִי.

95

and piety in my nobles,

וְהַחֲסִידוּת בַּאֲצִילַי.

96

and prophecy in my elders and my infants.

וְהַנְּבוּאָה בִּזְקֵנַי וְעוֹלָלַי.

97

And now, through the sin of the men of my council [Job 19:19],

וְעַתָּה בַּעֲוֹן מְתֵי סוֹדִי.

98

my glory has gone into exile [1 Sam 4:21],

גָּלָה כְבוֹדִי.

99

and my foundation has been laid bare [Ps 137:7].

וְהָעֳרָה יְסוֹדִי.

100

And the place where the Shekhinah dwelt,

וְהַמָּקוֹם אֲשֶׁר שָׁכְנָה בוֹ הַשְּׁכִינָה.

101

a cloud settles upon it [Job 3:5].

תִּשְׁכֹּן עָלָיו עֲנָנָה.

102

And in the place of holiness and prophecy,

וּבִמְקוֹם הַקְּדֻשּׁה וְהַנְּבוּאָה.

103

the rebel is defiled [Zeph 3:1]⚠8,

נִגְאָלָה מֹרְאָה.

104

and the spirit of impurity [Zech 13:2] is there.

וְרוּחַ הַטֻּמְאָה.

105

And in the abode of the halls

וּבִנְוֵה הָאוּלַמִּים.

106

are mute idols [Hab 2:18],

אֱלִילִים אִלְּמִים.

107

and the forms of images.

וְצוּרַת צְלָמִים.

108

Yet for all this I have rank and pre-eminence in the eyes of the peoples,

וּבְכָל זֹאת יֵשׁ לִי בְּעֵינֵי הָעַמִּים מַעֲלָה וְיִתְרוֹן.

109

and my name is over all the lands for glory and remembrance.'

וּשְׁמִי עַל כָּל הָאֲרָצוֹת לִפְאֵר וּלְזִכָּרוֹן.

110

And she took up her parable and said:

וַתִּשָּׂא מְשָׁלָהּ וַתֹּאמַר:

111

Let every man's tongue cleave to his jaws [Ps 22:16]⚠9 in the abode of the Lord, the city of Jerusalem.

תִּדְבַּק לְשׁוֹן כָּל-אִישׁ לְמַלְקוֹחַיִם בִּנְוֵה אֲדֹנָי עִיר יְרוּשָׁלָיִם.

112

She is the camp of God, and the Rock opened her gates facing the gates of heaven.

מַחְנֵה אֱלֹהִים הִיא וְצוּר פָּתַח שְׁעָ- רֶיהָ לְנֹכַח שַׁעֲרֵי שָׁמָיִם.

113

For she is the light of the world for every eye; she is the life of hearts, the desire of the eyes.

כִּי הִיא מְאוֹר עוֹלָם לְכָל עַיִן הִיא חַיֵּי לְבָבוֹת תַּאֲוַת עֵינָיִם.

114

Then Acre answered and said: 'I am the headstone [Zech 4:7],

וַתַּעַן עַכּוֹ וַתֹּאמַר: אָנכֹיִ הָאֶבֶן הָרֹאשָׁה.

115

and my land is a holy land,

וְאַרְצִי אֶרֶץ קְדוֹשָׁה.

116

for I am the rose of Sharon [Song 2:1],

כִּי אֲנִי חֲבַצֶּלֶת הַשָּׁרוֹן.

117

and around me are Carmel and Lebanon,

וּסְבִיבוֹתַי הַכַּרְמֶל וְהַלְּבָנוֹן.

118

Tabor and Hermon [Ps 89:13].'

וְתָבוֹר וְחֶרְמוֹן.

Uncertain renderings

  • ⚠1 נָסַעְתִּי מִפְּתוֹר. לְאֶרֶץ כַּפְתּוֹר — Biblical names. Pathros (Isa 11:11, Jer 44:1) is Upper Egypt. Caphtor (Deut 2:23, Amos 9:7) was taken by medieval Jewish writers (Saadia, Benjamin of Tudela) to be Damietta in the Nile Delta. The two names rhyme.
  • ⚠2 בֵּין אַחִים — Hos 13:15 "though he be fruitful among brothers (אַחִים)". Many read the word there as "reeds" (from אָחוּ, meadow grass), which suits the shade of the plants here. Literally "among brothers".
  • ⚠3 וּפָגַשְׁתָּ אִישׁ חֶבְרָה — חֶבְרָה "company, fellowship" plays on the name Ḥever (חֶבֶר), whom the narrator has not yet recognized.
  • ⚠4 לִלְקֹט בְּדָלְחֵי נָאֳמוֹ — Obscure. The root דלח is used in Ezek 32:2, 32:13 of stirring up water; "streams" is a guess from that. The form may be corrupt.
  • ⚠5 וַתַּעַן נֹא אָמוֹן — No-amon (Nah 3:8) is Egyptian Thebes. Medieval Jewish writers took it to be Alexandria, which suits her boasts of the sea here.
  • ⚠6 וָאֶהְיֶה אֶצְלוֹ אָמוֹן — Prov 8:30, said by Wisdom: "I was beside Him as an אָמוֹן" (a nursling, or a craftsman). The city explains her name No-amon by this word.
  • ⚠7 וַתַּעַן מִצְרַיִם — מִצְרַיִם is Egypt, and in medieval Hebrew also its capital Fustat-Cairo, which is the city meant here beside Alexandria.
  • ⚠8 נִגְאָלָה מֹרְאָה — Zeph 3:1 "Woe to her that is rebellious (מֹרְאָה) and defiled (נִגְאָלָה), the oppressing city", said of Jerusalem. Read here as "the rebel (city) is defiled"; "her sight is defiled" is also possible.
  • ⚠9 תִּדְבַּק לְשׁוֹן כָּל-אִישׁ לְמַלְקוֹחַיִם — The words are from Ps 22:16; the sense follows Ps 137:5–6, "let my tongue cleave to my palate if I do not remember you": a curse on anyone who does not speak her praise.
  • ⚠10 וְאָנֹכִי שְׁכֵנָה לַשְּׁכִינָה — A play on שְׁכֵנָה "neighbor" and שְׁכִינָה "Shekhinah", from the same root (dwell).
  • ⚠11 וּסְבִיבוֹתַי אֲמָנָה וּפַרְפַּר — The rivers of Damascus in 2 Kgs 5:12: Abana (the written text אבנה; the read form אמנה, Amana) and Pharpar, usually identified with the Barada and the Aʿwaj.
  • ⚠12 הִשְׁכִּין כְּבוֹדוֹ תוֹךְ / חַדְרָךְ וְדַמֶּשֶׂק מְנוּחָתוֹ — Zech 9:1 "the word of the Lord is in the land of Hadrach, and Damascus is its resting place". Hadrach is a place near Hamath; the rabbis (Sifre Deut 1) also read the verse to say that Jerusalem will extend to Damascus.
  • ⚠13 וַתַּעַן צוֹבָה — Zobah (Aram-zobah, 2 Sam 8:3, 10:6) is Aleppo in medieval Hebrew.
  • ⚠14 אֲנִי נֵר מַעֲרָכָה — Uncertain. Perhaps the Temple lamp kept in order (Exod 27:20–21, Lev 24:3–4, the verb ערך), or "the lamp of the battle line" (מַעֲרָכָה, battle array).
  • ⚠15 וְאַרְצִי לְבָנָה כַלְּבָנָה — A play on לְבָנָה "white" and לְבָנָה "moon".
  • ⚠16 מֵהֶם רוֹפְאֵי רְפָאִים — A play on רוֹפְאֵי "healers" and רְפָאִים, which also means "the shades, the dead" (Isa 26:14): "healers who heal", or "healers of the dead".
  • ⚠17 וּבְעֵת אֲלֻמּוֹת אִלְּמוּ כֻלָּם — Joseph's dream, Gen 37:7. אִלְּמוּ "were bound" also sounds like "were struck dumb" (אִלֵּם, mute): when the other cities fell silent, Zobah stood up.
  • ⚠18 וַתַּעַן אַשּׁוּר — Ashur (Assyria); in medieval Hebrew usually Mosul, across the Tigris from Nineveh. Her speech plays on the name with אֹשֶׁר "happiness" and Leah's words at the naming of Asher (Gen 30:13).
  • ⚠19 וּמִמֶּנִּי יָצְאוּ גְּאוֹנֵי הַתּוּשִׁיָּה — גְּאוֹנִים "prides, eminences" is also the title of the Geonim, the heads of the Babylonian academies of Sura and Pumbedita.
