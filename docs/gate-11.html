שַׁעַר אַחַד-עָשָׂר

בְּמַהֲלַל מְלִיצָה בְּכָל מִלּוֹתֶיהָ רֵי"שׁ, וְהַשְּׁנִיָה לָהּ

The Eleventh Gate

Three proofs of skill in Bethlehem: a poem in Hebrew, Arabic and Aramaic, a letter of praise with a resh in every word, and a second letter with no resh at all

Gate 11 on Project Ben-Yehuda ↗

About this text

271 lines. One row is one rhyme unit of the rhymed prose, or one line of verse (its two halves set apart). References in [gray brackets] are the translator's identifications of biblical phrases. ⚠ marks an uncertain rendering. Each line number is a link to that line. Hebrew text: Project Ben-Yehuda.

1

and from the second the resh is utterly driven out [Exod 11:1]⚠1.

הָרֵי"שׁ גָּרֵשׁ יְגָרֵשׁ

2

Thus says Heman the Ezrahite: the sons of my thoughts carried me,

נְאֻם הֵימָן הָאֶזְרָחִי: נְשָׂאוּנִי בְנֵי מַחֲשָׁבִי.

3

and the musings of my heart,

וְרַעְיוֹנֵי לְבָבִי.

4

to journey from the home where I dwelt

לִנְסוֹעַ מִנְּוֵה מוֹשָׁבִי.

5

to the land of beauty [Dan 11:16].

אֶל אֶרֶץ הַצְּבִי.

6

And I, on the mountains of Bether [Song 2:17], was lighter than a gazelle [2 Sam 2:18],

וַאֲנִי עַל הָרֵי בָתֵר קַל מִצְּבִי .

7

pursuing every lovely turn of eloquence,

רוֹדֵף אַחֲרֵי כָל מְלִיצָה חֲמוּדָה.

8

and panting to hear every parable and riddle.

וְשׁוֹאֵף לִשְׁמֹעַ כָּל מָשָׁל וְחִידָה .

9

I journeyed to Bethlehem of Judah [Ruth 1:1].

וְנָסַעְתִּי לְבֵית לֶחֶם יְהוּדָה.

10

And while I was in one of its towns,

וּבְעוֹדִי בְאַחַת עָרֶיהָ.

11

at one of its gates,

וּבְשַׁעַר מִשְּׁעָרֶיהָ.

12

cherishing its dust [Ps 102:15],

אֲחוֹנֵן עֲפָרֶיהָ.

13

and the pasture of its fawns,

וּמִרְעֵה עֳפָרֶיהָ.

14

I saw men gathered together,

רָאִיתִי אֲנָשִׁים נִקְבָּצִים.

15

coming and running from every corner.

וּמִכָּל פִּנָּה בָאִים וְרָצִים.

16

And when I asked them the reason for their gathering,

וְכַאֲשֶׁר שְׁאִלְתִּים עַל עִנְיַן קִבּוּצָם.

17

and the cause of their running,

וְסִיבַּת רוּצָם.

18

they told me that a wise man had passed by them,

אָמְרוּ לִי כִּי אִישׁ חָכָם עָבַר עֲלֵיהֶם .

19

and camped within their borders;

וְחָנָה בִגְבוּלֵיהֶם.

20

in the eloquence of song there is none who can summon him [Jer 49:19],

וּבִמְלִיצוֹת הַשִּׁיר אֵין מִי יוֹעִידֵהוּ.

21

and in contests of wisdom none who can stand against him.

וּבְמִלְחֲמוֹת תֻּשִׁיּוֹת אֵין מִי יַעַרְכֵהוּ.

22

I said in my heart: Let me run among them,

וְאָמַרְתִּי בְלִבִּי אָרוּצָה נָא בְתוֹכָם .

23

and hear what is in the mouth of the wise man.

וְאֶשְׁמְעָה מַה בְּפִי הֶחָכָם.

24

And when I came near the camp

וְכַאֲשֶׁר קָרַבְתִּי אֶל הַמַּחֲנֶה.

25

where he was encamped,

אֲשֶׁר הוּא שָׁם חוֹנֶה.

26

I saw among them an old man,

וָאֵרֶא בְתוֹכָם אִיש זָקֵן.

27

setting in order the choicest eloquence [Eccl 12:9].

מִבְחַר הַמְּלִיצוֹת מְתַקֵּן .

28

And he was saying: "Hear my words, you wise men,

וְהוּא אוֹמֵר: שִׁמְעוּ חֲכָמִים מִלָּי.

29

and you who know, give ear to me [Job 34:2].

וְיוֹדְעִים הַאֲזִינוּ אֵלָי.

30

For in my tongue is the fountain of wisdom and of eloquence,

כִּי בִלְשׁוֹנִי מְקוֹר הַתּוּשִׁיּוֹת וְהַמְּלִיצוֹת.

31

and in my thoughts the treasury of inlaid epistles,

וּבְרַעְיוֹנִי אוֹצַר הָאִגְּרוֹת הַמְשֻׁבָּצוֹת.

32

and of forceful compositions [Job 6:25].

וְהַמַּחְבָּרוֹת הַנִּמְרָצוֹת.

33

Were the lightnings to run in my courses,

וְאִלּוּ יְרוּצוּן הַבְּרָקִים בִּמְסִלּוֹתָי.

34

they would stray on my paths,

תָּעוּ בִנְתִיבוֹתָי.

35

and would not reach the dust of my chariots."

וְלֹא הִשִּׂיגוּ אֲבַק מַרְכְּבוֹתַי.

36

The chief of the guests said to him: "You have bared a lofty arm [Isa 52:10],

אָמַר לוֹ רֹאשׁ הַקְּרוּאִים: זְרוֹעַ רָמָה חָשַׂפְתָּ .

37

and drawn a sword of pride.

וְחֶרֶב גַּאֲוָה שָׁלַפְתָּ.

38

We have heard your speech, but we have not tested you;

וְהִנֵּה שָׁמַעְנוּ נִיבְךָ וְלֹא חֲקַרְנוּךָ .

39

when your word comes true, we will honor you [Judg 13:17]."

וְכִי יָבוֹא דְּבָרְךָ וְכִבַּדְנוּךָ.

40

The old man said: "Here I stand before you,

אָמַר הַזָּקֵן: הִנְנִי נִצָּב לִפְנֵיכֶם.

41

and I stand between the Lord and you [Deut 5:5].

וְאָנֹכִי עוֹמֵד בֵּין ה' וּבֵינֵיכֶם.

42

Choose for yourselves this day [Josh 24:15] the deepest eloquence,

בַּחֲרוּ לָכֶם הַיּוֹם הַמְּלִיצוֹת הָעֲמֻקּוֹת.

43

and the strongest poems,

וְהַשִּׁירוֹת הַחֲזָקוֹת.

44

and test me as you wish,

וְנַסּוּ כְפִי אֲשֶׁר תִּרְצוּ.

45

and search as you please;

וְחַפְּשׂוּ כַאֲשֶׁר תַּחְפְּצוּ.

46

for all my words are in righteousness [Prov 8:8],

כִּי כָל דְּבָרַי צֶדֶק.

47

and you will find no briar among my lilies."

וְלֹא תִמְצְאוּ בְּשׁוֹשַׁנַּי חֶדֶק.

48

They said to him: "If your words are true [Gen 42:19],

אָמְרוּ לוֹ: אִם כֵּנִים דְּבָרֶיךָ.

49

and your sayings just,

וְצֶדֶק אֲמָרֶיךָ.

50

make us a poem built of three languages,

עֲשֵׂה לָנוּ שִׁירָה בְנוּיָה מִשָּׁלשׁ לְשׁוֹנוֹת.

51

a deep work, that every man of understanding will recognize."

כִּמְלָאכָה עֲמֻקָּה יַכִּירֶנָּה כָל אִישׁ תְּבוּנוֹת.

52

And he took up his parable and said [Num 23:7]:

וַיִּשָּׂא מְשָׁלוֹ וַיֹּאמַר:

53

The word of God is faithful,⚠2 and obedience to the Merciful, and beautiful are His works.

דְּבַר אֵל יֵאָמַן וטאעת אלרחמן שְׁפַר עוֹבָדַיָּא.

54

I offer Him my song first, and the praise of the Creator, at the head of every word.

אֲקַדֵּם לוֹ שִׁירִי ותסביח אלבארי בְּרֵישׁ כָּל-מִלַּיָּא.

55

His name is exalted for ever; He is the Lord, the Most High, and the Master of the worlds.

שְׁמוֹ לָעַד נַעֲלָה הוא אלרב אלעלי וְרִבּוֹן עָלְמַיָּא.

56

By the light of His righteousness the brightness shines, like a light that gleams in the middle of the nights.

לְאוֹר צִדְקוֹ זֹהַר חכי נורא יזהר בּפַלְגּוּת לֵילַיָּא.

57

Over every secret He looks down, with an essence not revealed to any of those who stand.

וְעַל כֹּל סוֹד נִשְׁקָף בֹדאת לם יכשף לְחַד מִן קָאֲמַיָּא.

58

His mercies are mighty; His rivers give drink in a place where there is no water.

חֲסָדָיו גָּבְרוּ ורות אנהארה/ אֲתַר דִּי לֵית מַיָּא.

59

In the world He has set dominion; He holds all the regions, and His height reaches the heavens.

בְּתֵבֵל שָׂם מִשְׁטָר חוי כל אלאקטאר וְרוּמֵהּ לִשְׁמַיָּא.

60

By Him every living thing lives, and the balance of the world hangs in His right hand.

וּבוֹ כָל-חַי יִחְיֶה ומיזאן אלדניא בְּיַמִּינֵהּ תָּלְיָא.

61

Lord of all the living, His is the kingship that lasts, and kings perish.

אֲדוֹן כָּל הַחַיִּים לה אלמלך אלדאים וְיוֹבְדוּן מַלְכַיָּא.

62

His wonders are precious; and though time grows long, He changes the times [Dan 2:21].

פְּלִיאוֹתָיו יָקְרוּ ואן טאל אלדהר מְהַשְׁנֵא זִמְנַיָּא.

63

He makes the world quake, and He brings the miracle at the end of the times.

וְהוּא תֵבֵל יַרְגִיז ויאתי באלמעג'ז בְּסוֹף עִדָּנַיָּא.

64

His salvation will be revealed: He will raise the lowly, and bring down the high.

וְיִשְׁעוֹ יִגָּלֶה פיעלי מדלולא וְיָחִית רָמַיָּא.

65

The voice of the shofar will sound for those who sleep in the waste⚠3, and the dead will live.

וְקוֹל שׁוֹפָר יִתְקַע לנואם אלבלקע וְיֵיחוּן מֵתַיָּא.

66

His secret He will show: He will give life to His dead, and redeem the living.

וְסוֹדוֹ יַרְאֵהוּ פיחיי מותאה וְיִפְרוּק חַיַּיָא.

67

His house He will build, and raise its dwelling upon the top of the mountains [Isa 2:2].

וּבֵיתוֹ יִבְנֵהוּ ויעלי מתואה עֲלֵי רֵישׁ טוּרַיָּא.

68

And there we will set out a song to the Most High, in thanks, to Him who is fearful in praises [Exod 15:11].

וְשָׁם נַעְרֹךְ שִׁירָה לדי אלעליא שכרא דְּחִיל תֻּשְׁבָּחַיָּא.

69

My song shall be sung of Him, and my praises are spread abroad in every tongue.

וְשִׁירִי בוֹ יוּשַׁר ואמדאחי תנשר בְּכָל לִשָּׁנַיָּא.

70

The envious will moan at them; its arrow has gone out from them to the heart of the wise⚠4.

מְקַנְאִים בָּם יֶהְמוּ סרי מנהא סהמוֹ לְלֵב חַכִּימַיָּא.

71

Over the foes they have hissed, and the East sends it as a gift⚠5 to the land of Spain.

וְעַל צָרִים שָׁרְקּוּ ויהדיאה אלשרקו לְאַרַע אַסְפַּמְיָא

72

In words they have dripped honey, like musk in its fragrance, and its memory reaches the heavens.

בְּמִלִּים צוּף נָטְפוּ חכי אלמסך אלערפו וְדִכְרֵהּ לִשְׁמַיָּא.

73

It is a precious poem; it has become a glory at the head of all nations.

וְהִיא שִׁירָה יָקְרָה לקד אצֹחת פכרא לְרֵישׁ כֹּל אֻמַּיָּא.

74

The edge of its sword is cast upon the heads of the heroes, and of all the mighty.

וְצוּר חַרְבָּה יוּטַל עלי האם אלאבטאל וְכֹל גִּבָּרַיָּא.

75

By it every heart is watered, and its memory remains to the end of days.

וּבָה כָּל-לֵב רוֹהֶה ויבקא דֹכראהא לְסוֹפֵי יוֹמַיָּא.

76

He said to them: "Here is a precious poem, bound with a cord of three tongues, that shall not be loosed [Eccl 12:6],

וַיֹּאמֶר לָהֶם: הִנֵּה שִׁירָה יְקָרָה קְשׁוּרָה בְּחֶבֶל שָׁלֹש לְשׁוֹנוֹת בַּל יֵרָתֵק .

77

and the threefold cord is not quickly broken [Eccl 4:12].

וְהַחוּט הַמְשֻׁלָּש לֹא בִמְהֵרָה יִנָּתֵק.

78

For fear of it the hearers trembled, and were nipped to the quick⚠6,

מִפַּחְדָּהּ חָרְדוּ הַשּׁוֹמְעִים וְנִקְרְצוּ קָרֶץ.

79

and under three things the earth quakes [Prov 30:21].

וְתַחַת שָׁלֹש רָגְזָה אָרֶץ.

80

I built it in the tongue of Aram, of Eber, and of Arabia,

בְּנִיתִיהָ בִלְשׁוֹן אֲרָם וְעֵבֶר וַעֲרָב.

81

and there is none who comes near it,

וְאֵין אֲשֶׁר אֵלֶיהָ יִקְרָב.

82

in the East or in the West."

בַּמִּזְרָח וּבַמַּעֲרָב.

83

All the people said: "You have done well, you have done great things [Joel 2:21],

אָמְרוּ כָל הָעָם: הֵטַבְתָּ וְלַעֲשׂוֹת הִגְדַּלְתָּ.

84

you have been wise and shown insight,

וְחָכַמְתָּ וְהִשְׂכַּלְתָּ .

85

and made the wise foolish [Isa 44:25].

וְחֲכָמִים סִכַּלְתָּ.

86

We testify that in the craft of poems there is none to compare with you [Ps 40:6],

וְיָעַדְנוּ כִּי בִמְלֶאכֶת הַשִּׁירִים אֵין עֲרֹךְ אֵלֶיךָ.

87

in the sweetness of your words,

בְּנֹעַם מִלֶּיךָ.

88

and in the eloquence of your parables.

וּבִמְלִיצוֹת מְשָׁלֶיךָ.

89

And if you can show your strength in a work set apart⚠7,

וְאִם תּּוכַל לְהַרְאוֹת כֹּחֲךָ בִּמְפֹרָד.

90

you will be head over all the learned of Sepharad.

תִּהְיֶה לְרֹאשׁ לְכָל מְבִינֵי סְפָרָד.

91

Now make us an epistle,

וְעַתָּה עֲשֵׂה לָנוּ אִגֶּרֶת.

92

that gathers every good turn of eloquence,

כָּל מְלִיצָה טוֹבָה אוֹגֶרֶת.

93

desirable to all who seek it [Ps 111:2],

חֲמוּדָה לְכָל דּוֹרֵשׁ.

94

and let there be a resh in every word of it,

וְיִהְיֶה בְכָל מִלָּה מִמִּלוֹתֶיהָ רֵי"שׁ.

95

and let it be in praise of one of the nobles,

וְתִהְיֶה שֶׁבַח לְאֶחָד מִן הַנִּכְבָּדִים.

96

and of the delightful princes."

וְהַשָּׂרִים הַנֶּחְמָדִים.

97

He said: "Incline your ear to my words,

וַיֹּאמַר: הַטּוּ אָזְנְכֶם לְמִלָּי.

98

and give ear to me."

וְהַאֲזִינוּ אֵלָי.

99

And he answered and said: Cry aloud with the throat [Isa 58:1]⚠8,

וַיַּעַן וַיֹּאמַר: קְרָא בְגָרוֹן.

100

speak sayings of joy,

אִמְרֵי רֹן.

101

rouse your thoughts,

וְעוֹרֵר רַעְיוֹנֶיךָ.

102

and widen your throat;

וְהַרְחֵב גְּרוֹנֶךָ .

103

make straight the steps of your sayings,

וְהַיְשַׁר אֲשׁוּרֵי אֲמָרֶיךָ.

104

race the chariots of your songs through the broad street of speech,

וְהָרֵץ בִּרְחוֹב הַמַּאֲמָר רִכְבֵי זְמִירֶיךָ.

105

and let the showers of your words drip down [Deut 32:2],

וְהַרְעֵף רְבִיבֵי דְבָרֶיךָ.

106

to water with your rains

לְרַוּוֹת בִּמְטָרֶיךָ.

107

the land of the plain [Deut 3:10],

אֶרֶץ הַמִּישׁוֹרָה.

108

and to lay out a resting place of splendor;

238

and the living, but for his kindness, would perish and be as if they had not been [Obad 1:16].

וְהַחַיִּים לוּלֵי חַסְדּוֹ אָבָדוּ וְהָיוּ כְלֹא הָיוּ.

239

The tongue of the days tells his praises,

לְשׁוֹן הַיָּמִים יָשִׂיחַ מַהֲלָלָיו .

240

and the mouth of time pours forth the goodness of his deeds.

וּפִי הַזְּמַן יַבִּיעַ טוּב מִפְעָלָיו.

241

Grace is turned toward him,

וְהַחֵן נָטוּי אֵלָיו.

242

and righteousness has pitched its tents in his heart,

וְהַצֶּדֶק בְּלִבּוֹ תָּקַע אֹהָלָיו.

243

and lifts its banners in his name.

וּבִשְׁמוֹ יִשָּׂא דְגָלָיו.

244

May God set him as a banner of the peoples [Isa 11:10],

הָאֵל לְנֵס עַמִּים יְשִׂימֵהוּ.

245

and make him strong above his adversaries,

וּמִשּׂוֹטְנָיו יַעֲצִימֵהוּ.

246

and lead him beside still waters [Ps 23:2],

וְעַל מֵי מְנוּחוֹת יְנַהֲלֵהוּ.

247

and lift him up and carry him [Isa 63:9].

וִינַשְּׂאֵהוּ וִינַטְּלֵהוּ.

248

And he took up his parable and said:

וַיִּשָּׂא מְשָׁלוֹ וַיֹּאמַר:

249

Beloved of God, perfect in lovely virtues, and his heart is a fountain of the testimonies.

יְדִיד הָאֵל כְּלִיל מִדּוֹת חֲמוּדוֹת אֲבָל לִבּוֹ כְמַעְיָן לַתְּעוּדוֹת.

250

Prince of God's people, and he is singular in the world, and so his praises too are singular.

נְגִיד עַם אֵל וְהוּא יָחִיד בְּתֵבֵל וְכֵן הֵמָּה תְּהִלּוֹתָיו יְחִידוֹת.

251

He has built towers of kindness and righteousness, and set his fathers' virtues as his foundations.

וּבָנָה מִגְדְּלוֹת חֶסֶד וְצֶדֶק וְשָׂם מִדּוֹת אֲבוֹתָיו לוֹ יְסוֹדוֹת.

252

And since his deeds are lovely, his name is known among God's people as a man greatly beloved [Dan 10:11].

וְיַעַן כִּי חֲמוּדוֹת הֵם פְּעָלָיו שְׁמוֹ נוֹדַע בְּעַם אֵל אִישׁ חֲמוּדוֹת.

253

The teller said: When the people heard the wonders of his thought,

אָמַר הַמַּגִּיד: וְכִשְׁמֹעַ הָעָם פְלִיאוֹת רַעְיוֹנוֹ .

254

and the marvels of his musing,

וְנוֹרְאוֹת הֶגְיוֹנוֹ.

255

and the purity of his tongue,

וְצַחוּת לְשׁוֹנוֹ.

256

they bore him on palms and on shoulders,

נְשָׂאוּהוּ עַל כַּפַּיִם וּבִכְתֵפַיִם .

257

and opened their hands to him,

וּפָתְחוּ לוֹ הַיָּדַיִם.

258

until they loaded him with the abundance of their bounty,

עַד עֲמָסוּהוּ בְּרוֹב נִדְבָתָם.

259

and he grew weary of thanking them for their goodness.

וְנִלְאָה לְהוֹדוֹת עַל טוֹבָתָם.

260

And when he journeyed from them,

וְכַאֲשֶׁר נָסַע מֵהֶם.

261

he called down peace upon them,

קָרָא שְׁלוֹמוֹ עֲלֵיהֶם.

262

and answered and said to them:

וַיַּעַן וַיֹּאמֶר לָהֶם:

263

Truly I am Ḥever, a composer⚠13 of song, for whose sake the souls of the envious grow weary.

אָמְנָם אֲנִי חֶבֶר מְחַבֵּר שִׁיר אֲשֶׁר נַפְשׁוֹת מְקַנְאִים בַּעֲדוֹ תִּיגַעְנָה.

264

I rest quiet and at ease in my house, but my eloquence travels as far as the east and the sea.

אֶשְׁקֹט וְאָנוּחַ בְּבֵיתִי אַךְ מְלִי- צוֹתַי עֲדֵי מִזְרָח וְיָם תִּסַּעְנָה.

265

I have made my words like burnished arrows in this age, and they rend the enclosure of my enemies' heart [Hos 13:8].

שַׂמְתִּים כְּמוֹ חִיצִּים מְרוּקִים בַּזְּמַן אוּלָם סְגוֹר לֵב אוֹיְבַי תִּקְרַעְנָה.

266

They go without feet as far as the east and the sea; even the everlasting mountains [Gen 49:26] they split with might.

הוֹלְכוֹת בְּלִי רֶגֶל עֲדֵי מִזְרָח וְיָם גַּם הַרֲרֵי עוֹלָם בְּעֹז תִּקְרַעְנָה.

267

I join together the tongue of Eber, of Kedar and of Aram, for all three of them bow to me in truth.

אַחְבִּיר לְשׁוֹן עֵבֶר וְקֵדָר וַאֲרָם כִּי לִי שְׁלָשְׁתָּן בֶּאֱמֶת תִּכְרַעְנָה.

268

I kindle their fire up to the skies, and day and night it rises on high and does not sink.

אַבְעִיר מְדוּרָתָם עֲדֵי שַׁחַק. וְיוֹם וָלֵיל לְרוֹם עוֹלוֹת וְלֹא תִּשְׁקַעְנָה.

269

From the radiance of my light the blind see, and the lips of the mute pour forth the goodness of my speech.

מִזִּיו מְאוֹרִי יֶחֱזוּ עִוְרִים וְשִׂפְ- תוֹת אִלְּמִים טוּב נָאֳמִי תַבַּעְנָה.

270

The souls of the men of song moan in envy against me every moment, and find no rest.

נַפְשׁוֹת מְתֵי הַשִּׁיר בְּקִנְאָה יֶהֱמוּ עָלַי בְּכָל רֶגַע וְלֹא תִרְגַּעְנָה.

271

They run after me to reach the height of my poems, and they toil, and they do not touch it.

הֵם רוֹצְצוֹת אַחְרַי לְהַשִּׂיג אֶל מְרוֹם שִׁירִי וְתִיגַעְנָה וְלֹא תַגַּעְנָה.

Uncertain renderings

  • ⚠1 הָרֵי"שׁ גָּרֵשׁ יְגָרֵשׁ — This line ends the gate's Hebrew subtitle ("In praise of an eloquent piece with a resh in every word, and the second to it"). "Utterly driven out" is Exod 11:1 (גָּרֵשׁ יְגָרֵשׁ), said there of Pharaoh sending Israel away.
  • ⚠2 דְּבַר אֵל יֵאָמַן / וטאעת אלרחמן / שְׁפַר עוֹבָדַיָּא — A poem in three languages. In each line the first part is Hebrew, the second Arabic (written in Hebrew letters, without vowels), and the third Aramaic. Ḥarizi names the three tongues below ("Aram, Eber, and Arabia"). The Arabic is rendered from the consonants; where the reading is doubtful there is a note.
  • ⚠3 לנואם אלבלקע — Read as Arabic li-nuwwām al-balqaʿ, "for the sleepers of the barren land", that is, the dead in the earth. The reading is uncertain.
  • ⚠4 סרי מנהא סהמו — Read as Arabic sarā minhā sahmuhu, "its arrow went out from them". The referent (the poem? the words?) is not clear; the reading is uncertain.
  • ⚠5 ויהדיאה אלשרקו — Read as Arabic wa-yuhdīhā al-sharqu, "and the East sends it as a gift". Ḥarizi wrote the Taḥkemoni in the East after leaving Spain. Uncertain.
  • ⚠6 וְנִקְרְצוּ קָרֶץ — Literally "were pinched (or nipped) a pinching"; compare Job 33:6 קֹרַצְתִּי, "I was pinched off from clay", and Jer 46:20 קֶרֶץ. The English keeps the image; the sense is probably that they were struck with dread. Uncertain.
  • ⚠7 בִּמְפֹרָד — Literally "in something separate". It may mean a single, set-apart feat (the letter game that follows), or a composition on one theme. Uncertain.
  • ⚠8 קְרָא בְגָרוֹן — The first composition: in the epistle and in the poem after it, every Hebrew word contains the letter resh (ר). The only exceptions are the frame words ("and he answered" here, and the lines that introduce the poem) and the word אֵל ("God") in the poem. The English does not imitate the constraint.
  • ⚠9 וְרוֹפֵא הַחֶרֶץ — חֶרֶץ is rare; it is read here from Isa 10:22 כִּלָּיוֹן חָרוּץ, "destruction decreed". A meaning "cut, wound" (from חרץ, to cut) is also possible.
  • ⚠10 רְפָאִים... רְפָאִים — A pun on two senses of רְפָאִים: "shades" of the dead (Isa 26:14), whom his favor rouses, and the "giants" (Deut 2:11), whom his wrath slays.
  • ⚠11 יְרִימוּ צוּר וְיוֹצְרוֹ יִצְּרֵהוּ / בְּצָרָתוֹ כְּמִבְצָר מִמְּגוֹר צַר — The syntax of this line is unclear. "They exalt the Rock" (or "may they exalt") and "his Maker formed him" may be two clauses, or "the Rock and his Maker" one subject. The rendering is a guess.
  • ⚠12 אֲצִילֵנוּ — The second composition: in the epistle and in the poem after it, no Hebrew word contains the letter resh. The exceptions are the frame word "and said" (וַיֹּאמַר), here and before the poem, and the word זִכְרוֹ ("his fame") in the line "The dead heard his fame and lived". The English does not imitate the constraint.
  • ⚠13 חֶבֶר מְחַבֵּר — A pun: the name Ḥever (חֶבֶר) and מְחַבֵּר, "composer" (literally "one who joins together"). This is where the old man shows who he is.
