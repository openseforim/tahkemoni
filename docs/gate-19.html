שַׁעַר תִּשְׁעָה עָשָׂר

מִשְׁבְעָה בַחוּרֵי חֶמֶד טוֹבָה. מִתְוַכְּחִים אֵיזוֹ מִדָּה טוֹבָה

The Nineteenth Gate

Seven young men by the Euphrates argue which quality is best, and an old man, Ḥever in disguise, gives the prize to generosity

Gate 19 on Project Ben-Yehuda ↗

About this text

186 lines. One row is one rhyme unit of the rhymed prose, or one line of verse (its two halves set apart). References in [gray brackets] are the translator's identifications of biblical phrases. ⚠ marks an uncertain rendering. Each line number is a link to that line. Hebrew text: Project Ben-Yehuda.

1

Thus says Heman the Ezrahite: I was in the land of Pethor,

נְאֻם הֵימָן הָאֶזְרָחִי: הָיִיתִי בְאֶרֶץ פְּתוֹר.

2

the city of Balaam the son of Beor [Num 24:3].

עיר בִּלְעָם בְּנוֹ בְעוֹר.

3

And while I was walking by the river Euphrates under the shade of the plants

וּבְעוֹדִי עַל יַד נְהַר פְּרָת מִתְהַלֵךְ תַּחַת צִלְלֵי הַצְּמָחִים.

4

and the thickets of the flowers,

וְסִבְכֵי הַפְּרָחִים.

5

I saw seven desirable young men [Ezek 23:6],

רָאִיתִי שִׁבְעָה בַחוּרֵי חֶמֶד.

6

the choicest of the company⚠1.

מִמִּבְחַר הַצֶּמֶד.

7

They were sitting on the bank of the river,

וְהֵם עַל שְׂפַת הַנָּהָר יוֹשְׁבִים.

8

and making their hearts merry with words of eloquence.

וּבְדִבְרֵי מְלִיצוֹת אֶת לִבָּם מֵטִיבִים.

9

And one of them spoke up and said: "Which is the good quality⚠2,

וַיַּעַן אִישׁ מֵהֶם וַיֹּאמַר: אֵיזוֹ הִיא הַמִּדָּה הַטוֹבָה.

10

that is loved above all the qualities,

אֲשֶׁר עַל כָּל הַמִּדּוֹת אֲהוּבָה.

11

and esteemed in the eyes of God and man?"

וּבְעֵינֵי אֱלֹהִים וְאָדָם חֲשׁוּבָה.

12

One of them said: "I know that all the qualities

אָמַר אֶחָד מֵהֶם: יָדַעְתִּי כִּי כָל הַמִּדּוֹת.

13

are lovely;

הֵם חֲמוּדוֹת.

14

but among them all there is no quality so comely

אֲבָל אֵין בְּכֻלָם מִדָּה נָאוָה.

15

as humility.

כְּמוֹ הָעֲנָוָה.

16

For it covers every blemish,

כִּי הִיא תְּכֵסֶּה כָל דֹּפִי .

17

and reveals every beauty,

וּתְגַלֶה כָל יֹפִי.

18

and passes over all transgressions,

וְתַעֲבִיר עַל כָּל הַפְּשָׁעִים.

19

and joins its owner to the modest,

וְתַחְבִּיר בְּעָלֶיהָ לַצְּנוּעִים.

20

and multiplies for him lovers and friends,

וְתַרְבֶּה לוֹ דוֹדִים וְרֵעִים.

21

and gives him for inheritance a name precious and pleasant."

וְתַנְחִילֵהוּ שֵׁם יָקָר וְנָעִים.

22

And he took up his parable and said:

וַיִּשָּׂא מְשָׁלוֹ וַיֹּאמַר:

23

Among the qualities of man there is none like humility; it is fair and comely for every man of sense.

בְּמִדּוֹת הָאֱנוֹשׁ אֵין כָּעֲנָוָה וְהִיא יָפָה לְכָל מַשְׂכִּיל וְנָאוָה.

24

It awakens love in the heart of those who hate, and hides from a man every sin and perversity.

תְּעוֹרֵר אַהֲבָה מִלֵּב מְשַׂנְאִים וְתַסְתִּיר מֵאֱנוֹשׁ כָּל חֵטְא וּמַעְוָה.

25

His companion said to him: "From the straight road you have strayed,

אָמַר לוֹ חֲבֵרוֹ: מִדֶּרֶךְ נְכוֹחָה תָּעִיתָ.

26

and you have seen wind.

וְרוּחַ רָאִיתָ.

27

Humility, or boldness⚠3, is counted as nothing

וּכְאַיִן נֶחְשָׁבָה הָעֲנָוָה אוֹ הָעַזּוּת.

28

beside zeal.

לְנֶגֶד הַזְרִיזוּת.

29

For by it a man wins souls,

כִּי בָהּ יִכְבּשׁ אָדָם הַנְפָשִׁים.

30

and finds favor in the eyes of God and men,

וְיִמְצָא חֵן בְּעֵינֵי אֱלֹהִים וַאֲנָשִׁים.

31

and inherits honor and great greatness,

וְיִנְחַל כָּבוֹד וּגְדֻלָּה רַבָּה.

32

in this world and in the world to come.

בָּעוֹלָם הַזֶּה וּבַבָּא.

33

By it he heaps up wealth that flourishes,

וּבָהּ יִצְבֹּר חַיִל יָנוּב.

34

and houses full of all good things [Deut 6:11]."

וּבָתִּים מְלֵאִים כָּל טוּב.

35

And he took up his parable and said:

וַיִּשָּׂא מְשָׁלוֹ וַיֹּאמַר:

36

Truly zeal has no equal, and happy is the man who walks in its way.

אֱמֶת כִּי הַזְּרִיזוּת אֵין כְּעֶרְכָּה וְאַשְׁרֵי אִישׁ אֲשֶׁר יֵלֵךְ בְּדַרְכָּהּ.

37

The precious qualities are all handmaids, and zeal is like a queen over them.

וּמִדּוֹת הַיְקָר כֻּלָן שְׁפָחוֹת וְלָהֶן הַזְּרִיזוּת הִיא כְמַלְכָּה.

38

His third companion said: "You have acted foolishly

אָמַר חֲבֵרוֹ הַשְׁלִישִׁי; נִסְכַּלְתָּ.

39

in what you have spoken.

בַּאֲשֶׁר מִלַּלְתָּ.

40

For there is no quality good and precious

כִּי אֵין מִדָּה טוֹבָה וִיקָרָה.

41

like stoutness of heart and might.

כְּאֹמֶץ לֵב וּגְבוּרָה.

42

For by it a man subdues all his enemies,

כִּי בָה יַכְנִיעַ אָדָם כָּל אוֹיְבָיו.

43

and does good to those who love him,

וְיֵיטִיב לְאוֹהֲבָיו.

44

and becomes joined to the great;

וְיִהְיֶה לִגְדוֹלִים נֶחְבָּר.

45

he shouts, yea, he roars, he prevails against his enemies [Isa 42:13];

וְיָרִיע אַף יַצְרִיחַ עַל אוֹיְבָיו יִתְגַבָּר.

46

and he goes up to the throne of greatness,

וְיַעֲלֶה לְכִסֵּא הַגְּדֻלָּה.

47

until they cry before him:

עֲדֵי יִקְרְאוּ לְפָנָיו.

48

'Cast up, cast up the highway [Isa 62:10]!'"

סֹלּוּ סֹלּוּ הַמְסִלָּה.

49

And he took up his parable and said:

וַיִּשָּׂא מְשָׁלוֹ וַיֹּאמַר:

50

Truly a man has no quality so precious as stoutness of heart mixed with might.

אֱמֶת אֵין לָאֱנוֹשׂ מִדָּה יְקָרָה כְּאֹמֶץ לֵב מְעֹרָב עם גְבוּרָה.

51

Is it not by it that a man subdues those who hate him, and brings them down in sorrow to the grave?

הָכִי בָהּ הָאֱנוֹשׁ יִכְבּשׁ מְשַׂנְאָיו. וְיוֹרִידֵם בְּיָגוֹן לַקְבוּרָה.

52

The fourth said: "You have strayed from the road,

אָמַר הָרְבִיעִי: תָּעִיתָ מִן הַדֶּרֶךְ.

53

and been made to serve folly with rigor [Exod 1:13].

וְנֶעֶבַדְתָּ אֶל הַסִכְלוּת בְּפֶרֶךְ.

54

For among all the qualities there is no quality so fitting

כִּי אֵין בְּכָל הַמִּדּוֹת מִדָּה הָגוּנָה.

55

as faithfulness.

כְּמוֹ הָאֱמוּנָה.

56

For by it a man lifts up his head,

כִּי בָהּ יָרִים אָדָם רֹאשׁוֹ.

57

and his soul is precious,

וְתִיקַר נַפְשׁוֹ.

58

and he is honored in the eyes of all flesh and blood,

וְיִהְיֶה נִכְבָּד בְּעֵינֵי כָל בָּשָׂר וָדָם.

59

and finds favor and good understanding in the eyes of God and man [Prov 3:4]."

וְיִמְצָא חֵן וְשֵׂכֶל טוֹב בְּעֵינֵי אֱלֹהִים וְאָדָם.

60

And he took up his parable and said:

וַיִּשָּׂא מְשָׁלוֹ וַיֹּאמַר:

61

Know that you have no quality so fitting in the eye of God as the quality of faithfulness.

דְּעֶה כִּי אֵין לְךָ מִדָה הָגוּנָה בְּעֵין הָאֵל כְּמִדַּת הָאֱמוּנָה.

62

And if prophecy had a body, faithfulness would then be its form.

וְאִלוּ לַנְּבוּאָה יִהְיֶה גוּף תְּהִי אָז הָאֱמוּנָה לָהּ תְּמוּנָה.

63

The fifth said: "There is nothing right in your mouth,

אָמַר הַחֲמִישִׁי: אֵין בְּפִיךָ נְכוֹנָה.

64

and your saying is without understanding.

וְאִמְרָתְךָ בְּלִי תְּבוּנָה.

65

For the lofty quality

כִּי הַמִּדָּה הָרָמָה.

66

is wisdom.

הִיא הַחָכְמָה.

67

It lifts up its lowly,

תָּרִים שְׁפָלֶיהָ.

68

and sets their feet upon the heads of others⚠4;

וְתִשָׂא עַל רֹאשָׁם רַגְלֶיהָ.

69

and wisdom gives life to those who have it [Eccl 7:12].

וְהַחָכְמָה תְחַיֶּה בְעָלֶיהָ.

70

Were it not for wisdom,

וְלוּלֵי הַחָכְמָה.

71

man would have no advantage over the beast [Eccl 3:19]."

לֹא הָיָה יִתְרוֹן לְאָדָם מִן הַבְּהֵמָה.

72

And he took up his parable and said:

וַיִּשָּׂא מְשָׁלוֹ וַיֹּאמַר:

73

There is in the age no quality so very lofty, and a glory to a man's head, as wisdom.

אֵין בַּזְמַן מִדָּה מְאֹד רָמָה וּפְאֵר לְראשׁ אָדָם כְּמוֹ חָכְמָה.

74

For by it a man goes up to the throne of kingship; by it too the weary find strength [Isa 40:29].

כִּי בָהּ אֱנוֹשׁ יַעַל לְכֵס מַלְכוּת גַם בָּהּ עֲיֵפִים יִמְצְאוּ עָצְמָה.

75

The sixth said: "You have seen a vain vision [Ezek 13:7],

אָמַר הַשִּׁשִׁי: מַחֲזֶה שָׁוְא חָזִיתָ.

76

and answered falsely.

וְשֶׁקֶר עָנִיתָ.

77

For there is no quality good for all flesh

כִּי אֵין מִדָּה טוֹבָה לְכָל בָּשָׂר.

78

like good conduct⚠5.

כְּמוֹ הַמּוּסָר.

79

For by good conduct a man is honored by those who know him,

כִּי בַּמּוּסָר יְכַבְּדוּ לָאָדָם יוֹדְעָיו.

80

and loved by those who hear him,

וְיֶאֱהָבוּהוּ שׁוֹמְעָיו .

81

and they cover over all his transgressions.

וִיכַסּוּ עַל כָּל פְּשָׁעָיו.

82

His memory is sweet to every mouth,

וְזִכְרוֹ מָתוֹק לְכָל פִּיוֹת.

83

and his praise is like a tower built for an armory [Song 4:4].

וּמַהֲלָלוֹ כְּמִגְדָּל בָּנוּי לְתַלְפִּיוֹת.

84

He is the delight of hearts,

וְהוּא מַחְמָד לַלְּבָבוֹת.

85

and his praises stand for all generations."

וּתְהִלוֹתָיו לְדוֹר דּוֹרִים נִצָבוֹת.

86

And he took up his parable and said:

וַיִּשָּׂא מְשָׁלוֹ וַיֹּאמַר:

87

A man has no quality like good conduct, for it is glory and splendor to all flesh.

אֵין לֶאֱנוֹשׁ מִדָּה כְּמוֹ מוּסָר כִּי הִיא פְאֵר וָהוֹד לְכָל בָּשָׂר.

88

If a man has every precious thing, but lacks good conduct, know that every precious thing is lacking.

אִם כָּל־יְקָר יִהְיֶה לְאִישׁ מֵאֵין מוּסָר. דְעֶה כִּי כָּל יְקָר יֶחְסָר.

89

The seventh said: "You trust in emptiness,

אָמַר הַשְׁבִיעי: אַתָּה עַל רִיק בָּטוּחַ.

90

pursuing the east wind and feeding on wind [Hos 12:2].

רוֹדֵף קָדִים וְרוֹעֶה רוּחַ.

91

For among all the qualities there is nothing good

כִּי אֵין בְּכָל הַמִדּוֹת דָּבָר טוֹב.

92

like the good heart.

כְּמוֹ הַלֵּב הַטּוֹב.

93

For by it a man is beloved to all creatures,

כִּי בוֹ יִהְיֶה הָאָדָם אָהוּב לַבְּרוּאִים.

94

and they set him at the head of those who are invited [1 Sam 9:22].

וְיַעֲלוּהוּ לְרֹאשׁ הַקְּרוּאִים.

95

He is honored in the eyes of those who hear and those who see; all who see him love him,

וְהוּא נִכְבָּד בְּעֵינֵי הַשּׁוֹמְעִים וְהָרוֹאִים כָּל רוֹאָיו יֶאֱהָבוּהוּ.

96

and those who hate him praise him,

וְשׂוֹנְאָיו יְשַׁבְּחוּהוּ.

97

and men laud him,

וּבְנֵי אָדָם יְהַלְלוּהוּ.

98

and the angels of heaven remember him for good."

וּמַלְאֲכֵי שַׁחַק לְטוֹבָה יִזְכְּרוּהוּ.

99

And he took up his parable and said:

וַיִּשָּׂא מְשָׁלוֹ וַיֹּאמַר:

100

Truly there is no quality like a good heart; by it a man flourishes like a watered garden.

אֵין בֶּאֱמֶת מִדָּה כְמוֹ לֵב טוֹב בָּהּ הָאֱנוֹשׁ יִפְרַח כְּגַן רָטֹב.

101

For by it he is beloved to his Rock as well; he finds in His eyes favor and good understanding [Prov 3:4].

כִּי בוֹ יְהִי אָהוּב לְצוּרוֹ גַם. יִמְצָא בְעֵינָיו חֵן וְשֵׂכֶל טוֹב.

102

When the old man⚠6 heard their words,

וְכִשְׁמֹעַ הַזְקֵן דִּבְרֵיהֶם.

103

he said to them: "You are all perplexed,

אָמַר לָהֶם: כֻּלְכֶם נְבוּכִים.

104

and walkers in darkness;

וְהוֹלְכִי חֲשֵׁכִים.

105

the right answer is hidden from you,

וְהַנְּכוֹחָה מִכֶּם נֶעְלֶמֶת.

106

and you did not know how to choose the truth.

וְלֹא יְדַעְתֶּם לבְחֹר הָאֱמֶת.

107

For all the qualities bow down at its feet,

כִּי כָל הַמִּדּוֹת לרַגְלֶיהָ תִכְרַעֲנָה.

108

and it has risen above them all [Prov 31:29].

וְהִיא עָלְתָה עַל כֻּלָּנָה.

109

By it all guilt is atoned,

בָּהּ יִתְכַּפְּרוּ כָּל הָאֲשָׁמוֹת.

110

and enmities depart from the heart.

יָסוּרוּ מִן הַלֵּב הַמַּשְׂטְמוֹת.

111

By it a man reaches his far-off desires⚠7,

וּבָהּ יַשִׂיג אִישׁ חֲפָנָיו הָרְחוֹקִים.

112

even if they were in the heavens.

וְאִלּוּ הָיוּ בַשְׁחָקִים.

113

By it he is counted among the pious,

Uncertain renderings

  • ⚠1 מִמִּבְחַר הַצֶּמֶד — צֶמֶד is a pair or yoke. Read here as "the choicest of their company", rhyming with חֶמֶד; the exact sense is uncertain.
  • ⚠2 אֵיזוֹ הִיא הַמִּדָּה הַטוֹבָה — The question echoes m. Avot 2:9, where Rabban Yoḥanan ben Zakkai asks his five disciples which is the good way a man should cleave to; there too one answer is "a good heart". "Quality" renders מִדָּה, a trait of character.
  • ⚠3 הָעֲנָוָה אוֹ הָעַזּוּת — Later in this gate עַזּוּת is impudence (zeal without generosity turns to עַזּוּת). Here it may mean boldness or strength; the speaker sets zeal above both humility and its opposite.
  • ⚠4 וְתִשָׂא עַל רֹאשָׁם רַגְלֶיהָ — Literally "and it lifts its feet upon their head". Read as wisdom setting the lowly above others; it can also mean that wisdom treads on the heads of the haughty.
  • ⚠5 הַמּוּסָר — מוּסָר is discipline, moral training and good manners together (Arabic adab). "Good conduct" renders it in this speech and its poem.
  • ⚠6 הַזְקֵן — No old man is brought in before this line: the text goes from the seventh youth's poem straight to "the old man". He is Ḥever in disguise, as the end of the gate shows. A line that introduced him may have dropped out, or the author leaves him unannounced.
  • ⚠7 חֲפָנָיו הָרְחוֹקִים — The edition prints חֲפָנָיו, "his handfuls", which gives little sense. Translated as if חֲפָצָיו, "his desires" (one letter different).
  • ⚠8 הֲלֹא מֵהַנְדָבָה הִיא גְנוּבָה — That is, every other quality borrows its worth from generosity, as the old man's speech said.
  • ⚠9 אֲנִי חֶבֶר מְחַבֵּר — A pun on the name חֶבֶר (Ḥever) and מְחַבֵּר, "composer", from the same root ("to join").
  • ⚠10 לְהָרִיחַ בָּשְׁמוֹ — The edition points בָּשְׁמוֹ. Read as בְּשָׂמוֹ, "his perfume" (with "to breathe in"), with a play on בִּשְׁמוֹ, "in his name": the scent of his fame.
