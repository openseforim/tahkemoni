שַׁעַר חֲמִישִׁי

בְּשֶׁבַח י"ב מְשׁוֹרְרִים, אֲשֶׁר כָּל אֶחָד עַל חֹדֶשּ אֶחָד קוֹלוֹ יָרִים.

The Fifth Gate

A company of friends in spring: twelve poets each praise one month of the year, from Nisan to Adar, and the drinkers of Damascus learn their praises by heart

Gate 5 on Project Ben-Yehuda ↗

About this text

381 lines. One row is one rhyme unit of the rhymed prose, or one line of verse (its two halves set apart). References in [gray brackets] are the translator's identifications of biblical phrases. ⚠ marks an uncertain rendering. Each line number is a link to that line. Hebrew text: Project Ben-Yehuda. Passages missing there are added from Kaminka's edition (Warsaw 1899), unvocalized, and marked.

1

Thus says Heman the Ezrahite: I was in a gathering of friends,

נְאֻם הֵימָן הָאֶזְרָחִי: הָיִיתִי בְּמַעֲמַד יְדִידִים.

2

lovely ones,

חֲמוּדִים.

3

held fast in the hand of beauty,

בְּיַד הַיֳפִי צְמוּדִים.

4

and they became one [Ezek 37:17].

וְהָיוּ לַאֲחָדִים.

5

All of them were nobles and princes;

כֻּלָּם אֲצִילִים וּנְגִידִים.

6

from their tongues they scattered princely words,

יָפִיצוּ מִלְּשׁוֹנָם אִמְרֵי נְגִידִים.

7

and their eloquence was like sparks of burning coals,

וּמְלִיצוֹתָם כְּרִשְׁפֵּי יְקוֹדִים.

8

burning like the appearance of torches [Ezek 1:13].

בּוֹעֲרוֹת כְּמַרְאֵה הַלַּפִּידִים.

9

And the time was the month of Aviv [Exod 13:4],

וְהָיָה הַזְּמַן חֹדֶשׁ הָאָבִיב.

10

that is, the month of Nisan, the beloved and the dear.

הוּא חֹדֶשׁ נִיסָן הָאָהוּב וְהֶחָבִיב.

11

From the tears of the dew there were showers on the cheek of the lily,

וּמִדִּמְעוֹת הַטַּל בִּלְחִי הַשּׁוֹשָׁן רָבִיב.

12

and the garden had a binding of embroidered lilies round about its opening [Exod 28:32].

וְהַגַּן מֵרִקְמַת הַשּׁוֹשַׁנִּים שָׂפָה יִהְיֶה לְפִיו סָבִיב.

13

The earth had put on her embroidered garments of blossoms,

וְהָאֲדָמָה לָבְשָׁה מִן הַצִּצִּים בִּגְדֵי רִקְמָתָהּ.

14

and decked herself with her ring and her jewels [Hos 2:15].

וַתַּעַד נִזְמָהּ וְחֶלְיָתָהּ.

15

The lily, like a lamp, shone before her eyes,

וְהַשּׁוֹשָׁן כַּנֵּר יַזְהִיר לְעֵינֶיהָ.

16

and gave light over against her face [Exod 25:37],

וְהֵאִיר אֶל עֵבֶר פָּנֶיהָ.

17

and upon her gardens,

וְעַל גַנֶּיהָ.

18

her flowers and her buds,

פְּרָחֶיהָ וְנִצָּנֶיהָ.

19

and upon her meadows, her joys;

וְעַל שָׁרוֹנֶיהָ שְׂשׂוֹנֶיהָ.

20

and greenery greened in her face [Num 12:14]⚠1.

וְיָרָק יָרַק בְּפָנֶיהָ.

21

The spices of the garden were drawn to our noses;

וּבָשְׂמֵי הַגַּן לְאַפֵּינוּ יִמָּשֵׁכוּ.

22

wherever the wind would go, they went [Ezek 1:12].

אֶל אֲשֶׁר יִהְיֶה שָׁמָּה הָרוּחַ לָלֶכֶת יֵלֵכוּ.

23

The sun increased her light and was revealed,

וְהַחַמָּה הוֹסִיפָה אוֹרָהּ וַיִּגָּלֶה.

24

and encamped in the sign of the Ram.

וְחָנְתָה בְּמַזַּל טָלֶה.

25

The plants came out from the depths of the earth,

וְהַצְּמָחִים יָצְאוּ מֵעִמְקֵי אָרֶץ.

26

and broke through a breach,

וּפָרְצוּ פָרֶץ.

27

and appointed themselves one head, and went up out of the land [Hos 2:2],

וְשָׂמוּ לָהֶם רֹאשׁ אֶחָד וְעָלוּ מִן הָאָרֶץ.

28

and covered the face of the world with spreads of delight.

וְכִסּוּ פְּנֵי תֵבֵל בְּמִפְרְשֵׂי נֹעַם.

29

The cloud commanded the messengers of the thunder to bring good tidings to the earth;

וְהָעָב צֻוְּתָה לְבַשֵּׂר הָאֲדָמָה שְׁלוּחֵי הָרַעַם.

30

she sent and called Barak the son of Abinoam [Judg 4:6]⚠2,

וַתִּשְׁלַח וַתִּקְרָא לְבָרָק בֶּן אֲבִינֹעַם.

31

and said to him: "Draw your sword and your quiver upon the earth;

וַתֹּאמֶר לוֹ: שְׁלֹף עַל הָאָרֶץ חַרְבְּךָ וְתֶלְיְךָ.

32

arise, Barak, and lead your captives captive [Judg 5:12],

קוּם בָּרָק וּשְׁבֵה שֶׁבְיְךָ.

33

and go, bring the earth tidings that I have set my host in array against her."

וְלֵךְ לְבַשֵּׂר הָאֲדָמָה כִּי חֵילִי עָלֶיהָ עָרַכְתִּי.

34

And Barak said to her: "If you will go with me, I will go [Judg 4:8]."

וַיֹּאמֶר אֵלֶיהָ בָּרָק: אִם תֵּלְכִי עִמִּי וְהָלַכְתִּי.

35

She said to him: "I will not let my hand go from you;

וַתֹּאמֶר לוֹ: לֹא אֶרֶף יָדִי מִמְּךָ.

36

I will surely go with you [Judg 4:9]."

וְהָלוֹךְ אֵלֵךְ עִמְּךָ.

37

The thunders lifted up their voice, and the earth answered,

וַיִּשְׂאוּ קוֹלָם הָרְעָמִים וְהָאָרֶץ תַּעֲנֶה.

38

and the troop of the rain encamped for her battle,

וּגְדוּד הַגֶּשֶׁם לְמִלְחַמְתָּהּ חוֹנֶה.

39

and Barak pursued after the chariots and after the host [Judg 4:16].

וּבָרָק רָדַף אַחֲרֵי הָרֶכֶב וְאַחֲרֵי הַמַּחֲנֶה.

40

The garden put on her garments of beauty,

וְהַגַּנָּה לָבְשָׁה בִּגְדֵי יָפְיָהּ.

41

and the lily, for joy at the layer of dew [Exod 16:13] that had revived her,

וְהַשּׁוֹשַׁנָּה מִשִּׁמְחָתָהּ בְּשִׁכְבַת הַטַּל אֲשֶׁר הֶחְיָהּ.

42

weeps sore in the night, and her tear is on her cheek [Lam 1:2].

בָּכוֹ תִבְכֶּה בַּלַּיְלָה וְדִמְעָתָהּ עַל לֶחְיָהּ.

43

The garden looked out like a maiden from the windows of her palaces,

וְהַגַּנָּה הִשְׁקִיפָה כְעַלְמָה מֵאֶשְׁנַבֵּי הֵיכָלֶיהָ.

44

and put her veil off from upon her [Gen 38:19];

וַתָּסַר צְעִיפָהּ מֵעָלֶיהָ.

45

her robes were of chequered gold [Ps 45:14],

וּמִשְׁבְּצוֹת זָהָב מְעִילֶיהָ.

46

the perfumes of myrrh were on her hems,

וּמֶרְקְחוֹת הַמּוֹר בְּשׁוּלֶיהָ.

47

and the tongues of the myrtle were her talebearers,

וּלְשׁוֹנוֹת הַהֲדַס רְכִילֶיהָ.

48

for with every mouth they went about telling of her.

כִּי בְכָל פִיּוֹת יְרַגְּלוּ עָלֶיהָ.

49

And the wine revived the bones [Isa 58:11]

וְהַיַּיִן יַחֲלִיץ הָעֲצָמוֹת.

50

after death [Lev 16:1],

אַחֲרֵי מוֹת.

51

and gladdened the souls that were desolate⚠3.

וִישַׂמַּח הַנְּשָׁמוֹת הַנְשַׁמּוֹת.

52

And with us was the head of the sages and the geonim,

וְהָיָה עִמָּנוּ רֹאשׁ הַחֲכָמִים וְהַגְּאוֹנִים.

53

who takes away the speech of the trusted [Job 12:20],

וּמֵסִיר שָׂפָה לְנֶאֱמָנִים.

54

and with his mind takes away the discernment of the elders [Job 12:20],

וְלוֹקֵחַ בְּשִׂכְלוֹ טַעַם זְקֵנִים.

55

of the seed of the Kenites:

מִזֶּרַע הַקֵּינִים.

56

he was our friend, Ḥever the Kenite,

הוּא חֲבֵרֵנוּ חֶבֶר הַקֵּינִי.

57

who strings rows of sapphires

הַמְחַבֵּר טוּרֵי הַסַּפִּירִים.

58

on the necks of books,

בְּצַוְּארֵי הַסְּפָרִים.

59

and draws out pearls

וּמוֹשֶׁה הַפְּנִינִים.

60

from the deeps of thought.

מִמְּצוּלֹות הָרַעְיוֹנִים.

61

He answered and said to them: "You are like a tree of life, and how good is your shade!

וַיַּעַן וַיֹּאמֶר לָהֶם: הִנְּכֶם כְּעֵץ חַיִּים וּמַה טּוֹב צִלְּכֶם.

62

At the throne of glory you are standing this day, all of you [Deut 29:9].

וְעַל־כִּסֵּא הַיְקָר אַתֶּם נִצָּבִים הַיּוֹם כֻּלְּכֶם.

63

The days shine by the light of your faces,

וְהַיָּמִים בְּאוֹר פְּנֵיכֶם יָאִירוּ.

64

and gleam like the brightness of the firmament [Dan 12:3].

וּכְזֹהַר הָרָקִיעַ יַזְהִירוּ.

65

The times have made their faces shine today,

וְהַזְּמַנִּים צָהֲלוּ הַיּוֹם פְּנֵיהֶם.

66

and their eyes are opened [Gen 3:7].

וְנִפְקְחוּ עֵינֵיהֶם.

67

Now let men of valor be armed from among you [Num 31:3],

וְעַתָּה הֵחָלְצוּ מֵאִתְּכֵם גִּבּוֹרֵי חָיִל.

68

like the stars of the night,

כְּכוֹכְבֵי לָיִל.

69

masters of mind and understanding,

בַּעֲלֵי שֵׂכֶל וּתְבוּנָה.

70

as many as the months of the year,

וּמְסַפְּרִים בְּחָדְשֵׁי הַשָּׁנָה.

71

and let each of them praise his month,

וְכָל אֶחָד מֵהֶם יְשַׁבַּח חָדְשׁוֹ.

72

and set a crown of praises on its head.

וְיָשֵׂם נֵזֶר תְּהִלּוֹת עַל רֹאשׁוֹ.

73

Let the first begin by praising the glorious month of Nisan,

יַתְחִיל הִראשׁוֹן לְשַׁבֵּחַ חֹדֶשׁ נִיסָן הַנֶּאְדָּר.

74

and let the last complete it with the month of Adar.

וְיַשְׁלִים הָאַחֲרוֹן בְּחֹדֶשׁ אֲדָר.

75

By this the power of your poetry will be known,

וּבַדָּבָר הַזֶּה יִוָּדַע כֹּחַ שִׁירְכֶם.

76

and your words will be proved,

וְיִבָּחֲנוּ דִבְרֵיכֶם.

77

whether there is truth in you [Gen 42:16]."

הַאֱמֶת אִתְּכֶם.

78

All the people said to him: "You have begun to show your servant your deep wisdom

אָמְרוּ לוֹ כָל הָעָם: אַתָּה הַחִלּוֹתָ לְהַרְאוֹת אֶת עַבְדְּךָ חָכְמָתְךָ הָעֲמֻקָּה.

79

and your strong hand [Deut 3:24].

וְאֶת יָדְךָ הַחֲזָקָה.

80

Now begin you with the eloquence of your sayings,

וְעַתָּה הָחֵל אַתָּה בִּמְלִיצוֹת אֲמָרֶיךָ.

81

and we will come after you, and fill out your words."

וַאֲנַחְנוּ נָבוֹא אַחֲרֶיךָ. וּמִלְּאֵנוּ אֶת דְּבָרֶיךָ.

82

And Ḥever the Kenite came near and said:

וַיִּגַּשׁ חֶבֶר הַקֵּינִי וַיֹּאמַר:

83

"The month of Nisan is the head of heads,

חֹדֶשׁ נִיסָן רֹאשׁ הָרָאשִׁים.

84

and king over the months;

וּמֶלֶךְ עַל הֶחֳדָשִׁים.

85

for in it the time is hallowed and the world made new,

כִּי בוֹ הַזְּמַן מְקֻדָּשׁ וְהָעוֹלָם מְחֻדָּשׁ.

86

and there arose a new king [Exod 1:8].

וַיָּקָם מֶלֶךְ חָדָשׁ.

87

In it the swallows chirp,

וּבוֹ יְצַפְצְפוּ הַסִּיסִים.

88

and the dewdrops drip [Song 5:2],

וְיִטְּפוּ הָרְסִיסִים.

89

and the myrtles blossom.

וְיִפְרְחוּ הַהֲדַסִּים.

90

Time's dawns gleam,

וְהַזְּמַן יַזְהִירוּ שְׁחָרָיו.

91

and its lights shine,

וְיָאִירוּ מְאוֹרָיו.

92

and it renews its youth like the eagle [Ps 103:5].

וִיחַדֵּשׁ כַּנֶּשֶׁר נְעוּרָיו.

93

It puts off from itself the black sackcloth,

וְיָסִיר מֵעָלָיו הַשַּׂקִּים הַשְּׁחוֹרִים.

94

and takes off its garments and puts on other garments [Lev 6:4],

וּפָשַׁט אֶת בְּגָדָיו וְלָבַשׁ בְּגָדִים אֲחֵרִים.

95

and bathes in pure water.

וְרָחַץ בְּמַיִם טְהוֹרִים.

96

After its old age and length of days,

וְאַחֲרֵי זִקְנָתוֹ וְאֹרֶךְ יָמָיו.

97

it returns to the days of its youth [Job 33:25].

יָשׁוּב לִימֵי עֲלוּמָיו.

98

Then the gardens blossom,

וְאָז הַגַּנִּים יִפְרָחוּ.

99

and the nights shine,

וְהַלֵּילוֹת יִזְרָחוּ.

100

and the sorrows flee,

וְהַיְגוֹנִים יִבְרָחוּ.

101

and the joys spring up,

וְהַשְּׂשׂוֹנִים יִצְמָחוּ.

102

and the lilies rejoice.

וְהַשּׁוֹשַׁנִּים יִשְׂמָחוּ.

103

The earth brings forth her growth [Isa 61:11],

וְהָאָרֶץ תּוֹצִיא צִמְחָהּ.

104

and uncovers her flower,

וּתְגַלֶּה פִּרְחָהּ.

105

and yields her strength again [Gen 4:12].

וְתוֹסֵף תֵּת כֹּחָהּ.

106

And after the earth has been wedded to autumn,

וַאֲחֵרי הֱיוֹת הָאֲדָמָה לַסְּתָו בְּעוּלָה.

107

and joined as a bride on the bed of love,

וּבְעֶרֶשׂ דּוֹדִים כְּלוּלָה.

108

she divorces her husband, who is old and stale [Lev 26:10],

תְּגָרֵשׁ בַּעְלָהּ אֲשֶׁר נוֹשָׂן יָשָׁן.

109

and longs for the month of Nisan,

וְתִכְסֹף לְחֹדֶשׁ נִיסָן.

110

who is the delight of every eye,

אֲשֶׁר הוּא מַחְמַד כָּל אִישׁוֹן.

111

and says: 'I will go and return to my first husband [Hos 2:9].'

וְאָמְרָה אֵלְכָה וְאָשִׁיבָה אֶל אִישִׁי הָרִאשׁוֹן.

112

And after she has been divorced from the season of cold that was with her,

וְאַחֲרֵי נִתְגָּרְשָׁה מִזְּמַן הַקֹּר אֲשֶׁר הָיָה אִתָּהּ.

113

her first husband, who sent her away, may return to take her [Deut 24:4]⚠4.

Uncertain renderings

  • ⚠1 וְיָרָק יָרַק בְּפָנֶיהָ — A pun. In Num 12:14 יָרֹק יָרַק בְּפָנֶיהָ means "(her father) spat in her face"; here יָרָק is "greenery" and יָרַק is read as "grew green".
  • ⚠2 לְבָרָק בֶּן אֲבִינֹעַם — Barak, the judge of Judg 4–5, is also בָּרָק "lightning". The cloud is Deborah, the thunder and rain are her army, and the scene borrows the verses of Judg 4–5.
  • ⚠3 הַנְּשָׁמוֹת הַנְשַׁמּוֹת — A play on two words of nearly the same letters: נְשָׁמוֹת "souls" and נְשַׁמּוֹת "desolate".
  • ⚠4 יוּכַל בַּעְלָהּ הָרִאשׁוֹן אֲשֶׁר שִׁלְּחָהּ לָשׁוּב לְקַחְתָּהּ — Deut 24:4 says the first husband "may not" (לֹא יוּכַל) take back his divorced wife after she has married another. The negation is dropped: Nisan, the earth's first husband, may take her back from winter.
  • ⚠5 מִידֵי לוּדִים — The Ludim are a son of Mizraim (Egypt) in Gen 10:13; here they stand for the Egyptians, for the rhyme.
  • ⚠6 וְהַקֶּרֶב וְהַכְּרָעַיִם יִרְחַץ בַּמַּיִם — Lev 1:9 (the burnt offering) and Lev 6:21 (the vessel scoured after a sin offering): the drowning of Pharaoh is told in the language of washing a sacrifice.
  • ⚠7 וְרֵיקָם הֱשִׁיבַנִי אֲדֹנַי — Ruth 1:21 has ה' (the Lord); here the cup says אֲדֹנַי, pointed as the divine name, but meaning the drinker, "my lord", who has emptied it.
  • ⚠8 וְרָם נִסָּן — Read as נֵס "banner" with a suffix, "their banner", made to rhyme with נִיסָן. Uncertain.
  • ⚠9 כִּי בוֹ רִפְאוּת אֲנָשִׁים — Iyyar was held to be a month of healing; a later tradition reads its letters as the initials of אֲנִי ה' רֹפְאֶךָ (Exod 15:26). The feminine "which cheers" (הַמְשַׂמַּחַת) seems to go with "healing"; in Judg 9:13 the words are said of wine.
  • ⚠10 שְׁקַעֲרוּרוֹת יְרַקְרְקּוֹת אוֹ אֲדַמְדַּמּוֹת — Lev 14:37 describes the marks of leprosy in the walls of a house; here the same words describe the green and red patches of new growth on the ground.
  • ⚠11 רֹאשׁ הַשָּׁלִישִׁים — שָׁלִישִׁים are "captains" or "officers" (Exod 14:7, 2 Kgs 9:25), but the word also means "third ones": Sivan is the third month, and its praise is given by the third poet.
  • ⚠12 מְכֻסּוֹת דַּר — Kaminka's edition (p. 59) reads מכסות אור, "covered with light", for מְכֻסּוֹת דַּר, "covered with pearl"; the translation follows the Ben-Yehuda text. The Ben-Yehuda text of this gate ends after this line; the rest of the gate is taken from Kaminka (see note 13).
  • ⚠13 כי הנפשות בחזות הכוס — This passage is missing from the Ben-Yehuda text and is taken from Kaminka's edition (Warsaw 1899, pp. 59–69), unvocalized. It runs from the second line of the poem on Sivan to the end of the gate.
  • ⚠14 יושבות מבכות את התמוז — Ezek 8:14 tells of women "weeping for the Tammuz", a Mesopotamian god; here the congregations weep in the month Tammuz, over the breaking of the tablets and the breach of Jerusalem's wall (both on 17 Tammuz, m. Taanit 4:6).
  • ⚠15 כי יומי ארוך — Kaminka prints יומי, which reads as a poetic form of "its days"; the summer days of Av are long.
  • ⚠16 והשקדים משקדים — A pun: מְשֻׁקָּדִים in Exod 25:33 are the almond-shaped cups of the lampstand; here the almonds are "almonded", in bloom. Each pair in this line rhymes the fruit with its praise.
  • ⚠17 ויש לנו אב זקן — Gen 44:20, the brothers to Joseph: "we have an old father". אָב is also the month Av, which has grown old with mourning. The 1,150 years since the destruction of the Temple (70 CE) give about 1220, the time al-Ḥarizi wrote the book. The "father" and the "wise son" (wine) play on the month's name through the rest of the passage; Kaminka's footnote explains אב as "the month Av".
  • ⚠18 והיגון מקלטת — Kaminka prints מקלטת clearly; the word is taken as "takes in, absorbs" (קלט). Perhaps a misprint of מלקטת, "gathers up".
  • ⚠19 ופרע ... והשתחוה לבול — Kaminka prints ופרע; the translation follows the parallel "worship" and "do homage" (perhaps read וכרע, "bow down"). Isa 44:19 mocks the man who bows "to a block of wood" (לְבוּל עֵץ); here בּוּל is the fruit of the field, and also the old name of the eighth month (1 Kgs 6:38).
  • ⚠20 הימים הפסולים והכשרים — The calendar rules by which certain feasts may not fall on certain weekdays (for example, Rosh Hashanah not on Sunday, Wednesday or Friday) are fixed from Tishri.
  • ⚠21 כי אש יצאה מחשבון — A pun: חֶשְׁבּוֹן in Num 21:28 is the city Heshbon; here it is the "account" a man gives of his deeds. Kaminka prints בציד; read בציר, "in pangs" (ד and ר are close in this print).
  • ⚠22 וכוסה ראויה — Kaminka's print can be read וכוסה or ובוסה; the sense needs וכוסה, "its cup". ראויה ("fitting") may stand for רְוָיָה, "overflowing" (Ps 23:5).
  • ⚠23 כי הוא הציר והצרי — צִיר "pang" and צֳרִי "balm" have the same letters.
  • ⚠24 כי בה תפלם לך נתיבות גיל בידי יגונות הזמן ענן — Kaminka prints תפלם, taken as תְּפַלֵּס "level" (Prov 4:26, 5:6); עִנָּן, "afflicted them", is rendered freely as "made hard". Uncertain.
  • ⚠25 כושית מבנות חם לובשת מעשה פחם — The Cushite woman is the fire of coals in the brazier: black as coal, "of the daughters of Ham" (חָם, also "hot"), and fighting the cold.
  • ⚠26 זה ינחמנו ממעשינו ומעצבון ידינו — Gen 5:29, Lamech naming Noah. Noah planted the first vineyard (Gen 9:20), so wine is the comfort that was foretold.
  • ⚠27 תכבדהו — Kaminka prints תכבדהו ("honor it"); the sense needs תכבהו, "quench it", and the translation follows that.
  • ⚠28 וחשוב דמיהם כמו נפש ולהם רוחך גו — The red wine is the "blood" of the cups (Kaminka's footnote), and "the blood is the soul" (Deut 12:23); your own spirit is only the body (גֵּו) that holds it.
  • ⚠29 שבטך בימי שבט — שֵׁבֶט "rod" is also the month שְׁבָט.
  • ⚠30 למדוה הקהלה — Kaminka's print looks like למרוה or למרזה; read לְמָדוּהָ, "they learned it", which the rest of the line requires (ד and ר, ו and ז are close in this print).
