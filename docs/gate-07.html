שער שביעי

מִשְּׁנֵי גִבּוֹרִים, הַנִּלְחָמִים כִּשְׁנֵי כְפִירִים

The Seventh Gate

Two champions at war: a battle between armies seen by travellers caught between them, and the boasts of two warriors who meet in single combat

Gate 7 on Project Ben-Yehuda ↗

About this text

174 lines. One row is one rhyme unit of the rhymed prose, or one line of verse (its two halves set apart). References in [gray brackets] are the translator's identifications of biblical phrases. ⚠ marks an uncertain rendering. Each line number is a link to that line. Hebrew text: Project Ben-Yehuda.

1

Thus says Heman the Ezrahite: I journeyed from Tyre the crowning city [Isa 23:8]

נְאֻם הֵימָן הָאֶזְרָחִי: נָסַעְתִּי מִצּוֹר הַמַּעֲטִירָה.

2

to Shushan the capital [Esth 1:2].

לְשׁוּשָׁן הַבִּירָה.

3

And with me was Ḥever the Kenite, gladdening hearts with the sweetness of his riddles,

וְעִמִּי חֶבֶר הַקֵּינִי מְשַׂמֵּחַ הַלְּבָבוֹת בְּנֹעַם חִידוֹתָיו.

4

and gladdening souls with the best of his delights.

וּמְשַׂמֵּחַ הַנְּפָשׁוֹת בְּמֵיטַב חֲמֻדוֹתָיו.

5

He began to tell me of what his eyes had seen

וְהֵחֵל לְסַפֵּר לִי מֵאֲשֶר רָאוּ עֵינָיו.

6

in the length of his days and his years.

בְּאֹרֶךְ יָמָיו וְשָׁנָיו.

7

He said to me: "In the days of my youth I journeyed from the land of Persia

וַיֹּאמֶר לִי : נָסַעְתִּי בִּימֵי נְעוַּרי מֵאֶרֶץ פָּרַס.

8

to the lands of Meshech and Tiras [Gen 10:2],

לְאַרְצוֹת מֶשֶךְ וְתִירָס.

9

with men of valor, mighty men,

עְם אַנְשֵׁי חַיִל גִּבּוֹרִים.

10

and noble princes,

וְשָׂרִים אַדִּירִים.

11

highborn and pure,

אֲצִילִים וּבָרִים.

12

who had the grace of fawns

לָהֶם חֵן עֳפָרִים.

13

and the hearts of young lions.

וְלִבּוֹת כְּפִירִים.

14

And when we passed by the tents of Kedar [Song 1:5],

וכַאֲשֶׁר עָבַרְנוּ עַל אָהֳלֵי קֵדָר.

15

we found the breach of the roads fenced up,

מָצָאנוּ פֶרֶץ הַדְּרָכִים נִגְדָּר.

16

and the deserts, for fear of the bandits, were full of thorn and thistle [Gen 3:18].

וְהַמִּדְבָּרוֹת מִן הַלִּסְטִים מָלְאוּ קוֹץ וְדַרְדָּר.

17

We came upon no way out and no crossing,

וְלֹא פָגַעְנוּ מוֹצָא וּמַעֲבָר.

18

and the wilderness had shut us in [Exod 14:3].

וְסָגַר עָלֵינוּ הַמִּדְבָּר.

19

For the men of those lands

כִּי אַנְשֵׁי הָאֲרָצוֹת הָהֵם.

20

had a war among them,

הָיְתָה מִלְחָמָה בֵּינֵיהֶם.

21

until the highways were desolate for fear of them,

עַד שָׁמְמוּ מִפַּחְדָּם הַמְּסִלּות.

22

and the paths that had been wedded became virgins again⚠1,

וְהָיוּ הַנְּתִיבוֹת הַבְּעוּלוֹת בְּתוּלוֹת.

23

and the roads turned to mourning, with none passing through [Lam 1:4],

וְשָׁבוּ הַדְּרָכִים בְּלִי עוֹבֵר אֲבֵלוֹת.

24

and those who walked the paths went by crooked ways [Judg 5:6].

וְהוֹלְכֵי נְתִיבוֹת יֵלְכוּ אֳרָחוֹת עֲקַלְקַלּוֹת.

25

And the elders of our company said to us:

וַיֹּאמְרוּ לָנוּ זִקְנֵי חֲבוּרָתֵנוּ.

26

'We will not move from our places

לֹא נָמוּשׁ מִמְּקוֹמוֹתֵינוּ.

27

until the war is quiet,

עַד תִּשְׁקֹט הַמִּלְחָמָה.

28

and the fire of wrath and fury is quenched;

וְתִכְבֶּה אֵש הַזַּעַם וְהַחֵמָה.

29

and then we will wash our steps in butter [Job 29:6]⚠2,

וְאָז נִרְחַץ הֲלִיכֵינוּ בְחֵמָה.

30

and we can journey with a high hand [Exod 14:8].'

וְנוּכַל לִנְסֹעַ בְּיָד רָמָה.

31

So we stayed some days in their dwellings,

וְעָמַדְנוּ יָמִים בִּמְעוֹנֵיהֶם.

32

to see the end of the enmity between them.

לִרְאוֹת סוֹף הָאֵיבָה בֵּינֵיהֶם.

33

And at the end of ten days we heard a voice in Ramah [Jer 31:14],

וּלְמִקְצָת יָמִים עֲשָׂרָה שָׁמַעְנוּ קוֹל בָּרָמָה.

34

and the city in uproar [1 Kgs 1:41],

וְהַקִּרְיָה הוֹמָה.

35

and the troops going out to set the battle in array

וְהַגְּדוּדִים יוֹצְאִים לַעֲרֹךְ מִלְחָמָה.

36

with a high hand.

בְּיָד רָמָה.

37

We heard, and our heart melted [Josh 2:11],

וַנִּשְׁמַע וַיִּמַּס לְבָבֵנוּ.

38

and the children of our thoughts trembled,

וְחָרְדוּ בְּנֵי מַחֲשָׁבֵנוּ.

39

for we did not know how the war would end for the two of them,

כִּי לֹא יָדַעְנוּ אֵיךְ תִּהְיֶה סוֹף הַמִּלְחָמָה לִשְׁנֵיהֶם.

40

and we were shut in between them,

וַאֲנַחְנוּ עֲצוּרִים בֵּינֵיהֶם.

41

as if they were lions and we the prey between their teeth⚠3.

כְּאִלוּ הֵם אֲרָיוֹת וַאֲנַחְנוּ טְרֵפָה בֵּין שִׁנֵּיהֶם.

42

And when they had all gathered in the valley of Baca [Ps 84:7],

וְכַאֲשֶׁר נוֹעֲדוּ כֻּלָּם בְּעֵמֶק הַבָּכָא.

43

they set the battle in array, these facing those,

עָרְכוּ אֵלֶּה נֹכַח אֵלֶּה מַעֲרָכָה.

44

all of them masters of might and strength;

וְכֻלָּם בַּעֲלֵי גְבוּרָה וְעָצְמָה.

45

in their necks lodges strength [Job 41:14], and a mane⚠4.

בְּצַוָּארָם יָלִין עֹז וְרַעְמָה.

46

Some of them were of the sons of Sabtah and Raamah [Gen 10:7],

קְצָתָם מִבְּנֵי סַבְתָּא וְרַעְמָה.

47

and some of the sons of Togarmah [Gen 10:3],

וּקְצָתָם מִבְּנֵי תּוֹגַרְמָה.

48

and Hadad and Tema joined them,

וְנִלְווּ עֲלֵיהֶם חֲדַד וְתֵימָא.

49

Jetur, Naphish and Kedemah [Gen 25:15],

יְטוּר נָפִישׁ וָקֵדְמָה.

50

and Mishma and Dumah [Gen 25:14];

וּמִשְׁמָע וְדוּמָה.

51

and among them all our soul dwelt in silence [Ps 94:17]⚠5.

וּבֵין כֻּלָּם שָׁכְנָה נַפְשֵׁנוּ דּוּמָה.

52

They set their ranks in array,

וַיַעַרְכוּ מַעַרְכוֹתֵיהֶם.

53

and put on their coats of mail,

וְלָבְשׁוּ שִׁרְיוֹנוֹתֵיהֶם.

54

and polished their spears [Jer 46:4],

וּמֵרְקוּ חֲנִיתוֹתֵיהֶם.

55

and hastened to meet death with joy and song,

וַיְמַהֲרוּ לִקְרַאת הַמָּוֶת בְּשִׂמְחָה וָרֹן.

56

and stood in the place of wrath and fury:

וְעָמְדוּ בְּמַעֲמַד הַזַּעַם וְהֶחָרוֹן.

57

this one strong as an oak,

זֶה כְּאַלּוֹן חָסוֹן.

58

and this one meeting disaster with gladness,

וְזֶה יִפְגּשׁ הָאָסוֹן בְּשָׂשׂוֹן.

59

and this one offering his life willingly to die,

וְזֶה יִתְנַדֵּב בְּנַפְשׁוֹ לָמוּת בְּרָצוֹן.

60

and this one finding the taste of the sword

וְזֶה יִמְצָא טַעַם הַחֶרֶב.

61

sweeter than honey, and pleasant.

מָתוֹק מִדְּבַשׁ וְעָרֵב.

62

And I took up my parable and said:

וָאֶשָּׂא מְשָׁלִי וָאֹמַר :

63

Men who count their death as their life, and for death their spears are polished.

גְּבָרִים יַחְשְׁבוּ מוֹתָם חֲיוֹתָם וְלַמָּוֶת חֲנִיתֵיהֶם מְרוּקִים

64

They embrace those who fight them as lovers hasten to embrace their beloved.

וְהֵם מִתְחַבְּקִים עִם לוֹחֲמֵיהֶם כְּחוֹשְׁקִים מִהֲרוּ לַחְבֹּק חֲשׁוּקִים.

65

All of them long for death, as if they were cast in the furnace of death.

וְכֻלָּם יִכְסְפוּ אֶל הַתּמוּתָה כְּאִלוּ הֵם בְּכוּר מָוֶת יְצוּקִים.

66

For fear of them the mountains tremble, though they were as strong as Mount Tabor.

וִּמַּפַחְדָּם הֲרָרִים יֶחֱרָדוּן וְלוּ הָיוּ כְהַר תָּבוֹר חֲזָקִים

67

Disaster to their palate is like the honeycomb, and the wounds of the spear are sweet to them.

וְהָאָסוֹן לְחִכָּם הוּא כְנֹפֶת וּפִצְעֵי הַחֲנִית לָהֶם מְתוּקִים.

68

Death is a crown upon their heads, and the sword a necklace for their necks.

וְהַמָּוֶת עֲלֵי רֹאשָׁם עֲטֶרֶת וְהַחֶרֶב לְצַוָּארָם עֲנָקִים

69

The lion's heart melts at their roar, until the giants become grasshoppers [Num 13:33].

וְיִמַּס לֵב אֲרִי מִגַּעֲרָתָם עֲדֵי שָׁבוּ חֳגָבִים הָעֲנָקִים.

70

You would think they were lightnings running [Nah 2:5] in their war, and the earth the skies.

וְתַחְשׁב כִּי בְרָקְים הֵם יְרוּצוּן בְּמִלְחַמְתָּם וְהָאָרֶץ שְׁחָקִים

71

In their hands rise the stars of the spears, as if wrested from the host on high.

בְּיָדָם יַעֲלוּ כוֹכְבֵי חֲנִיתוֹת כְּאִלּוּ מִצְּבָא מָרוֹם עֲשׁוּקִים.

72

The earth is like a fire-pit from the swords, but with blood it turned into stream-beds.

וְאֶרֶץ כַּמְּדוּרָה מֵחֲרָבוֹת אֲבָל שָׁבָה בְדָמִים לַאֲפִיקִים.

73

The heavens dropped blood at their voice, and their swords fly like lightnings.

וְהִרְעִיפוּ שְׁמֵי הַדָּם לְקוֹלָם וְחַרְבוֹתָם יְעוּפוּן כַּבְּרָקִים.

74

How many skulls have fallen to the ground, their lips kissed by the mouth of the sword⚠6.

וְכַמָּה גֻלְגֳלוֹת נָפְלוּ לְאֶרֶץ וְשִׂפְתֵיהֶם בְּפִי חֶרֶב נְשׁוּקִים.

75

As if the battle were a garden, and their sword red as the lily of the valleys [Song 2:1].

כְּאִלּוּ הַקְּרָב גַּנָּה וְחַרְבָּם מְאָדֶּמֶת כְּשׁוֹשַׁנַּת עֲמָקִים.

76

And first two champions went out from them,

וַיֵּצְאוּ מֵהֶם בַּתְּחִלָּה שְׁנֵי גִבּוֹרִים.

77

as if they were two young lions.

כְּאִלּוּ הֵם שְׁנֵי כְפִירִים.

78

Theirs was an arm with might [Ps 89:14],

לָהֶם זְרוֹעַ עִם גְּבוּרָה.

79

and theirs a voice of rebuke,

וְלָהֶם קוֹל גְּעָרָה.

80

and their rebuke hews flames [Ps 29:7] in the hearts,

וְגַעֲרָתָם תַּחְצֹב בַּלְּבָבוֹת לֶהָבוֹת.

81

and sets battles in array with the warriors⚠7.

וְתַעֲרֹךְ עִם הַקְּרָבִים קְרָבוֹת.

82

And when they stood one facing the other, each roared at his brother and grew bitter,

וְכַאֲשֶׁר נִצַּב זֶה לְעֻמַּת זֶה גָּעַר אִישׁ בְּאָחִיו וַיִּתְמַרְמַר.

83

and one called to the other and said:

וְקָרָא זֶה אֶל זֶה וְאָמַר:

84

I am a son of battle from the day I came to be, and my death in battle I count as my life.

אֲנִי בֶן הַקְּרָב מִיּוֹם הֱיוֹתִי וּמוֹתִי בַקְּרָב אֶחְשֹׁב חֲיוֹתִי.

85

Life on my palate is like poison, and death grows sweet to my lips.

וְהַחַיִּים לְחִכִּי הֵם כְּמוֹ רוֹשׁ וְתִמְתַּק הַתְּמוּתָה אֶל שְׂפָתִי.

86

My sword declares: "There are wonders in me: part of me is fire, but water is my other part"⚠8.

וְחַרְבִּי תַעֲנֶה יֵשׁ בִּי פְּלָאוֹת קְצָתִי אֵשׁ אֲבָל מַיִם קְצָתִי.

87

I split the breakers of the sea of hardships, and my shield and my spear too are mine forever.

אֲבַקֵּעַ מִשְּבְּרֵי יָם הַתְלָאוֹת וְצִנָּתִי לְעֵד לִי גַּם חֲנַיתִי.

88

And when my sword thirsts for water, I give it its fill with the blood of those who break my covenant.

וְעֵת כִּי צָמְאָה חַרְבִּי לְמַיִם אֲרַו אוֹתָהּ בְּדַם עוֹבְרי בְרִיתִי.

89

I spurn the inheritance of fathers and their wealth; but my sword and my bow are my inheritance.

וְאֶמְאַס נַחֲלַת אָבוֹת וְהוֹנָם. אֲבָל חַרְבִּי וְקַשְׁתִּי נַחֲלָתִי.

90

I loathe to dwell in houses of cedar [2 Sam 7:2]; with the beasts of the forests are my camps.

וְאָקוּץ לַחֲנוֹת בָּתֵּי אֲרָזִים וְעִם חַיּוֹת יְעָרִים תַּחֲנוֹתִי.

91

And whoever draws near to fight me and sees my beginning, let him become a son of my end⚠9.

וּמִי נִגַּשׁ לְמִלְחַמְתִּי וְרָאָה תְּחִלָּתִי יְהִי בֶן אַחֲרִיתִי.

92

The second went out to him before his poem was done:

וַיֵּצֵא אֵלָיו הַשֵּׁנִי בְּטֶרֶם שִׁירוֹ נִגְמַר.

93

while this one was yet speaking, that one came and said [Job 1:16]:

עוֹד זֶה מְדַבֵּר וְזֶה בָא וַיֹאמַר:

94

In truth I and death are twins, and we sucked a mother's breasts from our youth.

אֲנִי בֶאְמֶת וְהַמָּוֶת תְּאוֹמִים וְיָנַקְנוּ שְׁדֵי אֵם מֵעֲלוּמִים.

95

And when I roar in the midst of the battles, I shake the heights and the footstools⚠10.

וְעֵת אֶשְׁאַג בְּקֶרֶב הַקְּרָבוֹת אֲנִי מַרְעִישׁ מְרוֹמִים וַהֲדוֹמִים.

96

I have a sword that refuses to enter its sheath until blood touches blood [Hos 4:2].

וְלִי חֶרֶב תְּמָאֵן בּוֹא בְּתַעְרָהּ עֲדֵי כִי יִגְּעוּ דָמִים בְּדָמים.

97

Champions' sleep wanders from them because of it, when they behold it in dreams.

וְגִבּוֹרִים יְנִידוּן לָהּ שְׁנָתָם בְּעֵת כִּי יֶחֱזוּהָ בַחֲלוֹמִים.

The Emim dwelt in it formerly [Deut 2:10], and it is a lair for every fear and terror⚠11.

It brings the giants down to Sheol, and leaves their bodies as images.

וְתוֹרִיד הָעֲנָקִים עַד שְׁאוֹלָה וְתַעְזֹב אֶת גְּוִיָּתָם צְלָמִים.

It flings their skulls to the ground, as if binders had bound them in sheaves [Gen 37:7]⚠12.

And when they had bent their bows and their arrows,

וְהַקְּשָׁתוֹת עֲטוּפִים.

Uncertain renderings

  • ⚠1 הַנְּתִיבוֹת הַבְּעוּלוֹת בְּתוּלוֹת — Literally "the married paths became virgins": roads that were once trodden became untrodden. The Hebrew rhymes בְּעוּלוֹת (married) with בְּתוּלוֹת (virgins).
  • ⚠2 וְאָז נִרְחַץ הֲלִיכֵינוּ בְחֵמָה — Job 29:6 "when I washed my steps in butter", a figure of prosperity. The rhyme word חֵמָה (butter) plays on חֵמָה (fury) in the line before.
  • ⚠3 בֵּין שִׁנֵּיהֶם — שִׁנֵּיהֶם (their teeth) rhymes with and puns on שְׁנֵיהֶם (the two of them) two lines before.
  • ⚠4 בְּצַוָּארָם יָלִין עֹז וְרַעְמָה — Job 41:14 "in his neck lodges strength" and Job 39:19 "have you clothed his neck with a mane (רַעְמָה)?", said of the war horse. רַעְמָה can also be heard as "thunder", and the next line uses Raamah as a name of a people (Gen 10:7).
  • ⚠5 שָׁכְנָה נַפְשֵׁנוּ דּוּמָה — Ps 94:17 "my soul would soon have dwelt in silence (דּוּמָה)". The word repeats the name Dumah at the end of the line before.
  • ⚠6 וְשִׂפְתֵיהֶם בְּפִי חֶרֶב נְשׁוּקִים — נָשַׁק "kiss" sounds like נֶשֶׁק "weapon", and the "mouth of the sword" (פִּי חֶרֶב) is its edge.
  • ⚠7 וְתַעֲרֹךְ עִם הַקְּרָבִים קְרָבוֹת — Read as "those who join battle". The word also means "innards" (קְרָבַיִם), so the rebuke may wage war "within the inward parts". It plays on קְרָבוֹת (battles).
  • ⚠8 קְצָתִי אֵשׁ אֲבָל מַיִם קְצָתִי — The blade is fire in its effect and water in its sheen; Arabic poetry often calls a polished sword "water". Compare line 88, where the sword "thirsts for water".
  • ⚠9 תְּחִלָּתִי יְהִי בֶן אַחֲרִיתִי — Uncertain. Perhaps: whoever sees how I begin a fight will share the end I bring on my foes, that is, his death.
  • ⚠10 מְרוֹמִים וַהֲדוֹמִים — הֲדוֹם is a footstool; the earth is God's footstool (Isa 66:1). So: heaven and earth.
  • ⚠11 וְהָאֵמִים לְפָנִים יָשְׁבוּ בָהּ / וְהִיא מָעוֹן לְכָל-פַּחַד וְאֵימִים — Deut 2:10 "the Emim dwelt in it formerly", said there of the land of Moab, here of the sword. The rhyme word אֵימִים (terrors) repeats the name Emim.
  • ⚠12 כְּאִלּוּ אִלְּמוּ אוֹתָם אֲלֻמִּים — Gen 37:7 "we were binding sheaves" (מְאַלְּמִים אֲלֻמִּים), Joseph's dream. The verb and its noun are of the same root.
  • ⚠13 הַכְּפִירִים וְהַכְּפָרִים — A pun: כְּפִירִים "young lions" and כְּפָרִים "villages" differ only in vowels.
  • ⚠14 בֵּין שְׁפַתָּיִם — Ps 68:14 "between the sheepfolds" (or "hearths"); a word of uncertain meaning. Here perhaps "between the two battle lines".
  • ⚠15 בְּפֶתַח עֵינַיִם — Gen 38:14, a place name (Petaḥ Enaim). Literally "at the opening of the eyes", that is, in full view of each other.
  • ⚠16 כְּאִישׁ הַבֵּינַיִם — 1 Sam 17:4, of Goliath: "the man between the two camps", the champion who stands between the armies and calls out to them. The Hebrew plays on בֵּינֵיהֶם (between them) earlier in the line.
  • ⚠17 וְכָבוּ מוֹקְדֵי הַבַּיִת הַדּוֹלְקִים — Literally "the burning hearths of the house". Like the fallen "mountains" and "pillars" before it, probably a figure for the great men who died.
