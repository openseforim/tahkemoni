שַׁעַר שְׁלשִׁים וְתִשְׁעָה

וִכּוּחַ הַלַּיְלָה וְהַיּוֹם. מִי הוּא יוֹתֵר נוֹרָא וְאָיֹם

The Thirty-Ninth Gate

Day and Night argue about which of them is greater, until Night gives way, and the storyteller is revealed as Ḥever

Gate 39 on Project Ben-Yehuda ↗

About this text

142 lines. One row is one rhyme unit of the rhymed prose, or one line of verse (its two halves set apart). References in [gray brackets] are the translator's identifications of biblical phrases. ⚠ marks an uncertain rendering. Each line number is a link to that line. Hebrew text: Project Ben-Yehuda.

1

Thus says Heman the Ezrahite: I was in Rimmon-perez⚠1,

נְאֻם הֵימָן הָאֶזְרָחִי: הָיִיתִי בְרִמּוֹן פָּרֶץ.

2

with nobles who build up the wall and stand in the breach [Ezek 22:30],

עִם אֲצִילִים גּוֹדְרֵי גָדֵר וְעוֹמְדִים בַּפָּרֶץ.

3

lords of congregations,

אֲדוֹנֵי קְהִלּוֹת.

4

and foundations of praises,

וְאַדְנֵי תְהִלּוֹת.

5

who draw from their tongue a two-edged sword [Ps 149:6],

יְרִיקוּן מִלְּשׁוֹנָם חֶרֶב פִּיפִיּוֹת.

6

and with their mind crumble the rocks of sound wisdom.

וִיפוֹרְרוּ בְשִׂכְלָם סַלְעֵי תוּשִׁיּוֹת.

7

One of them spoke up and said: "Let me tell you the parables of the ancients and their riddles,

וַיַּעַן אִישׁ מֵהֶם וַיֹּאמַר: אֲסַפְּרָה לָכֶם מִשְׁלֵי קַדְמוֹנִים וְחִידוֹתָם.

8

which wise men have told and not hidden, from their fathers [Job 15:18]."

אֲשֶׁר חֲכָמִים יַגִּידוּ וְלֹא כִחֲדוּ מֵאֲבוֹתָם.

9

They said to him: "Recall your eloquence, and we will know it;

אָמְרוּ לוֹ: זְכֹר מְלִיצָתְךָ וְנֵדָעֶנָּה.

10

tell us your saying, and we will hear it."

וְסַפֵּר לָנוּ אִמְרָתְךָ וְנִשְׁמָעֶנָּה.

11

He answered and said: "Know that at the time when the sun encamped in the sign of the Ram,

וַיַּעַן וַיֹּאמַר: דְּעוּ כִּי בְעֵת חָנְתָה הַשֶּׁמֶשׁ בְּמַזַּל טָלֶה.

12

and its light grew strong and was revealed,

וַיִּתְחַזֵּק אוֹרָהּ וַיִּגָּלֶה.

13

and dark places shone,

וְהֵאִירוּ חֲשֵׁכִים.

14

and the kings began to go out [2 Sam 11:1]⚠2,

וְהֵחֵלּוּ לָצֵאת הַמְּלָכִים.

15

and the nights went down to a little and made their place low,

וְהַלֵּילוֹת יָרְדוּ לְמִצְעָר וּמְקוֹמָם הִשְפִּילוּ.

16

and the days grew great,

וְהַיָּמִים הִגְדִּילוּ.

17

and presumed to go up [Num 14:44],

וְלַעֲלוֹת הֶעְפִּילוּ.

18

the light of the day put on great pride,

לָבַשׁ אוֹר הַיּוֹם גַּאֳוָה גְדוֹלָה.

19

and spoke arrogance against the night.

וַיְדַבֵּר עָתָק עַל הַלַּיְלָה.

20

He spoke up and said: 'I am the prince of princes,

וַיַּעַן וַיֹּאמַר: אָנֹכִי נְגִיד הַנְּגִידִים.

21

and the nights are slaves to me.

וְהַלֵּילוֹת לִי לַעֲבָדִים.

22

God has appointed me over them, to be their chief and their governor,

וְהָאֵל הִפְקִידַנִי עֲלֵיהֶם לִהְיוֹת קְצִינָם וּפֶחָם.

23

for I am white and they are black as coal⚠3,

כִּי אֲנִי לָבָן וְהֵם שְׁחוֹרִים כְּפֶחָם.

24

and I am of the sons of Shem and they of the sons of Ham.

וַאֲנִי מִבְּנֵי שֵׁם וְהֵם מִבְּנֵי חָם.

25

Therefore they are my slaves all the days of their eternity,

וְעֶל כֵּן הֵם עֲבָדַי כָּל יְמֵי נִצְחָם.

26

willing or against their will.'

בִּרְצוֹנָם אוֹ עַל כָּרְחָם.

27

And when the night heard the words of the day,

וְכִשְׁמֹעַ הַלַּיְלָה דִבְרֵי הַיּוֹם.

28

the night said: 'Alas for the day [Ezek 30:2],

אָמַר הַלַּיְלָה: הָהּ לַיּוֹם.

29

who thinks in his soul that he is terrible and dreadful [Hab 1:7],

הַחוֹשֵׁב בְּנַפְשׁוֹ כִּי הוּא נוֹרָא וְאָיֹם.

30

and he is sold with no redemption⚠4!

וְהוּא נִמְכַּר בְּלִי פִדְיוֹם.

31

And in what does he boast over me, except in his sun that scorches,

וּבַמֶּה יִתְגָּאֶה עָלַי כִּי אִם בְּשִׁמְשׁוֹ הַשּׁוֹזֶפֶת.

32

and his fire that burns?

וְאִשּׁוֹ הַשּׂוֹרֶפֶת.

33

He does not know that the day is born to toil [Job 5:7],

וְלֹא יֵדַע כִּי הַיּוֹם לְעָמָל יוּלָּד.

34

and is made ready for weariness.

וְלִיגִיעָה הוּא מְעֻתָּד.

35

For by day a man has no rest,

כִּי בַיּוֹם אֵין לְאִיׁש מְנוּחָה.

36

and by night there is relief.

וּבַלַּיְלָה תִּהְיֶה הָרְוָחָה.

37

In it the toilers rest,

בּוֹ יָנוּחוּ הָעֲמֵלִים.

38

and the poor forget their poverty [Prov 31:7].

וְיִשְׁכְּחוּ רִישָׁם הַדַּלִּים.

39

In it the weary find rest,

וּבֹו הָעֲיֵפִים יִמְצְאוּ מָנוֹחַ.

40

and there the wearied in strength are at rest [Job 3:17].

וְשָׁם יָנוּחוּ יְגִיעֵי כֹחַ.

41

In it every worrier forgets his griefs,

וּבוֹ יְנַשֶּׁה כָּל דּוֹאֵג יְגוֹנָיו.

42

and the slave is free from his master [Job 3:19].

וְעֶבֶד חָפְשִׁי מֵאֲדוֹנָיו.

43

Were it not for the night, the sons of men would find no rest,

וְלוּלֵא הַלַּיְלָה בְּנֵי אִישׁ מְנוּחָה לֹא מָצָאוּ.

44

and from slavery to freedom would not go out.

וּמֵעַבְדוּת לַחֹפֶשׁ לֹא יָצָאוּ.

45

And if you have reviled me for my darkness, how many times have your eyes seen a white thing that is trampled and despised,

וְאִם בְּקַדְרוּתִי חֵרַפְתַּנִי כַּמָּה רָאוּ עֵינֶיךָ דָּבָר לָבָן וְהוּא נִרְמָס וְנִמְאָס.

46

and how many a black thing precious and laid up in store!

וְכַמָּה שָׁחוֹר יָקָר וְנִכְמָס.

47

Behold, the white of the eye

הִנֵּה לֹבֶן הָעַיִן.

48

is counted as nothing,

נֶחְשָׁב לְאַיִן.

49

and its black is the honored and the enlightened one,

וְשַׁחֲרוּתוֹ הוּא הַנִּכְבָּד וְהַנָּאוֹר.

50

and the way where light dwells [Job 38:19]⚠5.'

וְהַדֶּרֶךְ יִשְׁכֹּן אוֹר.

51

The day answered and said: 'God has made laughter for me [Gen 21:6], when I see the lofty fallen,

וַיַּעַן הַיּוֹם וַיֹּאמַר: צְחוֹק עָשָׂה לִי אֱלֹהִים בִּרְאוֹתִי הַגְּבוֹהִים נָפָלוּ.

52

and the lowly gone up to the sky.

וְהַשְּׁפָלִים לַשַּׁחַק עָלוּ.

53

Every valley is lifted up, and every mountain and hill made low [Isa 40:4]!

כָּל גֶּיא יִנָּשֵׂא וְכָל הַר וְגִבְעָה יִשְׁפָּלוּ.

54

And in what does the night glory, when there is nothing desirable in it?

וּבַמֶּה יִתְפָּאֵר הַלַּיְלָה וְאֵין בּוֹ דָבָר חָמוּד.

55

Lo, let that night be barren [Job 3:7],

הִנֵּה הַלַּיְלָה הַהוּא יְהִי גַלְמוּד.

56

for in it the eyes of those who see grow dark,

כִּי בוֹ תֶּחֱשַׁכְנָה עֵינֵי הָרוֹאִים.

57

and all creatures walk about bewildered.

וְיֵלְכוּ נְבוּכִים כָּל הַבְּרוּאִים.

58

But when the dawn rises their eyes light up,

וּבַעֲלוֹת הַשַּׁחַר עֵינֵיהֶם יָאִירוּ.

59

and they shine like the brightness of the firmament [Dan 12:3].

וּכְזֹהַר הָרָקִיעַ יַזְהִירוּ.

60

For by the light of the day a man prospers in all his ways,

כִּי בְאוֹר הַיּוֹם יַצְלִיחַ אָדָם בְּכָל דְרָכָיו.

61

and can do all he needs:

וְיוּכַל לַעֲשׂוֹת כָּל צְרָכָיו.

62

to sell or to buy,

לִמְכֹּר אוֹ לִקְנוֹת.

63

to tear down or to build.

וְלַהֲרֹס אוֹ לִבְנוֹת.

64

But by night he can do nothing but steal or whore,

וּבַלַּיְלָה לֹא יוּכַל לַעֲשׂוֹת כִּי אִם לִגְנֹב אוֹ לִזְנוֹת.

65

and dig through the walls [Job 24:16] and come in through the windows [Joel 2:9].'

וְלַחְתֹּר הַקִּירוֹת וְלָבוֹא בְּעַד הַחַלּוֹנוֹת.

66

The night answered and said: 'Have you not known,

וַיַּעַן הַלַּיְלָה וַיֹּאמַר: הַלֹא יָדַעְתָּ.

67

have you not heard [Isa 40:28],

אִם לֹא שָׁמַעְתָּ.

68

that I am the foundation of fellowship,

כִּי אֲנִי יְסוֹד הַחֶבְרָה.

69

and of perfect love?

וְהָאַהֲבָה הַגְּמוּרָה.

70

For in me lovers and friends join together,

כִּי בִי יִתְחַבְּרוּ הַחוֹשְׁקִים וְהַיְדִידִים.

71

and in me they take their fill of love [Prov 7:18],

וּבִי יִרְווּ דוֹדִים.

72

and through you they have their fill of wanderings [Job 7:4]⚠6.

וּבְךָ יִשְׂבְּעוּ נְדוּדִים.

73

I join the lovers, and you part them;

וַאֲנִי אֲחַבֵּר הַחוֹשְׁקִים וְאַתָּה תַפְרִידֵם.

74

I part the griefs, and you bind them fast.

וְאַפְרִיד הַיְגוֹנִים וְאַתָּה תַצְמִידֵם.

75

Friends embrace in me,

וְהַיְדִידִים בִּי יִתְחַבָּקוּ.

76

one cleaves to another [Job 41:9],

אִישׁ בְּאָחִיהוּ יְדֻבָּקוּ.

77

and through you they are far from each other.

וּבְךָ זֶה מִזֶּה יִרְחָקוּ.

78

And when they see the light of your dawn,

וּבִרְאוֹתָם אוֹר שְׁחָרֶיךָ.

79

they say: "Rise, let us all flee [2 Sam 15:14],

יֹאמְרוּ קוּמוּ וְנִבְרַח כֻּלָּנוּ.

80

for the man has come who consumed us,

כִּי בָא הָאִישׁ אֲשֶׁר כִּלָּנוּ.

81

and who devised against us [2 Sam 21:5]."'

וַאֲשֶׁר דִּמָּה לָנוּ.

82

The day answered and said: 'Let the day perish on which I was born [Job 3:3],

וַיַּעַן הַיּוֹם וַיֹּאמַר: יֹאבַד יוֹם אִוָּלֶד בּוֹ.

83

and let its flame not shine [Job 18:5],

וְאַל יִגַּהּ שְׁבִיבוֹ.

84

now that empty and worthless men have made light of me,

אַחֲרֵי אֲשֶׁר הֲקִלּוּנִי רֵקִים וּנְעוּרִים.

85

and the young have laughed at me [Job 30:1],

וְצָחֲקוּ עָלַי צְעִירִים.

86

and moth and worm have gone up

וְעָלְתָה עָשׁ וְרִמָּה.

87

over the Bear and the Pleiades [Job 9:9]⚠7,

עַל עָשׁ וְכִימָה.

88

and the deeps have risen proudly

וְגָאוּ תְהוֹמוֹת.

89

over the places!

עַל הַמְּקוֹמוֹת.

90

Do you not know that in me is the life of the world,

הֲלֹא יָדַעְתָּ כִּי בִי חַיֵּי הָעוֹלָם.

91

and my sun is the mistress of all creatures?

וְשִׁמְשִׁי גְּבֶרֶת הַבְּרוּאִים כֻּלָּם.

92

In my light the flowers bloom,

וּבְאוֹרִי יִפְרְחוּ הַפְּרָחִים.

93

and the plants sprout,

וְיִצְמְחוּ הַצְּמָחִים.

94

from the precious things brought forth by the sun and the precious things put forth by the moons [Deut 33:14].

מִמֶּגֶד תְּבוּאֹות שֶׁמֶשׁ וּמִמֶּגֶד גֶּרֶשׁ יְרָחִים.

95

But you have no strength in you to save,

וְאַתָּה אֵין בְּךָ כֹּחַ לְהַצִּיל.

96

neither to help nor to profit [Isa 30:5].'

לֹא לְעֵזֶר וְלֹא לְהוֹעִיל.

97

The night said: 'Do you boast over me of your sun, when she is a female,

אָמַר הַלַּיְלָה: הֲתִתְגָּאֶה עָלַי בְּשִׁמְשְׁךָ וְהִיא נְקֵבָה.

98

hewn from the quarry [Isa 51:1] of women?

וּמִמַּקֶּבֶת הַנָּשִים חֲצוּבָה.

99

Are not your evils many [Job 22:5]⚠8,

הֲלֹא רָעוֹתֶיךָ רַבּוֹת.

100

that you send out the females to the war of the males?

כִּי לְמִלְחֶמֶת הַזְּכָרִים תּוֹצִיא הַנְּקֵבוֹת.

101

And how can the sun retain strength [Dan 10:8]

וְאֵיךְ תַּעֲצֹר הַשֶּׁמֶשׁ כֹּחַ.

102

before the moon,

לִפְנֵי הַיָּרֵחַ.

103

my king,

מַלְכִּי.

104

who lights up my darkness [Ps 18:29]?

וּמַגִּיהַּ חָשְׁכִּי.

105

When my moon shines, does not your shame hide herself⚠9,

הֲלֹא בְּעֵת יָאִיר סַהֲרִי תִסָּתֵר בָּשְׁתֶּךָ.

106

and your lady hide in the women's chamber?

וְתַחְבִּיא בַחֲדַר הַנָּשִׁים גְּבִרְתֶּךָ.

107

And your sun is alone,

וְשִׁמְשְׁךָ יְחִידָה.

108

without assembly or congregation,

בְּלִי קָהָל וְעֵדָה.

109

while the moon has many troops, and he walks about among them,

וְהַיָּרֵחַ גְּדוּדָיו רַבִּים וְהוּא בְתוֹכָם מִתְהַלֵּךְ.

110

for in the multitude of people is the king's glory [Prov 14:28].

כִּי בְרָב עָם הַדְרַת מֶלֶךְ.

111

And when my moon rises, your sun goes down and departs,

וּבַעֲלוֹת סַהֲרִי שִׁמְשְׁךָ נוֹטָה וְאוֹרַחַת.

112

like a scab that flees⚠10,

כְּסַפַּחַת בּוֹרַחַת.

113

and draws near to the pit,

וְתִקְרַב לַשַּׁחַת.

114

and is like the dove that nests in the sides of the mouth of the pit [Jer 48:28],

Uncertain renderings

  • ⚠1 בְרִמּוֹן פָּרֶץ — Rimmon-perez is a station of the Israelites in the wilderness (Num 33:19). The name is chosen for the rhyme with בַּפָּרֶץ ("in the breach") in the next line; it is not a real town of the author's time.
  • ⚠2 וְהֵחֵלּוּ לָצֵאת הַמְּלָכִים — 2 Sam 11:1 "at the time when kings go out", that is, spring. Here the "kings" may also be the lights of heaven that come out in the spring sky.
  • ⚠3 כְּפֶחָם — The rhyme words of these lines are פֶּחָם (their governor, from פֶּחָה), פֶּחָם (coal), חָם (Ham) and נִצְחָם: the same sound in different senses.
  • ⚠4 וְהוּא נִמְכַּר בְּלִי פִדְיוֹם — That is, the day is himself a slave to toil (as the next lines say), sold with no right of redemption. The phrase has no clear biblical source.
  • ⚠5 וְהַדֶּרֶךְ יִשְׁכֹּן אוֹר — Job 38:19 "where is the way where light dwells". The night argues that the dark pupil, not the white of the eye, is where sight (light) lives.
  • ⚠6 וּבְךָ יִשְׂבְּעוּ נְדוּדִים — Job 7:4 "I am full of tossings until the dawn", of sleepless nights. The night turns the verse: it is the day that fills lovers with wandering (נְדוּדִים also means being driven apart). The rhyme דוֹדִים / נְדוּדִים (love / wanderings) is lost.
  • ⚠7 וְעָלְתָה עָשׁ וְרִמָּה. עַל עָשׁ וְכִימָה — A pun: the first עָשׁ is the moth, with the worm the image of what is lowly (Job 25:6, Isa 51:8); the second עָשׁ is a constellation (Job 9:9, usually the Bear). The low (the night) has risen over the high (the day).
  • ⚠8 הֲלֹא רָעוֹתֶיךָ רַבּוֹת — Job 22:5 "is not your evil great". Unpointed, רעותיך can also be read "your female companions", which fits the next line about females sent to war.
  • ⚠9 תִסָּתֵר בָּשְׁתֶּךָ — "Your shame" is the sun, mocked as a shameful thing (בֹּשֶׁת is a biblical name for an idol, Jer 11:13). The next line pairs it with גְּבִרְתֶּךָ, "your lady".
  • ⚠10 כְּסַפַּחַת בּוֹרַחַת — סַפַּחַת is a skin eruption in the laws of leprosy (Lev 13:2). The day answers in kind below, calling the moon a בַּהֶרֶת, a bright spot from the same verse. "Flees" is literal; the sense may be a mark that fades away.
  • ⚠11 כְּבַהֶרֶת לְבָנָה — Lev 13:4 "a white bright spot", a sign of leprosy. לְבָנָה (white) is also a word for the moon, so the phrase can be heard as "like the spot that is the moon".
  • ⚠12 כִּי מַעֲלָתְךָ נְסוּכָה — נְסוּכָה is read as "anointed, installed", from Ps 2:6 "I have set (נָסַכְתִּי) my king".
  • ⚠13 אֲנִי חָבֵר לְכָל מִי יִדְרְשֵׁנִי — A pun on the hero's name: חֶבֶר (Ḥever) and חָבֵר (companion) have the same letters. Compare Ps 119:63 "I am a companion to all who fear You".
