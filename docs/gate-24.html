שַׁעַר עֶשְׂרִים וְאַרְבָּעָה

בִּשְׁלִיחַ צִבּוּר וּתְפִלּוֹתָיו. וְסִפּוּר שִׁבּוּשָׁיו וְטָעֻיוֹתָיו.

The Twenty-Fourth Gate

The ignorant cantor: his blunders in the Sabbath prayers, a dispute on whether piyyutim belong in the service, and a poem against those who call prayer optional

Gate 24 on Project Ben-Yehuda ↗

1

Thus says Heman the Ezrahite: Ḥever the Kenite told me plainly [1 Sam 10:16],

נְאֻם הֵימָן הָאֶזְרָחִי: הַגֵּד הִגִּיד לִי חֶבֶר הַקֵּינִי.

2

and said: Let me tell you some of the wonders that my eye has seen.

וְאָמַר: אַגִּידָה נָא לְךָ מִן הַנִּפְלָאוֹת אֲשֶׁר רָאֲתָה עֵינִי.

3

I journeyed from the land of Geshur to Ashur,

כִּי נָסַעְתִּי מֵאֶרֶץ גְּשׁוּר לְאַשּׁוּר.

4

at a time when the hands of its nobles had dried up like the flinty rock [Deut 32:13],

בְּעֵת חָרְבוּ יְדֵי נְדִיבֶיהָ כְּחַלְמִישׁ צוּר.

5

and there was none left, shut up or free [Deut 32:36]⚠1.

וְאֶפֶס עָזוּב וְעָצוּר.

6

The city was fair and esteemed,

וְהָעִיר נָאָה וַחֳשׁוּבָה.

7

very great and wide,

גְּדוֹלָה מְאוֹד וּרְחָבָה.

8

with a great multitude and wealth found within it.

וְהָמוֹן רָב וְעשֶׁר נִמְצָא בְּקִרְבָּהּ.

9

The Emim dwelt there in times past [Deut 2:10].

וְהָאֵמִים לְפָנִים יָשְׁבוּ בָהּ.

10

In all the lands there is none like its beauty,

וְאֵין בְּכָל הָאֲרָצוֹת כְּיָפְיָהּ.

11

nor like its splendor and its glory.

וְלֹא כַהֲדָרָהּ וְצִבְיָהּ.

12

It also flows with milk and honey, and this is its fruit [Num 13:27];

וְגַם זָבַת חָלָב וּדְבַשׁ הִיא וְזֶה פִרְיָהּ.

13

except that the people who dwell in it are strong [Num 13:28]:

אֶּפֶס כִּי עַז הָעָם הַיּוֹשֵׁב עָלֶיהָ.

14

those of our own people who live within its borders.

הַשּׁוֹכְנִים מִבְּנֵי עַמֵּנוּ בִּגְבוּלֶיהָ.

15

For not one of them makes a free gift, to the honored or to the base,

כִּי לֹא יִתְנַדֵּב אַחַד מֵהֶם לְנִכְבָּד אוֹ בְלִיַּעַל.

16

from a thread to a shoelace [Gen 14:23].

מִחוּט וְעַד שְׂרוֹךְ נַעל.

17

All of them keep their bundles of money [Gen 42:35]

וְכֻלָּם צְרוֹרוֹת כַּסְפֵּיהֶם.

18

bound up in their palms,

צְרוּרוֹת בְּכַפֵּיהֶם.

19

with no going out and no breach [Ps 144:14],

בְּלִי יוֹצֵאת וָפָרֶץ.

20

and not a pebble falls to the ground [Amos 9:9]⚠2.

וְלֹא יִפֹּל צְרוֹר אָרֶץ.

21

When I came to it with a troubled soul,

וּבְבוֹאִי אֵלֶיהָ בְּנֶפֶשׁ נְבוֹכָה.

22

on the Sabbath eve as darkness fell [m. Shabbat 2:7],

עֶרֶב שַׁבָּת עִם חֲשֵׁכָה.

23

I said: Perhaps the house of prayer is near;

אָמַרְתִּי: אוּלַי בֵּית הַתְּפִלָּה קְרוֹבָה.

24

I will go to it and seek there.

וְאֵלְכָה אֵלֶיהָ וְאֶדְרְשָׁה בָהּ.

25

And when I came to the house of prayer,

וְכַאֲשֶׁר בָּאתִי לְבֵית הַתְּפִלָּה.

26

with the congregation,

עִם הַקְּהִלָּה.

27

I saw it was fair as a temple and a palace.

רְאִיתִיהָ יָפָה כְּהֵיכָל וְאַרְמוֹן.

28

I sat down in the midst of the crowd,

וְיָשַׁבְתִּי בְּתוֹךְ הֶהָמוֹן.

29

and beside me were two elders,

וְהָיוּ לְצִדִּי שְׁנֵי זְקֵנִים.

30

long of beard⚠3,

אֲרֻכֵּי זְקָנִים.

31

sturdy as the oaks [Amos 2:9],

חֲסוֹנִים כָּאַלּוֹנִים.

32

sellers of praises and not buyers,

מִמּוֹכְרֵי הַתְּהִלּוֹת וְלֹא מִן הַקּוֹנִים.

33

wreckers of the houses of righteousness and not builders,

וּמֵהוֹרְסֵי בָתֵּי צֶדֶק וְלֹא מִן הַבּוֹנִים.

34

men of stature,

בַּעֲלֵי קוֹמוֹת.

35

their bellies like heaps [Song 7:3].

וּבִטְנָם כַּעֲרֵמוֹת.

36

Their souls are base and their eyes lofty.

לָהֶם נְפָשׁוֹת בְּזוּיוֹת וְעֵינֵיהֶם רָמוֹת.

37

When I asked them about their great men,

וְכַאֲשֶׁר שְׁאִלְתִּים עַל גְּדוֹלֵיהֶם.

38

and the rest of their congregation,

וְיֶתֶר קַהֲלֵיהֶם.

39

and the cantor they had,

וְהַחַזָּן אֲשֶׁר לָהֶם.

40

they said: We have heads of the exiles,

אָמְרוּ: יֵשׁ לָנוּ רָאשֵׁי גָלֻיּוֹת.

41

and pearls of the purest,

וּפְנִינֵי מַרְגָּלִיּוֹת.

42

and besides this we have a cantor who is a preacher,

וּמִלְּבַד זֶה יֵשׁ לָנוּ חַזָּן דַּרְשָׁן.

43

humble and bashful,

עָנָו וּבַיְשָׁן.

44

fat and sleek as rams of Bashan [Deut 32:14].

דָּשֵׁן וְשָׁמֵן כְּמוֹ אֵילִים בְּנֵי בָּשָׁן.

45

His prayer is sweet,

תְּפִלָּתוֹ עֲרֵבָה.

46

his company is loved,

וְחֶבְרָתוֹ אֲהוּבָה.

47

and his chanting is good.

וּנְגִינָתוֹ טוֹבָה.

48

He teaches the Torah,

יוֹרֶה בַתּוֹרָה.

49

he reads in Scripture,

וְיִקְרָא בַמִּקְרָא.

50

and he reasons on the Prophets with many a reasoning.

וְיִסְבֹּר בַּנְּבִיאִים כַּמָּה סְבָרָא.

51

He knows precious piyyutim,

וְיוֹדֵעַ פִּיּוּטִים יְקָרִים.

52

and sings the songs.

וְשָׁר בַּשִּירִים.

53

When I heard their words I said: Praise and glory to God,

וּכְשָׁמְעִי דִבְרֵיהֶם אָמַרְתִּי: תְּהִלָּה לָאֵל וּשְׁבָח.

54

who has granted me to see this praised cantor!

כִּי זִכַּנִי לִרְאוֹת זֶה הַחַזָּן הַמְשֻׁבָּח.

55

While I was still in this thought,

וּבְעוֹדִי בְזֹאת הַמַּחֳשָׁבָה.

56

behold, the cantor came,

וְהִנֵּה הַחַזָּן בָּא.

57

and on his forehead a frontlet⚠4,

וְעַל מִצְחוֹ טוֹטֶפֶת.

58

and on his head a turban,

וּבְרֹאשׁוֹ מִצְנֶפֶת.

59

white and pleasant,

לְבָנָה וּנְעִימָה.

60

two hundred cubits long.

בַּת מָאתַיִם אַמָּה.

61

The hair of his beard reached down to his navel,

וְשַׂעֲרַת זְקָנוֹ עַד טַבּוּרוֹ הֶאֱרִיכָה.

62

and covered him like a blanket.

וַתְּכַסֵּהוּ בַשְּׂמִיכָה.

63

He dragged its fringes along the ground,

וְהוּא יִסְחֹב עַל הָאָרֶץ גְּדִילֶיהָ.

64

and nearly stumbled over its hems.

וְכִמְעַט יִכָּשֵׁל בְּשׁוּלֶיהָ.

65

When we saw him,

וּבִרְאוֹתֵנוּ אוֹתוֹ.

66

we all rose trembling to meet him,

חָרַדְנוּ כֻלָּנוּ לִקְרָאתוֹ.

67

bowed down before him,

וְהִשְׁתַּחֲוִינוּ לְעֻמָּתוֹ.

68

and kept silent in awe of him,

וְהֶחֱרַשְׁנוּ לְאֵימָתוֹ.

69

until he began his sweet chanting

עַד הֵחֵל בִּנְעִימוּתוֹ.

70

and opened his prayer.

וּפָתַח תְּפִלָּתוֹ.

71

In the prayer I counted more than a hundred mistakes,

וּמָנִיתִי לוֹ בַּתְּפִלָּה יוֹתֵר מִמֵּאָה טָעֻיּוֹת.

72

plain and open,

בְּרוּרוֹת וּגְלוּיוֹת.

73

besides the others not worth remembering.

מִלְבַד הָאֲחֵרוֹת שֶׁאֵינָם לִזְכֹּר רְאוּיוֹת.

74

But I did not answer him,

אַךְ לֹא עֲנִיתִיהוּ.

75

for I said: Perhaps it is by chance,

כִּי אָמַרְתִּי אוּלַי מִקְרֶה הוּא.

76

or the bustle of the Sabbath has hurried him,

אוֹ טִרְדַּת הַשַּׁבָּת הִבְהִילַתְהוּ.

77

or sleep has overcome him.

אוֹ שֵׁנָה אֲנָסַתְהוּ.

78

And when the dawn came up [Gen 19:15],

וּכְמוֹ הַשַּׁחַר עָלָה.

79

I rose early to the house of prayer.

הִשְׁכַּמְתִּי לְבֵית הַתְּפִלָּה.

80

Behold, the cantor came and sat in the seat of greatness,

וְהִנֵּה הַחַזָּן בָּא וְיָשַׁב בְּמוֹשַׁב הַגְּדֻלָּה.

81

and opened with the hundred blessings [b. Menahot 43b],

וּפָתַח בְּמֵאָה בְרָכוֹת.

82

which on his tongue are set in order and arranged.

אֲשֶׁר עַל לְשׁוֹנוֹ סְדוּרוֹת וַעֲרוּכוֹת.

83

He said in a loud crying voice:

וְאָמַר בְּקוֹל צְעָקָה רָמָּה.

84

"Blessed are You, O LORD, who formed man a beast!"⚠5

בָּרוּךְ אַתָּה יְיָ' אֲשֶׁר יָצַר אֶת הָאָדָם בְּהֵמָה.

85

And in the Verses of Song,

וּבִפְסוּקֵי זִמְרָה.

86

he made many mistakes, past counting.

טָעָה טָעֻיּוֹת רַבּוֹת וְאֵין לָהֶם סְפִירָה.

87

Instead of "Keep back Your servant also from presumptuous sins" [Ps 19:14],

וּבִמְקוֹם גַּם מִזֵּדִים חֲשׂךְ עַבְדֶּךָ.

88

he said: "Train Your servant also with olives."⚠6

אָמַר: גַּם מִזֵּיתִים חֲנֹךְ עַבְדֶּךָ.

89

Instead of "He satisfies you with the fat of the wheat" [Ps 147:14],

וּבִמְקוֹם חֵלֶב חִטִּים יַשְׂבִּיעֶךָּ.

90

he said: "He satisfies you with a sword of sharp ones."⚠7

אָמַר: חֶרֶב חַדִּים יַשְׂבִּיעֶךָּ.

91

Instead of "who covers the heavens with clouds" [Ps 147:8],

וּבִמְקוֹם הַמְכַסֶּה שָׁמַיִם בֶּעָבִים.

92

he said: "who covers the heavens with clothes."⚠8

אָמַר: הַמְכַסֶּה שָׁמַיִם בְּגָדִים.

93

Instead of "Let Israel rejoice in his Maker" [Ps 149:2],

וּבִמְקוֹם יִשְמַח יִשְׂרָאֵל בְּעוֹשָׂיו.

94

he said: "Let Ishmael rejoice in Esau."⚠9

אָמַר: יִשְׂמַח יִשְׁמָעֵאל בְּעֵשָׂו.

95

Instead of "Praise Him with strings and pipe" [Ps 150:4],

וּבִמְקוֹם הַלְלוּהוּ בְּמִנִּים וְעוּגָב.

96

he said: "Praise Him, cheeses and cake."⚠10

אָמַר: הַלְלוּהוּ גְבִינִים וְעוּגָה.

97

Instead of "and in Your hand it is to make great and to give strength to all" [1 Chr 29:12],

וּבִמְקוֹם וּבְיָדְךָ לְגַדֵּל וּלְחַזֵּק לַכֹּל.

98

[he said:] "and in Your hand it is to blaspheme and to do harm to all."⚠11

וּבְיָדְךָ לְגַדֵּף וּלְהַזִּיק לַכֹּל.

99

Until I was put to shame,

עַד אֲשֶׁר נִכְלַמְתִּי.

100

and regretted that I had come to the synagogue,

וְעַל בּוֹאִי לְבֵית הַכְּנֶסֶת נִחַמְתִּי.

101

and was struck dumb,

וְנֶאֱלַמְתִּי.

102

and laid my hand upon my mouth [Job 40:4].

וְיָדִי לְפִי שַׂמְתִּי.

103

When he had finished his hymns,

וְכַאֲשֶׁר כִּלָּה זְמִירוֹתָיו.

104

he stood up in his place to set out his praises.

עָמַד עַל עָמְדוֹ לַעֲרֹךְ תְּהִלּוֹתָיו.

105

He covered his face, not from humility,

וְכִסָּה פָנָיו לֹא מֵעֲנָוָה.

106

and stood in pride.

וְעָמַד בְּגַאֲוָה.

107

He made many motions,

וְהִרְבָּה תְנוּעוֹתָיו.

108

and shook his shoulders,

וְהֵנִיעַ כִּתְפוֹתָיו.

109

lifted his right foot,

וְהֵרִים רַגְלוֹ הַיְמָנִית.

110

and put down the other,

וְהוֹרִיד הַשֵּׁנִית.

111

and went back a little, backward.

וְחָזַר מְעַט לַאֲחֹרַנִּית.

112

He opened to them the hoards of his wisdom,

וּפָתַח לָהֶם גִּנְזֵי חָכְמָתוֹ.

113

and showed them his treasure house [Isa 39:2].

וְהֶרְאָה בֵית נְכֹתֹה.

114

He began to say piyyutim and songs,

וְהִתְחִיל לוֹמַר פִּיּוּטִים וְשִׁירִים.

115

all of them broken,

כֻּלָּם שְׁבוּרִים.

116

lame and blind [Deut 15:21],

וּפִסְּחִים וְעִוְרִים.

117

their way crooked,

וְדַרְכָּם מְעֻקָּל.

118

without rhyme and without meter,

בְּלֹא חֶרֶז וּבְלֹא מִשְׁקָל.

119

without structure,

וּבְלֹא בִנְיָן.

120

and without sense.

וּבְלֹא עִנְיָן.

121

And while he drew out his piyyutim

וְכַאֲשֶר הֶאֱרִיךְ פִּיּוּטָיו.

122

for his fools,

לְשׁוֹטָיו.

123

his songs of mockery for his dullards,

וְשִׁירֵי הֲתֻלָיו לִכְסִילָיו.

124

and his psalms for his asses,

וּמִזְמוֹרָיו לַחֲמוֹרָיו.

125

some of the people sat on,

ישֵ מִׁקְצָת הָעָם אֲשֶׁר יָשְבוּ.

126

some of them slept an everlasting sleep [Jer 51:39] and lay down,

וּמֵהֶם יָשְׁנוּ שְׁנַת עוֹלָם וְשָׁכְבוּ.

127

and some fled and did not come back,

וּקְצָתָם בָּרְחוּ וְלֹא שָׁבוּ.

and speaks of the trees and of the stones [1 Kgs 5:13].

For every one of them gropes and walks in the dark [Deut 28:29],

for he comes in vanity and goes in darkness [Eccl 6:4].

כִּי בַהֶבֶל בָּא וּבַחשֶׁך יֵלֵךְ.

Therefore I say that the qerovot and the hymns

are not forbidden to all congregations, but permitted;

Behold, the place is a place for cattle [Num 32:1]."

וַיָּשֶׂם יָדוֹ עַל פִּיו הָאִישׁ.

Now there was in the city of Arbel a man

וְהָיָה בְעִיר אַרְבֵּל גֶּבֶר.

of the children of Eber [Gen 10:21],

מִבְּנֵי עֵבֶר.

and his name was Benjamin the Ḥaver,

and he was a caster of spells [Deut 18:11]⚠20.

וְהוּא חוֹבֵר חָבֶר.

281

He wrote a reply to the words of this claimant,

וְהֵשִׁיב עַל דִּבְרֵי זֶה הַטּוֹעֵן.

282

and God punished him for this sin:

וְהָאֵל עֲנָשָׁהוּ בְּזֶה הֶעָוֹן.

283

he was cut off,

וַיִּצָּמֵת.

284

for he died in his own sin [Num 27:3].

כִּי בְחֶטְאוֹ מֵת.

285

He piled up arguments in a letter of his;

וְהַרְבֵּה טְעָנוֹת בַּאִגֶּרֶת אֲשֶׁר לוֹ.

286

all who hear it will laugh at him [Gen 21:6].

כָּל הַשּׁוֹמֵעַ יִצְחַק לוֹ.

His folly

וְהָיָה פְתַיּוּתוֹ.

288

had been hidden in his silence,

נִסְתָּר בִּשְׁתִיקָתוֹ.

289

until he threw the jug⚠21,

עַד זָרַק הַקִּיתוֹן.

For he brought contemptible arguments,

all of them thrust aside without a hand [Dan 8:25],

כָּלָּם בְּאֶפֶס יָד דְּחוּיוֹת.

293

and built upon no foundation.

וְעַל לֹא יְסוֹד בְּנוּיוֹת.

All his words are mockeries,

וְכָל דְּבָרָיו תַּעְתּוּעִים.

the arguments of madmen.

וְטַעֲנוֹת הַמִּשְׁתַּגְּעִים.

He said in his words that in the portion Va-yeḥal Moshe there is a hint of the selihot, because in every selihah they say "And the LORD passed before him" [Exod 34:6]⚠22.

וְאָמַר בִּדְבָרָיו כִּי יֵשׁ בְּפָרָשַׁת וַיְחַל משֶׁה רֶמֶז לִסְלִיחוֹת מִפְּנֵי שֶׁיֹּאמְרוּ וַיַּעֲבֹר ה' עַל פָּנָיו בְּכָל סְלִיחָה וּסְלִיחָה.

297

He said that prayer is not a binding duty upon us,

וְאָמַר כִּי הַתְּפִלָּה אֵינָהּ עָלֵינוּ חוֹבָה מְחֻיֶּבֶת.

298

because it is not written in the Torah.

מִפְּנֵי שֶׁאֵינָהּ בַתּוֹרָה נִכְתֶּבֶת.

299

And as for what is said, "You shall serve the LORD your God" [Exod 23:25], and the sages said, "This service is prayer" [b. Ta'anit 2a],

וּמַה שֶּׁנֶּאֱמַר וַעֲבַדְתֶּם אֶת ה' אֱלֹהֵיכֶם וְאָמרוּ חֲכָמִים עֲבוֹדָה זוֹ תְּפִלָּה.

this Ḥaver said that it is a light opinion

אָמַר זֶה הֶחָבֵר כִּי הִיא סְבָרָה קַלָּה.

301

and a poor proof.

וּרְאָיָה דַלָּה.

302

Whoever says that the selihot have a proof in the Torah,

וְכָל הָאוֹמֵר כִּי יֵשׁ לִסְלִיחוֹת רְאָיָה בַתּוֹרָה.

303

and that prayer has no proof from the Torah,

וְאֵין לִתְפִלָּה רְאָיָה מִן הַתּוֹרָה.

304

for his sins there is no atonement,

אֵין לַחֲטָאָיו כַּפָּרה.

305

for he has spoken rebellion [Deut 13:6]

כִּי דִבֶּר סָרָה.

306

against God, the great, the mighty and the awesome [Deut 10:17].

עַל הָאֵל הַגָּדוֹל הַגִּבּוֹר וְהַנּוֹרָא.

And I took up my parable and said [Num 23:7]:

וָאֶשָּׂא מְשָׁלִי וָאֹמַר:

Whoever says that prayer is a free choice, and the qerovah a duty for the congregation,

אֲשֶׁר יֹאמַר רְשׁוּת הִיא הַתְּפִלָּה וְחוֹבָה הַקְּרוֹבָה לַקְּהִלָּה.

truly he is struck with madness and folly, and no healing is found for his wound [Jer 30:13].

אֱמֶת הֻכָּה בְהוֹלֵלוּת וְסִכְלוּת וְלֹא נִמְצֵאת לְמַכָּתוֹ תְעָלָה.

310

For his faith is broken, and he heals its breaking lightly [Jer 6:14].

לְמַעַן כֹּי אֱמוּנָתוֹ שְׁבוּרָה וְאֶת שִׁבְרָהּ יְרַפֵּא עַל נְקַלָּה.

311

Truly silence is precious; it weighs as much as all the lovely virtues.

אֱמֶת כִּי הַשְּׁתִיקָה הִיא יְקָרָה בְּכַל מִדּוֹת חֲמוּדוֹת הִיא שְׁקוּלָה.

312

Had the fool kept silent, he would have covered the folly he has laid bare and exposed.

וְאִלּוּ הַכְּסִיל שָׁתַק יְכַסֶּה אֱוִילוּתוֹ אֲשֶׁר חָשַׂף וְגִלָּה.

In his heart folly was shut up, and he opened his mouth and gave it its release⚠23.

All much talk is to the talker's harm, and too much oil puts out the wick.

וְכָל מַרְבֶּה דְבָרִים הֵם לְרַע לוֹ וְרֹב שֶׁמֶן יְכַבֶּה הַפְּתִילָה.

I have seen a fool wise in his own eyes [Prov 26:12], who does not understand, and his heart is sick [Ezek 16:30].

How can one struck with blindness [Gen 19:11] see, when the eye of his heart is painted with the kohl of folly?

וְאֵיךְ יוּכַל רְאוֹת מֻכֶּה בְסַנְוֵר וְעֵין לִבּוֹ בְּפוּךְ סִכְלוּת כְּחוּלָה.

The weakness of his argument is a sign that his opponent's argument is the better.

וְחֹלֶשׁ טַעֲנָתוֹ הוּא לְאוֹת הֲכִי טַעְנַת מְרִיבוֹ הִיא מְעֻלָּה.

He testified that the sages said there is no duty of prayer, and his testimony is invalid.

וְהֵעִיד כִּי חֲכָמִים אָמְרוּ אֵין תְּפִלָּה חוֹב וְעֵדוּתוֹ פְּסוּלָה.

He thinks with his words to rob the truth, and does not know that his own soul is robbed.

וְלִגְזֹל הָאֱמֶת יַחְשֹׁב בְּמִלָּיו וְלֹא יָדַע הֲכִי נַפְשׁוֹ גְזוּלָה.

320

He toils in vain to justify his opponents' cause, and so a soul may toil for another⚠24.

Uncertain renderings

  • ⚠1 וְאֶפֶס עָזוּב וְעָצוּר — Deut 32:36 "וְאֶפֶס עָצוּר וְעָזוּב", an obscure phrase ("none shut up or left free"). Here it seems to mean that nothing at all was left to give.
  • ⚠2 וְלֹא יִפֹּל צְרוֹר אָרֶץ — Amos 9:9 "not a pebble (צְרוֹר) falls to the ground". צְרוֹר is also a bundle or purse, as in the "bundles of money" above: not one purse is let go.
  • ⚠3 שְׁנֵי זְקֵנִים. אֲרֻכֵּי זְקָנִים — A pun that English cannot keep: זְקֵנִים "elders" and זְקָנִים "beards".
  • ⚠4 וְעַל מִצְחוֹ טוֹטֶפֶת — טוֹטֶפֶת is the word of the tefillin (Exod 13:16), which are not worn on the Sabbath. It is not clear if the joke is his ignorance or only his showy dress.
  • ⚠5 אֲשֶׁר יָצַר אֶת הָאָדָם בְּהֵמָה — The morning blessing "Asher yatsar" says "who formed man in wisdom (בְּחָכְמָה)" (b. Berakhot 60b). He says בְּהֵמָה, "a beast".
  • ⚠6 גַּם מִזֵּיתִים חֲנֹךְ עַבְדֶּךָ — Ps 19:14 "גַּם מִזֵּדִים חֲשֹׂךְ עַבְדֶּךָ" (keep back Your servant from presumptuous sins). He reads מִזֵּדִים as מִזֵּיתִים "from olives" and חֲשֹׂךְ as חֲנֹךְ "train, dedicate". The result has no sense.
  • ⚠7 חֶרֶב חַדִּים יַשְׂבִּיעֶךָּ — Ps 147:14 "חֵלֶב חִטִּים" (the fat of the wheat). With one letter changed in each word (ל to ר, ט to ד) it becomes "a sword of sharp ones": a blessing turns into a threat.
  • ⚠8 הַמְכַסֶּה שָׁמַיִם בְּגָדִים — Ps 147:8 "בְּעָבִים" (with clouds). He reads בְּגָדִים "clothes"; the words look alike in Hebrew script.
  • ⚠9 יִשְׂמַח יִשְׁמָעֵאל בְּעֵשָׂו — Ps 149:2 "יִשְׂמַח יִשְׂרָאֵל בְּעֹשָׂיו" (Israel in his Maker). He says Ishmael and Esau, the usual names for Islam and Christendom: the cantor makes Israel's enemies rejoice.
  • ⚠10 הַלְלוּהוּ גְבִינִים וְעוּגָה — Ps 150:4 "בְּמִנִּים וְעוּגָב" (with strings and pipe). He says גְבִינִים "cheeses" and עוּגָה "a cake".
  • ⚠11 וּבְיָדְךָ לְגַדֵּף וּלְהַזִּיק לַכֹּל — 1 Chr 29:12, said in the morning service: "לְגַדֵּל וּלְחַזֵּק" (to make great and to strengthen). He says לְגַדֵּף "to blaspheme" and לְהַזִּיק "to do harm". The Hebrew has no "he said" in this line.
  • ⚠12 כִּיקַר כָּרִים וְאֶשְׁכָּרִים — כָּרִים are lambs (Deut 32:14); אֶשְׁכָּר is a gift or tribute (Ps 72:10, Ezek 27:15). The sense is: as precious as the offerings.
  • ⚠13 הַשִּׁירוֹת וְהַקְּרוֹבוֹת — A qerovah (plural qerovot) is a cycle of piyyutim set into the blessings of the Amidah, said by the cantor. The whole debate is on these additions to the fixed prayer.
  • ⚠14 שִׁירֵי הַקְּדֵשִׁים — A pun: not the songs of the holy ones (קְדוֹשִׁים) but of the קְדֵשִׁים, the cult prostitutes of 1 Kgs 14:24.
  • ⚠15 יְדַמּוּ עִדִּים. לַעֲדָיִים — A pun: עִדִּים are the soiled rags of Isa 64:5 ("בֶגֶד עִדִּים"); עֲדָיִים are ornaments.
  • ⚠16 וְהַחַזָּן לֹא הוֹצִיאָהוּ יְדֵי חוֹבָה — A legal term: the prayer leader's prayer discharges the duty of one who cannot pray for himself (m. Rosh Hashanah 4:9). This cantor's prayer does not.
  • ⚠17 וּקְהִלּוֹת מַעֲרָב — "The West" (מַעֲרָב) is probably North Africa (the Maghreb). "Greece" (יָוָן) is the Byzantine lands.
  • ⚠18 תְּפִלָתוֹ תִּפְלָה — A pun: his prayer (תְּפִלָּה) is folly (תִּפְלָה, as in Job 1:22, Jer 23:13).
  • ⚠19 וּתְהִלָּתוֹ תָּהֳלָה — A pun: his praise (תְּהִלָּה) is error (תָּהֳלָה, Job 4:18).
  • ⚠20 וְהוּא חוֹבֵר חָבֶר — Deut 18:11 "וְחֹבֵר חָבֶר", one who casts spells, among the forbidden diviners. A pun on his title הֶחָבֵר, "the Ḥaver" (an associate, a scholar). Nothing more is known here about this Benjamin of Arbel.
  • ⚠21 עַד זָרַק הַקִּיתוֹן — Literally "until he threw the jug". The idiom is not clear; the sense from context: his folly stayed hidden until he poured it out in writing.
  • ⚠22 בְּפָרָשַׁת וַיְחַל משֶׁה — Va-yeḥal Moshe (Exod 32:11-14 and 34:1-10) is the Torah reading of fast days. It holds the thirteen attributes, "And the LORD passed before him" (Exod 34:6), which are said in every selihah.
  • ⚠23 וּפָתַח פִּיו וְקָרָא לָהּ גְּאֻלָּה — Literally "called redemption to it": the release of what was held, as of land or a pledge (Lev 25:24). The folly shut up in his heart went free when he spoke.
  • ⚠24 וְיֵשׁ נֶפֶשׁ לְזוּלָתָה עֲמֵלָה — A turn of Prov 16:26 "נֶפֶשׁ עָמֵל עָמְלָה לּוֹ" (the worker's soul toils for itself): his toil helps only others. The reading of "his opponents" (מְרִיבָיו) in the first half is uncertain.
  • ⚠25 בְּמוֹתוֹ יִמְצְאָה מֵאֵל גְּמוּלָה — "His due" (גְּמוּלָה) can be reward or punishment. The next lines show punishment, so "this" is probably the qerovah: whoever calls it a duty.
