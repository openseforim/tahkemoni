שער שני

בְּשֶבַח הַמּוֹכִיחַ, הַמַּהְבִּיל עִנְיְנֵי הָעוֹלָם וּמֵפִיץ וּמַדִּיחַ

The Second Gate

A preacher in the isles of the sea: a sermon on the false bride of this world, the grave and the hypocrites, and the preacher is Ḥever in disguise

Gate 2 on Project Ben-Yehuda ↗

About this text

353 lines. One row is one rhyme unit of the rhymed prose, or one line of verse (its two halves set apart). References in [gray brackets] are the translator's identifications of biblical phrases. ⚠ marks an uncertain rendering. Each line number is a link to that line. Hebrew text: Project Ben-Yehuda.

1

Thus says Heman the Ezrahite: I have surely heard [Jer 31:18]

נְאֻם הֵימָן הָאֶזְרָחִי: שָמוֹעַ שָמַעְתִי.

2

that there was a wise preacher in the isles of the sea,

כִּי הָיָה מוֹכִיחַ חָכָם בְּאִיֵּי הַיָּם.

3

who could still the waves of eloquence and their breakers⚠1.

יַשְבִּיחַ גַּלֵּי הַמְּלִיצוֹת וְדָכְיָם.

4

And with his words he took from hearts their hardness,

וְיָסִיר בְּמִלָּיו מִן הַלְּבָבוֹת קָשְׁיָם.

5

and from souls their chill,

וּמִן הַנְּפָשׁוֹת קִרְיָם.

6

until their rebellion was gathered in [Num 12:15]⚠2.

עַד הֵאָסֵף מִרְיָם.

7

With his rebukes he crumbled the rocks of the hearts and made the eyes shed tears:

יְפוֹרֵר בְּתוֹכְחוֹתָיו סַלְעֵי הַלְּבָבוֹת וְיַזִיל דִּמְעוֹת הָעֵינַיִם.

8

behold, he struck the rock and the waters gushed out [Ps 78:20].

הֵן הִכָּה צוּר וַיָּזוֹבוּ מָיִם.

9

And when I heard the noise of the people as they shouted [Exod 32:17],

וְכַאֲשֶר שָׁמַעְתִּי אֶת קוֹל הָעָם בְּרֵעֹה.

10

telling one another of the preacher's insight and his knowledge,

מְסַפְּרִים אִישׁ לְרֵעוֹ שֵׂכֶל הַמּוֹכִיחַ וְדֵעוֹ.

11

I longed to subdue by his rebukes my harlot inclination,

נִכְסַפְתִּי לְהַכְנִיעַ בְּתוֹכְחוֹתָיו יִצְרִי הַזּוֹנֶה.

12

which turns to its pleasures,

אֲשֶׁר לְתַעֲנוּגוֹתָיו פּוֹנֶה.

13

and encamps at the Graves of Lust [Num 11:34],

וּבְקִבְרוֹת הַתַּאֲוָה חוֹנֶה.

14

so that I might break its pride and bring it under,

לְמַעַן אֶשְׁבֹּר גְּאוֹנוֹ וְאֶכְבְּשֶׁנּוּ.

15

perhaps I shall be able to smite it and drive it out [Num 22:6].

אוּלַי אוּכַל נַכֶּה בּוֹ וַאֲגָרְשֶנּוּ.

16

So I arose and journeyed from my land,

וְקַמְתִּי וְנָסַעְתִּי מְאַרְצִי.

17

and left the pasture where I lay,

וְעָזַבְתִּי נְוֵה רִיבְצִי.

18

and rode upon a ship.

וְרָכַבְתִּי עַל צִי.

19

And when I reached the districts of the preacher and his pasture lands,

וְכַאֲשֶר הִגַעְתִּי אֶל גְלִילוֹת הַמּוֹכִיחַ וּמִגְרָשָם.

20

the place where he was encamped,

אֶל הַמָּקוֹם אֲשֶר הוּא חוֹנֶה שָׁם.

21

I came into the house of prayer

בָּאתִי בְּבֵית הַתְּפִילָּה.

22

with the congregation,

עִִם הַקְּהִילָּה.

23

and set out to God my praise.

וְעָרַכְתִּי לָאֵל תְּהִילָּה.

24

And I looked, and behold, the preacher

וָאֵרָא וְהִנֵּה הַמּוֹכִיחַ.

25

from the place of his habitation gazed out [Ps 33:14];

מִמְּכוֹן שִבְתּוֹ הִשְגִּיחַ.

26

he shouted, yes, he roared [Isa 42:13],

וְיָרִיעַ אַף יַצְרִיחַ.

27

and with his words he rinsed away the blood of the sinners [Isa 4:4].

וּבְמִלָּיו דְּמֵי הַחוֹטְאִים יָדִיחַ.

28

And he answered and said:

וְיַּעַן וַיֹּאמַר:

29

"To you, O men, I call [Prov 8:4]:

אֲלֵיכֶם אִישִים אֶקְרָא.

30

remember the awesome God,

זִכְרוּ הָאֵל הַנּוֹרָא.

31

and awake, you who are asleep to knowledge,

וְהָקִיצוּ יְשֵנֵי דֵעָה.

32

and put away your wine [1 Sam 1:14], you who are struck down by error,

וְהָסִירוּ יֵינְכֶם הֲלוּמֵי תוֹעָה.

33

who sell the life of the world to come for the life of an hour.

הַמּוֹכְרִים חַיֵי עוֹלָם בְּחַיֵּי שָׁעָה.

34

Know that this is not the place of rest.

דְּעוּ כִּי לֹא זֹאת הַמַּרְגֵּעָה.

35

Therefore make ready for the day of the journey,

לָכֵן הִתְעַתְּדוּ לְיוֹם הַנְּסִיעָה.

36

and turn, go down from the hill, and arise, go out to the valley [Ezek 3:22],

וְסוּרוּ רְדוּ מִן הַגִּבְעָה וְקוּמוּ צְאוּ אֶל הַבִּקְעָה.

37

the place where your fathers dwell,

מְקוֹם מִשְׁכַּן אֲבוֹתֵיכֶם.

38

and the land of your sojournings.

וְאֶרֶץ מְגוּרוֹתֵיכֶם.

39

Do you know

הַיְדַעְתֶּם.

40

that you are strangers and sojourners [Lev 25:23]

כִּי גֵרִים וְתוֹשָׁבִים אַתֶּם.

41

in a land that eats up its inhabitants [Num 13:32],

בְּאֶרֶץ אוֹכֶלֶת יוֹשְׁבֶיהָ.

42

and is the enemy of those who love her?

וְאוֹיֶבֶת לְאוֹהֲבֶיהָ.

43

Her taste is bitter,

טַעְמָהּ מַר.

44

and in her hand is a cup of foaming wine [Ps 75:9];

וּבְיָדָהּ כּוֹס חָמֶר.

45

for fear of it all flesh bristles [Ps 119:120],

מִפַּחְדוֹ כָּל בָּשַׂר סָמַר.

46

and every heart is in turmoil [Lam 1:20].

וְכָל לֵב חֲמַרְמַר.

47

They called her Tevel, the world,

קְרָאוּהָ תֵבֵל.

48

and she is as her name: tevel, perversion [Lev 18:23]⚠3.

וְהִיא כִשְמָהּ תֶּבֶל.

49

Her beginning is mourning,

רֹאשָהּ אַבֵל.

50

and her end is pangs and pain [Isa 13:8].

וְסוֹפָהּ צִיר וָחַבֵל.

51

She beguiles you with her ornaments,

תַּשִיא אֶתְכֵם בְּעֶדְיָהּ.

52

and dotes on you with her beauty.

וְתַעְגֵּב עֲלֵיכֶם בְּיָפְיָהּ.

53

She is like a bride who adorns herself with her jewels [Isa 61:10]:

תִּדְמֶה לְכַלָּה תַּעֲדֶה כֵלֶיהָ.

54

ruin is her coronets,

הַכִּלְיוֹן כֶּלִילֶיהָ.

55

and treachery her robes,

וְהַמַּעַל מְעִילֶיהָ.

56

and stumbling blocks her skirts,

וְהַמִּכְשׁוֹלִים שׁוּלֶיהָ.

57

and the twistings of misfortune her cords,

וְנַפְתּוּלֵי הַקּוֹרוֹת פְּתִילֶיהָ.

58

and the chambers of Sheol her palaces [Prov 7:27],

וְחַדְרֵי שְׁאוֹל הֵיכָלֶיהָ.

59

and the blood of the innocent the blood of her maidenhood.

וּדְמֵי נְקִיִּים דְמֵי בְתוּלֶיהָ.

60

Every day she sends away her husbands,

בְּכָל יוֹם מְגָרֶשֶת בְּעָלֶיהָ.

61

and kills every man who comes in to her.

וְהוֹרֶגֶת כָּל הַבָּא עָלֶיהָ.

62

Her dawns are darkness,

שְחָרֶיהָ חֲשֵכִים.

63

and her lights are snuffed out,

וּמְאוֹרֶיהָ נִדְעָכִים.

64

and her wise men are perplexed [Exod 14:3],

וּנְבוֹנֶיהָ נְבוּכִים.

65

and her kings brought low,

וּמְלָכֶיהָ נְמוּכִים.

66

and her merry ones weep,

וּשְׂמֵחֶיהָ בוֹכִים.

67

and her gardens are tangled thorns [Nah 1:10],

וְגַּנֶיהָ סִירִים סְבוּכִים.

68

and her wines are mixed with wormwood.

וְיֶינֵיהָ בְּלַעֲנָה מְסוּכִים.

69

She humbles her kings and destroys her princes,

תַּשְפִּיל מְלָכֶיהָ וְתַשְׁמִיד נְסִיכֶיהָ.

70

a swift young camel running back and forth on her ways [Jer 2:23].

בִּכְרָה קַלָּה מְשַׂרֶכֶת דְּרָכֶיהָ.

71

Her good is uprooted,

טוֹבָתָהּ נֶעֱקֶרֶת.

72

and her sickness stirred up [Ps 39:3],

וּמַחֲלָתָהּ נֶעְכֶּרֶת.

73

and her hope is a lie,

וְתִקְוָתָהּ מְשַׁקֶרֶת.

74

and her cup shatters,

וְכּוֹסָהּ מְשַׁבֶּרֶת.

75

and her shame remains from generation to generation.

וְחֶרְפָּתָה מִדוֹר לְדוֹר נִשְׁאֶרֶת.

76

And now, you who trust in despised wealth,

וְעַתָּה הַבּוֹטְחִים עַל הַהוֹן הַנִבְזֶה.

77

let not this little honey be sweet to you,

אַל יִמְתַּק לָכֶם מְעַט הַדְּבַש הַזֶּה.

78

for beneath it is the venom of asps [Deut 32:33],

כִּי תַחְתַּיו רֹאשׁ פְּתָנִים.

79

and sicknesses evil and lasting [Deut 28:59].

וָחֳלָיִים רָעִים וְנֶאֱמָנִים.

80

Do you not know that the end of riches

הֲלֹא יְדַעָתֶּם כִּי סוֹף הַהון.

81

is dismay,

תִּמָּהוֹן.

82

and the outcome of money

וָאַחֲרִית הַמָּמוֹן.

83

is ruin; and the goal of silver

שִׁמָמוֹן וְתַכְלִית הַכֶּסֶף.

84

is wrath,

קֶצֶף.

85

and the root of gold

וְעִקַּר הַזָּהָב.

86

is flame,

לַהַב.

87

and the likeness of wealth

וְדִימְיוֹן הַעֹשֶׁר.

88

is an eagle⚠4?

נָשֶּׁר.

89

If today you seize it with your hands,

אִם הַיוֹם תִּתְפְּשׂוּהוּ בְיָדַיִם.

90

tomorrow it will make itself wings [Prov 23:5].

מָחָר יֵעֲשה לוֹ כְנָפַיִם.

91

Therefore do not think of this world as your dwelling house, in which you may rejoice;

לָכֵן אַל תַּחְשְׁבוּ הָעוֹלָם הַזֶּה כְּבֵית מוֹשַׁבְכֶם אֲשֶׁר בּוֹ תִּשְׂמְחוּ.

92

it is rather an inn, where the guests have gathered:

אֲבָל הוּא מָלוֹן אֵלָיו הַאוֹרְחִים נִסְפְּחוּ.

93

the morning was light, and the men were sent away [Gen 44:3].

הַבֹּקֶר אוֹר וְהָאֲנָשִים שֻׁלְחוּ.

94

And now, my sons, save yourselves, and do not be slack;

וְעַתָּה בָנַי הִנָּצִלוּ וְאַל תֵּעָצְלוּ.

95

keep apart, and take warning,

וְהִנָזְּרוּ וְהִזָּהַרוּ.

96

and purify yourselves,

וְהִטָּהֲרוּ.

97

and draw near⚠5,

וְהִתְנַגְּשוּ.

98

and sanctify yourselves [Josh 3:5], and kindle within you

וִהִתְקַדְשוּ וְהַבְעִירוּ בַקְרָבִים.

99

flames,

לְהָבִים.

100

and draw from the rocks of your ribs

וְהָקִירוּ מִסַּלְעֵי הַצְלָעוֹת.

101

tears [Jer 6:7],

דְּמָעוֹת.

102

and stir up against your hearts

וְהָעִירוּ עִם הַלְבָבוֹת.

103

battles.

קְרָבוֹת.

104

Make your intellect your prince⚠6,

וְשִׂכְלְכֶם הַשִׂירוּ.

105

and put away your folly,

וְסִכְלְכֶם הָסִירוּ.

106

and change your inclination,

וְיִצְרְכֶם הָמִירוּ.

107

and exalt your uprightness,

וְיָשְרְכֶם הַאֲמִירוּ.

108

and call to mind your transgression,

וּפִשְעֲכֶם הָזְכִּירוּ.

109

and acknowledge your wickedness.

וְרִשְעֲכֶם הָכִּירוּ.

110

And bring to your hearts the bitter and dreadful day,

וְהַעֲבִירוּ עַל לְבַבְכֶם הַיּוֹם הַמַּר וְהַנּוֹרָא.

111

a day of anguish as of a woman bearing her first child [Jer 4:31],

יוֹם צָרָה כְּמַבְכִּירָה.

112

a day when the books are opened [Dan 7:10],

יוֹם יִפָּתְחוּ הַסְפָרִים.

113

and the secrets laid bare;

וְיֵחָשְׂפוּ הַסְתָּרִים.

114

a day when sins will be bars upon the neck,

יוֹם יִהְיוּ הַעֲוֹנוֹת עַל הַצַּוַּאר מוֹטוֹת.

115

and death will stand in a narrow place where there is no way to turn [Num 22:26];

וְיַעֲמֹד הַמָוֶת בִּמְקוֹם צַר אֲשֶר אֵין דֶּרֶך לִנְטוֹת.

116

a day when a man falls sick with the sickness of which he will die [2 Kgs 13:14];

יוֹם יֶחֱלֶה הָאָדָם אֶת חָלְיוֹ אֲשֶר יָמּוּת בּוֹ.

117

a day when he burns in the coals of his own spark;

יוֹם יִבְעַר בְּגַחֲלֵי שְׁבִיבוֹ.

118

a day when his soul loathes the food it craved [Job 33:20],

יוֹם תְּתַעֵב נַפְשוֹ מַאֲכַל תַּאֲוָה.

119

and he is sick of every lovely delight;

וְיָקוּץ בְּכָל חֶמְדָּה נָאוָה.

120

a day when he chokes on his spittle,

יוֹם יֵחָנֵק בְּרֻקוֹ.

121

and his throat is stopped from eating the bread of his portion [Prov 30:8],

וְיִסָּתֵם גְּרוֹנוֹ מִלֶאֱכֹל לֶחֶם חֻקוֹ.

122

and every food and dainty

וְכָל מַאֲכָל וּמַטְעָם.

123

he refuses to swallow;

מֵאֵן בִּלְעָם.

124

and he receives his due;

וְיִקַּח גְּמוּלוֹ.

125

the day he is buried,

יוֹם יְהִי נִקְבָּר.

126

and all his work,

וְכָל פָּעֳלוֹ.

127

that he gathered and heaped up,

אֲשֶׁר אָסַף וְצָבַר.

128

will find him in a desert land [Deut 32:10].

יִמְצָאֵהוּ בְּאֶרֶץ מִדְבָּר.

129

Awake, you drunkards [Joel 1:5],

הָקִיצוּ שִׁיכּוֹרִים.

130

and wail, you mighty men!

וְהֵילִילוּ גִּבּוֹרִים.

131

What have you to do with love of the cursed world?

מַה לָּכֶם וּלֱאַהֲבַת תֵּבֵל הָאֲרוּרָה.

132

And why do you suck the breasts of a barren woman?

וּמַדוּעַ תִּנְקוּ שְׁדֵי עֲקָרָה.

133

You hold on to her, and she flees from you;

תַּחֲזִיקוּ בָהּ וְהִיא בוֹרַחַת מִכֶּם.

134

you draw near to her, and she drives you away.

וְתִקְרְבוּ אֵלֶיהָ וְהִיא מְגָרֶשֶׁת אֶתְכֶם.

135

Do you not know that on chaos are her foundations and her counsels,

Uncertain renderings

  • ⚠1 יַשְבִּיחַ גַּלֵּי הַמְּלִיצוֹת וְדָכְיָם — Words of Ps 89:10 (בְּשׂוֹא גַלָּיו אַתָּה תְשַׁבְּחֵם, "you still its waves") and Ps 93:3 (דָּכְיָם, "their pounding"). The sense is that he masters eloquence as God masters the sea. A reading "he makes the waves of eloquence excellent" (later Hebrew שבח, improve) is also possible.
  • ⚠2 עַד הֵאָסֵף מִרְיָם — Num 12:15 "until Miriam was brought in again". The edition points מִרְיָם as "their rebellion" (מְרִי with the suffix), to rhyme with קָשְׁיָם and קִרְיָם.
  • ⚠3 קְרָאוּהָ תֵבֵל. וְהִיא כִשְמָהּ תֶּבֶל — תֵּבֵל (world) and תֶּבֶל (perversion, Lev 18:23, 20:12) are written with the same letters. "She is as her name" echoes 1 Sam 25:25.
  • ⚠4 סוֹף הַהון. תִּמָּהוֹן ... וְדִימְיוֹן הַעֹשֶׁר. נָשֶּׁר — Each kind of wealth rhymes with its fate: הוֹן / תִּמָּהוֹן (dismay, Deut 28:28), מָמוֹן / שִׁמָּמוֹן (ruin, Ezek 4:16), כֶּסֶף / קֶצֶף, זָהָב / לַהַב, עֹשֶׁר / נֶשֶׁר. The eagle prepares Prov 23:5, quoted in the next lines. The Hebrew line "is ruin; and the goal of silver" has no full stop after שִׁמָּמוֹן.
  • ⚠5 וְהִתְנַגְּשוּ — Taken as "draw near" (to God), from נגשׁ. The form is unusual; in later Hebrew התנגשׁ is "collide".
  • ⚠6 וְשִׂכְלְכֶם הַשִׂירוּ — Read as a causative of שׂרר ("make it rule"), as the sin dot suggests. With shin it would be "make your intellect sing".
  • ⚠7 הַבֶּגֶד בְּגָדֶיהֶ — בֶּגֶד is both "treachery" and "garment": "treachery is her garments".
  • ⚠8 וְהַתְּמוּנוֹת הָאֲמוּנוֹת — Taken from Lam 4:5 הָאֱמֻנִים עֲלֵי תוֹלָע ("those reared in scarlet"). "Faithful (true) images" is also possible.
  • ⚠9 בִּלְשׁוֹנָם תְּפִלָה. וּבְלִבָּם תִּפְלָה — תְּפִלָּה (prayer) and תִּפְלָה (folly, Job 1:22) differ only in their vowels. The next pair rhymes תַּחֲנוּנִים with תַּזְנוּנִים.
  • ⚠10 וְנִסְתַּרְתֶּם בְּנַחַל כְּרִית — 1 Kgs 17:3, where Elijah hides and is fed. כְּרִית also suggests כָּרֵת, being cut off, the punishment of the soul.
  • ⚠11 וּמִצֵּל הַקִּירוֹת אֶל הַקּוֹרוֹת — A play on קִירוֹת (walls) and קוֹרוֹת (beams). The beams are perhaps the boards of the bier or coffin; compare בְּצֵל קֹרָתִי, Gen 19:8. The sense is uncertain.
  • ⚠12 וְאַחַר נָסְעוּ הָעָם מֵחֲצֵרוֹת — Num 12:16, a place name; here it is also read as "from the courtyards", the homes they leave. The next lines take up חֲצֵרִים and חֲצַר מָוֶת.
  • ⚠13 וְיִשְׂמַח לִבִּי בְּחֶבְרָתוֹ — A pun on חֶבֶר (Ḥever) and חֶבְרָתוֹ (his company), as at the end of gate 40.
