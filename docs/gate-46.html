שער ארבעים ושישה

סִפּוּר אַנְשֵׁי הַמְּדִינוֹת וּמַעֲלוֹתֵיהֶם. וְחֶסְרוֹנָם וּגְרִיעוּת מִדּוֹתֵיהֶם

The Forty-Sixth Gate

Ḥever the Kenite's tour of the Jewish communities of Spain, Provence, Egypt, the Land of Israel, Syria and Iraq: praise of their generous and learned men, and satire of the wicked and the mean

Gate 46 on Project Ben-Yehuda ↗

About this text

References in [gray brackets] are the translator's identifications of biblical phrases. ⚠ marks an uncertain rendering. Each line number is a link to that line. Hebrew text: Project Ben-Yehuda.

1

Thus says Heman the Ezrahite: I journeyed from Edinah⚠1

נְאֻם הֵימָן הָאֶזְרָחִי: נָסַעְתִּי מֵעֲדִינָה.

2

to Elam the province⚠2.

לְעֵילָם הַמְּדִינָה.

3

And while I was some days on the road, walking and striding on,

וּבְעוֹדִי בִקְצָת הַיָּמִים אֶתְהַלֵּךְ עַל פָּנַי וְאֶצְעַד.

4

behold, a caravan of Ishmaelites came from Gilead [Gen 37:25].

וְהִנֵּה אֹרְחַת יִשְׁמְעֵאלִים בָּאָה מִגִּלְעָד.

5

They pitched their tents in the plain,

תָּקְעוּ בָעֲרָבָה אָהֳלֵיהֶם.

6

and stretched out their shade against the heat of the sun.

וְנָטוּ מֵחֹם הַשֶּׁמֶשׁ צִלְלֵיהֶם.

7

And while I was going out and coming in among them,

וּבְעוֹד אֲנִי בְתוֹכָם אֵצֵא וְאָבוֹא.

8

behold, Ḥever the Kenite came in.

וְהִנֵּה חֶבֶר הַקֵּינִי בָא מִבּוֹא.

9

When I saw his face, my face shone,

וְכִרְאוֹתִי פָנָיו צָהֲלוּ פָנָי.

10

and my eyes lit up [1 Sam 14:27].

וַיֵּאוֹרוּ עֵינָי.

11

I said to him: "Peace be your coming!

אָמַרְתִּי לוֹ: שָׁלוֹם בּוֹאֶךָ.

12

And from where have you set out?"

וּמֵאַיִן מוֹצָאֶךָ.

13

He said: "From the land of Gilead I have come,

אָמַר: מֵאֶרֶץ גִּלְעָד בִּיאָתִי.

14

and the land of Elam is my aim."

וּלְאֶרֶץ עֵילָם מְגַמָּתִי.

15

I said: "Have you seen all the holy congregations

אָמַרְתִּי: הֲרָאִיתָ קְהִלּוֹת הַקֹּדֶש כֻּלָּם.

16

that are in the West, in Egypt and in Elam?"

אֲשֶׁר בְּמַעֲרָב וּבְמִצְרַיִם וּבְעֵילָם.

17

He said: "There was no town too high for me [Deut 2:36] that I did not pass through,

אָמַר: לֹא שָׂגְבָה קִרְיָה אֲשֶׁר בָּהּ לֹא עָבַרְתִּי.

18

and I searched out the ways of its people."

וּמִדּוֹת אֲנָשֶׁיהָ חָקַרְתִּי.

19

I said: "Tell me, pray, of the lands you have passed through,

אָמַרְתִּי: הַגֶּד-נָא-לִי הָאֲרָצוֹת אֲשֶׁר עָבַרְתָּ עֲלֵיהֶם.

20

and of the ways of the pious who are in them,

וּמִדּוֹת הַחֲסִידִים אֲשֶׁר בָּהֶם.

21

and the appearance of the wheels and their work [Ezek 1:16]⚠3;

וּמַרְאֵה הָאוֹפַנִּים וּמַעֲשֵׂיהֶם.

22

and after that tell me the matters of the wicked among them:

אַחֲרֵי כֵן הוֹדִיעֵנִי עִנְיְנֵי הָרָעִים מֵהֶם.

23

they shall set out last by their standards [Num 2:31]."

בָּאַחֲרוֹנָה יִסְעוּ לְדִגְלֵיהֶם.

24

He said: "Know for sure that all the congregations I saw are blameless and upright,

אָמַר: יָדוֹעַ תֵּדַע כִּי כָל הַקְּהִלּוֹת אֲשֶׁר רָאִיתִי תְּמִימִים וִישָׁרִים.

25

and counted happy for their deeds.

וּבְמַעֲשֵׂיהֶם מְאֻשָּׁרִים.

26

The fear of heaven is their yoke,

וְעֹל יִרְאַת שָׁמַיִם עֻלָּם.

27

and the shade of humility is their shade.

וְצֵל הָעֲנָוָה צִלָּם.

28

But not all of them;

אַךְ לֹא כֻלָּם.

29

for many of them are pious in their work,

כִּי רַבִּים מֵהֶם חֲסִידִים בְּמִפְעָלָם.

30

and some of them wicked in their doings:

וּקְצָתָם רָעִים בְּמַעֲלָלָם.

31

these to everlasting life,

אֵלֶּה לְחַיֵּי עוֹלָם.

32

and these to reproaches and everlasting abhorrence [Dan 12:2].

וְאֵלֶּה לַחֲרָפוֹת לְדֵרְאוֹן עוֹלָם.

33

They are divided into two kinds,

הֵם נֶחֱלָקִים לִשְׁנֵי מִינִים.

34

like the two baskets of figs [Jer 24:1]:

כִּשְׁנֵי דוּדָאֵי תְאֵנִים.

35

the good are good to eat and to be filled,

הַטּוֹבוֹת טוֹבוֹת לֶאֱכֹל וְלִשְׂבֹּעַ.

36

and the bad cannot be eaten, they are so bad [Jer 24:3].

וְהָרָעוֹת לֹא תֵאָכַלְנָה מֵרֹעַ.

37

And now I will name for you the good and the bad,

וְהִנֵּה אַזְכִּיר לְךָ הַטּוֹבִים וְהָרָעִים.

38

the churls and the noble [Isa 32:5],

וְהַנְּבָלִים וְהַשּׁוֹעִים.

39

so that the deeds of the noble

לְמַעַן יִהְיוּ מַעֲשֵׂה הַשּׁוֹעִים.

40

may stand fixed from generation to generation,

מִדּוֹר לְדוֹר קְבוּעִים.

41

and the deeds of the wicked

וּמַעֲשֵׂה הָרָעִים.

42

be known from the east to the sea."

מִמִּזְרָח וְעַד יָם יְדוּעִים.

43

And he took up his parable and said:

וַיִּשָּׂא מְשָׁלוֹ וַיֹּאמַר:

44

My heart, when you come into a precious garden, every tree that bears fruit, tend it and keep it [Gen 2:15].

לִבִּי בְּעֵת בּוֹאָךְ אֶל גַּן יְקָר כָּל עֵץ עֹשֶׂה-פְּרִי אוֹתוֹ תַעֲבֹד וְשָׁמָרְתָּ.

45

Only a tree that you know for sure is not a tree for food, destroy it and cut it down [Deut 20:20].

רַק עֵץ אֲשֶׁר תֵּדַע בֶּאֱמֶת הֲכִי לֹא הוּא עֵץ מַאֲכָל אוֹתוֹ תַשְׁחִית וְכָרָתָּ.

46

And he answered and said: "It was told, fully told to me [Ruth 2:11] in the days of my youth, that Spain was a delight to the eyes [Gen 3:6],

וַיַּעַן וַיֹּאמַר: הַגֵּד הֻגַּד לִי בִּימֵי נְעוּרַי כִּי סְפָרַד הָיְתָה תַאֲוָה לָעֵינָיִם.

47

its light like the sun in the midst of heaven,

וְאוֹרָהּ כַּשֶּׁמֶשׁ בַּחֲצִי הַשָּׁמָיִם.

48

the spice of its dust myrrh to the nostrils,

וְרֶקַח עֲפָרָהּ מוֹר אַפָּיִם.

49

the taste of its fruits like honey to the palate [Ps 22:16],

וְטַעַם מְגָדֶיהָ כִּדְבַשׁ לַמַּלְקוֹחַיִם.

50

its air the life of souls,

וַאֲוִירָהּ חַיֵּי נְשָׁמוֹת.

51

its soil the choicest of soils.

וְאַדְמָתָהּ מִבְחַר אַדָמוֹת.

52

It is the radiance of souls,

וְהִיא זֹהַר הַנְּפָשִׁים.

53

the joy of God and of men [Judg 9:13];

שִׂמְחַת אֱלֹהִים וַאֲנָשִׁים.

54

the flowers of its gardens are like the stars of the skies,

וּפִרְחֵי גַנֶּיהָ כְּכוֹכְבֵי הַשְּׁחָקִים.

55

and its land the rose of Sharon, the lily of the valleys [Song 2:1].

וְאַרְצָהּ חֲבַצֶּלֶת הַשָּׁרוֹן שׁוֹשַׁנַּת הָעֲמָקִים.

56

And the wind of longing carried me,

וַתִּשָּׂאֵנִי רוּחַ הַתְּשׁוּקָה.

57

the longed-for wind,

הַחֲשׁוּקָה.

58

to beat across furrows,

לִרְקֹעַ תְּלָמִים.

59

and to cleave lakes and seas.

וְלִקְרֹע אֲגַמִּים וְיַמִּים.

60

I went through deserts,

וְהָלַכְתִּי מִדְבָּרִים.

61

split breakers,

וּבָקַעְתִּי מִשְׁבָּרִים.

62

crossed fords,

וְעָבַרְתִּי מַעֲבָרִים.

63

and set Spain as my goal,

וְשַׂמְתִּי לִסְפָרַד מְגַמָּתִי.

64

far from my own land.

מֵאַדְמָתִי.

65

And when I came to it,

וְכַאֲשֶׁר בָּאתִי אֵלֶיהָ.

66

and encamped in its borders,

וְחָנִיתִי בִגְבוּלֶיהָ.

67

I breathed myrrh and aloes [Prov 7:17] from among its tents⚠4,

שָׁאַפְתִּי מוֹר אֲהָלִים מִבֵּין אֹהָלֶיהָ.

68

and I found the praises too few for the goodness of its deeds,

וּמָצָאתִי הַשְּׁבָחִים מְעַטִּים בְּטוּב מַעֲלַלֶיהָ.

69

and tongues too short for the greatness of its works.

וְהַלְּשׁוֹנוֹת קְצָרוֹת בְּרֹב מַעֲלָלֶיהָ.

70

I roamed in it from corner to corner,

וְשׁוֹטַטְתִּי בָּהּ מִפִּנָּה לְפִנָּה.

71

and from city to city.

וּמִמְּדִינָה לִמְדִינָה.

72

I passed through the lands of the Ishmaelites,

וְעָבַרְתִּי בְאַרְצוֹת הַיִּשְׁמְעֵאלִים.

73

and from there I went out to the lands of the uncircumcised,

וּמִשָּׁם יָצָאתִי לְאַרְצוֹת הָעֲרֵלִים.

74

where the Israelites dwell.

אֲשֶׁר שָׁם יִשְׁכְּנוּ הַיִּשְׂרְאֵלִים.

75

And I came to the princely city,

וּבָאתִי אֶל עִיר הַנְּסוּכָה.

76

that is Toledo, the royal city,

הִיא טוּלִיטוּלָה עִיר הַמְּלוּכָה.

77

whose garment is the grace of rule, and discipline her ornament,

אֲשֶׁר לְבוּשָׁהּ חֵן הַמִּשְׂרָה וְהַמּוּסָר עֶדְיָהּ.

78

to show the peoples and the princes her beauty [Esth 1:11].

לְהַרְאוֹת הָעַמִּים וְהַשָּׂרִים אֶת יָפְיָהּ.

79

For there the tribes go up, the tribes of the Lord [Ps 122:4];

שֶׁשָּׁם עָלוּ שְׁבָטִים שִׁבְטֵי יָהּ.

80

and how many a fair castle is within it,

וְכַמָּה בְתוֹכָהּ טִירָה יְפֵה-פִיָּה.

81

that shames the lights of heaven with the splendor of its beauty and glory,

תַּחְפִּיר הַמְּאוֹרִים בְּהוֹד יָפְיָה וְצִבְיָהּ.

82

and how many a synagogue is in it,

וְכַמָּה בֵית כְּנֶסֶת בָּהּ.

83

whose beauty has no equal;

אֲשֶׁר אֵין יֳפִי כְיָפְיָהּ.

84

and there every soul praises the Lord [Ps 150:6].

וְשָׁם כֹּל הַנְּשָׁמָה תְּהַלֵּל יָהּ.

85

And within it, of the holy seed [Isa 6:13], is a congregation that puts on the ornament of uprightness,

וּבְתוֹכָהּ מִזֶּרַע הַקֹּדֶשׁ קְהִלָּה עֲדִי הַיּשֶׁר תַּעְדֶּה.

86

ten thousand, like the plant of the field [Ezek 16:7].

רְבָבָה כְּצֶמַח הַשָּׂדֶה.

87

And of the choicest of its princes is the Nasi Rabbi Solomon, son of the great Nasi Rabbi Joseph ben Shoshan, of blessed memory.

וּמִמִּבְחַר נְשִׂיאֶיהָ הַנָּשִׂיא רַבִּי שְׁלֹמֹה בֶּן הַנָּשִׂיא הַגָּדוֹל רַבִּי יוֹסֵף בֶּן שׁוֹשָׁן זִכְרוֹ לִבְרָכָה.

88

He is the plate of the crown [Exod 39:30],

הוּא צִיץ הַנֵּזֶר.

89

and the stone of help [1 Sam 7:12].

וְאֶבֶן הָעֶזֶר.

90

And there is Rabbi Abraham ben Shoshan, he is the goodly worth [Zech 11:13],

וְשָׁם רַבִּי אַבְרָהָם בֶּן שׁוֹשָׁן וְהוּא אֵדֶר הַיְקָר.

91

and his mind is past searching out.

וְשִׂכְלוֹ לֹא נֶחְקַר.

92

And of their nobles, Moses ben Shoshan,

וּמִנְּדִיבֵיהֶם משֶׁה בֶּן שׁוֹשָׁן.

93

who builds houses of rest

הַבּוֹנֶה בָתֵּי מְנוּחוֹת.

94

for wayfarers.

לְעוֹבְרֵי אֳרָחוֹת.

95

And of their princes, Rabbi Abraham ben Alfakhar, in whom are precious qualities

וּמִנְּשִׂיאֵיהֶם רַבִּי אַבְרָהָם בֶּן אַלְפַכָאר בּוֹ מִדּוֹת יְקָרוֹת.

96

and upright deeds.

וּפְעֻלּוֹת מְיֻשָּׁרוֹת.

97

And of their princes, ha-Levi Rabbi Meir ben Todros⚠5: in wisdom there is none to compare with him;

וּמִנְּשִׂיאֵיהֶם הַלֵּוִי רַבִּי מֵאִיר בֶּן טַדְרוֹס אֵין בְּחָכְמָה מִי יַעַרְכֶנּוּ.

98

only he is proud, and his pride will bring him low [Prov 29:23].

רַק הוּא גֵאֶה וְגַאֲוָתוֹ תַשְׁפִּילֶנּוּ.

99

And Rabbi Ziza ben Shoshan devises generous things [Isa 32:8],

וְרַבִּי זִיזָא בֶּן שׁוֹשָׁן יוֹעֵץ נְדִיבוֹת.

100

a sea of gifts.

וְיָם נְדָבוֹת.

101

And Rabbi Isaac son of Meir ibn Migash, a fountain of wisdom and humility,

וְרַבִּי יִצְחָק בֶּן מֵאִיר ן' מִיגַשׁ מְקוֹר הַחָכְמָה וְהָעֲנָוָה.

102

in whom is every comely quality.

וּבוֹ כָל מִדָּה נָאוָה.

103

And of them Rabbi Joseph the judge, a sea of testimonies,

וּמֵהֶם רַבִּי יוֹסֵף הַדַּיָּן יָם הַתְּעוּדוֹת.

104

and a man greatly beloved [Dan 10:11].

וְאִיש חֲמוּדוֹת.

105

And his cousin, the physician Rabbi Jacob ben Meir, master of all the sciences,

וּבֶן דּוֹדוֹ הָרוֹפֵא רַבִּי יַעֲקֹב בֶּן מֵאִיר כּוֹלֵל הַחָכְמוֹת.

106

and of good and pleasant qualities.

וּבַעַל מִדּוֹת טוֹבוֹת וּנְעִימוֹת.

107

From there I journeyed to Calatayud, and there was the physician Rabbi Joseph Constantino, a man of understanding,

וּמִשָּׁם נָסַעְתִּי לְקַלְעַת אִיּוֹב וְשָם הָרוֹפֵא רַבִּי יוֹסֵף קַצְטַנְטִינוֹ אִישׁ תְּבוּנָה.

108

and a master of uprightness and faith.

וְלֹא יִתְעוּ בִנְתִיבוֹ.

223

For he removed from his work⚠10 the names of the commentators,

כִּי הֵסִיר מֵחִבּוּרוֹ שְׁמוֹת הַמְפָרְשִׁים.

224

the homilies and the expositions,

וְהַדְּרָשׁוֹת וְהַדְּרוּשִׁים.

225

the aggadot and the novellae,

וְהַהַגָּדוֹת וְהַחִדּוּשִׁים.

226

by which thoughts are confused,

אֲשֶׁר בָּם הָרַעיוֹנִים מִשְׁתַּבְּשִׁים.

227

until he set the whole Talmud on a paved road,

עַד הֲשִׁיבוֹ כָל הַתַּלְמוּד בְּאֹרַח סְלוּלָה.

228

and caused a proclamation to pass through all the exile [Exod 36:6]:

וְהֶעֱבִיר קוֹל בְּכָל הַגּוֹלָה.

229

Enter his gates with thanksgiving, his courts with praise [Ps 100:4].

בּוֹאוּ שְׁעָרָיו בְּתוֹדָה חֲצֵרוֹתָיו בִּתְהִלָּה.

230

And it came to pass after the death of Moses [Josh 1:1],

וַיְהִי אַחֲרֵי מוֹת משֶׁה.

231

that every fierce and hard man assembled,

נוֹעֲדוּ כָל אִישׁ עַז וְקָשֶׁה.

232

and every fool opened wide his mouth,

וְכָל כְּסִיל פִּיהוּ פָּעַר.

233

in Spain and France, in the Land of the Gazelle and in Shinar⚠11,

בִּסְפָרַד וְצָרְפַת וְאֶרֶץ הַצְּבִי וְשִׁנְעָר.

234

and they took counsel to answer his work with empty words,

וַיִּוָּעֲצוּ לְהָשִׁיב עַל חִבּוּרוֹ דְבָרִים רֵקִים.

235

and with withered, thin matters [Gen 41:23],

וְעִנְיָנִים צְנוּמִים דַּקִּים.

236

and they broke through the fence that the blameless had fenced,

וּפָרְצוּ גָדֵר גָּדְרוּ הַתְּמִימִים.

237

little foxes that spoil the vineyards [Song 2:15].

שׁוּעָלִים קְטַנִּים מְחַבְּלִים כְּרָמִים.

238

Had they spoken to his face,

וְאִלּוּ דִבְּרוּ בְּפָנָיו.

239

they would have melted like wax in the fire of his wrath,

נָמַסּוּ כַדּוֹנַג בְּאֵש חֲרוֹנָיו.

240

and fled before him,

וּבָרְחוּ מִלְּפָנָיו.

241

as lambs from young lions,

כְּמוֹ הַטְּלָאִים מִן הַכְּפִירִים.

242

or birds from eagles,

אוֹ הַצִּפֳּרִים מִן הַנְּשָׁרִים.

243

and sunk like lead in his mighty waters [Exod 15:10].

וְצָלְלוּ כַּעוֹפֶרֶת בְּמֵימָיו הָאַדִּירִים.

244

But every man, when he is alone, thinks that he has no second in his wisdom,

אֲבָל כָּל אִישׁ בִּהְיוֹתוֹ לְבַדּוֹ יַחְשֹׁב כִּי אֵין לוֹ בְחָכְמָתוֹ שֵׁנִי.

245

and the weakling says: "I am a hero" [Joel 4:10].

וְהַחַלָּשׁ יֹאמַר גִּבּוֹר אֲנִי.

246

The weakling, when he is in his house, shows off his might to his wife;

וְהַחַלָּשׁ בְּעֵת יִהְיֶה בְּבֵיתוֹ יְגַלֶּה אֶת גְּבוּרָתוֹ לְאִשְׁתּוֹ.

247

but when he draws near to the battle, then his disgrace and shame are laid bare.

וְעֵת אֶל-הַקְּרָב יִגַּשׁ וְיִקְרַב – אֲזַי תִּתְגַּל כְּלִמָּתוֹ וּבָשְׁתּוֹ.

248

And of their nobles is the pious and honored, the exalted prince Rabbi Joseph, son of the great prince Rabbi Obadiah. His father was the choicest of the princes,

וּמֵאֲצִילֵיהֶם הֶחָסִיד הַנִּכְבָּד הַשַּׂר הַנַּעֲלֶה רַבִּי יוֹסֵף בֶּן הַשַּׂר הַגָּדוֹל רַבִּי עוֹבַדְיָה הָיָה אָבִיו מִבְחַר הַנְּסִיכִים.

249

and reared in the bosom of kings;

וְאָמוּן בְּחֵיק הַמְּלָכִים.

250

and the son, there is no comparing with him

וְהַבֵּן אֵין עֲרֹךְ אֵלָיו.

251

in his righteousness and the excellence of his works.

בְּצִדְקָתוֹ וְכִשְׁרוֹן מִפְעָלָיו.

252

From there I journeyed to Jerusalem,

וּמִשָּׁם נָסַעְתִּי אֶל יְרוּשָׁלַיִם.

253

and the heavens were opened to me,

וְנִפְתְּחוּ לִי הַשָּׁמָיִם.

254

and I saw visions of God [Ezek 1:1].

וָאֶרְאֶה מַרְאוֹת אֱלֹהִים.

255

And the angels of God met me [Gen 32:2]:

וַיִּפְגְּעוּ בִי מַלְאֲכֵי אֱלֹהִים.

256

they are the pious ones of the Most High,

הֵם חֲסִידֵי עֶלְיוֹן.

257

who have come from the land of France to dwell in Zion.

הַבָּאִים מֵאֶרֶץ צָרְפַת לִשְׁכֹּן בְּצִיּוֹן.

258

At their head is the pious master Rabbi Joseph, son of the master Rabbi Barukh⚠12,

וּבְרֹאשָׁם הָרַב הֶחָסִיד רַבִּי יוֹסֵף בֶּן הָרַב רַבִּי בָרוּךְ.

259

may he be blessed of his God,

יְהִי לֵאֱלֹהָיו בָּרוּךְ.

260

and his brother, the sage Rabbi Meir,

וְאָחִיו הֶחָכָם רַבִּי מֵאִיר.

261

who lights up dark places with the lamp of his mind.

בְּנֵר שִׂכְלוֹ חֲשֵׁכִים מֵאִיר.

262

And there, of the people of Ashkelon, is an eminent congregation,

וְשָׁם מִן הָאַשְׁקְלוֹנִים קְהִלָּה מְעֻלָּה.

263

and at their head the prince Rabbi Saadiah, a Benjaminite; he is wise and pleasant,

וּבְרֹאשָׁם הַשַּׂר רַבִּי סְעַדְיָה אִישׁ יְמִינִי וְהוּא מַשְׂכִּיל וְנֶחְמָד.

264

and his mind cannot be measured.

וְשִׂכְלוֹ לֹא יִמַּד.

265

And there, of the Maghrebis, is a worthy and good congregation,

וְשָׁם מִן הַמַּעֲרָבִים קְהִלָּה חֲשׁוּבָה וְטוֹבָה.

266

and at their head Rabbi Elijah the Maghrebi.

וּבְרֹאשָׁם רַבִּי אֵלִיָּה הַמַּעֲרָבִי.

267

He is a man of kindness,

הוּא בַעַל חֲסָדִים.

268

abounding in charity to the wandering poor.

וּמַרְבֶּה צְדָקוֹת לִמְרוּדִים.

269

But great and many evil reports went out about him,

אֲבָל יָצְאוּ עָלָיו דִּבּוֹת גְּדוֹלוֹת וְרַבּוֹת.

270

of wicked deeds and transgressions,

בְּמַעֲשִׂים רָעִים וַעֲבֵרוֹת.

271

and ugly doings;

וּפְעֻלּוֹת מְכֹעָרוֹת.

272

and God knows the hidden things.

וְהָאֵל יוֹדֵעַ הַנִּסְתָּרוֹת.

273

From there I came to Acre,

וּמִשָּׁם בָּאתִי לְעַכּוֹ.

274

and I have not seen the like of them from my birth until now,

וְלֹא רָאִיתִי כָהֵם מִהְיוֹתִי וְעַד כֹּה.

275

for they are all empty men and ignorant boors;

כִּי כֻלָּם רֵיקִים עַמֵּי אָרֶץ.

276

there is none among them to make up the fence and stand in the breach [Ezek 22:30].

אֵין בָּהֶם גּוֹדֵר גָּדֵר וְעוֹמֵד בַּפָּרֶץ.

277

From there I walked through all the borders of the Land of Israel,

וּמִשָּׁם הִתְהַלַּכְתִּי בְּכָל גְּבוּל אֶרֶץ יִשְׂרָאֵל.

278

and passed by Safed, and found there the chosen of God:

וְעָבַרְתִּי עַל צְפַת וּמָצָאתִי שָׁם בְּחִיר הָאֵל.

279

a prince and a great man, who makes every crooked place straight [Isa 40:4],

שַׂר וְגָדוֹל מְיַשֵּׁר כָּל עָקֹב.

280

Zadok the righteous, head of the academy Ge'on Ya'akov⚠13.

צָדוֹק הַצַּדִּיק רֹאשׁ יְשִׁיבַת גְּאוֹן יַעֲקֹב.

281

His fathers were heads of the academies,

אֲבוֹתָיו הָיוּ רָאשֵי הַיְשִׁיבוֹת.

282

and seas of generosity,

וְיָם הַנְּדָבוֹת.

283

and masters of good qualities.

וּבַעֲלֵי הַמִּדּוֹת הַטּוֹבוֹת.

284

Only many troubles surrounded him,

רַק אֲפָפוּהוּ צָרוֹת רַבּוֹת.

285

and turns of fortune rolled down upon him,

וְהִתְגַּלְגְּלוּ עָלָיו סִבּוֹת.

286

and he was brought down from his throne after he had reigned,

וְהוּרַד מִכִּסְאוֹ אַחֲרֵי מָלַךְ.

287

and emptied from vessel to vessel, and went into exile [Jer 48:11].

וְהוּרַק מִכְּלִי אֶל כְּלִי וּבַגּוֹלָה הָלַךְ.

288

But his taste remained in him, sweet without bitter,

אֲבָל עָמַד טַעְמוֹ בוֹ מָתוֹק בְּלִי מָר.

289

and his scent was not changed [Jer 48:11].

וְרֵיחוֹ לֹא נָמָר.

290

And if his good fortune departed,

וְאִם טוֹבָתוֹ סָרָה.

291

his dignity did not grow less.

מִשְׂרָתוֹ לֹא חָסָרָה.

292

And may God, who tried him with these hardships, bind up his break and his pain,

וְהָאֵל אֲשֶׁר בְּאֵלֶּה הַתְּלָאוֹת נִסָּהוּ יַחֲבשׁ שִׁבְרוֹ וְחִילוֹ.

293

and may he turn and be healed [Isa 6:10].

וְשָׁב וְרָפָא לוֹ.

294

And I took up my parable and said:

וָאֶשָּׂא מְשָׁלִי וָאֹמַר:

295

The Teaching: its shade has departed, its banner is bowed to Sheol, and the days have betrayed it and made its yoke heavy.

תְּעוּדָה סָר צִלָּהּ וְלִשְׁאוֹל שַׁח דִּגְלָהּ וְיָמִים בָּגְדוּ בָהּ וְהִכְבִּידוּ עֻלָּהּ.

296

But we trust that God will renew the light of its lamp [Job 29:3], and the Torah returns to its lodging-place [b. Bava Metzia 85a].

אֲבָל נִבְטַח כִּי אֵל יְחַדֵּש אוֹר הִלָּהּ וְתוֹרָה חוֹזֶרֶת לְאַכְסַנְיָא שֶׁלָּהּ.

297

From there I came to Damascus, and there are men of worth who fear God [Exod 18:21],

וּמִשָּׁם בָּאתִי לְדַמֶּשֶׂק וְשָׁם אַנְשֵׁי חַיִל יִרְאֵי אֱלֹהִים.

298

and climb on high steps.

וְעוֹלִים בְּמַעֲלוֹת גְּבוֹהִים.

299

Even though there are hard and wicked men there,

אַף עַל פִּי שֶׁיֵּשׁ שָׁם קָשִׁים וְרָעִים.

300

doers of base deeds,

בַּעֲלֵי מַעֲשִׂים גְּרוּעִים.

301

there are found among them men of uprightness and humility,

נִמְצְאוּ בָם אַנְשֵׁי ישֶׁר וַעֲנָוָה.

302

and gatherers of every comely quality.

וְאוֹסְפֵי כָל מִדָּה נָאוָה.

303

And when I tested their piety,

וּבְעֵת חֲסִידוּתָם בָּחַנְתִּי.

304

I said: Surely the Lord is in this place, and I did not know it [Gen 28:16].

אָמַרְתִּי אָכֵן יֵשׁ ה' בַּמָּקוֹם הַזֶּה וְאָנֹכִי לֹא יָדָעְתִּי.

305

And the choicest of all their great ones,

וּמִבְחַר כָּל גְּדוֹלֵיהֶם.

306

and the head of their nobles,

וְרֹאשׁ אֲצִילֵיהֶם.

307

the crown of the congregations,

כֶּתֶר הַקְּהִלּוֹת.

308

and the diadem of praises,

וְנֵזֶר הַתְּהִלּוֹת.

309

is the great physician Rabbi Moses bar Zedakah,

הָרוֹפֵא הַגָּדוֹל רַבִּי משֶׁה בַּר צְּדָקָה.

310

pillar of the pious,

עַמּוּד הַחֲסִידִים.

311

and a strong tower for the Jews,

וּמִגְדַּל עֹז לַיְהוּדִים.

312

who stands in the breach before our people,

הַעוֹמֵד לִפְנֵי עַמֵּנוּ בַּפָּרֶץ.

313

and there is no man like him in the land [Job 1:8].

וְאִישׁ אֵין כָּמוֹהוּ בָאָרֶץ.

314

And were I to keep silent from his praises,

וְאִלּוּ הֶחֱרַשְׁתִּי מִמַּהֲלָלָיו.

315

his works would praise him,

שִׁבְּחוּהוּ פְעָלָיו.

316

his deeds would bear witness to him.

מַעֲלָלָיו יָעִידוּ עָלָיו.

317

How many of the wandering poor he has saved,

כַּמָּה מְרוּדִים הוֹשִׁיעַ.

318

and how many wanderers he has given rest,

וְכַמָּה נוֹדְדִים הִרְגִּיעַ.

319

and the afflicted soul he has satisfied [Isa 58:10];

וְנֶפֶשׁ נַעֲנָה הִשְׂבִּיעַ.

320

he has delivered those taken to death,

וְהִצִּיל לְקוּחִים לַמָּוֶת.

321

and rescued those tottering to the slaughter [Prov 24:11], and revived those going down to the chambers of death [Prov 7:27];

וְחִלֵּץ מָטִים לַהֶרֶג וְהֶחֱיָה יוֹרְדִים אֶל חַדְרֵי מָוֶת.

322

and he has filled all the people of the generation with generosity,

וְהִשְׂבִּיעַ כָּל בְּנֵי הַדּוֹר בִּנְדָבָה.

323

and with every good quality.

וּבְכָל מִדָּה טוֹבָה.

324

And I took up my parable and said:

וָאֶשָּׂא מְשָׁלִי וָאֹמַר:

325

My tongue falls short of telling the greatness of his kindness, when I think to weigh in the balance the words of his praise.

תִּקְצַר לְשׁוֹנִי לְסַפֵּר רֹב חֲסָדָיו בְּעֵת אֶחְשֹׁב בְּפֶלֶס אֲמָרַי מַהֲלָלָיו שְׁקוֹל.

326

God listens to his cry when he calls; and when Moses speaks, God answers him with a voice [Exod 19:19].

יַקְשִׁיב אֱלֹהִים לְשַׁוְעָתוֹ בְּקָרְאוֹ. וְעֵת משֶׁה יְדַבֵּר אֱלֹהִים יַעֲנֶנּוּ בְקוֹל.

327

And there is the Nasi Rabbi Josiah ben Jesse⚠14, Nasi of the exiles of all Israel, crown of the honored,

וְשָׁם הַנָּשִׂיא רַבִּי יֹאשִׁיָה בֶּן יִשַׁי נְשִׂיא גָלֻיּוֹת כָּל יִשְׂרָאֵל כֶּתֶר הַנִּכְבָּדִים.

328

and a doer of lovely deeds,

וּבַעַל מַעֲשִׂים נֶחֱמָדִים.

329

gatherer of the lovely qualities,

אוֹסֵף הַמִּדּוֹת הַחֲמוּדוֹת.

330

and in all the world glorious things are spoken of him [Ps 87:3].

וּבְכָל תֵּבֵל מְדֻבָּר בּוֹ נִכְבָּדוֹת.

331

And I took up my parable and said:

And for this it is right to excommunicate him in all the cities,

May God destroy them and make them perish, and not be willing to forgive them [Deut 29:19],

Stinginess roamed the world and was not still, until in his heart it found repose.

כִּילוּת בְּתֵבֵל שׁוֹטְטָה לֹא שָׁקְטָה עַד כִּי בְלִבּוֹ מָצְאָה מַרְגּוֹעַ.

Should his hand touch the Red Sea, it would dry up, or the rivers, they would cease to flow;

or the grasses, they would wither as if burned, or the fish of the sea, they would perish and die.

אוֹ בַעֲשָׂבִים יָבְשׁוּ כִּי נִשְׂרְפוּ אוֹ עַל דְּגֵי יָם תַּמְנוּ לִגְוֹעַ.

598

From there I came to Mount Zemaraim⚠28,

וּמִשָּׁם בָּאתִי לְהַר צְמָרַיִם.

599

and there, of the men of kindness who are singled out by name, one is named:

וְשָׁם מֵאַנְשֵׁי הַחֶסֶד הַנְּקוּבִים נָקוֹב.

Rabbi Jacob,

רַבִּי יַעֲקֹב.

601

who in his knowledge refuses the evil and chooses the good [Isa 7:15].

And of the choicest of their congregation is Rabbi She'erit,

וּמִמִּבְחַר קְהִלָּתָם רַבִּי שְׁאֵרִית.

who is a survivor and a remnant of uprightness⚠29.

He has seven sons,

וְלוֹ שִׁבְעָה בָנִים.

wise and understanding.

מַשְׂכִּילִים וּנְבוֹנִים.

And of the good men of the city was Rabbi Petaḥiah the goldsmith, a man great in his affairs,

and honored among his people,

and he left behind him lovely sons,

and their deeds are honorable.

וּמַעֲשֵׂיהֶם נִכְבָּדִים.

And the prince Rabbi Peraḥiah the Egyptian, honored in his works,

וְהַשַּׂר רַבִּי פְרַחְיָה הַמִּצְרִי נִכְבָּד בְּמִפְעָלָיו.

611

and lovely in his doings.

וְנֶחְמָד בְּמַעֲלָלָיו.

And there are the two brothers,

וְשָׁם שְׁנֵי הָאַחִים.

613

the anointed priests [Num 3:3],

הַכֹּהֲנִים הַמְּשׁוּחִים.

Rabbi Eliezer and Rabbi Berakhot; their father was a pious man,

רַבִּי אֱלִיעֶזֶר וְרַבִּי בְּרָכוֹת הָיָה אִישׁ חָסִיד אֲבִיהֶם.

615

and the sons filled his place by their deeds.

וְהַבָּנִים מִלְּאוּ מְקוֹמוֹ בְּמַעֲשֵׂיהֶם.

And many are doers of upright deeds,

וְרַבִּים בַּעֲלֵי מַעֲשִׂים יְשָׁרִים.

and of works counted happy.

וּפְעָלִים מְאֻשָּׁרִים.

618

From there I journeyed to the land of Hamath⚠30,

and there is the great prince,

וְּשָׁם הַשַּׂר הַגָּדוֹל.

Rabbi Uzziel.

רַבִּי עֻזִּיאֵל.

621

He is second to the king [Esth 10:3],

הוּא מִשְׁנֶה לַמֶּלֶךְ.

622

and from the fruit of his lips he yields royal dainties [Gen 49:20].

וּמִפְּרִי שְׂפָתָיו יִתֵּן מַעֲדַנֵּי מֶלֶךְ.

623

In his days the Jews have dwelt safely in his shade,

בְּיָמָיו יָשְׁבוּ הַיְּהוּדִים בֶּטַח בְּצִלּוֹ.

624

and without him no man lifts up his hand or his foot [Gen 41:44],

וּבִלְעָדָיו לֹא יָרִים אִישׁ אֶת יָדוֹ וְאֶת רַגְלוֹ.

625

and all the princes of the kingdom wait for him as for the rain [Job 29:23],

וְכָל שָׁרֵי הַמְּלוּכָה מְיַחֲלִים כַּמָּטָר לוֹ.

626

and all the king's servants, because of his lovely deeds,

וְכָל עַבְדֵי הַמֶּלֶךְ בִּגְלַל מַעֲשָׂיו הַנֶּחֱמָדִים.

lift up the Jews [Esth 9:3].

מְנַשְּׂאִים אֶת הַיְּהוּדִים.

628

For he has worthy qualities,

כִּי לוֹ מִדּוֹת הֲגוּנוֹת.

629

and his fame goes through all the provinces [Esth 9:4].

וְשִׁמְעוֹ הוֹלֵךְ בְּכָל הַמְּדִינוֹת.

630

Blessed be the Lord, who has sent him to his people as a savior,

בָּרוּךְ ה' אֲשֶׁר שְׁלָחוֹ לְעַמּוֹ לְמוֹשִׁיעַ.

to save the lost,

הָאוֹבְדִים לְהוֹשִׁיעַ.

632

to give rest to the wanderers,

וְהַנּוֹדְדִים לְהַרְגִּיעַ.

633

and to satisfy the hungry.

וְהָרְעֵבִים לְהַשְׂבִּיעַ.

634

And in his days may the turn of the daughter of Zion come,

וּבְיָמָיו תּוֹר בַּת צִיּוֹן יַגִּיעַ.

and may He lay the charge on him with entreaty⚠31,

וִיצַו עָלָיו בְּמַפְגִּיעַ.

636

and out of Zion, the perfection of beauty, may God shine forth [Ps 50:2],

וּמִצִּיּוֹן מִכְלַל יֹפִי אֱלֹהִים הוֹפִיעַ.

and over the scattered of Israel may He sound with the blast of the shofar;

and may the sanctuary of Ariel [Isa 29:1] be built in his days,

641

And he took up his parable and said:

וַיִּשָּׂא מְשָׁלוֹ וַיֹּאמַר:

642

Uzziel, the chosen of God, has deeds that are lovely and beloved to those who hear of them.

לְעֻזִּיאֵל בְּחִיר הָאֵל פְּעֻלּוֹת לְשׁוֹמְעֵיהֶם חֲשׁוּקוֹת וַאֲהוּבוֹת.

Were he to put his right hand on the parched places [Jer 17:6], then the face of the world would be filled with fruit [Isa 27:6].

וְאִלּוּ שָׂם יְמִינוֹ בַחֲרֵרִים אֲזַי מָלְאוֹ פְּנֵי תֵבֵל תְּנוּבוֹת.

644

His counsel does for the kingdom what those who draw swords cannot do.

עֲצָתוֹ לַמְּלוּכָה יַעֲשׂוּ בָהּ אֲשֶׁר לֹא יַעֲשׂוּ שׁוֹלְפֵי חֲרָבוֹת.

645

Is it not by it that weak hearts grow strong, and the women turn back into men?

הֲכִי בָהּ יִגְבְּרוּ לִבּוֹת חֲלוּשִׁים וְשָׁבוּ כַּזְּכָרִים הַנְּקֵבוֹת.

Through him God has made room for his people, and for his sake I call him by a true name, Rehoboth [Gen 26:22]⚠32.

וּבוֹ הִרְחִיב לְעַמּוֹ הָאֱלֹהִים וּבַעְדּוֹ שֵׁם אֱמֶת אֶקְרָא רְחֹבוֹת.

647

And of the good men of Hamath is the judge Rabbi Mivḥar,

וּמִטּוֹבֵי חֲמָת הַדַּיָּן רַבִּי מִבְחָר.

whose tongue is choice silver [Prov 10:20].

לְשׁוֹנוֹ כֶסֶף נִבְחָר.

649

And there the physician Rabbi David; he is a blameless man,

וְשָׁם הָרוֹפֵא רַבִּי דָוִד הוּא אִישׁ תָּמִים.

650

and his deeds are pleasant.

וְלוֹ מַעֲשִׂים נְעִימִים.

651

And there Rabbi Isaac, the minister of Moses from his youth [Num 11:28]⚠33,

וְשָׁם רַבִּי יִצְחָק מְשָׁרֵת משֶׁה מִבְּחוּרָיו.

652

who learned the art of music from his youth,

וְלוֹמֵד מְלֶאכֶת הַנִּגּוּן מִנְּעוּרָיו.

653

and he is a wise and understanding man.

וְהוּא אִישׁ מַשְׂכִּיל וּמֵבִין.

654

From there I came to the royal city,

וּמִשָּׁם בָּאתִי לְעִיר הַמְּלוּכָה.

655

that is Aram Zobah⚠34, the blessed,

הִיא אֲרַם צוֹבָה הַבְּרוּכָה.

656

for there the Lord commanded the blessing [Ps 133:3].

כִּי שָׁם צִוָּה ה' אֶת הַבְּרָכָה.

657

It gathers all lovely qualities,

כָּל מִדּוֹת חֲמוּדוֹת אוֹסֶפֶת.

658

and it is a turban on the head of kings.

וְהִיא עַל רֹאשׁ הַמְּלָכִים מִצְנֶפֶת.

659

And besides the pleasantness of the qualities of its congregations,

וּמִלְּבַד נֹעַם מִדּוֹת קְהָלֶיהָ.

660

and the loveliness of its nobles,

וַחֲמוּדוֹת אֲצִילֶיהָ.

661

there came to it thirty years ago a savior and a great one [Isa 19:20]

בָּא אֵלֶיהָ זֶה שְׂלשִׁים שָׁנָה מוֹשִׁיעַ וָרָב.

662

from the land of the West:

מֵאֶרֶץ מַעֲרָב.

663

he is the sage Rabbi Joseph the Maghrebi⚠35,

הוּא הֶחָכָם רַבִּי יוֹסֵף מַעֲרָבִי.

his wisdom like Koheleth's,

חָכְמָתוֹ כְּקֹהֶלֶת.

665

his mind like a burning coal,

וְשִׂכְלוֹ כְּגַחֶלֶת.

וּלְשׁוֹנוֹ אֵשׁ אוֹכֶלֶת.

667

And he took up his parable and said:

וַיִּשָּׂא מְשָׁלוֹ וַיֹּאמַר:

668

A master who roars in every wisdom like a lion, and tears the heart of men of mind like a lioness.

גְּבִיר יִשְׁאַג בְּכָל חָכְמָה כְּאַרְיֵה וְיִטְרֹף לֵב מְתֵי שֵׂכֶל כְּלָבִיא.

The sea of wisdom is torn and split before his mouth when he says to its deep: "Be dry" [Isa 44:27].

וְיָם חָכְמוֹת לְפִיו נִקְרָע וְנִבְקָע בְּעֵת יֹאמַר לְצוּלָתוֹ חֳרָבִי.

670

From his mouth the wise learn wisdom; and if they are like Elisha, he is like the Tishbite [2 Kgs 2:9].

וּמִפִּיו יִלְמְדוּ חָכְמָה חֲכָמִים וְאִם הֵם כֶּעֱלִישָׁע הוּא כְתִשְׁבִּי:

671

Unique in his generation; but he has begotten children who are in this time for beauty and for glory [Isa 4:2].

יְחִיד הַדּוֹר אֲבָל הוֹלִיד יְלָדִים אֲשֶׁר הֵם בַּזְּמַן לִפְאֵר וְלִצְבִי.

672

They learned the matters of discipline before they knew how to cry "My father" and "My mother" [Isa 8:4].

וְלָמְדוּ עִנְיְנֵי מוּסָר בְּטֶרֶם אֲשֶׁר יָדְעוּ קְרֹא אִמִּי וְאָבִי.

673

He came from the West to dwell in the East, and gathered and mustered the hosts of every worth.

וּבָא מִמַּעֲרָב לִשְׁכֹּן בְּמִזְרָח וְצִבְאוֹת כָּל יְקָר אָסַף וְהִצְבִּיא.

674

Were this generation the generation of prophecy, God would have anointed him a prophet in Israel [1 Kgs 19:16].

וְלוּ הַדּוֹר יְהִי דוֹר הַנְּבוּאָה מְשָׁחוֹ אֵל בְּיִשְׂרָאֵל לְנָבִיא.

675

And had he lived of old in Zion, He would have set him like the western lamp⚠36.

וְלוּ קֶדֶם יְהֵי נִמְצָא בְצִיּוֹן נְתָנָהוּ כְמוֹ נֵר מַעֲרָבִי.

676

He is a sea of testimonies,

הוּא יָם הַתְּעוּדוֹת.

677

and in every wisdom he has ten parts [2 Sam 19:44],

וְלוֹ בְּכָל חָכְמָה עֶשֶׂר יָדוֹת.

678

and he is altogether lovely [Song 5:16].

וְכֻלּוֹ מָלֵא חֲמוּדוֹת.

679

By his wisdom he brought to life many of the sons of Zobah, and breathed the spirit of his wisdom into their corpses,

הוּא הֶחֱיָה בְחָכְמָתוֹ רַבִּים מִבְּנֵי צוֹבָה וְנָפַח רוּחַ חָכְמָתוֹ בְּפִגְרֵיהֶם.

and the spirit came into them, and they lived and stood up on their feet [Ezek 37:10].

681

And one of his pupils, who had received his kindnesses,

וְאֶחָד מִתַּלְמִידָיו וּגְמוּלֵי חֲסָדָיו.

hardened his face against him,

הֵעֵז פָּנָיו בְּפָנָיו.

683

and rebelled against him like a slave who rebels against his master,

וּמָרַד עָלָיו כְּעֶבֶד הַמּוֹרֵד עַל אֲדוֹנָיו.

and corrupted his way [Gen 6:12],

וְהִשְׁחִית דַּרְכּוֹ.

685

and lifted up his hand against his king [2 Sam 20:21].

וְנָשָׂא יָדוֹ בְּמַלְכּוֹ.

and of the rest of the sons of Damascus and Zobah,

he would have offered thanks to God for the good He had done him,

that He brought forth such a man from his loins.

Had he rebuked the sea in his righteousness, he would have dried it up [Ps 106:9];

had he been cast into the fire of the Chaldees⚠39, it would not have burned him;

And this family is the one crowned since the days of the patriarchs

with all good qualities:

and generosity of heart like the great and wide sea [Ps 104:25],

and humility on their faces as a covering of the eyes [Gen 20:16].

None in the congregations of Egypt reaches their rank,

nor among the people of the Land of the Gazelle are there men of qualities like theirs,

nor among all the nobles of Zobah one who does not glory in the lineage of their fathers,

nor among the multitudes of Ashur one who does not tremble at hearing their praises,

וְלֹא בַהֲמוֹנֵי אַשּׁוּר אֲשֶׁר לֹא יֶחֱרַד לִשְׁמֹעַ תְּהִלּוֹתָם.

nor among the people of Edinah one who attains to some of their lovely qualities.

וְלֹא בִבְנֵי עֲדִינָה אֲשֶׁר יַשִּׂיג לִקְצָת חֲמוּדוֹתָם.

And this great prince Rabbi Samuel has gathered the good qualities

as one gathers eggs that are forsaken [Isa 10:14].

כֶּאֱסֹף בֵּיצִים עֲזוּבוֹת.

His doors are open to all who pass to and fro,

and his house is an inn for travelers,

וּבֵיתוֹ מְלוֹן אוֹרְחִים.

735

where the settlers and the natives gather,

שָׁם יִתְקַבְּצוּ הַתּוֹשָׁבִים וְהָאֶזְרָחִים.

the westerners and the easterners.

וְהַמַּעֲרָבִים וְהַמִּזְרָחִים.

he would make every asker rich.

הֶעֱשִׁיר כָּל שׁוֹאֵל.

and his name is Mazhir⚠40,

and adds to them by the pleasantness of his qualities.

and lengthen his days and years in his father's lifetime.

And of the choicest of the nobles of Zobah and its great ones,

and the glory of all its congregations,

וּפְאֵר כָּל קְהָלֶיהָ.

is the king's physician Rabbi Eleazar.

בַּמְּלוּכָה נֶעֱזָר.

וְלוֹ בֵן מַשְׂכִּיל.

who holds two thousand baths [1 Kgs 7:26] of the qualities of mind.

מִמִּדּוֹת הַשֵּׂכֶל אַלְפַּיִם בַּת יָכִיל.

And also his brother's sons, worthy in their deeds,

וְגַם בְּנֵי אָחִיו הֲגוּנִים בְּמַעֲשֵׂיהֶם.

and Moses went up to God [Exod 19:3].

and all the men of the generation kept far from it, and Moses alone drew near [Exod 24:2].

And the second, of whom glorious things are spoken,

is Daniel, a man greatly beloved [Dan 10:11].

781

and his heart the heart of a watcher and a holy one [Dan 4:10].

and despised the pleasures of the time and did not defile himself with their bread,

וּמָאַס תַּעֲנוּגֵי הַזְּמָן וְלֹא הָיָה מִתְגָּאֵל בְמוֹ פִתָּיו.

and Daniel resolved in his heart that he would not defile himself with the king's food or with the wine he drank [Dan 1:8].

וְיוֹסֵף הוּא הַשַּׁלִּיט עַל הַמִּדּוֹת הַנְּעִימוֹת.

and the captor of the strongholds of thought.

And he took up his parable and said:

וַיִּשָּׂא מְּשָׁלוֹ וַיֹּאמַר:

789

Three sons of the eminent one, a necklace filled with pearls.

שְׁלשָׁה בְנֵי הַמְּעֻלֶּה עֲנָק מִפְּנִינִים מְמֻלֵּא.

790

Moses is lowly of heart, but he is exalted in the eyes of his God.

משֶׁה שְׁפַל לֵב אֲבָל הוּא בְּעֵינֵי אֱלֹהָיו מְעֻלֶּה.

791

Praises fall short for Daniel; he cannot be valued even with fine gold [Job 28:16].

קָצְרוּ תְהִלּוֹת בְּדָנִיאֵל רַק בְּפָז לֹא יְסֻלֶּה.

Joseph, foundation of understanding: his speech is balm for the pangs of the sick⚠41.

יוֹסֵף יְסוֹד הַתְּבוּנוֹת נִיבוֹ צְרִי צִיר מְחֻלֶּה.

רוֹאֶה גְדֻלַּת שְׁלָשְׁתָּם כָּל-יוֹם פְּנֵיהֶם יְחַלֶּה.

794

He will know that of all the sons of men the Lord has chosen these.

אֲבָל כֻּלָּם סְגוּרֵי יָדַיִם.

906

and their wealth is held in fetters and in bronze [Ps 149:8],

וְהוֹנָם עָצוּר בְכֶבֶל וְבִנְחֻשְׁתַּיִם.

907

and under bar and doors [Deut 3:5].

וְתַחַת בָּרִיחַ וּדְלָתַיִם.

908

At their head is Rabbi Zemaḥ; he is the branch of the Lord for beauty and for glory [Isa 4:2], and in him is every beloved quality,

וּבְרֹאשָׁם רַבִּי צֶמַח הוּא צֶמַח ה' לִצְבִי וּלְכָבוֹד וּבוֹ כָל מִדָּה אֲהוּבָה.

909

except generosity.

חוּץ מִן הַנְּדָבָה.

910

And his brother Rabbi Obadiah: had he the power [Gen 31:29],

וְאָחִיו רַבִּי עוֹבַדְיָה אִלּוּ יֵשׁ יָדוֹ לְאֵל.

911

he would make every asker rich with his generosity.

הָיָה מַעֲשִׁיר בְּנִדְבָתוֹ לְכָל שוֹאֵל.

912

And of them the wise young man Isaac bar Moses; he is wise and pleasant,

וּמֵהֶם הַבָּחוּר הַמַּשְׂכִּיל יִצְחָק בַּר משֶׁה הוּא מַשְׂכִּיל וְנֶחְמָד.

913

and his mind and his discipline cannot be measured.

וְשִׂכְלוֹ וּמוּסָרוֹ לֹא יִמָּד.

914

And there were Rabbi Simḥah and Rabbi Ḥalafta,

וְשָׁם הָיָה רַבִּי שִׂמְחָה וְרַבִּי חֲלַפְתָּא.

915

in whom was every good quality,

אֲשֶׁר כָּל מִדָּה טוֹבָה בָהֶם הָיְתָה.

916

and both of them feared heaven,

וּשְׁנֵיהֶם יִרְאֵי שָׁמַיִם.

917

and were generous of hand,

וּנְדִיבֵי יָדַיִם.

918

like two cherubim spreading their wings [Exod 25:20];

כִּשְׁנֵי כְרוּבִים פּוֹרְשֵׂי כְנָפַיִם.

919

and their generosity was a strong tower and a wall for the people of Calneh,

וְנִדְבָתָם הָיְתָה לִבְנֵי כַלְנֶה מִגְדַּל עֹז וְחוֹמָה.

920

and the two angels came to Sodom [Gen 19:1]⚠48.

וַיָּבוֹאוּ שְׂנֵי הַמַּלְאָכִים סְדוֹמָה.

921

From there I went to Ashur⚠49,

וּמִשָּׁם הָלַכְתִּי לְאַשּׁוּר.

922

and there I saw the prince Rabbi Nissim, unique in his humility and his generosity,

וְשָׁם רָאִיתִי הַשַּׂר רַבִּי נִסִּים יָחִיד בְּעַנְוָתוֹ וּבְנִדְבָתוֹ.

923

and in Ashur there is no house open wide but his house,

וְאֵין בְּאַשּׁוּר בַּיִת פָּתוּחַ לִרְוָחָה כִּי אִם בֵּיתוֹ.

924

and none but he does kindness to all who pass to and fro.

וְאֵין עוֹשֶׂה חֶסֶד לְכָל עוֹבֵר וָשָׁב זוּלָתוֹ.

925

And there I saw our lord David, head of the Jewish exile,

וְשָׁם רָאִיתִי אֲדוֹנֵנוּ דָוִד רֹאשׁ הַגּוֹלָה הַיְּהוּדִיָּה.

926

and his sister's son Hodaiah.

וּבֶן אֲחוֹתוֹ הוֹדִיָּה.

927

They have no second among the men of their time,

אֵין לָהֶם שֵׁנִי בִּבְנֵי זְמַנֵּיהֶם.

928

and there is none who does as they do,

וְאֵין מִי יַעֲשֶׂה כְּמַעֲשֵׂיהֶם.

929

and my tongue falls short of telling some of their qualities.

וְתִקְצַר לְשׁוֹנִי לְסַפֵּר קְצָת מִדּוֹתֵיהֶם.

930

And there is a villain who has turned aside from the ways of his Maker:

וְשָׁם אִישׁ נָבָל פוֹרֵשׁ מִדַּרְכֵי קוֹנֵהוּ.

931

Berakhot is his name⚠50, and among all the wicked of Israel there is none like him.

בְּרָכוֹת שְׁמוֹ וְאֵין בְּכָל רִשְׁעֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל כָּמוֹהוּ.

932

And his form

וְצוּרָתוֹ.

933

will lay bare his villainy to you,

תְּגַלֶּה לְךָ נַבְלוּתוֹ.

934

and when you see his despised likeness you will say:

וְתֹאמַר בִּרְאוֹתְךָ דְמוּתוֹ הַנִּבְזֶה.

935

"Surely there is no fear of God in this place" [Gen 20:11].

רַק אֵין יִרְאַת אֱלֹהִים בַּמָּקוֹם הַזֶּה.

936

And about him the makers of parables say [Num 21:27]:

וְעָלָיו יֹאמְרוּ הַמּוֹשְׁלִים:

937

The belly of Berakhot is like a ranging bear [Prov 28:15], his loins are fat like the boar of the forest [Ps 80:14].

בֶּטֶן בְּרָכוֹת הִיא כְּדֹב שׁוֹקֵק שָׁמְנוּ כְסָלָיו כַּחֲזִיר יָעַר.

938

They called his name Trusty, but he is a flatterer⚠51; no one trusts him but a brutish man [Ps 92:7].

קָרְאוּ שְׁמוֹ נֶאְמָן וְהוּא חָנֵף לֹא יַאֲמִין בּוֹ רַק אֱנוֹשׁ בָּעַר.

939

Do not trust him in three things: in money, in a woman and in a boy.

אַל תַּאֲמִינוּ בוֹ עֲלֵי שָׁלשׁ עַל הוֹן וְעַל אִשָּׁה וְעַל נָעַר.

940

And when I came to it⚠52,

וּכְבוֹאִי אֵלֶיהָ.

941

I saw its congregations,

רָאִיתִי קְהָלֶיהָ.

942

each man of them sunk in sleep in the bosom of folly;

כָּל אִישׁ מֵהֶם בְּחֵיק הַסִּכְלוּת נִרְדָּם.

943

and behold, there was no man there, nor voice of man,

וְהִנֵּה אֵין שָׁם אִישׁ וְקוֹל אָדָם.

944

except the horse tied,

כִּי אִם הַסּוּס אָסוּר.

945

and the donkey tied [2 Kgs 7:10].

וְהַחֲמוֹר אָסוּר.

946

And it is for the Jews like a pit,

וְהִיא לַיְּהוּדִים כְּמוֹ בוֹר.

947

and an ox or a donkey falls into it [Exod 21:33].

וְנָפַל שָׁמָּה שׁוֹר אוֹ חֲמוֹר.

948

And on the day the congregation of the Rock went into exile from Jerusalem,

וּבְיוֹם גָּלוּ מִירוּשָׁלַיִם עֲדַת הַצּוּר.

949

the pious went into exile to Damascus and to Egypt, and dwelt from Havilah to Shur [Gen 25:18],

גָּלוּ הַחֲסִידִים לְדַמֶּשֶׂק וּלְמִצְרַיִם וַיִּשְׁכְּנוּ מֵחֲוִילָה עַד שׁוּר.

950

and the lost came into the land of Ashur [Isa 27:13].

וּבָאוּ הָאוֹבְדִים בְּאֶרֶץ אַשּׁוּר.

951

From there I journeyed to Edinah,

וּמִשָּׁם נָסַעתִּי לַעֲדִינָה.

952

the glory of every province.

הִיא פאֵר כָּל מְדִינָה.

953

There were, of old, the Geonim of the world and its sages,

וְשָׁם הָיוּ מֵעוֹלָם גְּאוֹנֵי תֵבֵל וַחֲכָמֶיהָ.

954

but today its elders are lost,

אֲבָל אָבְדוּ הַיּוֹם זְקֵנֶיהָ.

955

and its striplings are left.

וְנוֹתְרוּ עֲלָמֶיהָ.

956

The fine flour is gone,

וְנֶעְדְּרָה הַסֹּלֶת.

957

and the refuse is left;

וְנִשְׁאֲרָה הַפּסֹלֶת.

958

the pious have ceased [Ps 12:2],

וּפַסּוּ הַחֲסִידִים.

959

and the rebels are left;

וְנוֹתְרוּ הַמּוֹרְדִים.

960

the lions have died,

וּמֵתוּ הַשְּׁחָלִים.

961

and foxes have risen in their place.

וְקָמוּ אַחֲרֵיהֶם שׁוּעָלִים.

962

And how many mishaps befell me when I came to it!

וְכַמָּה קוֹרוֹת קְרָאוּנִי בְּעֵת אֵלֶיהָ בָאתִי.

963

And I sought among them a man who would vow a vow, and I did not find one.

וָאֲבַקֵּשׁ מֵהֶם אִישׁ נוֹדֵר נֶדֶר וְלֹא מָצָאתִי.

964

For all of them hid their faces from me,

כִּי כֻלָּם הִסְתִּירוּ מִמֶּנִּי פְנֵיהֶם.

965

and I will surely hide my face from them [Deut 31:18].

וְאָנֹכִי הַסְתֵּר אַסְתִּיר פָּנַי מֵהֶם.

966

And the head of all their great ones, Joseph ben Shever, I did not see, nor did he see me;

וְרֹאשׁ כָּל גְּדוֹלֵיהֶם יוֹסֵף בֶּן שֶׁבֶר לֹא רְאִיתִיו וְלֹא רָאָנִי.

967

but I knew him, even if he did not know me.

אֲבָל יְדַעְתִּיו אִם לֹא יְדָעָנִי.

968

Had God loved him, he would have loved me;

וְאִלּוּ הָאֵל אֲהֵבוֹ אֲהֵבָנִי.

969

only his Maker rejected him, and therefore he rejected me.

רַק מְאָסָהוּ יוֹצְרוֹ עַל כֵּן מְאָסָנִי.

970

But I found, of the choicest of their sages and their pious, the sage Rabbi Isaac, a man of precious soul,

אֲבָל מָצָאתִי מִמִּבְחַר חַכְמֵיהֶם וַחֲסִידֵיהֶם הֶחָכָם רַבִּי יִצְחָק בַּעַל נֶפֶשׁ יְקָרָה.

971

whose way is a straight way.

וְדַרְכּוֹ דֶרֶךְ יְשָׁרָה.

972

From there I went to the city of Tavekh⚠53, and there is the seat of the great Nagid, the chosen of God,

וּמִשָּׁם הָלַכְתִּי לְעִיר תָּוֶךְ וְשָׁם מוֹשַב הַנָּגִיד הַגָּדוֹל בְּחִיר הָאֵל.

973

the prince Rabbi Samuel,

הַשַּׂר רַבִּי שְׁמוּאֵל.

974

who is a strong tower for all the sons of his people.

אֲשֶׁר הוּא מִגְדַּל עֹז לְכָל בְּנֵי עַמּוֹ.

975

In all the land of Shinar there is no generous man but he alone,

אֵין בְּכָל אֶרֶץ שִׁנְעָר נָדִיב רַק הוּא לְבַדּוֹ.

976

and he puts on the garments of discipline as his robe.

וְלוֹבֵש בִגְדֵי הַמּוּסָר כְּמַדּוֹ.

977

And his two brothers are two golden pipes [Zech 4:12],

וּשְׁנֵי אֶחָיו שְׁנֵי צַנְתּרוֹת הַזָּהָב.

978

in whom God has delighted, and whom he has loved.

בָּם חָשַׁק הָאֵל וְאָהָב.

979

All of them are lovely,

כֻּלָּם נֶחֱמָדִים.

980

and all of them honored,

וְכֻלָּם נִכְבָּדִים.

981

and in the three of them the wanderers find rest,

וּבִשְׁלָשְׁתָּם יִמְצְאוּ הַנּוֹדְדִים מָנוֹחַ.

982

and the weary renew their strength [Isa 40:31]:

וְהַיְּעֵפִים יַחֲלִיפוּ כֹחַ.

983

these three are the sons of Noah [Gen 9:19]⚠54.

שְׁלשָׁה אֵלֶּה בְּנֵי נֹחַ.

984

And he took up his parable and said:

וַיִּשָּׂא מְשָׁלוֹ וַיֹּאמַר:

985

Take the secret of the worthy Jews, who are named from the sea to the east.

קְחָה סוֹד הַיְּהוּדִים הַחֲשׁוּבִים אֲשֶׁר מִיָּם וְעַד מִזְרָח נְקוּבִים.

986

Some of them shut their hand on their wealth against every asker, and some of them are generous.

קְצָתָם קוֹפְצִים יָדָם בְּהוֹנָם לְכָל-שׁוֹאֵל וְיֵשׁ מֵהֶם נְדִיבִים.

987

I have recorded their names in my book, and set them in it, hewn with a pen of iron [Job 19:24],

וְזָכַרְתִּי בְסִפְרִי אֶת שְׁמוֹתָם וְשַׂמְתִּים בּוֹ בְּעֵט בַּרְזֶל חֲצוּבִים.

988

so that the last generation may see their deeds: behold, are they not written? [1 Kgs 11:41]

לְמַעַן יֶחֱזוּ דוֹר אַחֲרוֹן אֵת פְּעָלֵיהֶם הֲלֹא הִנָּם כְּתוּבִים.

Uncertain renderings

  • ⚠1 נָסַעְתִּי מֵעֲדִינָה — Edinah (עֲדִינָה, "the pampered one") is what Isa 47:8 calls Babylon. In this gate it stands for the chief city of Iraq, most probably Baghdad; it is named again near the end, in the tour of Iraq.
  • ⚠2 לְעֵילָם הַמְּדִינָה — Elam, the biblical name for the land east of Babylonia, in medieval Hebrew usually Persia (western Iran).
  • ⚠3 וּמַרְאֵה הָאוֹפַנִּים וּמַעֲשֵׂיהֶם — Ezek 1:16 "the appearance of the wheels and their work". אוֹפַנִּים "wheels" also sounds like אֳפָנִים "manners, ways": Heman asks to hear what the people are like and what they do.
  • ⚠4 שָׁאַפְתִּי מוֹר אֲהָלִים מִבֵּין אֹהָלֶיהָ — Prov 7:17 מֹר אֲהָלִים "myrrh and aloes"; אֲהָלִים "aloes" is spelled like אֹהָלִים "tents", so the line plays "aloes from among its tents".
  • ⚠5 הַלֵּוִי רַבִּי מֵאִיר בֶּן טַדְרוֹס — Meir ben Todros ha-Levi Abulafia of Toledo (died 1244), Talmudist and opponent of Maimonides' views on resurrection.
  • ⚠6 רַבֵּנוּ שֵׁשֶׁת — Sheshet Benveniste of Barcelona (died about 1209), physician, poet and patron, a defender of Maimonides.
  • ⚠7 לְעִיר בַּלְקִירִי — בַּלְקִירִי is read as Beaucaire on the Rhône, in Provence, between Narbonne and Marseille on the route. The identification is probable, not certain.
  • ⚠8 הַקָּרָאִים. / רֹאשׁ הַקְּרוּאִים — A pun: he is of the Karaites (קָרָאִים, qara'im) and head of the "called" (קְרוּאִים, qeru'im), the notables of the assembly (compare Num 1:16).
  • ⚠9 צֹעַן מִצְרַיִם הַנְּסוּכָה — Zoan (Num 13:22, Isa 19:11), the old royal city of Egypt. Here it is Cairo, the seat of the Ayyubid sultan. The "second city", where Abraham son of Maimonides lived, is Fustat (Old Cairo).
  • ⚠10 כִּי הֵסִיר מֵחִבּוּרוֹ — His work: the Mishneh Torah, Maimonides' code of Talmudic law, which gives the rulings without the names of the sages and without the Talmud's debate.
  • ⚠11 וְאֶרֶץ הַצְּבִי וְשִׁנְעָר — "The Land of the Gazelle" (Dan 11:16, 11:41) is the Land of Israel; Shinar (Gen 11:2) is Babylonia, that is, Iraq.
  • ⚠12 הָרַב הֶחָסִיד רַבִּי יוֹסֵף בֶּן הָרַב רַבִּי בָרוּךְ — Joseph ben Barukh and his brother Meir, Tosafists who went from France to the Land of Israel in the emigration of 1211.
  • ⚠13 רֹאשׁ יְשִׁיבַת גְּאוֹן יַעֲקֹב — "Head of the academy Pride of Jacob" (Ps 47:5) is the formal title of a Gaon, head of one of the great academies. The next lines tell that Zadok lost the office and went into exile.
  • ⚠14 הַנָּשִׂיא רַבִּי יֹאשִׁיָה בֶּן יִשַׁי — Josiah son of Jesse, Nasi of Damascus, the patron to whom the book is dedicated in gate 1.
  • ⚠15 וְתוֹךְ הַקְּדוֹשִׁים קְדֵשִׁים — A pun: קְדוֹשִׁים (qedoshim) "holy ones" and קְדֵשִׁים (qedeshim) "cult prostitutes" (Deut 23:18, 1 Kgs 14:24) differ by one vowel.
  • ⚠16 בָּרוּךְ הָרוֹפֵא הַנָּבָל הַמְקֻלָּל — The name Barukh means "blessed"; the satire calls him "accursed" and sets him on Mount Ebal, the mountain of the curses (Deut 27:13).
  • ⚠17 וַיְדַבֵּר חֲמוֹר אִתָּם לֵאמֹר — Gen 34:8 "And Hamor spoke with them, saying". The name Hamor means "donkey"; the verse prepares the joke below, where the son is named "Shechem son of Hamor". "Fathers of impurity" is the legal term for the main sources of ritual impurity (m. Kelim 1).
  • ⚠18 כֶּתֶר הַמְּאוֹרוֹת / יֶתֶר הַמְּאֵרוֹת — A rhyming title, perhaps an honorific of the Arabic kind ("Crown of ..."): כֶּתֶר הַמְּאוֹרוֹת (keter ha-me'orot) "crown of the lights" is turned into יֶתֶר הַמְּאֵרוֹת (yeter ha-me'erot) "the rest of the curses" by a change of one letter and one vowel.
  • ⚠19 שְׁכֶם בֶּן חֲמוֹר — Shechem son of Hamor, who violated Dinah (Gen 34:2); the name means "son of a donkey", since the father is as stupid as an ox and a donkey. Below, Shechem "violates" the poet's poem in the same words.
  • ⚠20 וַיִּקְרָא לָהּ נֹבַח בִּשְׁמוֹ — Num 32:42 "and he called it Nobah, after his own name". נֹבַח (Nobah) is read as נוֹבֵחַ "barking": he barks his poems and names his poetry "Barking".
  • ⚠21 שִׁירָתוֹ קְדֵשָׁה. / לֹא קְדוֹשָׁה. / וּמְחֻלֶּלֶת. / לֹא מְהֻלֶּלֶת — Two puns: קְדֵשָׁה "harlot" and קְדוֹשָׁה "holy"; מְחֻלֶּלֶת "profaned" and מְהֻלֶּלֶת "praised". The lines after this treat the poetry as Tamar at the entrance to Enaim (Gen 38).
  • ⚠22 וַיִּרְאֶהָ יְהוּדָה — Gen 38:15, Judah and Tamar. Judah is also the author's own name, Judah al-Ḥarizi, who in the next lines tells how he sent this man a poem.
  • ⚠23 לֶחֶם מַצָּה בְּלִי חָמֵץ. / וְהַיַּיִן חֹמֶץ — A pun that cannot be carried over: the bread is without חָמֵץ (ḥamets, leaven), and the wine is חֹמֶץ (ḥomets, vinegar), the same letters.
  • ⚠24 וְהַשְּׁקֵדִים בִּקְנֵה הַבּוּז מְשֻׁקָּדִים — Exod 25:33 "cups shaped like almonds on one branch (קָנֶה)" of the lampstand. Here the branch is the "branch of contempt" (בּוּז); the sense of the joke is uncertain.
  • ⚠25 אֶת הֶחָצִיר וְאֶת הַבְּצָלִים וְאֶת הַשּׁוּמִים — Num 11:5. חָצִיר means "leeks" in that verse, but "grass" elsewhere, as in the lines above ("pots full of grass", "all flesh is grass"). Both senses are meant.
  • ⚠26 כִּי הוּא מִתְעַסֵּק בִּגְנוּת — A pun: גְּנוּת "disgrace" has the same letters as גַּנּוֹת "gardens", the word of the verse quoted two lines below (Isa 66:17), which speaks of idolatrous rites in gardens.
  • ⚠27 אֶל רֹב יְבָשְׁתּוֹ הַרֲרֵי גִלְבּוֹעַ — Hermon is the mountain of dew (Ps 133:3); the mountains of Gilboa were cursed to have no dew or rain (2 Sam 1:21). At his touch even Hermon would be so dry that Gilboa would envy it.
  • ⚠28 לְהַר צְמָרַיִם — Mount Zemaraim (2 Chr 13:4; compare "the Zemarite", Gen 10:18). On the route between Damascus and Hamath it is probably Homs (Ḥimṣ); the identification is not certain.
  • ⚠29 רַבִּי שְׁאֵרִית. / הוּא לַיּשֶׁר פְּלֵיטָה וּשְׁאֵרִית — The name She'erit means "remnant", hence the play "a survivor and a remnant" (compare Gen 45:7).
  • ⚠30 אֶרֶץ חֲמָת — Hamath, the biblical name of Hama on the Orontes, in Syria.
  • ⚠31 וִיצַו עָלָיו בְּמַפְגִּיעַ — Uncertain. מַפְגִּיעַ is from הִפְגִּיעַ "entreat, intercede" (Isa 53:12, Jer 36:25). Read as a wish that God command the redemption for Uzziel's sake; perhaps "and may He command it at his entreaty".
  • ⚠32 וּבַעְדּוֹ שֵׁם אֱמֶת אֶקְרָא רְחֹבוֹת — Gen 26:22: Isaac named the well Rehoboth, "for now the Lord has made room (הִרְחִיב) for us". The first half of the line quotes the same verse.
  • ⚠33 מְשָׁרֵת משֶׁה מִבְּחוּרָיו — Num 11:28, said of Joshua, "the minister of Moses from his youth". Which Moses this Isaac served is not stated.
  • ⚠34 הִיא אֲרַם צוֹבָה הַבְּרוּכָה — Aram Zobah (2 Sam 8:3, Ps 60:2) is the medieval Hebrew name for Aleppo.
  • ⚠35 הֶחָכָם רַבִּי יוֹסֵף מַעֲרָבִי — Joseph ben Judah, the Maghrebi pupil of Maimonides to whom the Guide of the Perplexed is addressed; he settled in Aleppo as a physician. He is "the great physician Rabbi Joseph the Maghrebi" again below.
  • ⚠36 נְתָנָהוּ כְמוֹ נֵר מַעֲרָבִי — The "western lamp" of the Temple lampstand, which, the Talmud says, burned on after the others (b. Shabbat 22b). מַעֲרָבִי "western" is also his name, "the Maghrebi".
  • ⚠37 וְגֻלָּה לְרֹאשׁ הַגּוֹלָה — Zech 4:2-3 describes a lampstand with a bowl (גֻּלָּה) on its top and two olive trees beside it. גֻּלָּה "bowl" plays on גּוֹלָה "exile": the two brothers are like the two olive trees, and an ornament to the head of the exile.
  • ⚠38 הוּא הָרֹאשׁ רַבִּי נִסִּים — "He" is taken to be the brothers' father (or forebear), Rabbi Nissim; הָרֹאשׁ "the head" may be a title, as in the academies.
  • ⚠39 וְאִלּוּ הָשְׁלַךְ בְּאוּר כַּשְׂדִּים — אוּר כַּשְׂדִּים is Ur of the Chaldees (Gen 11:31), read in the Midrash as "the fire of the Chaldees", into which Nimrod threw Abraham, who came out unhurt (Gen. Rabbah 38:13).
  • ⚠40 וּשְׁמוֹ מַזְהִיר — The name Mazhir means "shining", which the line before takes up ("shining like the light of dawn").
  • ⚠41 נִיבוֹ צְרִי צִיר מְחֻלֶּה — Uncertain. צִיר is both "envoy" (Prov 13:17 "a faithful envoy is healing") and "pang"; read here as "pang". Perhaps "balm for a sick envoy".
  • ⚠42 רַבִּי יְשׁוּעָה וְרַבִּי יָכִין הֵם עַמּוּדֵי הַמְּלוּכָה — Jachin is the name of one of the two pillars of Solomon's Temple (1 Kgs 7:21), hence "the pillars of the kingdom".
  • ⚠43 לְמַעֲרָבָה — A place after Aleppo on the way to Saruj, not identified with certainty. The word could also be read "westward", but the next line speaks of a congregation "there".
  • ⚠44 לְעִיר סְרוּג — Saruj (biblical Serug, Gen 11:20), a town in Upper Mesopotamia near Edessa. Aram Naharaim (Gen 24:10), the next stop, is here a city between Saruj and Harran, probably Edessa (al-Ruha); the identification is not certain.
  • ⚠45 חַמְדָּן וְיִתְרָן. / רַק פַּגְעִיאֵל בֶּן עָכְרָן — Biblical names used for their sound: Hemdan and Ithran (Gen 36:26) suggest חֶמְדָּה "delight" and יֶתֶר "abundance"; Pagiel son of Ochran (Num 1:13) suggests פֶּגַע "mishap" and עֹכֵר "troubler".
  • ⚠46 הַמַּרְחִיקִים מִשּׁוֹר תָּם — A pun: the Talmud tells one to keep fifty cubits away from a תָּם ox, one not yet known to gore (b. Pesahim 112b); תָּם also means "simple, innocent", as the line before calls the scribe.
  • ⚠47 לְכַלְנֶה — Calneh (Gen 10:10, Amos 6:2), one of Nimrod's cities in Shinar. On this route between Harran and Mosul it is probably Raqqa on the Euphrates, which Benjamin of Tudela also calls Calneh.
  • ⚠48 וַיָּבוֹאוּ שְׂנֵי הַמַּלְאָכִים סְדוֹמָה — Gen 19:1. In rabbinic tradition Sodom is the city that refused charity to the poor; the two generous men are like the two angels who came to Sodom, alone among the close-fisted people of Calneh.
  • ⚠49 לְאַשּׁוּר — Ashur (Gen 10:11), the land of Assyria; in medieval Hebrew the city of Mosul on the Tigris, across from the ruins of Nineveh.
  • ⚠50 בְּרָכוֹת שְׁמוֹ — The name Berakhot means "blessings", against which the satire sets his wickedness. In the poem below, בֶּטֶן בְּרָכוֹת can also be heard as "a belly of blessings".
  • ⚠51 קָרְאוּ שְׁמוֹ נֶאְמָן — "Trusty" (נֶאֱמָן) may stand for an Arabic title such as al-Amīn, "the trustworthy"; this is a guess.
  • ⚠52 וּכְבוֹאִי אֵלֶיהָ — The city is not named. The block ends with Ashur (Isa 27:13), so it may still be Mosul, or a name may have fallen out of the text.
  • ⚠53 לְעִיר תָּוֶךְ — A town in Iraq ("the land of Shinar", in the lines below) that is not identified with certainty.
  • ⚠54 שְׁלשָׁה אֵלֶּה בְּנֵי נֹחַ — Gen 9:19. The name Noah is explained in Gen 5:29 as "rest" (compare נוּחַ, מָנוֹחַ); the three brothers are the place where the wanderers "find rest".
