שַׁעַר חֲמִשָּׁה עָשָׂר

תְּפִלָּה לְמשֶׁה אִישׁ הָאֱלֹהִים. הַפּוֹתַחַת לְכָל אוֹמְרָהּ שַׁעֲרֵי גְבוֹהִים.

The Fifteenth Gate

After a shipwreck, Ḥever the Kenite comforts Heman with a prayer to Moses the man of God, and the prayer restores his fortune

Gate 15 on Project Ben-Yehuda ↗

About this text

191 lines. One row is one rhyme unit of the rhymed prose, or one line of verse (its two halves set apart). References in [gray brackets] are the translator's identifications of biblical phrases. ⚠ marks an uncertain rendering. Each line number is a link to that line. Hebrew text: Project Ben-Yehuda.

1

Thus says Heman the Ezrahite: In the days of my youth I had riches and wealth,

נְאֻם הֵימָן הָאֶזְרָחִי: הָיָה לִי בִּימֵי נְעוּרַי עשֶׁר וָהוֹן.

2

and strength and vigor.

וְכֹחַ וָאוֹן.

3

But "I have grown rich, I have found myself wealth" [Hos 12:9]⚠1.

אַךְ עָשַׁרְתִּי מָצָאתִי אוֹן.

4

And it came to pass one day that I saw merchants

וַיְהִי הַיּוֹם רָאִיתִי סוֹחֲרִים.

5

crossing over to the other side of the sea [Deut 30:13].

אֶל עֵבֶר הַיָּם עוֹבְרִים.

6

And I went down with them [Jonah 1:3],

וָאֵרֵד עִמָּהֶם.

7

and joined myself to them.

וְנִצְמַדְתִּי אֲלֵיהֶם.

8

And when we had come into the midst of the sea,

וְכַאֲשֶׁר בְּתוֹךְ הַיָּם בָּאנוּ.

9

the hand of the Lord touched us [Job 19:21].

יַד ה' נָגְעָה בָנוּ.

10

And there was a great storm in the sea [Jonah 1:4],

וַיְהִי סַעַר גָּדוֹל בַּיָּם.

11

and its waves lifted up their roaring [Ps 93:3].

וְנָשְׂאוּ גַלָּיו דָּכְיָם.

12

And after great distress we escaped and were saved,

וְאַחֲרֵי צַעַר גָּדוֹל נִמְלַטְנוּ וְנִצַּלְנוּ.

13

and all our wealth we cast into the sea [Jonah 1:5].

וְכָל הוֹנֵנוּ אֶל תּוֹךְ הַיָּם הֵטַלְנוּ.

14

And I came out of the ship crushed and wretched,

וְיָצָאתִי מִן הַסְּפִינָה דַּכָּא וְאֻמְלָל.

15

naked and stripped [Mic 1:8],

עָרוֹם וְשׁוֹלָל.

16

a brother of sorrow and grief,

אֲחִי יָגוֹן וְתוּגָה.

17

like Jonah when he came out of the belly of the fish [Jonah 2:2]⚠2.

כּמוֹ יוֹנָה בְּצֵאתוֹ מִמְּעֵי הַדָּגָה.

18

And when I came into the city, to my house,

וּכְבוֹאִי הָעִירָה אֶל בֵּיתִי.

19

my friends came to comfort me.

בָּאוּ אוֹהֲבַי לְנַחֵם אוֹתִי.

20

And while I was still rocking in grief before the eyes of those who saw me,

וּבְעוֹדִי מִתְנוֹדֵד לְעֵינֵי הָרוֹאִים.

21

behold, Ḥever the Kenite came among those who came [Gen 42:5].

וְהִנֵּה חֶבֶר הַקֵּינִי בָא בְּתוֹךְ הַבָּאִים.

22

And he sat down beside me,

וַיֵּשֶׁב אֶצְלִי.

23

and wept over what had befallen me.

וַיִּבְכֶּה עַל אֲשֶׁר קָרָה לִי.

24

And he said to me: "If I had wealth in my hand, I would not have hidden it from you,

וַיֹּאמֶר לִי: אִלּוּ הָיָה בְיָדִי הוֹן מִמְּךָ לֹא צְפַנְתִּיהוּ.

25

but would have given it to you with a good heart.

וּלְךָ בְּלֵב טוֹב נְתַתִּיהוּ.

26

But from what God has allotted me I will allot to you,

אַךְ מֵאֲשֶׁר חָלַק לִי הָאֵל אֲחַלֵּק לָךְ.

27

and from what He has given me I will give to you.

וּמֵאֲשֶׁר נָתַן לִי אֶתֵּן לָךְ.

28

Behold, I will teach you a prayer of Moses the man of God [Ps 90:1].

וְהִנְנִי מְלַמֶּדְךָ תְּפִלָּה לִמשֶׁה אִישׁ הָאֱלֹהִים.

29

It will open for you the gates of the heights,

תִּפְתַּח לְךָ שַׁעֲרֵי גְבוֹהִים.

30

and make your salvation dawn in lights of brightness.

וְתַזְרִיחַ יִשְׁעֲךָ בְּאוֹרֵי נְגֹהִים.

31

And if you recite it in integrity of heart and innocence of hands [Gen 20:5],

וְאִם תִּקְרָאֶנָּה בְּתָם לֵבָב וּבְנִקְיוֹן כַּפַּיִם.

32

evening and morning and noon [Ps 55:18],

עֶרֶב וָבֹקֶר וְצָהֳרַיִם.

33

the Lord will open for you His treasure, the heavens [Deut 28:12],

יִפְתַּח ה' לְךָ אֶת אוֹצָרוֹ אֶת הַשָּׁמַיִם.

34

and pour out His blessing upon you [Mal 3:10],

וְיָרִיק עָלֶיךָ בִרְכָתוֹ.

35

and bestow on you of His goodness."

וְיַעֲנִיק לְךָ מִטּוֹבָתוֹ.

36

I said to him: "This is my comfort in my affliction [Ps 119:50],

אָמַרְתִּי לוֹ: זֹאת נֶחָמָתִי בְּעָנְיִי.

37

and this shall be my healing for my sickness."

וְזֹאת תִּהְיֶה רְפוּאָתִי לְחָלְיִי.

38

And he said: "Incline your ear to hear its wonders,

וַיֹּאמַר: הַט אָזְנְךָ לִשְׁמֹעַ פְּלִיאוֹתֶיהָ.

39

and to understand its awesome things;

וּלְהָבִין נוֹרְאוֹתֶיהָ.

40

for none like it has been composed

כִּי כָמוֹהָ לֹא חֻבַּר.

41

in pleasantness of saying and purity of speech."

בְּנֹעַם מַאֲמָר וְצַחוֹת דֻבַּר.

42

And he answered and said: "The hosts of the skies and their array,

וַיַּעַן וַיֹּאמַר: צִבְאוֹת שְׁחָקִים וְחֵילָם.

43

which are from of old,

אֲשֶׁר מֵעוֹלָם.

44

lift up gifts of peace,

יִשְׂאוּ מַשְׂאוֹת שְׁלוֹמִים.

45

and lovingkindness and mercy,

וְחֶסֶד וְרַחֲמִים.

46

to the portion of God from above and the heritage of the Almighty from on high [Job 31:2]⚠3.

לְחֵלֶק אֱלוֹהַּ מִמַּעַל וְנַחֲלַת שַׁדַּי מִמְּרוֹמִים.

47

He is the faithful envoy,

הוּא הַצִּיר הַנֶּאֱמָן.

48

the humble, the merciful,

הֶעָנָו הָרַחֲמָן.

49

the foundation of prophecy,

יְסוֹד הַנְּבוּאָה.

50

and the secret of humility and fear;

וְסוֹד הָעֲנָוָה וְהַיִּרְאָה.

51

the bright light,

הָאוֹר הַבָּהִיר.

52

and the shining splendor,

וְהַזִּיו הַמַּזְהִיר.

53

on whose praises agree the rash and the haughty,

אֲשֶׁר הִסְכִּימוּ עַל שְבָחָיו הַנִּמְהָר וְהַיָּהִיר.

54

the pious and the pure.

הֶחָסִיד וְהַבָּהִיר.

55

The bush and Sinai are his witnesses;

הַסְּנֶה וְסִינַי עֵדָיו.

56

the Red Sea and the Jordan and the Arnon testify of him and declare him.

וְיַם-סוּף וְיַרְדֵּן וְאַרְנוֹן מְעִידָיו וּמַגִּידָיו.

57

Egypt pours forth his praises,

מִצְרַיִם תַּבִּיעַ תְּהִלּוֹתָיו.

58

and the tongue of the sea [Isa 11:15] tells his deeds,

וּלְשׁוֹן הַיָּם תְּחַוֶּה פְעֻלּוֹתָיו.

59

and the Nile reveals his signs,

וְהַיְאֹר יְגַלֶּה אוֹתוֹתָיו.

60

and Mount Horeb is a proof of his mighty acts.

וְהַר חוֹרֵב מוֹפֵת עַל גְּבוּרוֹתָיו.

61

Tongues fall short,

הַלְּשׁוֹנוֹת קָצְרוּ.

62

and keep no strength [Dan 10:8],

וְכֹחַ לֹא עָצְרוּ.

63

for the virtues of a pearl-like soul, revealed within⚠4.

בְּמִדּוֹת נֶפֶשׁ פְנִינִיָּה גְּלוּיָה פְנִימָה.

64

Before his God formed him in the womb,

טֶרֶם אֱלֹהָיו בַּבֶּטֶן יְצָרָהוּ.

65

He made him a prophet to the nations [Jer 1:5].

נָבִיא לַגּוֹיִם נְתָנָהוּ.

66

And he called to the heavens above and to the earth below [Ps 50:4],

וְקָרָא אֶל הַשָּׁמַיִם מִמַּעַל וְאֶל הָאָרֶץ מִתָּחַת.

67

and from the joining of the two there came an awesome creation⚠5

וַיְהִי מֵחִבּוּר שְׁנֵיהֶם בְּרִיאָה נוֹרָאָה.

68

and a wondrous formation.

וִיצִירָה נִפְלָאָה.

69

He is our lord, who brings us out of gloom into light [m. Pesahim 10:5],

הוּא אֲדוֹנֵנוּ הַמּוֹצִיאֵנוּ מֵאֲפֵלָה לְאוֹרָה.

70

who gives us statute and Torah for our heritage,

הַמַּנְחִילֵנוּ חֻקָּה וְתוֹרָה.

71

who proclaims liberty from the service of the Inclination

הַקּוֹרֵא דְרוֹר מֵעֲבוֹדַת הַיֵּצֶר.

72

to the service of the Creator⚠6;

לַעֲבוֹדַת הַיּוֹצֵר.

73

who came down from the choicest loins

הַיּוֹרֵד מִמִּבְחַר הַחֲלָצַיִם.

74

to the best of wombs.

לְמֵיטַב הַמֵּעַיִם.

75

From every pious woman and pious man, people of uprightness and worth,

מִכָּל חֲסִידָה וְחָסִיד בַּעֲלֵי ישֶׁר וְכִשְׁרוֹן.

76

and men of righteousness and excellence,

וְאַנְשֵׁי צֶדֶק וְיִתְרוֹן.

77

the later ones received him

קִבְּלוּהוּ הָאַחֲרוֹנִים.

78

from the earlier ones;

מִן הָרִאשׁוֹנִים.

79

in a faithful tradition they carried him, through the chosen of every generation,

קַבָּלַת אֱמוּנָה נְשָׂאוּהוּ מִכָּל דּוֹר בְּרוּרָיו.

80

and through the upright of every age,

וּמִכָּל זְמַן יְשָׁרָיו.

81

until he reached the good root that made him sprout,

עַד הִגִּיעַ אֶל הַשֹּׁרֶשׁ הַטּוֹב אֲשֶׁר הִצְמִיחָהוּ.

82

and the bundle of myrrh [Song 1:13] that hid him [Exod 2:2].

וּצְרוֹר הַמּוֹר אֲשֶׁר צְפָנָהוּ.

83

And the earth made wide its dwelling for him,

וְהִרְחִיבָה לוֹ הָאָרֶץ מוֹשָׁבָהּ.

84

and set him a memorial at the head of a pillar⚠7.

וְנָתְנָה לוֹ יָד בְּרֹאשׁ מַצָּבָה.

85

And there were revealed from the tongue of the Almighty,

וְנִגְלָה מִלְּשׁוֹן הַגְּבוּרָה.

86

upon his tongue, the wonders of the Torah.

עַל לְשׁוֹנוֹ נִפְלְאוֹת הַתּוֹרָה.

87

He was sent to bring tidings to the humble [Isa 61:1],

שֻׁלַּח לְבַשֵּׂר עֲנָוִים.

88

and to heal the poor.

וּלְרַפְּאוֹת דַּלִּים.

89

He taught the perplexed,

הוֹרָה נְבוּכִים.

90

and gave light to those in darkness,

וְהֵאִיר חֲשֵׁכִים.

91

when the world was in darkness and gloom,

וְהָעוֹלָם בְּמַחְשָׁךְ וַעֲלָטָה.

92

and truth was missing in deed and in utterance,

וְהָאֱמֶת נֶעְדֶּרֶת בְּמִפְעָל וּבְמִבְטָא.

93

until the time that his word came [Ps 105:19],

עַד עֵת בֹּא דְבָרוֹ.

94

and his glory and his light were revealed,

וְנִגְלָה יְקָרוֹ וְאוֹרוֹ.

95

and the children of the world were lit by him,

וְהֵאִירוּ בוֹ בְּנֵי הָעוֹלָם.

96

and all the peoples believed in his signs.

וְהֶאֱמִינוּ בְאוֹתוֹתָיו עַמִּים כֻּלָּם.

97

The splendor of prophecy shone upon us, pure and clear in its radiance,

נָגַהּ עָלֵינוּ זִיו הַנְּבוּאָה זַךְ וָצַח לְזֹהַר.

98

and radiance upon radiance flowed down from the highest sphere.

וְנֶאֱצַל מִגְּלִיל עֶלְיוֹן זֹהַר עַל זֹהַר.

99

He went up to heaven in contemplations clean of every taint and error [Job 4:18],

וְעָלָה שָׁמַיִם בְּעִיּוּנִים נְקִיִּים מִכָּל שֶׁמֶץ וְתָהֳלָה.

100

and before his going up, the angels were the first to go up,

וְטֶרֶם עֲלוֹתוֹ הַמַּלְאָכִים הֵם הָעוֹלִים בַּתְּחִלָה.

101

to search out for him a place to walk

לָתוּר לוֹ מַהְלְכִים.

102

among the angels who walk [Zech 3:7]

בֵּין הַמַּלְאָכִים הַמְהַלְּכִים.

103

around the throne of God and His glory.

סְבִיב כִּסֵּא הָאֵל וְתִפְאַרְתּוֹ.

104

And he took his stand on the mount which God desired for His dwelling [Ps 68:17].

וְהִתְיַּצֵּב בָּהָר חָמַד אֱלֹהִים לְשִׁבְתּוֹ.

105

And he went on, going up higher, and the clouds of glory bore him upon their palms [Ps 91:12],

וַיֵּלֶךְ הָלוֹךְ וְעָלֹה וְעַנְנֵי הַכָּבוֹד עַל כַּפַּיִם יִשָּׂאוּהוּ.

106

and holy myriads [Deut 33:2] surrounded him,

וְרִבְבוֹת קֹדֶשׁ יַקִּיפוּהוּ.

107

and a voice called before him: "Open the gates [Isa 26:2],

וְקוֹל קוֹרֵא לְפָנָיו: פִּתְחוּ שְׁעָרִים.

108

and let the chosen of the chosen come in,

וְיָבֹא בְּחִיר הַבְּחִירִים.

109

the choicest of the choice!

וּבָרוּר מִבְּרוּרִים.

110

This is the man whom his Maker has chosen,

זֶה הָאִישׁ אֲשֶׁר יוֹצְרוֹ בְחָרוֹ.

111

the man whom the King delights to honor [Esth 6:9]."

אִישׁ אֲשֶׁר הַמֶּלֶךְ חָפֵץ בִּיקָרוֹ.

112

And when he came, the angels of the heavenly dwellings rose before him,

וּבְבוֹאוֹ קָמוּ לְפָנָיו אֶרְאֶלֵּי מְעוֹנִים.

113

and the holy ones on high said to him: "Peace be your coming!"

וְאָמְרוּ לוֹ: שָׁלוֹם בּוֹאֲךָ קְדוֹשִׁים עֶלְיוֹנִים.

114

And he spoke with his Maker face to face [Exod 33:11].

וְדִבֶּר עִם יוֹצְרוֹ פָנִים אֶל פָּנִים.

115

And he stood in the hiding place of strength [Hab 3:4] on feet at the dread of whose tread the earth trembled,

וְעָמַד בְּחֶבְיוֹן הָעֹז בְּרַגְלַיִם רָגְזָה הָאָרֶץ לְאֵימַת מִצְעֲדֵיהֶם.

116

and mouths longed to lick their dust [Isa 49:23].

וְחָמְדוּ הַפִּיּוֹת לִלְחֹךְ עַפְרוֹתֵיהֶם.

117

And he met the face of God with a face on which there shone

Uncertain renderings

  • ⚠1 אַךְ עָשַׁרְתִּי מָצָאתִי אוֹן — Hos 12:9 is Ephraim's proud boast before his fall. אוֹן is "wealth" in Hosea and "strength" in the line before ("strength and vigor"); the quotation hints at the loss that follows.
  • ⚠2 כּמוֹ יוֹנָה בְּצֵאתוֹ מִמְּעֵי הַדָּגָה — יוֹנָה is both "Jonah" and "a dove" (a common image of moaning, Isa 38:14). The masculine בְּצֵאתוֹ "when he came out" points to the prophet.
  • ⚠3 לְחֵלֶק אֱלוֹהַּ מִמַּעַל וְנַחֲלַת שַׁדַּי מִמְּרוֹמִים — In Job 31:2 "the portion of God from above" is a person's lot. Here the phrase names Moses, God's own portion, to whom the hosts of heaven bring peace; the next lines describe him.
  • ⚠4 בְּמִדּוֹת נֶפֶשׁ פְנִינִיָּה גְּלוּיָה פְנִימָה — This line has no rhyme partner (the lines before and after rhyme in pairs), and its sense is uncertain: perhaps a soul whose pearl-like qualities show from within (compare Ps 45:14 פְּנִימָה). A line may be missing from the source.
  • ⚠5 וַיְהִי מֵחִבּוּר שְׁנֵיהֶם בְּרִיאָה נוֹרָאָה — Uncertain. Perhaps the call to heaven and earth as witnesses (Deut 32:1) and the joining of the upper and lower worlds in the giving of the Torah.
  • ⚠6 מֵעֲבוֹדַת הַיֵּצֶר. לַעֲבוֹדַת הַיּוֹצֵר — A pun on הַיֵּצֶר "the (evil) inclination" and הַיּוֹצֵר "the Creator", two words from the same letters.
  • ⚠7 וְנָתְנָה לוֹ יָד בְּרֹאשׁ מַצָּבָה — יָד is "a monument, a memorial" (1 Sam 15:12, Isa 56:5). It can also be read "gave him a hand (a share) at the head of the pillar", that is, first place. The image is uncertain.
  • ⚠8 לִמְפַקֵּד הַיֶּשַׁע וְהָרַחֲמִים — Read as from פקד "to remember, visit" (God's remembering of the needy). The pointing מְפַקֵּד can also be a participle, "to the one who commands salvation", that is, Moses.
  • ⚠9 עֵת חָרְבוּ מֵימֵי יְשׁוּעוֹת הָ- / יִיתָ כְּמוֹ פֶלֶג בְּתוֹךְ צָיוֹן — The Hebrew splits the word הָיִיתָ "you were" across the two half-lines. צָיוֹן is pointed as "dry land" (Isa 32:2), a play on צִיּוֹן "Zion", which ends the last line of the poem.
