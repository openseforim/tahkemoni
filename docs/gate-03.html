The Third Gate

A glutton at a feast in Babylon silences the guests who mocked him, with a survey of the Hebrew poets of Spain that ends in praise of Judah Halevi

Gate 3 on Project Ben-Yehuda ↗

About this text

435 lines. One row is one rhyme unit of the rhymed prose, or one line of verse (its two halves set apart). References in [gray brackets] are the translator's identifications of biblical phrases. ⚠ marks an uncertain rendering. Each line number is a link to that line. Hebrew text: Project Ben-Yehuda.

1

Thus says Heman the Ezrahite: In the days of my youth I set my heart,

נְאֻם הֵימָן הָאֶזְרָחִי; נָטִיתִי לִבִּי בִּימֵי נַעֲרוּתִי.

2

in the time of my young manhood,

וּזְמַן בַּחֲרוּתִי.

3

on chasing pleasures with companions

לִרְדוֹף הַתַּעֲנוּגִים עִם חֲבֵרִים.

4

upon the mountains of Bether [Song 2:17],

עַל הָרֵי בְתָרִים.

5

as one hunts the partridge in the mountains [1 Sam 26:20].

כַּאֲשֶׁר יִרְדֹּף הַקּוֹרֵא בֶהָרִים.

6

I bound my eyelids to the eyelids of the dawn [Job 3:9],

וְקָשַׁרְתִּי עַפְעַפַּי בְּעַפְעַפֵּי שְׁחָרִים.

7

and my wings to the wings of eagles,

וּכְנָפַי בְּכַנְפֵי נְשָׁרִים.

8

to take my delight in the villages [Song 7:12],

לְהִתְעַלֵּס בַּכְּפָרִים.

9

in a place of nard with henna [Song 4:13],

בִּמְקוֹם נְרָדִים עִם כְּפָרִים.

10

and a pasture of young deer.

וּמִרְעֵה עֳפָרִים.

11

For the times were still my slaves,

בְּעוֹד הַזְּמַנִּים לִי לַעֲבָדִים.

12

and their children went out in troops to serve me.

וּבָנָיו לְשָׁרְתֵנִי יָצְאוּ גְדוּדִים.

13

Before I called them they would answer [Isa 65:24],

בְּטֶרֶם אֶקְרָאֵם יַעֲנוּ.

14

and after my words they spoke no more [Job 29:22].

וְאַחַרַי דְבָרִי לֹא יִשְׁנוּ.

15

The children of a day were led by the bridle of my desire;

וּבְנֵי יוֹם בְּרֶסֶן חֶפְצִי יִמְשְׁכוּ.

16

wherever the spirit was to go, they went [Ezek 1:12].

אֶל אֲשֶׁר יִהְיֶה שָׁמָּה הָרוּחַ לָלֶכֶת יֵלְכוּ.

17

But when the troop sounded the trumpet blast [Lev 25:9]

וְכַאֲשֶׁר הֶעֱבִיר הַגְדוּד שׁוֹפַר תְּרוּעָה.

18

to pull out the peg that was fastened [Isa 22:25],

לְהָמִישׁ הַיָתֵד הַתְּקוּעָה.

19

I girded my loins with the belt of travel,

שִׁנַּסְתִּי מָתְנַי בְּאֵזוֹר הַנְּסִיעָה.

20

took off the garment of rest,

וּפָשַׁטְתִּי סוּת הַמַּרְגֵּעָה.

21

and put on the clothing of toil.

וְלָבַשְׁתִּי כְּסוּת הַיְגִיעָה.

22

I rebuked love and chose to wander,

וְגָעַרְתִּי בְדוֹדִים וּבָחַרְתִּי נְדוֹדִים.

23

and set my aim to roam to the ends of the world,

וְשַׂמְתִּי מְגַמָּתִי לְשׁוֹטֵט אַפְסֵי עוֹלָם.

24

until I came to the border of Shinar and Elam.

עַד בָּאתִי גְבוּל שִׁנְעָר וְעֵילָם.

25

One day I was in one of the cities of Babylon,

וַיְהִי הַיּוֹם וַאֲנִי בְעִיר מֵעָרֵי בָבֶל.

26

going about like a ship without a pilot.

מִתְהַלֵּךְ כָּאֳנִיָה בְּלִי חוֹבֵל.

27

One of the sons of the nobles invited me to his feast,

וַיִקְרָאֵנִי לִסְעֻדָּתוֹ אִישׁ מִבְּנֵי הַנְּגִידִים.

28

one of the honored lords of the land,

וּמַאֲצִילֵי אֶרֶץ הַנִכְבָּדִים.

29

and gave me a place to walk among those who stand by [Zech 3:7].

וְנָתַן לִי מַהְלְכִים בֵּין הָעוֹמְדִים.

30

He brought me to a palace,

וַיְבִיאֵנִי לְאַרְמוֹן.

31

nursed in the bosom of beauty [Lam 4:5],

בְּחֵיק הַיֳפִי אָמוֹן.

32

cut and hewn from the quarry of delight;

וּמִמַּחֲצַב הַנֹּעַם גָּזוּר וְחָצוּב.

33

from its rocks flow honey and milk,

וּמִסְּלָעָיו דְּבַשׁ וְחָלָב יָזוּב.

34

and within it are houses full of all good things [Deut 6:11], and wealth increases [Ps 62:11].

וּבְתוֹכוֹ בָתִים מְלֵאִים כָּל טוּב וְחַיִל יָנוּב.

35

Around the court were castles,

וּסְבִיב הֶחָצֵר בִּירוֹת.

36

and within the castles turrets,

וְתוֹךְ הַבִּירוֹת טִירוֹת.

37

with circlets of gold [Song 1:11] carved on the walls.

בְּתוֹרֵי זָהָב מְפֻתָּחִים עַל הַקִּירוֹת.

38

In them souls rejoice,

בָּהֶם הַנְפָשׁוֹת תִּצְהַלְנָה.

39

and ears tingle [1 Sam 3:11]

וְהָאָזְנַיִם תִּצַּלְנָה.

40

at the stir of the sound of the harp,

לְרִגְשַׁת קוֹל הַכִּנּוֹר.

41

and the flow of the waters of the channel. And the houses were covered with settings of gold [Ps 45:14],

וְשִׁפְעַת מֵי הַצִּנּוֹר, וְהַבָּתִּים בְּמִשְׁבְּצוֹת זָהָב מְכֻסִּים.

42

and every house adorned with all kinds of headbands [Isa 3:18],

וְכָל בַּיִת מְעֻטָּר בְּמִינֵי שְׁבִיסִים.

43

and with embroidered sails [Ezek 27:7] spread over it,

וּבְמִפְרְשֵי רִקְמָה עָלָיו פְּרוּשִׂים.

44

and it had made itself a coat of many colors [Gen 37:3].

וְעָשָׂה לוֹ כְתֹנֶת פַּסִּים.

45

Within the houses were tables,

וּבְתוֹךְ הַבָּתִּים שֻלְחָנוֹת.

46

set with every good thing,

בְּכָל טוּב מוּכָנוֹת.

47

fat and fresh [Ps 92:15],

דְּשֵׁנוֹת וְרַעֲנַנּוֹת.

48

and around them carpets,

וּסְבִיבָם מַרְבָדִים.

49

that renew the heart

הַלֵב מְחַדִּים.

50

and drive away grief.

וְהַיָגוֹן מְנַדִּים.

51

Before us were flagons and jars,

לְפָנֵינוּ אֲשִׁישׁוּת וְכַדִּים.

52

holding delights for every eye,

בָּם לְכָל עַיִן מַחֲמַדִּים.

53

and the wine like torches within the jars [Judg 7:16].

וְהַיַּיִן כְּלַפִּידִים תּוֹךְ הַכַּדִּים.

54

And the goblets

וְהַגְּבִיעִים.

55

were firmaments for the stars of the wine,

לְכוֹכְבֵי הַיַּיִן רְקִיעִים.

56

and the basins

וְהַמִּזְרָקִים.

57

as if cast out of light,

כְּאִלּוּ מִן הָאוֹר יְצוּקִים.

58

and banded with the gold of desire⚠1.

וּבִזְהַב הַחֵשֶׁק מְחֻשָׁקִים.

59

And I answered and said:

וָאַעַן וָאֹמַר:

60

Marvel how coal and hail are joined together, and flames of fire burst out of the channels,

תְּמַהּ אֵיך נִצְמְדוּ גַחַל וּבָרָד וּמוֹקְדֵי אֵשׁ יְפוּצוּן מֵאֲפִיקִים.

61

in threads of chains of beaten gold, cast in the clay of the heart's plain [1 Kgs 7:46]⚠2.

בִּחוּטֵי שַׁרְשְׁרוֹת זָהָב מְרֻקָּע בְּכִכַּר מַעֲבֵה הַלֵב יְצוּקִים.

62

They are juices in cups covered with pearl [Esth 1:6], and they scatter lights up to the skies.

עֲסִיסִים הֵם בְּכוֹסוֹת דַּר מְכֻסִּים וְיָפִיצוּ מְאוֹרִים עַד שְׁחָקִים.

63

They water you with a dew of lights, and yet into your mouth they pour streams of flame.

יְרַוֻּךָ בְטַל אוֹרוֹת וְאוּלָם בְּפִיךָ נוֹזְלֵי לַהַב מְרִיקִים.

64

It is as if the goblets were firmaments, and the wine within them like lightnings.

כִּאִלוּ הַגְּבִיעִים הֵם רְקִיעִים וְהַיַיִן בְּתוֹכָם כַּבְּרָקִים.

65

There were set before us all kinds of dainties,

וַיוּשָׂם לְפָנֵינוּ מִינֵי מַעֲדַנִים.

66

and a feast of fat things [Isa 25:6],

וּמִשׁתֵּה שְׁמָנִים.

67

and rich savory dishes.

וּמַטְעַמִּים דְּשֵׁנִים.

68

Around us were lovely servants,

וּסְבִיבֵנוּ מְשָׁרְתִים חֲמוּדִים.

69

standing before us like lamps,

כִּמְנוֹרוֹת לְפָנֵינוּ עוֹמְדִים.

70

to bring every pleasant and beloved food;

לְהָבִיא כָל מַאֲכָל נָעִים וְנֶאֱהָב.

71

each of them hurrying with a vessel of shining brass [Ezra 8:27],

וְכָל אֶחָד מֵהֶם מְמַהֵר וְאִתּוֹ כְלִי מֻצְהָב.

72

the cupbearer with a cup of gold in his hand.

הַמַּשְׁקֶה בְּיָדוֹ כּוֹס זָהָב.

73

And in came also the hilt after the blade [Judg 3:22]⚠3,

וְיָּבֹא גַם הַנִּצָּב אַחַר הַלַּהַב.

74

with roasted kids in his hands,

וּבְיָדָיו גְּדָיִים צְלוּיִים.

75

and gathering lambs in his arm [Isa 40:11],

וּזְרוֹעוֹ יְקַבֵּץ טְלָאִים.

76

tender and fair,

רַכִּים וְנָאִים.

77

whom the heat of the fire had tormented⚠4

אֲשֶׁר חֹם הָאֵשׁ הֶאֱדִיבָם.

78

until it brought them back

עָד הֱשִיבָם.

79

bright and clean,

צַחִים וְזַכִּים.

80

moist and tender,

לַחִים וְרַכִּים.

81

crushing the pride of hunger,

גְּאוֹן הָרָעָב מְדַכִּים.

82

and striking its pride with the scourge of the tongue [Job 5:21].

גַּאֲוָתוֹ בְּשׁוֹט לָשׁוֹן מַכִּים.

83

Among the guests [1 Sam 9:22] was an old man,

וְהָיָה בְתוֹך הַקְּרוּאִים אִישׁ זָקֵן.

84

wandering like a bird from its nest [Prov 27:8].

נוֹדֵד כְּצִיפּוֹר מִקֵּן.

85

He reclined at the laid table,

וְהוּא מֵסֵב אֶל הַשֻׁלְחָן הֶעָרוּך.

86

coiled like a serpent,

כְּנָחָשׁ כָּרוּך.

87

with a long arm

וּזְרוֹעוֹ אָרֹך.

88

to bring near every far-off dish,

לְהַקְרִיב כָּל מַאֲכָל רָחוֹק.

89

and to grind it and pound it fine.

וְלִטְחוֹן אוֹתוֹ וְלִשׂחוֹק.

90

He had a palate

וְלוֹ חֵך.

91

that licked up all that was round about us [Num 22:4],

אֶת כָּל סְבִיבוֹתֵינוּ לוֹחֵך.

92

and a tongue

וְלָשׁוֹן.

93

that swept the food away like the brook Kishon [Judg 5:21],

יִגְרֹף הַמַּאֲכָל כְּנַחַל קִישׁוֹן.

94

and he had a mouth

וְלוֹ פֶה.

95

that neither cupbearer nor baker could fill.

לֹא יְמַלְּאֵהוּ הַמַשְׁקֶה וְהָאוֹפֶה.

96

Always he set his eye on the cup [Prov 23:31],

תָּמִיד יִתֵּן בַּכּוֹס עֵינוֹ.

97

and his tooth in the bread,

וּבַלֶּחֶם שִׁנוֹ.

98

and the goblet was found going round to his right hand [Hab 2:16].

וַיִּמָּצֵא הַגָּבִיעַ סוֹבֵב עַל יְמִינוֹ.

99

And the meat,

וְהַבָּשָׂר.

100

even if it were bound in fetters of iron [Ps 149:8],

אִלוּ בְּכַבְלֵי בַרְזֶל נֶאֱסָר.

101

he would draw to him with his tongue,

יִמְשְׁכֵהוּ אֵלָיו בִּלְשׁוֹנוֹ.

102

and let it go free for his tooth [Exod 21:27].

וְלַחָפְשִׁי יִשַׁלְּחֵנּוּ תַּחַת שִׁנּוֹ.

103

He took the fat of every bread,

וְהוּא לוֹקֵחַ מִכָּל לֶחֶם חֶלְבּוֹ.

104

and the best of every dish,

וּמִכָּל מַאֲכָל טוּבוֹ.

105

and into his mouth he pushed and thrust

וְאֶל פִּיו דּוֹחֵף וְהוֹדֵף.

106

every good piece, the thigh and the shoulder [Ezek 24:4].

כָּל נֵתַח טוֹב וְכָתֵף.

107

The fat meat he drew with both hands,

וְהַבָּשָׂר הַשָׁמֵן יְמְשְׁכֵהוּ בִּשְתֵּי יָדַיִם.

108

and the lips gathered it up,

וְיִלְקְטוּהוּ הַשְּׂפָתַיִם.

109

and ground it in the mill [Num 11:8], while his arms held up the bowl

וְטָחֲנוּ בָרֵחָיִם וּזְרוֹעוֹתָיו הַקְּעָרָה יִתְמֹכוּ.

110

and drew to him all that was in it,

וְכָל אֲשֶׁר בָּהּ אֵלָיו יִמְשֹׁכוּ.

111

and out of his mouth went torches [Job 41:11].

וּמִפִּיו לַפִּידִים יַהֲלוֹכוּ.

112

And the pieces,

וְהַנְּתָחִים.

113

the choice ones,

הַמְשֻׁבָּחִים.

114

if he took them in his palm,

אִם יֹאחֲזֵם בְּכַפּוֹ.

115

he sent upon them the fierceness of his anger [Ps 78:49].

יְשַׁלֵחַ בָּם חֲרוֹן אַפּוֹ.

116

And every fat slice

וְכָל חֲתִיכָה שְׁמֵנָה.

117

that he saw from afar,

אֲשֶׁר מֵרָחוֹק יִרְאֶנָּה.

118

he drew with cords of love [Hos 11:4],

בַּעֲבוֹתוֹת אַהֲבָה יִמְשְׁכֶנָּה.

119

and while it was still in his hand, he swallowed it [Isa 28:4].

בְּעוֹדָהּ בְּכַפּוֹ יִבְלָעֶנָּה.

120

The lambs

וְהַטְּלָאִים.

121

feared the sword of his teeth,

מֵחֶרֶב שִׁנָּיו יְרֵאִים.

122

the sheep

וְהַכְּבָשִׂים.

123

fled from dread of him,

מִפַּחְדּוֹ נָסִים.

124

and the fish

וְהַדָּגִים.

125

were anxious for terror of him.

מֵאֵימָתוֹ דוֹאֲגִים.

126

And the meat, while it was yet between his teeth [Num 11:33], cried out before all living things:

וְהַבָּשָׂר עוֹדֶנוֹ בֵּין שִׁנָּיו וְהוּא צוֹעֵק לְנֶגֶד הַיְקוּם.

Uncertain renderings

  • ⚠1 וּבִזְהַב הַחֵשֶׁק מְחֻשָׁקִים — Exod 27:17: the pillars are "banded" (חֲשֻׁקִים) with silver. The word plays on חֵשֶׁק, "desire". The same phrase comes back for Ibn al-Muʿallim.
  • ⚠2 בְּכִכַּר מַעֲבֵה הַלֵב יְצוּקִים — 1 Kgs 7:46: Solomon cast the bronze vessels "in the plain of the Jordan, in the clay ground" (בְּמַעֲבֵה הָאֲדָמָה). Here "the heart" takes the place of the ground. The exact sense is uncertain.
  • ⚠3 וְיָּבֹא גַם הַנִּצָּב אַחַר הַלַּהַב — Judg 3:22: the hilt of Ehud's sword went in after the blade. Here the carver comes in after the knife. לַהַב is both "blade" and "flame", so also "after the fire" of the roast.
  • ⚠4 אֲשֶׁר חֹם הָאֵשׁ הֶאֱדִיבָם — In the Bible הֶאֱדִיב is "to grieve, make pine" (1 Sam 2:33). Possibly also a play on אָדֹם, "red": the fire browned them.
  • ⚠5 וְשִׁנָּיו. אֲזֵנָיו — Literally "his ears". Onkelos renders עַל אֲזֵנֶךָ (Deut 23:14) as "on your weapon", and this is read so here.
  • ⚠6 וְיָּמוּתוּ גַם שְׁנֵיהֶם מַחְלוֹן וְכִילְיוֹן — Ruth 1:5. The names of Naomi's sons, Mahlon and Chilion, are the two words of the line before: מַחֲלוֹן "sickness" and כִּלָּיוֹן "failing". His eating put an end to both.
  • ⚠7 בִּמְחֻבָּר וּמְפֹרָד — Literally "in the joined and the separated". Perhaps poems in rhyme against prose, or strophic poems against single verses. Uncertain.
  • ⚠8 כִּי אֵין כְּשִׁירֵי שְׁלֹמֹה חֲזָקִים — Solomon ibn Gabirol, praised at length further on.
  • ⚠9 ר' יוֹסֵף בֶּן שַׁטְנַאש — Joseph ibn Abitur, called Ibn Shatanash (Satanas).
  • ⚠10 ר' מֹשֶׁה הַכֹּהֵן — Probably Moses ha-Kohen ibn Gikatilla (11th century). He is not named again in the survey below. Uncertain.
  • ⚠11 בֶן בָּרוּךְ — Probably Isaac ben Baruch ibn Albalia (11th century). The survey below calls him "the master Baruch". Uncertain.
  • ⚠12 רַבִּי אֲדוֹנִים — Adonim was the Hebrew name of Dunash ben Labrat, but Dunash is named separately among the early poets. So perhaps another poet of this name. Uncertain.
  • ⚠13 רַבִּי מֹשֶה בֶּן שַׁשָּׁת — The survey below has "Rabbi Joseph ibn Sheshet". Both names are kept as the edition prints them.
  • ⚠14 בֶן רְאוּבֵן מִצְּבָא רוֹם עֲשׁוּקִים — Isaac ben Reuben of Barcelona, author of azharot on the commandments (see below). עֲשׁוּקִים is usually "oppressed, robbed": perhaps poems taken by force from the heavenly host. Uncertain.
  • ⚠15 רַבִּי אַבְרָהָם אַלְחֲרִיזִי — Not otherwise known. Perhaps a relative of the author.
  • ⚠16 וְשִׁירֵי רַבִּי שְׁלֹמֹה הַקָּטָן — Solomon ibn Gabirol, who signs his poems "Solomon ha-Katan" (the Small). The next lines play on קָטָן: יִקְטָן, Joktan, "the small one shall become a thousand".
  • ⚠17 וּפָשַׁט אֶת בְּגָדָיו וְלָבַשׁ בְּגָדִים אֲחֵרִים — Lev 6:4, the priest changing his garments. Isaac ibn Ezra is reported to have converted to Islam in Baghdad.
  • ⚠18 הִפְלִיא נַבְלוּת וְהִמְרִיץ — The son is Samuel ibn Abbas (al-Samawʾal al-Maghribi), who converted to Islam and wrote a polemic against Judaism. הִמְרִיץ is read from נִמְרָץ "grievous" (1 Kgs 2:8); the exact sense is uncertain.
  • ⚠19 יוֹשֵׁב בְּשֶׁבֶת תַּחְכְּמוֹנִי — 2 Sam 23:8, David's first hero, "Josheb-basshebeth a Taḥkemonite, that is Adino the Eznite". The title of the book comes from this verse.
  • ⚠20 סָף אָסָף וְאָזְלַת יַד יְדּותוּן — Asaph, Jeduthun and the sons of Korah are the psalmists of the Psalter. סָף אָסָף is a sound play: "Asaph comes to an end" (סוּף).
  • ⚠21 וְאַיֵּה נְוֵה הֲדוֹמוֹ — Literally "the dwelling of his footstool" (הֲדֹם, Ps 110:1): where he lives. Uncertain.
  • ⚠22 בְּאַלוֹן צַעֲנַנִּים — Judg 4:11: Ḥeber the Kenite pitched his tent "at the oak of Zaanannim". So the verse gives away his name before the narrator says it.
  • ⚠23 וְיִשַׁח רֹאשׁ תְּלִי — תְּלִי is the celestial Dragon of medieval Hebrew astronomy (the constellation, or the nodes of the moon). In Gen 27:3 the word means "quiver".
