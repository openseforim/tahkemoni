שַׁעַר שְׁלשִׁים

הָרוֹפֵא הָאוֹרֵחַ. וּמִינֵי תְּרוּפוֹתָיו בָּרְחוֹבוֹת צוֹרֵחַ

The Thirtieth Gate

The traveling doctor: an old quack in the square sells cures for every disease to the crowd, and when unmasked, blames Time for his trade

Gate 30 on Project Ben-Yehuda ↗

About this text

203 lines. One row is one rhyme unit of the rhymed prose, or one line of verse (its two halves set apart). References in [gray brackets] are the translator's identifications of biblical phrases. ⚠ marks an uncertain rendering. Each line number is a link to that line. Hebrew text: Project Ben-Yehuda.

1

Thus says Heman the Ezrahite:

נְאֻם הֵימָן הָאֶזְרָחִי.

2

I journeyed from the brook of Arnon

נָסַעְתִּי מִנַּחַל אַרְנוֹן.

3

to Baal-gad, which is in the valley of Lebanon [Josh 11:17].

לְבַעַל גָּד אֲשֶׁר בְּבִקְעַת הַלְּבָנוֹן.

4

And while I was still wandering in its streets,

וּבְעוֹדִי מְשׁוֹטֵט בְּחוּצוֹתֶיהָ.

5

to gather from the tongue of its wise men the pearls of their eloquence,

לִלְקָט מִלְּשׁוֹן חֲכָמֶיהָ פְנִינֵי מְלִיצוֹתֶיהָ.

6

I saw a great crowd coming,

רָאִיתִי עַם רַב בָּאִים.

7

and massing together

וְנִצְבָּאִים.

8

from every highway,

מִכָּל מְסִלָּה.

9

and they formed a circle.

וְנַעֲשׂוּ כַעֲגֻלָּה.

10

And I looked, and in the midst of the throng

וָאֵרָא בְתוֹךְ הֶהָמוֹן.

11

was an old man, bent like a bulrush [Isa 58:5],

זָקֵן כָּפוּף כְּאַגְמוֹן.

12

and a great assembly stood around him,

וּסְבִיבָיו קָהָל רַב נִצָּבִים.

13

and he stood at the gate of Bath-rabbim [Song 7:5].

וְהוּא נִצָּב עַל שַׁעַר בַּת רַבִּים.

14

Before him were trays full

וּלְפָנָיו כַּפּוֹת מְלֵאוֹת.

15

of every kind of remedy:

בְּמִינֵי רְפוּאוֹת.

16

in these, compounded ointments,

בָּאֵלֶּה מֶרְקָחוֹת.

17

and in those, oils for anointing.

וּבָאֵלֶּה מְשִׁיחוֹת.

18

Before him were plasters

וּלְפָנָיו רְטִיּוֹת.

19

for fresh wounds [Isa 1:6],

לְמַכּוֹת טְרִיּוֹת.

20

and balms and cures

וּתְעָלוֹת וַאֲרוּכוֹת.

21

set ready for every sickness,

לְכָל מַחֲלָה עֲרוּכוֹת.

22

row facing row.

מַעֲרָכוֹת מוּל מַעֲרָכוֹת.

23

Beside him were tools of iron, and tongs,

וְאֶצְלוֹ כְּלֵי בַרְזֶל וּמֶלְקָחַיִם.

24

and the fork of three teeth [1 Sam 2:13],

וְהַמַּזְלֵג שְׁלשׁ הַשִּׁנַּיִם.

25

and the instruments for bloodletting and for burning,

וּכְלֵי הַהַקָּזָה וְהַכְּוִיוֹת.

26

and the tools for cutting flesh, sharp as a two-edged sword [Prov 5:4].

וּכְלֵי חִתּוּךְ הַבָּשָׂר חַדִּים כְּחֶרֶב פִּיּוֹת.

27

And he was calling out his words

וְהוּא קוֹרֵא בְּמִלָּיו.

28

to all who stood about him,

לְכָל הַנִּצָּבִים עָלָיו.

29

and he spoke up and said:

וַיַּעַן וַיֹּאמַר:

30

"Hear me, O peoples,

שִׁמְעוּ אֵלַי עַמִּים.

31

and hearken, throng of nations [Isa 49:1]!

וְהַקְשִׁיבוּ הֲמוֹן לְאֻמִּים.

32

I am he who comes from the battle-lines of the times,

אָנֹכִי הַבָּא מִמַּעַרְכוֹת הַזְּמַנִּים.

33

who escaped from the hand of the roaring springs,

וְהַנִּמְלָט מִיַּד מְקוֹרוֹת הַשְּׁאוֹנִים.

34

and the proud waters [Ps 124:5].

וְהַמַּיִם הַזֵּידוֹנִים.

35

Many hardships have passed over me,

עָבְרוּ עָלַי כַּמָּה תְּלָאוֹת.

36

my eyes have seen wonders,

וְרָאוּ עֵינַי פְּלָאוֹת.

37

and my ears have heard terrors.

וְשָׁמְעוּ אָזְנַי נוֹרָאוֹת.

38

I journeyed from the land of Elam,

נָסַעְתִּי מֵאֶרֶץ עֵילָם.

39

and wandered to the ends of the world,

וְשׁוֹטַטְתִּי בְּאַפְסֵי עוֹלָם.

40

to search out all the wise men of the earth.

לַחְקֹר חַכְמֵי תֵבֵל כֻּלָּם.

41

I learned the art of medicine before mighty physicians,

וְלָמַדְתִּי חָכְמַת הָרְפוּאָה לִפְנֵי רוֹפְאִים עֲצוּמִים.

42

and poured water on the hands of the wisest of the wise [2 Kgs 3:11]⚠1.

וְיָצַקְתִּי מַיִם עַל יְדֵי חֲכָמִים מְחֻכָּמִים.

43

I drank in their words with thirst [m. Avot 1:4],

וְשָׁאַבְתִּי בְצִמְאָה אֶת דִּבְרֵיהֶם.

44

and received them from their mouths, and not from the mouths of their books.

וְקִבַּלְתִּי מִפִּיהֶם וְלֹא מִפִּי סִפְרֵיהֶם.

45

I came to know their secrets,

וְעָמַדְתִּי עַל סוֹדוֹתֵיהֶם.

46

and the foundations of their wisdom were laid bare to me.

וְנִגְלוּ לִי יְסוֹדֵי חָכְמוֹתֵיהֶם.

47

With God's help I heal wounds,

אֲנִי בְעֶזְרַת הָאֵל אֶרְפָּא מְחָצִים.

48

and wall up breaches,

וְאֶגְדֹּר פְּרָצִים.

49

and bind up sorrows,

וְאֶחְבּשׁ מַעֲצָבִים.

50

and quiet pains.

וְאַשְׁקִיט מַכְאוֹבִים.

51

With me is a ready remedy

וְאִתִּי רְפוּאָה מְצוּיָה.

52

for every ache and burn

לְכָל כְּאֵב וּכְוִיָּה.

53

that is in the body,

אֲשֶׁר בַּגְּוִיָּה.

54

and for the bite of the adder and the wild beast.

וְלִנְשִׁיכַת צִפְעוֹן וְחַיָּה.

55

In my hand is a plaster

וּבְיָדִי רְטִיָּה.

56

for every fresh wound [Isa 1:6].

לְכָל מַכָּה טְרִיָּה.

57

Whoever a snake has bitten, let him come to me

וּמִי שֶׁנְּשָׁכָהוּ נָחָשׁ יָבוֹא אֵלַי.

58

and hear my words,

וְיִשְׁמַע מִלָּי.

59

and it shall come to pass that everyone who is bitten, when he looks upon me, shall live [Num 21:8].

וְהָיָה כָּל הַנָּשׁוּךְ וְרָאָה אוֹתִי וָחָי.

60

I have a powder,

וְלִי אָבָק.

61

which I ground until it was fine as dust [Deut 9:21]⚠2,

שְׁחַקְתִּיהוּ עַד אֲשֶׁר דָּק.

62

to give light to shut eyes,

לְהָאִיר רוֹאוֹת סְגוּרוֹת.

63

and to open blind eyes [Isa 42:7].

וְלִפְקֹחַ עֵינַיִם עִוְרוֹת.

64

With me is a remedy to make the barren conceive,

וְאִתִּי רְפוּאָה לְהַזְרִיעַ הָעֲקָרוֹת.

65

and to bring them back with child,

וְלַהֲשִׁיבָן הָרוֹת.

66

and to warm the cold women⚠3.

וּלְחַמֵּם הַנָשִׁים הַקָּרוֹת.

67

With me is a cure

וְאִתִּי מָזוֹר.

68

for every sore⚠4,

לְכָל מָזוֹר.

69

and every wound that has not been bound up nor pressed [Isa 1:6].

וְכֶל נֶגַע אֲשֶׁר לֹא חֻבַּשׁ וְלֹא זוֹר.

70

I will be your help out of the city [2 Sam 18:3]⚠5.

אֲנִי אֶהְיֶה לָכֶם מֵעִיר לַעְזֹר.

71

With God's help I lead the lame,

אֲנִי בְּעֶזְרַת הָאֵל אֲנַהֵל פִּסְחִים.

72

and make the blind see again,

וְאָשִׁיב הַעִוְרִים פִּקְחִים.

73

until the lame man leaps like a hart [Isa 35:6],

עַד יְדַלֵּג כָּאַיָּל פִּסֵּחַ.

74

and butts westward and northward [Dan 8:4]⚠6.

וְיָמָּה וְצָפוֹנָה יְנַגֵּחַ.

75

And every sickness and every blow

גַּם כָּל חֳלִי וְכָל מַכָּה.

76

that crushes the heart,

אֲשֶׁר הַלֵּב מְדַכָּה.

77

and turns a man to dust [Ps 90:3],

וְתָשִׁיב אֱנוֹשׁ עַד דַּכָּא.

78

I take it away and make it pass,

אֲנִי אֲסִירֶנָּה וְאַעֲבִירֶנָּה.

79

and exchange it for ease.

וּבְשַׁלְוָה אֲמִירֶנָּה.

80

In my hand is eye-salve

וּבְיָדִי פּוּךְ.

81

for every tear that is shed,

לְכָל דֶּמַע שָׁפוּךְ.

82

and every eye that is turned⚠7,

וְכָל עַיִן הָפוּךְ.

83

to dry up its waters,

לְהוֹבִישׁ מֵימַיו.

84

and hold back its rains in its heavens.

וְלַעְצֹר גְּשָׁמָיו בְּשָׁמָיו.

85

I have healing,

וְלִי אֲרוּכָה.

86

and a remedy set ready,

וּרְפוּאָה עֲרוּכָה.

87

for every leg that is knotted,

לְכָל רֶגֶל נִקְשֶׁרֶת.

88

and every hand that is broken.

וּלְכָל יָד נִשְׁבֶּרֶת.

89

And everyone who cries out from pain in the pupil of his eye,

וְכָל צוֹעֵק מִכְּאֵב אִישׁוֹן.

90

in the black and pupil of the night [Prov 7:9]⚠8,

בַּאֲפֵל לַיְלָה וְאִישׁוֹן.

91

and it does not let him sleep:

וְאֵינֶנּוּ מַנִּיחַ לוֹ לִישׁוֹן.

92

in my hand are the cures of his sickness,

בְּיָדִי רְפוּאוֹת חָלְיוֹ.

93

until those who saw it say, 'Where is it?' [Job 20:7]

עַד רוֹאָיו יֹאמְרוּ אַיּוֹ.

94

And whoever is lame in his feet [2 Sam 9:3] and has no standing,

וְכָל מִי הוּא נְכֵה רַגְלַיִם וְאֵין לוֹ מַעֲמָד.

95

and a man who has in him a broken foot or a broken hand [Lev 21:19],

וְאִישׁ כִּי יִהְיֶה בוֹ שֶׁבֶר רֶגֶל אוֹ שֶׁבֶר יָד.

96

or is sick with a pain in his neck,

אוֹ חוֹלֶה מַכְאוֹב צַוְּארוֹנוֹ.

97

or has a white spot in his eye [Lev 21:20],

אוֹ תְבַלּוּל בְּעֵינוֹ.

98

or a scab or a scurf [Lev 21:20]⚠9: in my hand is his cure.

אוֹ גָרָב אוֹ יַלֶּפֶת בְּיָדִי רְפוּאָתוֹ.

99

I have a remedy for every lasting sickness,

וְלִי רְפוּאָה לְכָל מַחֲלָה נִצַּחַת.

100

for consumption and for fever [Deut 28:22],

וְלַשַּחֶפֶת וְלַקַּדַּחַת.

101

and for the blazing fire

וּלְאֵשׁ מִתְלַקַּחַת.

102

that burns between the bones,

אֲשֶׁר בֵּין הָעֲצָמוֹת קוֹדַחַת.

103

and for leprosy that breaks out [Lev 13:12],

וּלְצָרַעַת פּוֹרַחַת.

104

and for the swelling and the scab [Lev 14:56],

וְלַשְּׂאֵת וְלַסַּפַּחַת.

105

if it be on the bald head behind

כִּי יִהְיֶה בַקָּרַחַת.

106

or on the bald brow [Lev 13:42];

אוֹ בַגַּבַּחַת.

107

and for the aching of the teeth,

וְלִכְאֵב הַשִּׁנַּיִם.

108

and the cracking of the lips,

וּבֶקַע הַשְּׂפָתַיִם.

109

and the slackness of the hands,

וְרִפְיוֹן הַיָּדַיִם.

110

and the flux of the bowels,

וְשִׁלְשׁוּל הַמֵּעַיִם.

111

and the weakness of the loins,

וְחֹלֶשׁ הַמָּתְנַיִם.

112

and the knocking of the knees [Nah 2:11]:

וּפִיק הַבִּרְכַּיִם.

113

I heal them with the help of Him who dwells in heaven.

אֲנִי אֶרְפָּאֵם בְּעֶזְרַת שׁוֹכֵן שָׁמַיִם.

114

And whoever goes mad and falls upon his belly,

וְכָל הַמִּשְׁתַּגֵּע וְנוֹפֵל עַל גְּחוֹנוֹ.

115

and finds a choking in his throat,

וְיִמְצָא מַחֲנָק בְּגְרוֹנוֹ.

116

and puts out his tongue from his mouth,

וְיוֹצִיא מִפִּיו לְשׁוֹנוֹ.

117

and lets his spittle run down on his beard [1 Sam 21:14]:

וַיּוֹרֶד רִיר עַל זְקָנוֹ.

118

I take away his madness.

אֲנִי אָסִיר שִׁגְעוֹנוֹ.

119

I have a healing

וְלִי מַרְפֵּא.

120

for every man who is seized with fits⚠10,

לְכָל אִישׁ נִכְפֶּה.

121

and for every heart that is faint and failing,

וּלְכָל לֵב נִרְפֶּה וְנִסְפֶּה.

122

and for the ailments of the mouth,

וּלְתַחְלוּאֵי הַפֶּה.

123

and for the bite of the viper or the horned snake [Gen 49:17],

וְלִנְשׁוּךְ אֶפְעֶה אוֹ שְׁפִיפוֹן.

124

and for jaundice and for blight [Deut 28:22]⚠11,

וּלְיֵרָקוֹן וּלְשִׁדָּפוֹן.

125

and for sinews that are torn,

וּלְגִידִים הַנְּתוּקִים.

126

and for limbs that are wrenched apart,

וּלְאֵבָרִים הַמִּתְפָּרְקִים.

127

and for the man whose ankles are twisted,

וְלָאִישׁ אֲשֶׁר שׁוֹקָיו הִתְעַוְּתוּ.

128

and whose feet have ceased from walking,

וְרַגְלָיו מֵהֲלֹךְ שָׁבְתוּ.

129

and for every leprous man,

וּלְכָל אִישׁ צָרוּעַ.

130

and him who is smitten of God, and stricken [Isa 53:4].

וּמֻכֵּה אֱלֹהִים וְנָגוּעַ.

131

And a woman, when she has a flow, I cleanse from her uncleanness [Lev 15:25];

וְאִשָּׁה כִּי תִהְיֶה זָבָה אֲטַהֲרֶנָּה מִטֻּמְאָתָהּ.

132

and for her who is sick in her impurity [Lev 15:33],

וְלַדָּוָה בְנִדָּתָהּ.

133

in my hand is her cure;

בְּיָדִי רְפוּאָתָהּ.

134

and from the man with an issue, his issue [Lev 15:33],

וְלַזָּב אֶת זוֹבוֹ.

135

I take away, and the grief of his heart.

אָסִיר יְגוֹן לִבּוֹ.

136

All the sicknesses named,

וְכָל הֶחֳלָאִים הַנִּזְכָּרִים.

137

and those that are not named:

וַאֲשֶׁר אֵינָם נִזְכָּרִים.

138

their cure is in my hand.

רְפוּאָתָם בְּיָדִי.

139

And even now, behold, my witness is in heaven,

Uncertain renderings

  • ⚠1 וְיָצַקְתִּי מַיִם עַל יְדֵי חֲכָמִים מְחֻכָּמִים — 2 Kgs 3:11: Elisha "poured water on the hands of Elijah", that is, he served him as a disciple. "Wisest of the wise" renders חכמים מחכמים (Prov 30:24).
  • ⚠2 שְׁחַקְתִּיהוּ עַד אֲשֶׁר דָּק — Deut 9:21 (compare Exod 32:20) is said of the golden calf, which Moses ground to dust. The quack uses it of his eye powder.
  • ⚠3 וּלְחַמֵּם הַנָשִׁים הַקָּרוֹת — In medieval humoral medicine a "cold" temperament in a woman was a cause of barrenness; warming remedies were given for it.
  • ⚠4 וְאִתִּי מָזוֹר. לְכָל מָזוֹר — The same word twice: מָזוֹר is "cure" (Jer 30:13) and also "sore, wound" (Hos 5:13). The pun cannot be kept in English.
  • ⚠5 אֲנִי אֶהְיֶה לָכֶם מֵעִיר לַעְזֹר — 2 Sam 18:3, where David's men ask him to stay in the city and send them help from there. Here the sense is only "I will be your helper".
  • ⚠6 וְיָמָּה וְצָפוֹנָה יְנַגֵּחַ — Dan 8:4, the ram in Daniel's vision goring west and north. The healed cripple leaps like a hart (Isa 35:6) and then charges like Daniel's ram: a comic overstatement.
  • ⚠7 וְכָל עַיִן הָפוּךְ — Literally "every eye overturned". Possibly a turned-out eyelid or a squint; the exact ailment is uncertain. The rhyme פּוּךְ / שָׁפוּךְ / הָפוּךְ carries the line.
  • ⚠8 בַּאֲפֵל לַיְלָה וְאִישׁוֹן — Prov 7:9 "in the blackness (אִישׁוֹן) of night". אִישׁוֹן is also "pupil of the eye", the word in the line before; the pun is the point of the couplet.
  • ⚠9 אוֹ תְבַלּוּל בְּעֵינוֹ. אוֹ גָרָב אוֹ יַלֶּפֶת — Lev 21:20, the bodily blemishes that bar a priest from service. The exact ailments are uncertain: תְּבַלּוּל is a spot or film in the eye; גָּרָב and יַלֶּפֶת are scaly or itching skin diseases.
  • ⚠10 לְכָל אִישׁ נִכְפֶּה — נִכְפֶּה is the rabbinic term for an epileptic (literally "one who is overpowered"). The lines before (falling, choking, slavering) also describe a fit.
  • ⚠11 וּלְיֵרָקוֹן וּלְשִׁדָּפוֹן — In the Bible (Deut 28:22, 1 Kgs 8:37) these are diseases of grain, mildew and blight. In rabbinic Hebrew יֵרָקוֹן is also jaundice in a person; שִׁדָּפוֹן has no human sense, which may be part of the joke.
  • ⚠12 וּכְלֵי הַשֶּׁתֶן בָּאִים עַל כַּמָּה מִינִים. וְכֵלִים מִכֵּלִים שׁוֹנִים — Uroscopy: patients brought flasks of urine for the physician to judge by colour. "Vessels of diverse vessels" is from Esth 1:7, the wine vessels at Ahasuerus' feast.
