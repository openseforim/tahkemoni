בְּמַהֲלַל אִגֶּרֶת יְשָׁרָה, וּכְעִירוּתָהּ בִּקְרִיאַת אוֹתָהּ נְזוֹרָה

The Eighth Gate

A letter that praises a prince when read forward and curses him when read backward, and two lines of verse that turn the same way

Gate 8 on Project Ben-Yehuda ↗

About this text

285 lines. One row is one rhyme unit of the rhymed prose, or one line of verse (its two halves set apart). References in [gray brackets] are the translator's identifications of biblical phrases. ⚠ marks an uncertain rendering. Each line number is a link to that line. Hebrew text: Project Ben-Yehuda.

1

Thus says Heman the Ezrahite: I was in the land of Egypt,

נְאֻם הֵימָן הָאֶזְרָחִי" הָיִיתִי בְּאֶרֶץ מִצְרַיִם.

2

and at that time it was a delight to the eyes [Gen 3:6],

וְהָיְתָה בָעֵת הַהִיא תַּאֲוָה לָעֵינַיִם.

3

and comely as Jerusalem [Song 6:4].

וְנָאוָה כִּירוּשָׁלַ ם.

4

Its pastures were full of dainties,

נְאוֹתֶיהָ מְלֵאוֹת מַעֲדַנִּים.

5

its choice fruits new and old [Song 7:14],

מְגָדֶיהֶ חֲדָשִׁים גַּם יְשָׁנִים.

6

and its gardens fat and green [Ps 92:15],

וְגַנֶּיהָ דְשֵׁנִים וְרַעֲנַנִּים.

7

planted with pleasant plants [Isa 17:10].

נְטוּעִים נִטְעֵי נַעֲמָנִים.

8

It was like a bride with the Nile a necklace on her neck,

וְהִיא כְּכַלָּה בְּצַוָּארָהּ עֲנַק הַיְאוֹר.

9

as if the world were dark and she the lamp for the light [Exod 35:14].

כְּאִלּוּ הָעוֹלָם חָשַׁךְ וְהִיא מְנוֹרַת הַמָּאוֹר.

10

I found there desirable young men [Ezek 23:6], wise and pleasant,

וּּמָצָאתִי בָּהּ בַּחוּרֵי חֶמֶד מַשְׂכִּילִים וּנְעִימִים.

11

and they were exceedingly wise [Prov 30:24], expounders of what is sealed,

וְהֵמָּה חֲכָמִים מְחֻכָּמִים , מְבָאֲרֵי סְתוּמִים.

12

and revealers of hidden things.

וּמְגַלֵּי תַעֲלוּמִים.

13

They rend the sea of knowledge into pieces,

יִקְרְעוּ יָם הַמַּדָּע קְרָעִים.

14

and crumble rocks to get at wisdom.

וִיפוֹרְרוּ לְחָכְמוֹת סְלָעִים.

15

Their figures of speech are like lampstands, and they the cups [Exod 25:31];

וְהַמְּלִיצוֹת כִּמְנוֹרוֹת וְהֵמָּה גְבִיעִים.

16

or like the lights, and they the firmament.

אוֹ כַמְּאוֹרוֹת וְהֵמָּה רְקִיעִים.

17

From the necklace of their mind they scatter splendour to the sky,

יָפִיצוּ מֵעֲנַק שִׂכְלָם יִפְעַת לְשָׁמִים.

18

and from the cloud of their teaching they drip abundance of rain.

וְיַטִּיפוּ מֵעֲנַן לִקְחָם שִׁפְעַת גְּשָׁמִים.

19

Their speech is to the thirsty as streams of water in a dry place [Isa 32:2],

וְנִיבָם לִצְמֵאִים כְּפַלְגֵי מַיִם בְּצָיוֹן.

20

and their heart, for the prophecy of song, a valley of vision [Isa 22:1].

וְלִבָּם לִנְבוּאַת הַשִּׁיר גֵּיא חִזָּיוֹן.

21

All of them were pure of heart and thought,

וְכֻלָּם בָּרֵי לֵבָב וְרַעְיוֹן.

22

and knew the knowledge of the Most High [Num 24:16].

וְיוֹדְעֵי דַעַת עֶלְיוֹן.

23

Among them was an old man who gathered from the testimonies their scattered parts,

וּבְתוֹכָם אִישׁ זָקֵן יְקַבֵּץ מִן הַתְּעוּדוֹת נְפוּצוֹתֵיהֶם.

24

and set in the figures of speech their ornaments.

וִישַׁבֵּץ מִן הַמְּלִיצוֹת חֲלִיצוֹתֵיהֶם.

25

At times he embroidered his writings with embroidery of fine cloth [Exod 31:10],

פַּעַם יִרְקֹם בִּכְתָבָיו רִקְמַת שְׂרָד.

26

and spread over them the gold of his mind, in hanging work [1 Kgs 7:29].

וְיִשְׁטַח עֲלֵיהֶם זְהַב שִׂכְלוֹ מַעֲשֵׂה מוֹרָד.

27

At times he came into the sea of understanding to split its breakers with his words,

פַּעַם יָבוֹא בְיָם הַבִּינָה לִבְקֹעַ מִשְׁבָּרָיו בִּדְבָרָיו.

28

and to bring out of its chambers its pearls,

וּלְהוֹצִיא מֵחֲדָרָיו דָּרָיו.

29

and to raise up from its depths

וּלְהַעֲלוֹת מִמְּצוּלוֹתָיו.

30

the pearls of its eloquence.

פְנִינֵי מְלִיצוֹתָיו.

31

Those who stood before him asked him,

וַיִּשְׁאָלוּהוּ הָעוֹמְדִים לְפָנָיו.

32

those who drew from his springs,

וְהַשּׁוֹאֲבִים מִמַּעְיָנָיו.

33

to teach them the craft of strong eloquence,

לְלַמְּדָם מְלֶאכֶת הַמְּלִיצָה הַחֲזָקָה.

34

and its way, deep and far off,

וְדַרְכָּה הָעֲמֻקָּה וְהָרְחוֹקָה.

35

which the heroes of thought are too weary,

אֲשֶׁר יִלְאוּ גִּבּוֹרֵי הַמַּחֲשָׁבוֹת.

36

and the young lions of the heart,

וּכְפִירֵי הַלְּבָבוֹת.

37

and the vanguard of the battles,

וַחֲלוּצֵי הַקְּרָבוֹת.

38

to tread in its paths,

לִדְרֹךְ שְׁבִילֶיהָ.

39

and to walk in its tracks;

וְלַהֲלֹךְ בְּמַעְגָּליהָ.

40

for it is a path no bird of prey knows [Job 28:7],

כִּי הִיא נָתִיב לֹא יְדָעוֹ עַיִט.

41

and its eloquence a great sea where no galley goes [Isa 33:21].

וּמְלִיצָתָהּ יָם גָּדוֹל בַּל תֵּלֶךְ בּוֹ אֳנִי שַׁיִט.

42

He said to them:

וַיֹאמֶר לָהֶם:

43

"I will make all my goodness pass before you [Exod 33:19],

אֲנִי אַעֲבִיר כָּל טוּבִי עַל פְנֵיכֶם.

44

and set the riddles of the testimonies before you.

וְאָשִׂים חִידוֹת הַתְּעוּדוֹת לִפְנֵיכֶם.

45

Now incline your ears, and know that the happy poems,

וְעַתָּה הַטּוּ אָזְנְכֶם וּדְעוּ כִּי יֵשׁ לַשִּׁירוֹת הַמְאֻשָּׁרוֹת.

46

and the delightful riddles,

וְלַחִידוֹת הַמֲחמוּדוֹת.

47

and the precious letters,

וְלָאִגְּרוֹת הַיְקָרוֹת.

48

and the forceful phrases,

וְלַמְּלִיצוֹת הַנִּמְרָצוֹת.

49

have many ways,

כַּמָּה דְרָכִים.

50

and many courses,

וְכַמָּה מַהְלְכִים.

51

in which the guides of the perplexed go astray⚠1.

אֳשֶׁר יִתְעוּ בָהּ מוֹרֵי הַנְּבוּכִים.

52

And the highest craft,

וְהַמְּלָאכָה הָעֶלְיוֹנָה.

53

of those who sit first in the kingdom [Esth 1:14],

הַיּוֹשְׁבִים בַּמַלְכוּת רִאשׁוֹנָה.

54

is the craft of the letters that turn,

הִיא מְלֶאכֶת הָאִגְּרוֹת הַהֲפוּכוֹת.

55

whose bows are drawn backward,

אֲשֶׁר קַשְּׁתוֹתָם לְאָחֹר דְּרוּכוֹת.

56

and set out in two ways.

וּמִשְּׁנֵי דְרָכִים עֲרוּכוֹת.

57

If you read them in their way,

אִם בְּדַרְכָּם תִּקְרָאֵם.

58

you will find them straight [Prov 8:9];

נְכֹוחִים תִּמְצָאֵם.

59

and if you turn them backward,

וְאִם תַּהַפְכֵם לְאָחוֹר.

60

you would think the streams of Shihor had compassed you [Ps 18:5],

תַּחְשֹׁב כִי אֲפָפוּךָ נַחֲלֵי שִיחוֹר.

61

or the Jordan was turning back [Ps 114:3]."

אוֹ הַיַּרְדֵּן יִסֹּב לְאָחוֹר.

62

All the people said to him:

אָמְרוּ לוֹ כָּל הָעָם:

63

"This is a way no man has passed through [Jer 2:6],

זֹאת דֶּרֶךְ לֹא עָבַר בָּהּ אִישׁ.

64

and this craft is a virgin whom no man has known [Gen 24:16].

וְזֹאת הַמְּלָאכָה בְּתוּלָה לֹא יְדָעָהּ אִישׁ.

65

If you will betroth her to us,

וְאִם תְּאָרֵשׂ אוֹתָהּ אֵלֵינוּ.

66

ask of us ever so much dowry and gift [Gen 34:12],

הַרְבֵּה מֹהַר וּמַתָּן עָלֵינוּ.

67

and you shall have a share in our wealth and our substance."

וִיהִי לְךָ חֵלֶק בְּהוֹנֵנוּ וְחֵילֵנוּ.

68

He said to them:

אָמַר לָהֶם:

69

"Come, children, listen to me; I will teach you the fear of the LORD [Ps 34:12].

לְכוּ בָנִים שִׁמְעוּ לִי יִרְאַת ה' אֲלַמֶּדְכֶם.

70

And I will not accept an offering from your hand [Mal 1:10].

וּמִנְחָה לֹא אֶרְצֶה מִיֶּדְכֶם.

71

Behold, I will rain down for you bread of delights [Exod 16:4]

וְהִנְנִי מַמְטִיר לָכֶם לֶחֶם חֲמוּדוֹת.

72

from the heavens of the testimonies,

מִשְּׁמֵי הַתְּעוּדוֹת.

73

and let you hear a lovely letter, fair and beautiful [Jer 46:20],

וְאַשְׁמִיעֲכֶם אִגֶּרֶת חֲמוּדָה יְפֵה-פִיָּה.

74

like apples of gold in settings of silver [Prov 25:11],

כְּתַפּוּחֵי זָהָב בְּמַשְׂכִּיָּה.

75

hewn from mountains of sound wisdom,

חֲצוּבָה מֵהַרְרֵי תוּשִׁיָּה.

76

built for a showpiece [Song 4:4].

בְּנוּיָה לְתַלְפִיָה.

77

She has the grace of a gazelle,

לָהּ חֵן צְבִיָּה.

78

and the terror of a lioness.

וְאֵימַת לְבִיָּא.

79

She turns like a deceitful bow [Ps 78:57], and has no second in the world.

תִּתְהַפֵּךְ כְּקֶשֶׁת רְמִיָּה וְאֵין לָהּ בְּתֵבֵל שְׁנִיָּה.

80

She is the only one of her mother [Song 6:9],

אַחַת הִיא לְאִמָּהּ.

81

and a wonder among the children of her people.

וּפְלִיאָה בֵּין בְּנֵי עַמָּהּ.

82

The daughters saw her and called her happy [Song 6:9];

רָאוּהָ בָנוֹת וַיְּאַשְּרוּהָ.

83

noble women heard her fame and praised her.

שָׁמְעוּ שִׁמְעָהּ עֲדִינוֹת וַיְהַלְלוּהָ.

84

If a man reads her from the beginning to the end,

אִם יִקְרָאֶנָּה אִישׁ מֵרֹאשׁ וְעַד סוֹף.

85

his heart will long for her,

לְבָבוֹ אֵלֶיהָ יִכְסֹף.

86

and he will find her a lovely praise,

וְיִמְצָאֶנָּה תְּהִלָּה חֲמוּדָה.

87

like a modest and pious woman.

כְּאִשָּׁה צְנוּעָה וַחֲסִידָה.

88

But if he reads her from her end to her head,

וְאִם מִסּוֹפָהּ וְעַד רֹאשָׁהּ יִקְרָאֶנָּה.

89

and turns her backward,

וּלְאָחוֹר יַהַפְכֶנָּה.

90

the pious one, the stork, becomes a heron⚠2,

תָּשׁוּב הַחֲסִידָה אֲנָפָה.

91

and the fair speech is turned

וְתִתְהַפֵּךְ הַשָּׂפָה הַיָּפָה.

92

to desolation and to splinters [Joel 1:7],

לְשַׁמָּה וְלִקְצָפָה.

93

and the praise becomes folly⚠3,

וְתַחְזֹר הַתְהִלָּה תָּהֳלָה.

94

and the blessing a curse.

וְהַבְּרָכָה קְלָלָה.

95

By her light she makes the dark places shine,

וּבְאוֹרָהּ תָּאִיר הָאֲפֵלֹות.

96

and turns the sun back ten degrees backward [Isa 38:8],

וְתָשִׁיב הַשֶּׁמֶשׁ אֲחֹרַנִּית עֶשֶׂר מַעֲלוֹת.

97

to make all who see her tremble

לְהַחֲרִיד כָּל רוֹאֶיהָ.

98

at her wonders.

בִּפְלָאֶיהָ.

99

She is black and comely [Song 1:5];

שְׁחוֹרָה הִיא וְנָאוָה.

100

with her mouth she laughs, and her soul is sick.

בְּפִיהָ שׂוֹחֶקֶת וְנַפְשָׁהּ דָּוָה.

101

In her face is humility,

בְּפָנֶיהּ עֲנָוָה.

102

and in her heart perversity.

וּבְלִבָּהּ מַעֲוָה.

103

On her tongue is honeycomb,

בִּלְשׁוֹנָהּ נֹפֶת.

104

and in her inward parts Topheth.

וּבְקִרְבָּהּ תֹּפֶת.

105

Though she is sweet at her beginning,

אִם תִּמְתַּק בְּרֵאשִׁיתָהּ.

106

her end is more bitter than death [Eccl 7:26].

מַר מִמָּוֶת אַחֲרִיתָהּ.

107

She has two faces,

לָהּ שְׁנֵי פָנִים.

108

like the bread of the faces⚠4.

כְּלֶחֶם הַפָּנִים.

109

Whoever reads her in her way,

וְכָל קוֹרְאָהּ כְּדַרְכָּהּ.

110

and tastes the sweetness of her palate [Song 5:16],

וְיִטְעַם מֶתֶק חִכָּהּ.

111

will say: 'This is the bread of the LORD,

יֹאמַר: זֶה לֶחֶם ה'.

112

and this is the table that is before the LORD [Ezek 41:22].'

וְזֶה הַשֻּׁלְחָן אֲשֶׁר לִפְנֵי ה'.

113

But if he turns her round, he will find the bread of the faces taken away from before the LORD [1 Sam 21:7].

וְאִם יַהַפְכֶנָּה יִמְצָא לֶחֶם הַפָּנִים הַמּוּסָרִים מִלִּפְנֵי ה'.

114

For good and evil are joined in her, and this man and that man was born in her [Ps 87:5],

כִּי הַטּוֹב וְהָרַע נִצְמְדוּ בָהּ , וְאִישׁ וָאִישׁ יוּלַּד בָּהּ.

115

and the children struggled together within her [Gen 25:22].

וַיִתְרוֹצְצוּ הַבָּנִים בְּקִרְבָּהּ.

116

The one came out a plain man, dwelling in tents [Gen 25:27],

וַיֵּצֵא הָאֶחָד אִישׁ תָּם יוֹשֵׁב אֹהָלִים.

117

and after that his brother came out [Gen 25:26], worthless and evil in deeds.

וְאַחֲרֵי כֵן יָצָא אָחִיו בְּלִיַּעַל וְרַע מַעֲלָלִים.

118

This letter

וְזֹאת הָאִגֶּרֶת.

119

I have set as a chain on the settings of eloquence [Exod 28:14].

שַׂמְתִּיהָ עַל מִשְׁבְּצוֹת הַמְּלִיצוֹת שַׁרְשֶרֶת.

120

In the furnace of my mind I refined her [Isa 48:10],

בְכוּר שִׂכְלִי צְרַפְתִּיהָ.

266

וְאָמַרְנוּ לוֹ: הוֹדִיעֵנוּ זֶה הַכֶּתֶם הַטּוֹב מֵאַיִן מַחְצָבוֹ.

267

And this bright light [Job 37:21], from which heights did its star come forth [Num 24:17]? And wisdom, from where does it come [Job 28:20]?"

וְזֶה הָאוֹר הַבָּהִיר מֵאֵיזֶה הַמְּרוֹמוֹת דָּרַךְ כּוֹכָבוֹ , וְהַחָכְמָה מֵאַיִן תָּבוֹא.

268

וַיִּשְׂחַק לְמִילֵּנוּ.

269

וַיֹּאמֶר אֵלֵינוּ:

270

I am Ḥever, joiner of praises⚠13, and my sayings are the glory of the heads of congregations.

אֲנִי חֶבֶר מְחַבֵּר הַתְּהִלּוֹת וְאִמְרוֹתַי פְּאֵר רָאשֵׁי קְהִלּוֹת.

271

For princes I compose blessings from my mouth⚠14, and on the head of my foes they turn into curses.

אֲחַבֵּר מִנְגִידֵי פִי בְרָכוֹת וְיֵהָפְכוּ לְרֹאשׁ צָרַי קְלָלוָת.

272

My sayings give light to the eyes of the blind, and turn to darkness for the eyes of my enemies.

וְאִמְרוֹתַי לְעֵין עִוְרִים מְאִירוֹת וְיָשׁוּבוּ לְעֵין אֹיְבַי אֲפֵלוֹת.

273

With me all the daughters of song are wedded, though to others they are virgins.

וְאִתִּי כָל בְּנוֹת הַשִּׁיר בְּעוּלוֹת וְאִם הֵנָּה לְזוּלָתִי בְּתוּלוֹת.

274

וְהָרוֹדֵף לְהַשִּׂיג מַעֲלָתִי עֲנֵהוּ: אַל תְּבַקֶּשׁ לָךְ גְּדוֹלוֹת.

הֲכִי תִיגַע וְלֹא תִגַּע וְלָכֵן שְׁמֹר נַפֵשָׁךְ וְלֹא תַעְלֶה בְּמַעְלוֹת.

276

Will you run to disgrace your honour, and to lay bare your shame and your nakedness?"

הֲכִי תָרוּץ לְנַבֵּל אֶת כְּבוֹדָךְ וְחֶרְפָּתָךְ וְעֶרְוָתָךְ לְגַלּוֹת.

When I heard his poem, I knew him and recognized him,

וּכְשָׁמְעִי שִׁירוֹ יְדַעְתִּיהוּ וְהִכַּרְתִּיהוּ.

Uncertain renderings

  • ⚠1 אֳשֶׁר יִתְעוּ בָהּ מוֹרֵי הַנְּבוּכִים — "Guides of the perplexed" is the title of Maimonides' Moreh Nevukhim, which al-Ḥarizi himself translated into Hebrew. Here the phrase means simply the teachers who lose their way in these crafts.
  • ⚠2 תָּשׁוּב הַחֲסִידָה אֲנָפָה — Lev 11:19 lists הַחֲסִידָה הָאֲנָפָה, the stork and the heron, side by side. חֲסִידָה also means "pious (woman)", as in the line before; אֲנָפָה sounds like אנף, "be angry". The pious woman becomes an angry one.
  • ⚠3 וְתַחְזֹר הַתְהִלָּה תָּהֳלָה — A pun that cannot be carried over: תְּהִלָּה, praise, becomes תָּהֳלָה, folly or error (Job 4:18), a word with almost the same letters.
  • ⚠4 לָהּ שְׁנֵי פָנִים. כְּלֶחֶם הַפָּנִים — לֶחֶם הַפָּנִים, the showbread, is literally "bread of the faces". The letter has two faces, a forward one and a backward one. The next lines play on the showbread "before the LORD" and the showbread "taken away from before the LORD" (1 Sam 21:7).
  • ⚠5 גְּבִירֵנוּ צִירֵנוּ — The letter (lines from here to "May our lord never perish!") is built so that it can be read word by word from the last word to the first. Read so, each verb takes the noun that stood before it, and every praise becomes a curse. It starts: "Our lord, forever may he perish; may his Creator not grant him grace; ... may He cut off those who know him, and help his enemies, and subdue those who love him, and exalt his adversary ... and bring low his friends ... and remove his glory ... may his light and his lamp go out ... disgrace without kindness, blemish without beauty, rebellion without piety ... he loves backslidings, loathes what is fitting, pursues riches, hates uprightness, wraps himself in folly ... head of our reproach, who ends our greatness, renews our grief, puts our joy to flight, stirs up our sighing, takes away our honour, makes great our pain (צִיר also means pang), our lord." The forward text is translated here; the reversed sense is only in this note.
  • ⚠6 חֶסֶד. בְּלִי חֶסֶד — The same word twice: חֶסֶד is kindness, and also disgrace (Lev 20:17, Prov 14:34). Forward, "kindness without disgrace"; reversed, "disgrace without kindness".
  • ⚠7 הַשַּׂר (פְּלוֹנִי) יוֹצְרֵהוּ יִצְּרֵהוּ — "So-and-so" (פלוני) stands for the prince's name, which the author leaves out. יִצְּרֵהוּ, "may He guard him", has the letters of יוֹצְרֵהוּ, "his Maker". In the reversed reading אַל, "not", from the next line comes before it: "may his Maker not guard him".
  • ⚠8 גְּבִירֵנוּ לָנֶצַח יֹאבַד אַל יַמְצִיאֵהוּ. חֶסֶד בוֹרְאֵהוּ — These are the last words of the letter, "May his Creator grant him grace! May our lord never perish!", read in reverse order. The word אַל, "not", which forward goes with "perish", now goes with "grant".
  • ⚠9 הַשָּׂר, בְּךָ ישֶׁר וְאֵין אִתָּךְ — The two lines of praise below are the same words, in reverse order, as the two lines of blame after them. Line 1 reversed is line 1 of the second poem, and line 2 reversed is its line 2.
  • ⚠10 נִמְצָא בְךָ חֶסֶד — Here חֶסֶד is read as disgrace (Lev 20:17), the same word that is "kindness" in the forward poem. שִׁמְצָה, "taint", is a rare word (Exod 32:25).
  • ⚠11 מִבְּנֵי עֵבֶר וּמִבְּנֵי עֲרָב — A play on two words with nearly the same letters: עֵבֶר, Eber, the ancestor of the Hebrews, and עֲרָב, Arabia, the Arabs. The east and west of the line before are the Jews of the Islamic East and of Spain and the West.
  • ⚠12 וּלְשׁוֹן הַקֹּדֶשׁ עֵרַב הַשְּׁנַיִם — עֵרֶב is the woof of a fabric (שְׁתִי וָעֵרֶב, warp and woof, Lev 13:48). It is also a pun on עֲרָב, Arabic: the reader who knows both tongues has the whole fabric of both.
  • ⚠13 אֲנִי חֶבֶר מְחַבֵּר הַתְּהִלּוֹת — A pun on the name: חֶבֶר (Ḥever) and מְחַבֵּר, composer, literally "joiner".
  • ⚠14 אֲחַבֵּר מִנְגִידֵי פִי בְרָכוֹת — מִנְגִידֵי is read as "for princes" (מִן with נְגִידִים), in contrast with "my foes" in the second half. "From the noble words of my mouth" is also possible. The line is the gate in small: the same words bless and curse.
  • ⚠15 הֲכִי תִיגַע וְלֹא תִגַּע — A pun that cannot be carried over: תִיגַע, you will toil, and תִגַּע, you will touch (reach). The next words are from Exod 20:23, the altar that may not be climbed by steps; here, do not climb to my rank.
  • ⚠16 עַד צָעַק בֵּינֵינוּ עוֹרֵב הַפְּרִידָה — The raven of parting is the ghurāb al-bayn of Arabic poetry, the bird whose cry foretells the separation of friends.
