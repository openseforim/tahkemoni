שַׁעַר עֶשְׂרִים וּשְׁנַיִם

עִנְיַן הָאִישׁ הַחוֹזֶה בַכּוֹכַבִים וּמַזָּלוֹת וּבְעָרְמָתוֹ וְשִׂכְלוֹ יְדַבֵּר גְּדוֹלוֹת

The Twenty-Second Gate

An Arab astrologer reads the stars and the sand at the city gate; when Ḥever and his friends ask when the exile will end, he denounces them, and only a just judge saves them

Gate 22 on Project Ben-Yehuda ↗

About this text

221 lines. One row is one rhyme unit of the rhymed prose, or one line of verse (its two halves set apart). References in [gray brackets] are the translator's identifications of biblical phrases. ⚠ marks an uncertain rendering. Each line number is a link to that line. Hebrew text: Project Ben-Yehuda.

1

Thus says Heman the Ezrahite: I was in the days of my prime [Job 29:4],

נְאֻם הֵימָן הָאֶזְרָחִי: הָיִיתִי בִימֵי חָרְפִּי.

2

when the stars of my twilight [Job 3:9] were shining,

וּבִזְרֹחַ כּוֹכְבֵי נִשְׁפִּי.

3

and the sphere turned at my command.

וְהַגַּלְגַל סוֹבֵב עַל פִּי.

4

I took my delight [Prov 7:18] in the gardens,

מִתְעַלֵּס בַּגַּנִּים.

5

and grazed among the lilies [Song 2:16],

וְרוֹעֶה בַשּׁוֹשַׁנִים.

6

by the places where water is drawn from the streams [Judg 5:11],

עַל מַשְׁאַבֵּי נְחָלִים.

7

and under the thickets of the shade.

וְתַחַת סִבְכֵי הַצְּלָלִים.

8

I would breathe in breezes,

אֶשְׁאַף רוּחוֹת.

9

compounded with the oil of myrrh,

בֶּשֶׁמֶן הַמּוֹר רְקוּחוֹת.

10

in which there was rest for the soul,

בָּהֶם לַנֶּפֶשׁ מְנוּחוֹת.

11

and joys for the heart.

וְלַלֵּב שְמָחוֹת.

12

And at the end of some days,

וַיְהִי בִּקְצָת הַיָּמִים.

13

I went out to roam in the desert and the steppe,

יָצָאתִי לְשׁוֹטֵט בַּמִּדְבָּר וּבָעֲרָבָה.

14

and I walked about at large [Ps 119:45].

וָאֶתְהַלְּכָה בָרְחָבָה.

15

And I saw at the gate of the city a man standing,

וָאֵרָא לְשַׁעַר הָעִיר אִישׁ נִצָּב.

16

anxious and sad.

דּוֹאֵג וְנֶעֱצָב.

17

And when I set my eyes upon him,

וְכַאֲשֶׁר שַׂמְתִּי עָלָיו עֵינִי.

18

I found him to be Ḥever the Kenite.

מְצָאתִיהוּ חֶבֶר הַקֵּינִי.

19

My grief over his sorrow grew great,

וִיְגַּדל עַל עָצְבּוֹ יְגוֹנִי.

20

and I hurried to him to ask him

וּמִהַרְתִּי אֵלָיו לְשָׁאֳלוֹ.

21

about the thing that had happened to him.

עַל הַמִּקְרֶה אֲשֶׁר קָרָה לוֹ.

22

He said to me: "Yesterday I was with some companions,

אָמַר לִי: הָיִיתִי אֶתְמוֹל עִם חֲבֵרִים.

23

of the children of the Hebrews [Exod 2:6].

מִיַּלְדֵי הָעִבְרִים.

24

While we were still in the city gate, we saw a great crowd gathered,

וּבְעוֹדֵנוּ בְשַׁעַר הָעִיר רָאִינוּ עַם רַב נִקְבָּצִים.

25

coming and running from every side,

וּמִכָּל עֵבֶר בָּאִים וְרָצִים.

26

pressing and jostling one against another [Isa 3:5].

וְאִישׁ בְּאָחִיו נִגָּשִׁים וְנִלְחָצִים.

27

I and my companions hurried to them,

וּמִהַרְתִּי אֲנִי וַחֲבֵרַי אֲלֵיהֶם.

28

and we asked one of them

וְשָׁאַלְנוּ לְאִישׁ מֵהֶם.

29

to tell us where they were set on going [Hab 1:9].

לְהוֹדִיעֵנוּ מְגַמַּת פְּנֵיהֶם.

30

He said to us that at the city gate there was a sage, one of the sages of the Arabs,

וַיֹּאמֶר לָנוּ כִּי יֵשׁ בְּשַׁעַר הָעִיר חָכָם מֵחַכְמֵי הָעֲרָבִים.

31

who gazes at the stars;

חוֹזֶה בַכּוֹכָבִים.

32

he reveals what is to come before it comes to be,

יְגַלֶּה הָעֲתִידוֹת בְּטֶרֶם הֱיוֹתָם.

33

and tells the secrets before they have settled⚠1.

וְיַגִּיד הַסּוֹדוֹת עַד לֹא חֲנוֹתָם.

34

My companions said to me: 'Come, let us hear his prophecies,

וַיֹּאמְרוּ לִי חֲבֵרַי: לְכָה נָא וְנִשְמְעָה נְבוּאוֹתָיו.

35

and we shall see what will become of his dreams [Gen 37:20].'

וְנִרְאֶה מַה יִהְיוּ חֲלוֹמוֹתָיו.

36

And when we came to the crowd, we saw an old man well on in years [Gen 24:1],

וְכַאֲשֶׁר בָּאנוּ אֶל הֶהָמוֹן רָאִינוּ זָקֵן בָּא בַיָּמִים.

37

and from the splendor of wisdom there was awe upon him [Job 20:25].

וּמֵהֲדַר הַחָכְמָה עָלָיו אֵימִים.

38

In his hands was an instrument of bronze,

וּבְיָדָיו כְּלִי נְחשֶׁת.

39

and on it a work of network [Exod 27:4]⚠2.

וְעָלָיו מַעֲשֵׂי רֶשֶׁת.

40

And he was measuring the sun in its spheres,

וְהוּא שׁוֹקֵל הַשֶּמֶשׁ בְּגַלְגַּלֶּיהָ.

41

and its course in its circuits,

וּמְרוּצָתָהּ בְּמַעְגָּלֶיהָ.

42

and the breadth of its regions,

וּמֶרְחֲבֵי גְלִילֶיהָ.

43

and the slant of its shadows.

וּנְטִיַּת צְלָלֶיהָ.

44

And he was saying to all who stood around him: 'Ask me about the things to come [Isa 45:11],

וְהוּא אוֹמֵר לְכָל הַנִּצָבִים עָלָיו: הָאוֹתִיּוֹת שֶׁאָלוּנִי.

45

and question me about the future.

וְעַל הָעֲתִידוֹת חִקְרוּנִי.

46

Do not test me in the secrets of the earth;

וּבְסוֹדוֹת הָאָרֶץ אַל תִּבְחָנוּנִי.

47

only in the secrets of heaven put me to the proof.

רַק בְּסוֹדוֹת הַשָּׁמַיִם נַסּוּנִי.

48

For I have not spoken in secret, and I have not said, Seek me in vain [Isa 45:19].

כִּי לֹא בַסֵּתֶר דִּבַּרְתִּי וְלֹא אָמַרְתִּי תֹהוּ בַקּשׁוּנִי.

49

I will tell you the ordinances of heaven [Jer 33:25],

אֲנִי אַגִּיד לָכֶם חֻקּוֹת שָׁמַיִם.

50

and the secret of the two great lights [Gen 1:16].

וְסוֹד הַמְּאוֹרוֹת הַשְּׁנַיִם.

51

And for the five wandering stars

וְלַחֲמֵשֶׁת כּוֹכְבֵי לֶכֶת.

52

there is an array in my heart.

בְּלִבִּי מַעֲרֶכֶת.

53

I know the secrets of their signs,

יָדַעְתִּי סוֹדוֹת מַזְּלוֹתָם.

54

and their courts and their castles [Gen 25:16],

וְחַצְרֵיהֶם וְטִירוֹתָם.

55

and their going down and their going up⚠3,

וְרִדְתָּם וַעֲלוֹתָם.

56

twelve princes according to their nations [Gen 25:16]⚠4.

שְׁנֵים עָשָׂר נְשִיאִים לְאֻמּוֹתָם.

57

If they have paths in the sky,

אִם יֵשׁ לָהֶם בַּשַּׁחַק דְּרָכִים.

58

in my heart they have their courses;

לָהֶם בְּלִבִּי מַהְלָכִים.

59

and if their circuits are on high,

וְאִם בַּמָּרוֹם מַעְגְּלֵיהֶם.

60

in my thoughts are their spheres.

בְּרַעְיוֹנִי גַּלְגַּלֵּיהֶם.

61

And there is not hidden from me Saturn and its evils,

וְלֹא נֶעְלַם מִמֶּנִּי שַׁבְּתַי וְרָעוֹתָיו.

62

and Jupiter and its blessings,

וְצֶדֶק וְטוֹבוֹתָיו.

63

and Mars and its wars,

וּמַאְדִּים וּמִלְחֲמוֹתָיו.

64

and the sun and its dominions,

וְהַשֶּׁמֶשׁ וּמֶמְשְׁלוֹתָיו.

65

and Venus and its delights,

וְנֹגַהּ וַחֲמוּדוֹתָיו.

66

and Mercury and its wisdoms,

וְכוֹכַב חַמָּה וְחָכְמוֹתָיו.

67

and the moon and its births⚠5,

וְסַהַר וְתוֹלְדוֹתָיו.

68

and the secret of the sphere and its degrees,

וְסוֹד הַגַּלְגַּל וּמַעֲלוֹתָיו.

69

and the ascendant and its signs⚠6,

וְהַצּוֹמֵחַ וּמַזְּלוֹתָיו.

70

and its chords and its pegs⚠7,

וּמֵיתָרָיו וְיִתְדוֹתָיו.

71

upon its four feet [Exod 25:12].

עַל אַרְבַּע פַּעֲמוֹתָיו.

72

And the sign of the Ram⚠8

וּמַזַּל טָלֶה.

73

hangs on the ropes of my mind;

בְּחַבְלֵי שִׂכְלִי יִתָּלֶה.

74

and the horns of the Bull

וְקַרְנֵי הַשּׁוֹר.

75

I bind without cords [Ps 118:27].

בְּלִי עֲבוֹת אֶקְשֹׁר.

76

And I have left the Twins

וְעָזַבְתִּי תְאוֹמִים.

77

orphans in their mother's lifetime.

בְּחַיֵּי הָאֵם יְתוֹמִים.

78

And the height of the Crab

וְגֹבַהּ סַרְטָן.

79

is small beside my rank.

לְמַעֲלָתִי יִקְטַן.

80

And the sign of the Lion

וּמַזַּל אַרְיֵה.

81

at the dread of me is sick and faint [Dan 8:27].

הוּא לְאֵימָתִי חוֹלֶה וְנִהְיֶה.

82

And with me the Virgin

וְאִתִּי בְתוּלָה.

83

is taken as a wife in the chamber of my heart⚠9.

בַּחֲדַר לִבִּי בְעוּלָה.

84

And I count the Scales

וְחָשַׁבְתִּי מֹאזְנַיִם.

85

as the small dust of the scales [Isa 40:15].

כְּשַׁחַק מֹאזְנַיִם.

86

And the Scorpion is bitten

וְנָשַׁךְ לְעַקְרָב.

87

by the scorpion of my tongue.

מִלְּשׁוֹנִי עַקְרָב.

88

And he who handles the bow [Amos 2:15]

וְתוֹפֵשׂ הַקֶּשֶׁת.

89

is afraid to come near me [Exod 34:30].

יִירָא אֵלַי מִגֶּשֶׁת.

90

And I have torn the sign of the Goat

וְשִׁסַּעְתִּי מַזַּל הַגְּדִי.

91

as one tears a kid [Judg 14:6]⚠10.

כְּשַׁסַּע הַגְּדִי.

92

And beside the waters of my mind,

וּלְמֵימֵי שִׂכְלִי.

93

the waters of the Bucket

נֶחְשְׁבוּ מֵי הַדְּלִי.

94

are counted as a drop from a bucket [Isa 40:15].

כְּמַר מִדְּלִי.

95

And the sign of the Fishes

וּמַזַּל דָּגִים.

96

is caught in my net like fish [Eccl 9:12].

נִתְפַּס בִּמְצוּדָתִי כְדָגִים.

97

I know what is to come from them,

יָדַעְתִּי עֲתִידוֹתֵיהֶם.

98

and I have searched out their hidden things,

וְחָקַרְתִּי צְפוּנוֹתֵיהֶם.

99

and brought their judgments out to the light.

וְהוֹצֵאתִי לָאוֹר מִשְׁפְּטֵיהֶם.

100

I judge by them in righteousness,

אֶשְׁפֹּט עֲלֵיהֶם בְּצֶדֶק.

101

and through them I repair every breach [2 Kgs 12:6].

וַאֲחַזֵּק מֵהֶם כָּל בֶּדֶק.

102

I make men hear awesome things,

וְאַשְׁמִיעַ לִבְנֵי אִישׁ נוֹרָאוֹת.

103

and words like the words of prophecies.

וּדְּבָרִים כְּדִבְרֵי נְבוּאוֹת.

104

And I tell every man what will befall him,

וְאַגִּיד לְכָל אִישׁ אֲשֶׁר יִמְצָא אוֹתוֹ.

105

from the day he came to be

מִיּוֹם הֱיוֹתוֹ.

106

until the day of his death,

וְעַד יוֹם מוֹתוֹ.

107

whether he will prosper or prevail,

אִם יַצְלִיחַ אוֹ יִגְבַּר.

108

and whether he will die at sea or in the desert.

וְאִם יָמוּת בַּיָּם אוֹ בַמִּדְבָּר.

109

I make known to him his fortune and his success,

וְאוֹדִיעֵהוּ מַזָּלוֹ וְהַצְלָחָתוֹ.

110

his going down and his going up,

וְרִדְתּוֹ וַעֲלוֹתוֹ.

111

and the full account of his greatness [Esth 10:2],

וּפָרָשַׁת גְּדֻלָּתוֹ.

112

and all the acts of his power and his might [Esth 10:2].

וְכָל מַעֳשֵׂה תָקְפוֹ וּגְבוּרָתוֹ.

113

The number of his years is cut off [Job 21:21] in my heart,

וּמִסְפַּר שְׁנוֹתָיו בְּלִבִּי חֻצָּצוּ.

114

and on my tongue it is determined [Job 14:5].

וּבִלְשׁוֹנִי נֶחֱרָצוּ.

115

I know when he will grow rich or poor,

וְיָדַעְתִּי מָתַי יַעֲשִׂיר אוֹ יִדַּל.

116

and be brought low or grow great.'

וְיִשְּׁפַּל אוֹ יִגְדַּל.

117

And he took up his parable and said:

וַיִּשָּׂא מְשָלוֹ וַיֹּאמַר:

118

I am a tongue for the events of the times; I reveal to man the hidden secrets.

אֲנִי לָשׁוֹן לְקוֹרוֹת הַזְּמַנִּים אֲגַלֶּה לֶאֱנוֹשׁ רָזִים צְפוּנִים.

119

I tell him what will befall him before it comes to be, and what was before.

וְאַגִּיד לוֹ מוֹצְאוֹתָיו בְּטֶרֶם הֱיוֹתָם, וַאֲשֶׁר הָיָה לְפָנִים.

120

Whether he will prosper, whether grow poor or great, whether he will go backward or forward.

וְאִם יִצְלַח וְאִם יִדַּל וְיִגְדַּל וְאִם יֵלֵךְ לְאָחוֹר אוֹ לְפָנִים.

121

Whether he will grow rich or be dispossessed, and when, and whether he will be granted daughters or sons.

וְאִם יַעְשִׁיר וְיִוָּרֵשׁ וּמָתַי וְאִם יִזְכֶּה לְבָנוֹת אוֹ לְבָנִים.

122

Whether he will live, and whether his days will be long, or whether he will die short of days and years.

וְאִם יִחְיֶה וְאִם יָמָיו אֲרֻכִּים וְאִם יָמוּת קְצַר יָמִים וְשָׁנִים.

123

Whether he will wander far from the city where he dwells, or heap up wealth and riches like the sand.

וְאִם יַרְחִיק נְדוֹד מֵעִיר מְגוּרָיו וְאִם יִצְבֹּר כְּחוֹל עשֶׁר וְהוֹנִים.

124

I make known to him what he has hidden in his heart; and who can understand the hidden thoughts?

וְאוֹדִיעוֹ אֲשֶׁר צָפַן בְּלִבּוֹ וּמִי יָבִין צְפוּנֵי רַעְיוֹנִים.

Uncertain renderings

  • ⚠1 עַד לֹא חֲנוֹתָם — Literally "before their encamping". חנה is "to encamp, to come to rest"; the word is chosen for the rhyme with הֱיוֹתָם. Read as "before they have settled", that is, before they happen. Uncertain.
  • ⚠2 וּבְיָדָיו כְּלִי נְחשֶׁת. וְעָלָיו מַעֲשֵׂי רֶשֶׁת — An astrolabe: a bronze disc with an open-work "net" (the rete) that carries the star pointers. The phrase is that of the bronze grating of the altar, Exod 27:4.
  • ⚠3 וְרִדְתָּם וַעֲלוֹתָם — Probably the technical terms for a planet's "fall" (dejection) and "exaltation", the signs where its power is weakest and strongest; also simply its setting and rising.
  • ⚠4 שְׁנֵים עָשָׂר נְשִיאִים לְאֻמּוֹתָם — Gen 25:16 says this of the twelve princes of Ishmael, "by their courts and their castles". Here it is the twelve signs of the zodiac, with the astrological "houses" as their courts and castles.
  • ⚠5 וְסַהַר וְתוֹלְדוֹתָיו — תולדות is "generations, offspring". The moon was held to rule birth and growth; "its phases" is also possible.
  • ⚠6 וְהַצּוֹמֵחַ וּמַזְּלוֹתָיו — הצומח, "the one that sprouts", is the Hebrew astrological term for the ascendant (the degree of the zodiac rising on the eastern horizon at a given moment).
  • ⚠7 וּמֵיתָרָיו וְיִתְדוֹתָיו. עַל אַרְבַּע פַּעֲמוֹתָיו — יתדות, "pegs", is the Hebrew term (as in Abraham Ibn Ezra) for the four cardinal points of a horoscope: ascendant, midheaven, descendant and lowest point. "Chords" (literally "cords, strings") are probably the lines that join them in the chart. The "four feet" are those of the Ark, Exod 25:12.
  • ⚠8 וּמַזַּל טָלֶה — The twelve signs follow in order, each with a pun or a verse: Ram (Aries), Bull (Taurus), Twins (Gemini), Crab (Cancer), Lion (Leo), Virgin (Virgo), Scales (Libra), Scorpion (Scorpio), the one who handles the bow (Sagittarius), Kid or Goat (Capricorn), Bucket (Aquarius), Fishes (Pisces).
  • ⚠9 בַּחֲדַר לִבִּי בְעוּלָה — Play on בְּתוּלָה (Virgin, Virgo) and בְּעוּלָה (a married woman, one who is no longer a virgin): even the Virgin is his.
  • ⚠10 כְּשַׁסַּע הַגְּדִי — מזל גדי, "the sign of the kid", is Capricorn. Samson tore the lion "as one tears a kid" (Judg 14:6); here the kid itself is torn. In the next lines דְּלִי, "bucket" (Aquarius), meets "a drop from a bucket" (Isa 40:15).
  • ⚠11 יָשִׂים אִישׁ מִכֶּם יָדוֹ על הַחוֹל — Geomancy (Arabic ʿilm al-raml, "the science of the sand"): the diviner makes rows of dots in sand or dust, reduces them to figures, and reads them, here together with the astrolabe and the horoscope.
  • ⚠12 כִּי בוֹ יִוָּדַע הַקֹּדֶשׁ מִן הַחֹל — Pun: חוֹל "sand" and חֹל "profane" sound the same. "By the sand the holy is known from the profane"; the pun comes back in the astrologer's oath.
  • ⚠13 כּוֹכְבֵי עָרֶץ — עֶרֶץ (literally "might") is a poetic name for the heavens, as the note in the Project Ben-Yehuda text explains.
  • ⚠14 כִּי אֵינְכֶם יִשְׁמְעֵאלִים. וְלֹא עֲרֵלִים — Ishmaelites are Muslims; the uncircumcised are Christians. So the visitors can only be Jews.
  • ⚠15 וְעַל שֶׁה פְזוּרָה הֲתִמָּלֵט מִשִּׁנֵי אֲרָיוֹת — Jer 50:17: "Israel is a scattered sheep; lions have driven it away". The edition points מִשִּׁנֵי, "from the teeth of". The same letters can be read מִשְּׁנֵי, "from two lions", that is, Islam and Christendom, which the astrologer has just named.
  • ⚠16 כִּי אִם רוּחֵנוּ בֵּין שִׁנֵּינוּ — Literally "except with our breath between our teeth": barely alive. Compare Job 13:14, "I take my flesh in my teeth".
