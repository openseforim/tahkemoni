שַׁעַר שְׁלשִׁים וּשְׁלשָׁה

בְּשִׁירִים עַל אַלְפָ"א בֵיתָ"א יְקָרִים. מֵהַרֲרֵי שֵׂכֶל גְּזוּרִים.

The Thirty-Third Gate

A silent old man shows a company of poets how to make the "linked" poem, in a sermon and an alphabet of short poems with double-meaning rhymes, and then reveals that he is Ḥever

Gate 33 on Project Ben-Yehuda ↗

About this text

171 lines. One row is one rhyme unit of the rhymed prose, or one line of verse (its two halves set apart). References in [gray brackets] are the translator's identifications of biblical phrases. ⚠ marks an uncertain rendering. Each line number is a link to that line. Hebrew text: Project Ben-Yehuda.

1

Thus says Heman the Ezrahite: I inclined my heart [Ps 119:112],

נְאֻם הֵימָן הָאֶזְרָחִי: נָטִיתִי לִבִּי.

2

while I was still fresh in my greenness [Job 8:12],

בְּעוֹדִי רַעֲנָן בְּאִבִּי.

3

and my help was in me [Job 6:13],

וְעֶזְרָתִי בִי.

4

to hear the riddles of the wise,

לִשְמֹעַ חִידוֹת חֲכָמִים.

5

and to gather from their mouths things fit for heaven⚠1,

וְלִלְקֹט מִפִּיהֶם לְשָׁמִים.

6

and to compound from the powder of their intellect a perfume of spices,

וְלִרְקֹחַ מֵאֲבַק שִׂכְלָם רֶקַח בְּשָׂמִים.

7

to spend my days in good and my years in pleasures [Job 36:11].

לְבַלּוֹת יָמַי בַּטּוֹב וּשְׁנוֹתַי בַּנְּעִימִים.

8

One day, when I was in Mount Seir,

וַיְהִי הַיּוֹם וַאֳנִי בְּהַר שֵׂעִיר.

9

walking about the city,

מִתְהַלֵּךְ בָּעִיר.

10

I saw a company of poets,

וְרָאִיתִי חֶבְרַת מְשׁוֹרְרִים.

11

filling up pearls of speech,

מְמַלְּאִים פְּנִינֵי אֲמָרִים.

12

and pouring rivers of song from their tongues.

וּמְפִיצִים מִלְּשׁוֹנָם נַחֲלֵי שִׁירִים.

13

I drew near to them,

וְקָרַבְתִּי אֲלֵיהֶם.

14

to drink my fill of the rivers of their waters,

לִרְווֹת בְּנַחֲלֵי מֵימֵיהֶם.

15

and to gaze on their delights.

וְלַחֲזוֹת בְּמַנְעַמֵּיהֶם.

16

One of them said to his companions: "Is there one among you who can compose a linked poem⚠2,

וַיֹּאמֶר אֶחָד מֵהֶם לַחֲבֵרָיו: הֲיֵשׁ בָּכֶם מִי יְחַבֵּר שִׁיר נִצְמָד.

17

in which every heart will find delight,

יִהְיֶה בוֹ לְכָל לֵב מַחְמָד.

18

and whose beauty cannot be measured?"

וְיָפְיוֹ לֹא יִמַּד.

19

Some of them asked him about the linked poem.

וַיִּשְאָׁלוּהוּ מִקְצָתָם עַל הַשִּׁיר הַנִּצְמָד.

20

He said to them: "Know that the linked poem

אָמַר לָהֶם: דְּעוּ כִּי הַשִּׁיר הַנִּצְמָד.

21

is one in which the end of every two lines is a single word, and the word does not change,

הוּא אֲשֶׁר כָּל סוֹף שְׁנֵי בָתִּים מִלָּה אַחַת וְהַמִּלָּה לֹא תִשְׁתַּנֶּה.

22

but the meaning is changed."

אֲבָל הָעִנְיָן יְשֻׁנֶּה.

23

When they heard his words,

וּכְשָׁמְעָם דְּבָרוֹ.

24

and understood the plan of his speech,

וְהֵבִינוּ תְכוּנַת מַאֲמָרוֹ.

25

each of them ran in the array of his eloquence,

רָץ כָּל אֶחָד מֵהֶם בְּמַעֲרֶכֶת נִיבוֹ.

26

and composed what God had put in his heart.

וַיְחַבֵּר אֲשֶׁר נָתַן אֱלֹהִים בְּלִבּוֹ.

27

There was an old man there, listening and keeping silent,

וְהָיָה שָׁם אִישׁ זָקֵן שׁוֹמֵעַ וּמַחֲרִישׁ.

28

who did not move from his place [Exod 33:11],

וּמִמְּקוֹמוֹ לֹא יָמִישׁ.

29

like the flint rock [Deut 8:15].

כְּצוּר הַחַלָּמִישׁ.

30

When he saw that they had toiled,

וְכִרְאוֹתוֹ כִי יָגְעוּ.

31

and not reached it,

וְלֹא נָגְעוּ.

32

and did not know the craft,

וְהַמְּלָאכָה לֹא יָדְעוּ.

33

he said to them: "Know that you have ridden a hard road,

אָמַר לָהֶם: דְּעוּ כִּי דֶרֶךְ קָשָׁה רְכַבְתֶּם.

34

drawn from a deep pit,

וּמִבּוֹר עָמֹק שְׁאַבְתֶּם.

35

and hewn from a strong mountain.

וּמֵהַר חָזָק חֲצַבְתֶּם.

36

If you wish to hear the linked poem,

וְאִם תִּרְצוּ לִשְׁמֹעַ הַשִּׁיר הַנִּצְמָד.

37

and the delightful speech,

וְהַנִּיב הַנֶּחְמָד.

38

incline your ears to me,

הַטּוּ אָזְנְכֶם אֵלָי.

39

and listen to my words."

וְהַקְשִׁיבוּ לְמִלָּי.

40

He answered and said: "Praise to God, whose praise is mighty,

וַיַּעַן וַיֹּאמַר: תְּהִלָּה לָאֵל אֲשֶׁר עָצְמָה תְהִלָּתוֹ.

41

and the earth is full of His praise [Hab 3:3]⚠3.

וּמָלְאָה הָאָרֶץ תְּהִלָּתוֹ.

42

Light and glory are His crowns and His diadem,

וְהָאוֹר וְהַכָּבוֹד כְּתָרָיו וְגֻלָּתוֹ.

43

and those who thirst for His goodness drink their fill at the spring of His well⚠4.

וּצְמֵאֵי טוּבוֹ יִרְווּ בְּמַעְיַן גֻּלָּתוֹ.

44

He gives as the healing of every sinner his repentance,

הַנּוֹתֵן רְפוּאַת כָּל חוֹטֵא תְשׁוּבָתוֹ.

45

that his spirit may go up on high, and his return be to the height [1 Sam 7:17]⚠5,

לַעֲלוֹת לְמַעְלָה רוּחוֹ וְהָרָמָתָה תְּשׁוּבָתוֹ.

46

to hide from the anger of the LORD and His wrath,

לְהִסָּתֵר מֵאַף ה' וַחֲמָתוֹ.

47

and to wash his steps in His cream [Job 29:6]⚠6.

וְלִרְחֹץ הֲלִיכָיו בַּחֲמָתוֹ.

48

He makes parting from the impulse a man's good⚠7,

הַנּוֹתֵן טוֹבַת הַיֵּצֶר פְּרִידָתוֹ.

49

and the company of the upright the fruit of his faith;

וְחֶבְרַת הַיּשֶׁר פְּרִי דָתוֹ.

50

to draw from the spring of the intellect and its channel,

לִשְׁאֹב בְּמַעְיַן הַשֵּׂכֶל וּתְעָלָתוֹ.

51

and to find in it balm for the heart and its healing [Jer 30:13]⚠8;

וְלִמְצֹא בוֹ צְרִי הַלֵּב וּתְעָלָתוֹ.

52

to raise his soul from the dust of contempt and its dunghill [1 Sam 2:8],

לְהָרִים נַפְשׁוֹ מֵעֲפַר הַבּוּז וְאַשְׁפָּתוֹ.

53

and to send into the kidneys of desire the sons of his quiver [Lam 3:13]⚠9;

וּלְהָבִיא בְּכִלְיוֹת הַתַּאֲוָה בְּנֵי אַשְׁפָּתוֹ.

54

to be his God's portion and His treasure,

וְלִהְיוֹת חֶבֶל אֱלֹהָיו וּסְגֻלָּתוֹ.

55

and to make His law the choice of his desires and his treasure;

וְלָשׂוּם דָּתוֹ מִבְחַר חֲפָצָיו וּסְגֻלָּתוֹ.

56

in the wars of troubles let His righteousness be his shield [Ps 91:4],

וּבְמִלְחֲמוֹת הַצָּרוֹת יְהִי צִדְקוֹ צִנָּתוֹ.

57

to hide in His shelter from its heat and its cold [Prov 25:13]⚠10;

לְהִסָּתֵר בְּמַחֲסֵהוּ מִפְּנֵי חֲמָתוֹ וְצִנָּתוֹ.

58

to run after his God and not cease from his running,

לָרוּץ אַחֲרֵי אֱלֹהָיו וְאַל יֶחְדַּל מִמְּרוּצָתוֹ.

59

to put an end to the oppression of the impulse and its crushing [Jer 22:17]⚠11;

לְהַשְׁבִּית עשֶׁק הַיֵּצֶר וּמְרוּצָתוֹ.

60

to gather from the fruit of knowledge its understanding and its choicest fruit [Gen 43:11],

וְלִלְקֹט מִפְּרִי הַמָּדָּע תְּבוּנָתוֹ וְזִמְרָתוֹ.

61

and to set out over it his song and his music⚠12;

וְלַעֲרֹךְ עָלָיו שִׁירוֹ וְזִמְרָתוֹ.

62

to go up out of the roaring of sin and its estate⚠13;

וְלַעֲלוֹת מִשְּׁאוֹן הַחֵטְא וְנַחְלָתוֹ.

63

to make it his business, while his only one is still with him⚠14,

וְלִהְיוֹת עִסְקוֹ בְּעוֹד יְחִידָתוֹ.

64

to return to God his only one [Ps 22:21];

וּלְהָשִׁיב אֶל אֵל יְחִידָתוֹ.

65

to fence the vineyard of uprightness and not remove its hedge [Isa 5:5],

וְלִגְדֹּר כֶּרֶם הַיּשֶׁר וְאַל יָסֵר מְשׂוּכָּתוֹ.

66

that in the garden of God every precious stone be his covering [Ezek 28:13]⚠15;

וְלִהְיוֹת בְּגַן אֱלֹהִים כָּל אֶבֶן יְקָרָה מְסֻכָּתוֹ.

67

to subdue his desire under the yoke of the intellect,

וְלִכְבּשׁ בְּעֹל הַשֵּׂכֶל תַּאֲוָתוֹ.

68

and to make the abode of peace his border and his boundary [Num 34:7]⚠16;

וְלִהְיוֹת בִּנְוֵה הַשָּׁלוֹם גְּבוּלוֹ וְתַאֲוָתוֹ.

69

to remove the blemish of wickedness and its corruption [Lev 22:25],

וּלְהָסִיר מוּם הָרֶשַׁע וּמָשְׁחָתוֹ.

70

and to make his anointing with the oil of a good name [Eccl 7:1]⚠17;

וּבְשֶׁמֶן הַשֵּׁם הַטּוֹב יָשִׂים מִשְׁחָתוֹ.

71

with the hands of thought to clear the stones from his highway [Isa 62:10],

וּבִידֵי הָרַעְיוֹן יְסַקֵּל מְסִלָּתוֹ.

72

and to make innocence a support for his house and its terraces [2 Chr 9:11]⚠18.

וְיָשִׂים הַתֹּם מִסְעָד לְבֵיתוֹ וּמְסִלָּתוֹ.

73

Then he will see the splendor of his Maker eye to eye [Num 14:14],

אָז יִרְאֶה זִיו יוֹצְרוֹ עַיִן בְּעַיִן.

74

and flourish like a fruitful bough by a spring [Gen 49:22],

וְיִפְרַח כְּבֵן פֹּרָת עֲלֵי עָיִן.

75

when he searches whence he came and from where,

בְּחָקְרוֹ מֵאֵי זֶה מוֹבָאוֹ וּמֵאַיִן.

76

and recognizes that his end is nothing.

וְיַכִּיר כִּי סוֹפוֹ אָיִן.

77

He will know that in his world he is a guest and a wayfarer [2 Sam 12:4],

וְיֵדַע כִּי הוּא בְעוֹלָמוֹ אוֹרֵחַ וָהֵלֶךְ.

78

and no flow from the honey of its pleasures [1 Sam 14:26] will entice him⚠19.

וְאַל יְסִיתֵהוּ מִדְּבַשׁ תַּעֲנוּגָיו הֵלֶךְ.

79

With the arrows of his intellect he will strike the impulse and shoot it down,

וּבְחִצֵּי שִׂכְלוֹ יַכֶּה הַיֵּצֶר וְיוֹרֵהוּ.

80

and in the good way he will cry to God, and He will teach him [1 Sam 12:23]⚠20.

וּבְדֶרֶךְ טוֹבָה יִצְעַק אֶל הָאֵל וְיוֹרֵהוּ.

81

And then his God will make His light shine,

וְאָז אֱלֹהָיו יָאִיר אוֹרוֹ.

82

and spread over him the cloud of His lightning [Job 37:11]⚠21.

וְעָלָיו יָפִיץ עֲנַן אוֹרוֹ.

83

The angel of His presence [Isa 63:9] will be his messenger and his envoy,

וּמַלְאַךְ פָּנָיו יְהִי שְׁלוּחוֹ וְצִירוֹ.

84

to bind up his wound and his pain⚠22.

לַחֲבשׁ נִגְעוֹ וְצִירוֹ.

85

And if he abstains from desire, he will rejoice at the end of the days of his Naziriteship [Num 6:13],

וְאִם יַזִּיר מִן הַתַּאֲוָה יִשְׂמַח בְּסוֹף יְמֵי נִזְרוֹ.

86

and upon him his crown will flourish [Ps 132:18]⚠23."

וְעָלָיו יָצִיץ נִזְרוֹ.

87

Said the teller: When he had finished his inlaid letter [Song 3:10],

אָמַר הַמַּגִּיד: וְכַאֲשֶׁר הִשְׁלִים אִגַּרְתּוֹ הָרְצוּפָה.

88

and his refined word [Ps 18:31],

וְאִמְרָתוֹ הַצְּרוּפָה.

89

and the drawn sword of his eloquence,

וְחֶרֶב מְלִיצָתוֹ הַשְּׁלוּפָה.

90

and his praise, wound in a turban of glory,

וּתְהִלָּתוֹ אֲשֶׁר בִּצְנִיף כָּבוֹד צְנוּפָה.

91

all the people wondered at the force of his words and his meanings,

תָּמְהוּ כָל הָעָם לִתֹקֶף מִלָּיו וְעִנְיָנָיו.

92

and at the sweetness of his thoughts.

וּלְנֹעַם הֶגְיוֹנָיו.

93

After that he said to them: "I will tell you more: lovely poems,

אַחֲרֵי כֵן אָמַר לָהֶם: וְעוֹד אַגִּיד לָכֶם שִׁירוֹת חֲמוּדוֹת.

94

and linked sayings,

וּמְלִיצוֹת צְמוּדוֹת.

95

with linked words at their beginnings and their ends⚠24,

בְּרֹאשָׁם וְסוֹפָם מִלּוֹת נִצְמָדוֹת.

96

full of wisdom and riddles."

מְלֵאוֹת שֵׂכֶל וְחִידוֹת.

97

And he took up his parable and said:

וַיִּשָּׂא מְשָׁלוֹ וַיֹּאמַר:

98

The letter Alef

אוֹת אָלֶ"ף

99

Faith is the ornament of nobles and princes; she is a queen, and every king is her prisoner.

אֱמוּנָה הִיא עֲדִי רוֹזְנִים וְשָׂרִים וְהִיא מַלְכָּה וְכָל מֶלֶךְ אֲסִירָהּ.

100

My soul was nursed in her bosom, and so I will not remove her from my heart⚠25.

אֲמוּנָה הָיְתָה נַפְשִׁי בְחֵיקָהּ וְעַל כֵּן מִלְבָבִי לֹא אֲסִירָהּ.

101

The letter Bet

אוֹת בֵּי"ת

102

The Tester of hearts [Prov 17:3] searches the secret of your heart; so hold fast always to uprightness, my son.

בּוֹחֵן לְבָבוֹת יַחֲקֹר סוֹד לִבְּךָ לָכֵן בְּישֶׁר הַחֲזֵק תָּמִיד בְּנִי.

103

In Him you will find grace and good when Time changes your joy and gladness into calamity and wailing⚠26.

בּוֹ חֵן וְטוֹב תִּמְצָא בְּעֵת כִּי הַזְּמַן יָמִיר בְּאֵיד גִּילָךְ וְשִׂמְחָתָךְ בְּנִי.

104

The letter Gimel

אוֹת גִּימֶ"ל

Gilead has no balm [Jer 8:22] for your pangs, until you drive out the army of your sin.

Uncertain renderings

  • ⚠1 וְלִלְקֹט מִפִּיהֶם לְשָׁמִים — The edition points לְשָׁמִים, "to (the) heavens". The sense is unclear; it may mean words worth lifting to heaven. It rhymes with חֲכָמִים and בְּשָׂמִים, and a reading close to "spices" may be meant.
  • ⚠2 שִׁיר נִצְמָד — "Linked poem" (shir nitsmad): the poem defines it in the next lines. Each pair of lines ends on the same word used in two senses (homonym rhyme, Arabic tajnīs). The prose speech that follows and the alphabet poems are built this way; the notes give the two senses.
  • ⚠3 עָצְמָה תְהִלָּתוֹ / וּמָלְאָה הָאָרֶץ תְּהִלָּתוֹ — Both lines end in תְּהִלָּתוֹ. Here the two senses are nearly the same ("praise"); the first may play on הלל "to shine" (His splendor). Hab 3:3 "and the earth was full of His praise".
  • ⚠4 וְגֻלָּתוֹ / בְּמַעְיַן גֻּלָּתוֹ — גֻּלָּה is the bowl or knob of a crown or lamp (Eccl 12:6, Zech 4:2), and a spring of water (Josh 15:19 "springs of water").
  • ⚠5 וְהָרָמָתָה תְּשׁוּבָתוֹ — תְּשׁוּבָה is "repentance" in the first line and "return" in the second. 1 Sam 7:17 says of Samuel "and his return was to Ramah" (הָרָמָתָה); here Ramah is read as "the height".
  • ⚠6 וְלִרְחֹץ הֲלִיכָיו בַּחֲמָתוֹ — חֵמָה is "wrath" in the first line. The second uses Job 29:6 "when my steps were washed in cream (חֵמָה)", so the same letters mean "cream", an image of plenty.
  • ⚠7 הַנּוֹתֵן טוֹבַת הַיֵּצֶר פְּרִידָתוֹ. / וְחֶבְרַת הַיּשֶׁר פְּרִי דָתוֹ — Rendered by the sense "the good (against) the impulse is parting from it"; the syntax is compressed. The rhyme splits פְּרִידָתוֹ "his parting" into פְּרִי דָתוֹ "the fruit of his faith (law)".
  • ⚠8 וּתְעָלָתוֹ / וּתְעָלָתוֹ — תְּעָלָה is a water channel (1 Kgs 18:32) and a healing or new skin over a wound (Jer 30:13, 46:11).
  • ⚠9 בְּנֵי אַשְׁפָּתוֹ — אַשְׁפָּה is a dunghill (1 Sam 2:8 "lifts the needy from the dunghill") and a quiver; Lam 3:13 "He sent into my kidneys the sons of His quiver", that is, arrows. Here the man shoots at desire.
  • ⚠10 צִנָּתוֹ / חֲמָתוֹ וְצִנָּתוֹ — צִנָּה is a shield (Ps 91:4) and cold (Prov 25:13). "Its heat" (חֲמָתוֹ) is the heat of the troubles; the word also echoes חֵמָה "wrath" above.
  • ⚠11 מִמְּרוּצָתוֹ / וּמְרוּצָתוֹ — מְרוּצָה is a running or race (Jer 8:6) and, from רצץ, crushing oppression (Jer 22:17 "oppression and crushing").
  • ⚠12 וְזִמְרָתוֹ / וְזִמְרָתוֹ — זִמְרָה is the choice produce of the land (Gen 43:11) and song or music (Exod 15:2).
  • ⚠13 וְלַעֲלוֹת מִשְּׁאוֹן הַחֵטְא וְנַחְלָתוֹ — This line has no partner line with the same end word; all the other lines of the speech come in pairs. A line may be missing in the source. שְׁאוֹן may also be the pit of Ps 40:3 (בּוֹר שָׁאוֹן).
  • ⚠14 בְּעוֹד יְחִידָתוֹ / אֶל אֵל יְחִידָתוֹ — יְחִידָה "only one" is a name for the soul (Ps 22:21). The difference between the two uses is not clear; the first may mean his solitude, or his life while the soul is still in him. Uncertain.
  • ⚠15 מְשׂוּכָּתוֹ / מְסֻכָּתוֹ — Near-homonyms: מְשׂוּכָּה "hedge" (Isa 5:5) and מְסֻכָּה "covering" (Ezek 28:13 "every precious stone was your covering").
  • ⚠16 וְתַאֲוָתוֹ / וְתַאֲוָתוֹ — תַּאֲוָה is "desire" in the first line and "boundary" in the second (Gen 49:26 "to the bound of the everlasting hills"; Num 34:7 "you shall mark out").
  • ⚠17 וּמָשְׁחָתוֹ / מִשְׁחָתוֹ — מָשְׁחָת "corruption, blemish" (Lev 22:25) and מִשְׁחָה "anointing"; same letters, different vowels.
  • ⚠18 מְסִלָּתוֹ / וּמְסִלָּתוֹ — מְסִלָּה is a highway (Isa 62:10 "clear it of stones") and, in 2 Chr 9:11, the terraces or steps that Solomon made for the house of the LORD.
  • ⚠19 אוֹרֵחַ וָהֵלֶךְ / מִדְּבַשׁ תַּעֲנוּגָיו הֵלֶךְ — הֵלֶךְ is a traveller (2 Sam 12:4) and a flow (1 Sam 14:26 "a flow of honey"). The world's pleasures should not tempt the wise man.
  • ⚠20 וְיוֹרֵהוּ / וְיוֹרֵהוּ — יוֹרֵהוּ from ירה: "shoots it" (with arrows) and "teaches him" (1 Sam 12:23 "I will teach you the good and right way").
  • ⚠21 עֲנַן אוֹרוֹ — אוֹר is "light" in the first line and "lightning" in the second, as in Job 37:11 "He spreads the cloud of His lightning".
  • ⚠22 וְצִירוֹ / וְצִירוֹ — צִיר is a messenger or envoy (Prov 25:13) and a pang or pain (Isa 13:8).
  • ⚠23 נִזְרוֹ / נִזְרוֹ — נֵזֶר is the Nazirite's separation (Num 6) and a crown (Ps 132:18 "upon him his crown shall flourish").
  • ⚠24 בְּרֹאשָׁם וְסוֹפָם מִלּוֹת נִצְמָדוֹת — In the alphabet poems that follow, the two lines of each poem start with words that look or sound alike (sometimes one word split into two) and end with the same word in two senses. Each poem's lines begin with its letter. The alphabet is complete, Alef to Tav.
  • ⚠25 אֲמוּנָה ... אֲסִירָהּ — אֱמוּנָה "faith" and אֲמוּנָה "nursed, reared" (Lam 4:5). אֲסִירָהּ "her prisoner" and "I will remove her" (from סור).
  • ⚠26 בּוֹחֵן / בּוֹ חֵן ... בְּנִי / בְּנִי — Start: בּוֹחֵן "Tester" and בּוֹ חֵן "in Him is grace". End: בְּנִי "my son" and בְּ־נִי "into wailing" (נִי, lament, Ezek 27:32).
  • ⚠27 גִּלְעָד / גִּיל-עַד ... גוֹרֵשׁ / גּוֹרֵשׁ — Start: גִּלְעָד "Gilead" and גִּיל־עַד "everlasting joy". End: גּוֹרֵשׁ "drive out" and "cast up" (Isa 57:20, the sea whose waters cast up mire and mud). The person of the first verb is compressed in the Hebrew.
  • ⚠28 דּוֹר שָׁוְא / דּוֹרְשָׁיו ... דוֹרְךָ / דּוֹרְכָה — Start: דּוֹר שָׁוְא "a worthless generation" and דּוֹרְשָׁיו "His seekers". End: דוֹרְךָ "your generation" and דּוֹרְכָה "bending (a bow)".
  • ⚠29 הַצֵּל / הַצֵּל ... הָרִיק / הָרִיק — Start: הַצֵּל "save" and הַצֵּל "the shadow". End: הָרִיק "unsheathe" (Exod 15:9 "I will draw my sword") and הָרִיק "the empty".
  • ⚠30 וְשָׁנִים / וְשָׁנִים ... וַחֲסִידָה / וַחֲסִידָה — Start: שָׁנִים "years" and שָׁנִים "scarlet" (Prov 31:21). End: חֲסִידָה "stork" and "pious (soul)".
  • ⚠31 זֶרַע / זֶה רַע ... זְרוֹעַ / זְרוֹעַ — Start: זְרַע "sow" and זֶה רַע "this is evil". End: זְרוֹעַ "sowing" and "arm"; the first line has the word twice, at the end of each half. The syntax of the second line (who reaps what) is compressed.
  • ⚠32 חֲנִי-נָא / חֲנִינָה ... חֲלִי לָהּ / חֲלִילָּהּ — Start: חֲנִי־נָא "encamp, please" and חֲנִינָה "grace". End: חֲלִי לָהּ "a jewel to her" (Prov 25:12) and חֲלִילָהּ "her flute".
  • ⚠33 טוֹבְעִים / טוֹב עִם ... טָבְלוֹ / טוֹב לוֹ — Start: טוֹבְעִים "sinking" and טוֹב עִם "good with". End: טָבְלוֹ "his dipping" and טוֹב לוֹ "good for him".
  • ⚠34 יִמָּאֲסוּ ... יְקָר / יְקָר — Start: יִמָּאֲסוּ "are despised" and "melt away" (Ps 58:8, from מסס). End: יְקָר "precious thing" and "honor". The second half-line is compressed and the rendering is uncertain.
  • ⚠35 כִּידוֹד / כִּי דוֹד ... כְסוֹף / כְּסוֹף — Start: כִּידוֹד "spark" (Job 41:11) and כִּי דוֹד "for an uncle". End: כְסוֹף "long for" and כְּסוֹף "like the end".
  • ⚠36 לוֹחֵץ / לוֹ חֵץ ... לְמַד / לְמַד — Start: לוֹחֵץ "pressing" and לוֹ חֵץ "it has an arrow". End: לְמַד "learn" and לְ־מַד "as a garment" (2 Sam 20:8 מַדּוֹ לְבֻשׁוֹ).
  • ⚠37 מוֹרִים / מוֹרִים ... מִדִּין / מְדִּין — Start: מוֹרִים "rebelling" and "aiming" (from ירה, to shoot). End: מִדִּין "rich carpets" (Judg 5:10) and מִ־דִּין "from judgment".
  • ⚠38 נְצַפֶּה / נְצַפֶּה ... נְסוּכָה / נְסוּכָה — Start: נְצַפֶּה "we look out" and "we overlay" (with gold). End: נְסוּכָה "poured out" and "woven, spread" (Isa 25:7 "the veil that is spread over all the nations").
  • ⚠39 סוּגָה / סוּגָה ... סוֹדָהּ / סוֹדָהּ — Start: סוּגָה "hedged" (Song 7:3 "hedged with lilies") and "turned back" (from סוג, Ps 53:4). End: סוֹד "council" and "secret". Gen 49:6 "into their council let my soul not come".
  • ⚠40 עָרֹם / עָרוּם ... עֲדֵה / עֲדֵה — Start: עָרֹם "naked" and עָרוּם "wise, crafty". End: עֲדֵה "adorn yourself" (Job 40:10) and "pass over" (Job 28:8 "the lion has not passed over it").
  • ⚠41 פִּנָּה / פִּנָּה ... פּוֹרֵק / פּוֹרֵק — Start: פִּנָּה "he cleared" (Isa 40:3 "clear the way") and "cornerstone". End: פּוֹרֵק "throws off" (a yoke) and "tears apart".
  • ⚠42 צָדוּ / צָדוּ ... צָפוּ / צָפוּ — Start: צָדוּ "they hunted" and "laid waste" (from צדה). End: צָפוּ "they flowed" (Lam 3:54) and "they watched". The second half of the second line is compressed.
  • ⚠43 קְרָב / קְרַב ... קְלָעִים / קְלָעִים — Start: קְרָב "war" and קְרַב "draw near". End: קְלָעִים "slingstones" and "carvings" (1 Kgs 6:32 קִלַּע, carved with open flowers). The worldly house is the thing to avoid.
  • ⚠44 רָאשִׁים / רָשִׁים ... רָדוּ / רָדוּ — Start: רָאשִׁים "heads" and רָשִׁים "poor", which sound alike. End: רָדוּ "they ruled" and "they scraped out" (honey, Judg 14:9).
  • ⚠45 שָׁעַר / שַׁעַר ... שָׁמָּה / שַׁמָּה — Start: שָׁעַר "weighed, reckoned" (Prov 23:7) and שַׁעַר "gate". End: שָׁמָּה "there" and שַׁמָּה "desolation".
  • ⚠46 תַּבַל / תֶּבֶל ... תִכְלָה / תִכְלָה — Start: תֵּבֵל "world" and תֶּבֶל "perversion" (Lev 18:23). End: תִּכְלָה "end, limit" (Ps 119:96) and תִּכְלֶה "you cease". This is the last letter; the alphabet is complete.
  • ⚠47 אֲנִי חֶבֶר וְתוֹךְ פי נֶחְבְּאוּ — נֶחְבְּאוּ "are hidden" sounds like his name חֶבֶר. Isa 11:12 "He will gather the outcasts of Israel and the scattered of Judah".
  • ⚠48 לְכָל רוּחַ ... בְּרוּחַ פִּי — רוּחַ is "wind" (scattered to every wind) and "breath" (the breath of my mouth, Ps 33:6).
