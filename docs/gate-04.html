שַׁעַר רְבִיעִי

בְּשֶׁבַח שְׁנֵי מְשׁוֹרְרִים, מִי יָשִׁיר שִׁירִים יוֹתֵר יְקָרִים

The Fourth Gate

Two poets quarrel before a judge over who is more eloquent: one praises the ant, the other the flea, and they turn out to be Ḥever and his son

References in [gray brackets] are the translator's identifications of biblical phrases. ⚠ marks an uncertain rendering. Each line number is a link to that line. Hebrew text: Project Ben-Yehuda.

1

(The poem of the ant and the poem of the flea)

(שִׁיר הַנְּמָלָה וְשִׁיר הַפַּרְעשׁ)

2

Thus says Heman the Ezrahite: My eye saw, in the days of my youth

נְאֻם הֵימָן הָאֶזְרָחִי: רָאֲתָה עֵינִי בִּימֵי נַעֲרוּתִי.

3

and the time of my boyhood,

וּזְמַן יַלְדוּתִי.

4

two Hebrew men striving [Exod 2:13],

שְׁנֵי אֲנָשִׁים עִבְרִים נִצִּים.

5

like warriors armed for war,

כְּגִבּוֹרִים חֲלוּצִים.

6

with forceful matters in their mouths,

בְּפִיהֶם עִנְיָנִים נִמְרָצִים.

7

and on their tongues spear and arrows.

וּבִלְשׁוֹנָם חֲנִית וְחִצִּים.

8

To their quarrel the sons of men were gathering,

וּלְרִיבָם בְּנֵי אִישׁ נִקְבָּצִים.

9

running from every corner,

וּמִכָּל פִּנָּה רָצִים.

10

and each man pressing against his brother.

וְאִישׁ בְּאָחִיו נִלְחָצִים.

11

One of them was old; his strength had left him [Judg 16:19].

הָאֶחָד מֵהֶם זָקֵן סָר מִמֶּנּוּ כֹּחוֹ.

12

The other was a young man; his eye was not dim, nor his natural force abated [Deut 34:7].

וְהַשֵּׁנִי בָּחוּר לֹא כָהֲתָה עֵינוֹ וְלֹא נָס לֵחֹה.

13

The two of them were like two lions fighting with strong hands,

וּשְׁנֵיהֶם כִּשְׁנֵי אֲרָיוֹת נִלְחָמִים בְּחֹזֶק יָדַיִם.

14

or like two eagles raging at each other in the midst of the heavens.

אוֹ כִשְׁנֵי נְשָׁרִים יִתְמַרְמְרוּ זֶה אֶל זֶה בַּחֲצִי הַשָּׁמַיִם.

15

This one would say: "I am the Lord's [Isa 44:5];

זֶה יֹאמַר לַה' אָנִי.

16

from the womb He called me [Isa 49:1],

וּמִבֶּטֶן קְרָאָנִי.

17

and created me to compose eloquence."

וּלְחַבֵּר הַמְּלִיצוֹת בְּרָאָנִי.

18

And that one exalted himself, and walked on the circle of the heavens [Job 22:14],

וְזֶה יִתְגָּאֶה וְעַל חוּג הַשָּׁמַיִם יַהֲלֹךְ.

19

and lifted himself up, saying: "I will be king" [1 Kgs 1:5].

וִּמִתְנַשֵּׂא לֵאמָר אֲנִי אֶמְלֹךְ.

20

And when the bonfires of the quarrels blazed between them,

וְכַאֲשֶׂר דָּלְקוּ מְדוּרוֹת הַמְּרִיבוֹת בֵּינֵיהֶם.

21

and the torches and the voices [Exod 20:15] went up to the heart of the heavens [Deut 4:11] between the two,

וְעָלוּ עַד לֵב הַשָּׁמַיִם הַלַּפִּידִים וְהַקּוֹלוֹת בֵּין שְׁנֵיהֶם.

22

they came near to the judge, their faces like faces of flame [Isa 13:8],

נִגְּשׁוּ אֶל הַשּׁוֹפֵט וּפְנֵי לְהָבִים פְּנֵיהֶם.

23

and sparks of fire leapt from their eyes,

וְכִידוֹדֵי אֵשׁ יִתְמַלְּטוּ מֵעֵינֵיהֶם.

24

and the case of the two of them came before God [Exod 22:8]⚠1.

וְעַד הָאֱלֹהִים יָבֹא דְּבַר שְׁנֵיהֶם.

25

When they stood before him, they said to him: "If it seems good to our lord, let him search out which of us has the truth,

וּבְעָמְדָם לְפָנָיו אָמרוּ לוֹ: אִם טוֹב בְּעֵינֵי אֲדוֹנֵנוּ, יַחְקֹר לְמִי הָאֱמֶת בֵּינֵינוּ.

26

and let him lay his hand upon us both [Job 9:33]."

וְיָשֵׁת יָדוֹ עַל שְׁנֵינוּ.

27

The judge said: "Tell me the matter of your quarrels,

אָמַר הַשּׁוֹפֵט: הַגִּידוּ עִנְיַן מְרִיבוֹתֵיכֶם.

28

and bring forward your strong arguments [Isa 41:21];

וְהַגִּישׁוּ עַצֻּמוֹתֵיכֶם.

29

and the righteous, his righteousness will help him,

וְהַצַּדִיק יַעַזְרֵהוּ צִדְקוֹ.

30

and the wicked, his wickedness will return to his own bosom."

וְהָרָשָׁע יָשׁוּב זְדוֹנוֹ לְחֵיקוֹ.

31

The elder of them said: "Oh my lord [Gen 43:20], incline your ear to my speech,

אָמַר הַגָּדוֹל מֵהֶם: בִּי אֲדוֹנִי הַטֵּה אָזְנְךָ לְנִיבִי.

32

for I cry out to you of violence [Job 19:7] from my adversary,

וְאֶצְעַק אֵלֶיךָ חֲמַס אִישׁ רִיבִי.

33

who wants to fly through the heavens

הָרוֹצֶה לָעוּף הַשָּׁמַיִם.

34

without wings,

בְּלִי כְנָפַיִם.

35

who seeks to come into battle with me, when he is weary and weak of hands [2 Sam 17:2]."

הַדּוֹרֵשׁ לָבוֹא בְּמִלְחַמְתִּי וְהוּא יָגֵעַ וּרְפֵה יָדַיִם.

36

His adversary said to him: "Behold, pride is your necklace [Ps 73:6] over all of us,

אָמַר לוֹ בַּעַל רִיבוֹ: הִנֵּה עֲנָקַתְךָ גַאֲוָה עַל כֻּלָּנוּ.

37

and from on high you speak to us [Ps 73:8].

וּמִמָּרוֹם תְּדַבֵּר לָנוּ.

38

Now let us know this strength of yours,

וְעַתָּה הוֹדִיעֵנוּ כֹחֲךָ זֶה.

39

and we shall know whether it is fat or lean [Num 13:20];

וְנֵדַע הֲשָׁמֵן הוּא אִם רָזֶה.

40

show us whether your troops are weak or strong,

וְהַרְאֵנוּ אִם גְּדוּדֶיךָ חַלָּשִׁים אוֹ גִבּוֹרִים.

41

and your dwellings, whether in camps or in strongholds [Num 13:19].

וּמִשְׁכְּנוֹתֶיךָ הַבְּמַחֲנִים אִם בְּמִבְצָרִים.

42

And when your hidden things are laid bare,

וּבְעֵת יִגָּלוּ סְתָרֶיךָ.

43

then your words will be tested [Gen 42:16],

אָז יִבָּחֲנוּ דְּבָרֶיךָ.

44

and then your light will break forth like the dawn [Isa 58:8]."

וְאָז יִבָּקַע כַּשַּׁחַר אוֹרֶךָ.

45

His companion said: "And how can you set yourself against me, when on my tongue is the source of understanding and counsel,

אָמַר חֲבֵרוֹ: וְאֵיךְ תַּעֲרֹך אֵלַי וּבִלְשׁוֹנִי מְקוֹר הַשֵּׂכֶל וְהַמּוֹעֵצוֹת.

46

and in my heart the spring of eloquence?

וּבְלִבִּי מַעְיַן הַמְּלִיצוֹת.

47

For I have the power, by the strength of my thought

כִּי יֶשׁ לִי יְכֹלֶת בְּתֹקֶף הֶגְיוֹנִי.

48

and by the eloquence of my tongue,

וּבִמְלִיצוֹת לְשׁוֹנִי.

49

to exalt every despised and lowly thing,

לְרוֹמֵם כָּל דָּבָר נִבְזֶה וְנִקְלֶה.

50

and to bring low every exalted thing.

וּלְהַשְׁפִּיל כָּל דָבָר נַעֲלֶה.

51

And you, if the storms were your camps,

וְאַתָּה אִלּוּ הָיוּ הַסְּעָרוֹת מַחֲנוֹתֶיךָ.

52

and the winds your chariots,

וְהָרוּחוֹת מַרְכְּבוֹתֶיךָ.

53

and the clouds your hosts,

וְהָעֲנָנִים צִבְאוֹתֶיךָ.

54

and the lightnings the bearers of your weapons,

וְהַבְּרָקִים נוֹשְׂאִים כְּלֵי מִלְחֳמוֹתֶיךָ.

55

and the stars, at the light of your arrows,

וְהַכּוֹכָבִים לְאוֹר חִצֶּיךָ.

56

went by the gleam of your flashing spear [Hab 3:11],

יֵלְכוּ לְנֹגַהּ בְּרַק חֲנִיתֶיךָ.

57

their heart would melt at standing before me,

יִמַּס לִבָּם מֵהִתְיַצֵּב לְפָנַי.

58

and your hands would grow slack at matching my thoughts,

וְיִרְפּוּ יָדֶיךָ לַעֲרֹךְ לְהֶגְיוֹנַי.

59

before you could praise an ant,

עַד תּוּכַל לְשַׁבֵּחַ נְמָלָה.

60

though it is despised and poor,

אַף עַל פִּי שֶׁהִיא בְּזוּיָה וְדַלָּה.

61

and make it a name and a praise."

וּתְשִׂימֶיהָ לְשֵׁם וְלִתְהִלָּה.

62

His adversary said to him: "How you have helped him that has no power [Job 26:2]!

אָמַר לוֹ בַעַל רִיבוֹ: וּמֶה עָזַרְתָּ לְלֹא כֹחַ.

63

Will you frighten the cedars of Lebanon with nettle and thorn [Hos 9:6]? How trifling is your question,

הֲתַפְחִיד אַרְזֵי לְבָנוֹן בְּקִמּוֹשׁ וָחוֹחַ? כַּמָּה נִקְלָה שְׁאֵלָתֶךָ.

64

and how light your riddle!

וְקַלָּה חִידָתֶךָ.

65

Now hear what you asked for."

וְעַתָּה שְׁמַע בַּקָּשָׁתֶךָ.

66

And he answered and said of the ant: "A diligent handmaid,

וַיַּעַן וַיֹּאמֶר עַל הַנְּמָלָה: שִׁפְחָה חָרוּצָה.

67

formed out of the dust,

מֵעָפָר קְרוּצָה.

68

armed like a warrior,

כְּגִבּוֹר חֲלוּצָה.

69

and in haste to seek her food.

וּלְבַקֵּשׁ מְזוֹנָהּ נְחוּצָה.

70

She is thin of loins,

הִיא דַלַּת חֲלָצַיִם.

71

light of foot,

קַלַּת רַגְלַיִם.

72

sitting at the opening of the ways [Gen 38:14]⚠2.

יוֹשֶבֶת בְּפֶתַח עֵינַיִם.

73

Black and comely,

שְׁחוֹרָה וְנָאוָה.

74

yet not of the daughters of Eve.

וְאֵינֶנָּה מִבְּנוֹת חַוָּה.

75

She dresses in black,

לוֹבֶשֶׁת שְׁחוֹרִים.

76

and comes out of the holes,

וְיוֹצֵאת מִן הַחוֹרִים.

77

and every day she wakes the dawn,

וּבְכָל יוֹם תָּעִיר שְׁחָרִים.

78

and rises early at the openings of the gates,

וְתַשְׁכִּים בְּפִתְחֵי שְׁעָרִים.

79

to go around the paths,

לְסוֹבֵב נְתִיבוֹת.

80

on the hills and in the plains,

בִּגְבָעוֹת וּבַעֳרָבוֹת.

81

now in the street, now in the squares [Prov 7:12].

פַּעַם בַּחוּץ פַּעַם בָּרְחוֹבוֹת.

82

And all the days of winter she hides herself, if they seek her.

וּבְכָל יְמֵי הַסְּתָו תִתְעַלֵּם אִם יִדְרְשׁוּנְהָ.

83

And to roam after her food,

וְלָשׁוּט אַחֲרֵי מַאֲכָלֶיהָ.

84

her feet do not stay in her house [Prov 7:11].

בְּבֵיתָהּ לֹא יִשְׁכְּנוּ רַגְלֶיהָ.

85

Every day she goes up hills,

בְּכָל יוֹם תַּעֲלֶה גְבָעוֹת.

86

and goes down valleys;

וְתֵרֵד בְּקָעוֹת.

87

she toils

וְתִיגַע.

88

and does not rest;

וְלֹא תֵרָגַע.

89

she runs

וְתָרוּץ.

90

and does not weary;

וְלֹא תָקוּץ.

91

she gathers and is never still,

וְתִלְקֹט וְלֹא תִשְׁקֹט.

92

to seek her living,

לְבַקֵּשׁ מִחְיָתָהּ.

93

and to find food for her household [Prov 31:15].

וְלִמְצֹא טֶרֶף לְבֵיתָהּ.

94

She is the poorest of the poor,

הִיא דַּלָּה מִן הַדַּלּוֹת.

95

and carries great loads;

וְנֹוׂשֵׂאת מַשָּׁאוֹת גְּדוֹלוֹת.

96

from the fields she plucks ears of grain [Deut 23:26],

וּמִן הַשָּׂדוֹת תִּקְטֹף מְלִילוֹת.

97

and makes under the ground caves and holes [Isa 2:19],

וְתָכִין תַּחַת הָאָרֶץ מְעָרוֹת וּמְחִלוֹת.

98

to fill them with produce,

לְמַלֹּאת אוֹתָם תְּנוּבוֹת.

99

and ears full and good [Gen 41:22].

וְשִׁבֳּלִים מְלֵאוֹת וְטוֹבוֹת.

100

Every day she stands at the crossroads [Prov 8:2];

וּבְכָל יוֹם בֵּית נְתִיבוֹת נִצָּבָה.

101

in the evening she comes, and in the morning she returns [Esth 2:14].

בָּעֶרֶב הִיא בָאָה וּבַבֹּקֶר הִיא שָׁבָה.

102

All the days of cold she hides in prison houses [Isa 42:22],

וְכָל יְמֵי הַקֹּר תִּסְתַּתֵּר בְּבָתֵּי כְלָאִים.

103

and comes down wonderfully low [Lam 1:9].

וְתֵרֵד פְּלָאִים.

104

In the days of summer she goes out with those who go out,

וּבִימֵי הַקַּיִץ תֵּצֵא עִם הַיּוֹצְאִים.

105

to buy grain among those who came [Gen 42:5].

לִשְׁבֹּר בְּתוֹךְ הַבָּאִים.

106

And when harvest time arrives,

וּבְהַגִּיעַ זְמַן הַקָּצִיר.

107

and the messenger of the vintage comes,

וּבַא צִיר הַבָּצִיר.

108

she girds her loins with strength [Prov 31:17],

חָגְרָה בְּעֹז מָתְנֵיהָ.

109

and sets out from her dwellings,

וְתִסַּע מִמְּעוֹנֵיהָ.

110

and sets her face toward the threshing floor,

וַתָשֵׂם אֶל הַגֹּרֶן מְגַמְתַ פַּנֶיהָ.

111

and goes out with the laborers

וְתֵצֵא עִם הַפּוֹעֲלִים.

112

to beat out the ears of grain [Ruth 2:17],

לַחְבֹּט מִן הַשִּׁבֳּלִים.

113

and goes beside the reapers [Ruth 2:14]

וְתֵלֵךְ מִצַּד הַקּוֹצְרִים.

114

to gather among the sheaves [Ruth 2:7].

לֶאֱסֹף מִן הָעֳמָרִים.

115

Among the threshing floors she roams until they take pity on her,

וּבְתוֹךְ הַגְּרָנוֹת תְּשׁוֹטֵט עַד יְרַחֲמוּהָ.

116

and among the sheaves she gleans, and they do not shame her [Ruth 2:15];

וּבֵין הָעֳמָרִים תְּלַקֵּט וְלֹא יַכְלִימוּהָ.

117

of their blessing they furnish her [Deut 15:14],

וּמִבִּרְכָתָם יַעֲנִיקוּן לָהּ.

118

and from the bundles they pull out some for her,

Uncertain renderings

  • ⚠1 וְעַד הָאֱלֹהִים יָבֹא דְּבַר שְׁנֵיהֶם — In Exod 22:8 הָאֱלֹהִים is traditionally read as "the judges"; here it stands for the judge the two men come to.
  • ⚠2 יוֹשֶבֶת בְּפֶתַח עֵינַיִם — Gen 38:14, where Tamar sits at Petaḥ-Enaim ("the opening of the eyes", or of the springs) to wait for Judah. The ant sits at the opening of her hole by the road.
  • ⚠3 וְזֶרַע אֵין לָהּ — זֶרַע is both "seed" (grain) and "offspring": the ant has no grain in her house, as a widow has no child.
  • ⚠4 וְהִיא נִמְצֵאת בְּמַחְתֶּרֶת — Exod 22:1 "if the thief is found breaking in (בַּמַּחְתֶּרֶת)". מַחְתֶּרֶת is also a tunnel: the ant is found in her burrow.
  • ⚠5 וּמַצָּתָהּ מְשֻׁמֶּרֶת — Exod 12:17 "you shall guard the matzot". "Guarded matzah" (מַצָּה שְׁמוּרָה) is wheat watched from the harvest so that it does not become leaven; the ant's wheat is guarded in the same way.
  • ⚠6 וְתִגְלֶה מִמְּעוֹנָהּ רַק / לְבֵית לֶחֶם מְמַהֶרֶת — תִּגְלֶה is "goes into exile" and also "comes into the open". Bethlehem (בֵּית לֶחֶם) is read as "the house of bread": she leaves her hole only to hurry to food. The next line goes on with the idea of exile.
  • ⚠7 וְנִשְׂרְפָה הָאֵשׁ מֵאֵש הֲמָסֶיךָ — הֲמָסִים follows Isa 64:1 כִּקְדֹחַ אֵשׁ הֲמָסִים, usually "brushwood" (a word found only there). The sense of the line is uncertain.
  • ⚠8 לֶחֶם רֶשַׁע יִלְחַם. וּבְלִי שֶׁלַח יִלְחַם — The two lines end with the same word יִלְחַם, "he eats" (from לֶחֶם, bread) and "he fights".
  • ⚠9 וְיִקְרָא שֵׁם הַמָּקוֹם הַהוּא מַחֲנָיִם — Gen 32:3, Jacob names the place Maḥanaim, "two camps". The dual form fits the place between the two breasts.
  • ⚠10 בָּא אֵלַי הָעֶבֶד הַכּוּשִׁי — Gen 39:17, Potiphar's wife: "the Hebrew slave came in to me". Here "Hebrew" is changed to "Cushite", the black flea.
  • ⚠11 וְלָקַח לוֹ לְמִשְׁכָּב וּלְמַצֵּבָה — מַצֵּבָה is a pillar or a standing place; the word seems chosen for the rhyme with קֵבָה. The sense is uncertain.
  • ⚠12 וְאוּלָם הוּא בְטֶרֶם צָץ קְטָפוֹ — The subject is uncertain: the flea picks the red "lily" (the blood) before it blooms, or one picks off the flea before he grows.
  • ⚠13 יְקַצֵּר אֶת תְּנוּמָתִי וְאַךְ יַ־ / אֲרִיךְ לֵילִי וְשָׂם רֹאשׁוֹ בְּסוֹפוֹ — The Hebrew splits the word יַאֲרִיךְ ("he lengthens") across the two half-lines; the English does the same. "Sets its head at its end" seems to mean that the night has no end: its start is joined to its close.
  • ⚠14 וְכַאֲשֶׁר הֶחְבִּיר הָאֵל הַמְּלִיצוֹת בֵּינֵיכֶם. הִתְחַבְּרוּ בִידִידוּת שְׁנֵיכֶם — הֶחְבִּיר and הִתְחַבְּרוּ ("joined", "be joined") play on the name חֶבֶר (Ḥever), which the old man reveals at the end.
